UN Endorses New Map Projection for More Accurate Global Representation

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to more accurately represent the true proportions of continents, particularly highlighting Africa's actual size. Sponsored by Togo and strongly backed by members of the African Union, the resolution passed with an overwhelming 164 member states voting in favor, actively encouraging global governments and institutions to adopt it over the traditional Mercator map. The Mercator projection, historically designed for navigation, significantly distorts landmasses by enlarging regions near the poles while shrinking continents like Africa and South America. This landmark UN decision, hailed as an "act of truth" by French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot, aims to correct historical geographical misrepresentations in education and public perception, fostering a more accurate understanding of the world.
