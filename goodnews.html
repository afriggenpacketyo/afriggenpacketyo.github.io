Psychologist Offers Practical Strategies to Combat Performance Anxiety

2026-10-03 05:00:00 EDT

Psychologist Cindra Kamphoff, who works with elite athletes, shares practical tools from her book, "The Confidence Habit," to effectively manage performance anxiety and transform self-doubt into self-belief. Key techniques include "learn, burn, return" for quickly moving past mistakes, where one learns from an error, uses a physical cue to "burn" it, and then "returns" to a confident mindset. She also advocates speaking powerfully with "power phrases" like "I am strong," setting "gold, silver, and bronze" goals to reduce pressure, and fact-checking self-doubt by questioning negative thoughts. These strategies aim to help individuals perform their best in high-stakes situations, emphasizing that confidence is a learnable skill.
