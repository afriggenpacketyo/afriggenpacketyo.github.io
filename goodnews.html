Antarctic krill found in deep-sea hydrothermal vents, prompting scientific inquiry

2026-09-15 12:53:39 EDT

Researchers have discovered Antarctic krill, typically found in surface waters, congregating at deep-sea hydrothermal vents, a surprising habitat for these crucial crustaceans. The findings, published in Communications Biology, suggest these vents may play a significant role in krill reproduction, as the observed krill were reproductive females laden with eggs. Scientists hypothesize that the vents offer a year-round food source of microbes and potentially crucial minerals like manganese, which are scarce in the Southern Ocean, and that warmer vent waters might accelerate embryo development. This discovery raises questions about the krill's adaptability and resilience in a rapidly changing environment, underscoring the need for further research and potential conservation measures for these deep-sea habitats.

Understanding annuity withdrawal penalties and flexibility

2026-09-15 12:27:05 EDT

Accessing funds from annuities without incurring penalties depends on the specific contract terms, as there isn't a universal withdrawal limit. Many deferred annuities have a surrender period, during which withdrawals exceeding a "free withdrawal" provision (often around 10% annually) may incur surrender charges. These charges typically decrease over time. Once the surrender period ends, larger withdrawals are generally possible without contractual penalties, though tax implications may still apply. The flexibility of penalty-free withdrawals can be valuable in retirement for unexpected expenses, providing an alternative to tapping other accounts or borrowing. It's crucial to review individual annuity contracts to understand their specific provisions and how withdrawals might affect guarantees or future income.
