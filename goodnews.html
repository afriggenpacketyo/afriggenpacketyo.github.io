Study Reveals How Fatty Diets Increase Colorectal Cancer Risk Through Gut Bacteria

2026-08-17 02:49:00 EDT

German researchers have established a clear causal link between high-fat, low-fiber "Western diets" and an increased risk of colorectal cancer, particularly in adults under 50. Their study, published in the journal Gut, reveals that these unhealthy diets foster specific gut bacteria that produce deoxycholic acid (DCA), a secondary bile acid directly promoting tumor growth. This groundbreaking research, validated through mouse models and human stool samples, offers vital insights into the escalating mortality rate of early-onset colorectal cancer. Understanding this precise biological mechanism could pave the way for innovative preventative strategies and targeted treatments, offering hope in combating this deadly disease.
