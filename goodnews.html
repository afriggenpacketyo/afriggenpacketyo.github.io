Perseid Meteor Shower Peaks Alongside Partial Solar Eclipse This Week

2026-08-10 21:59:24 EDT

The annual Perseid meteor shower is scheduled to reach its peak between midnight and dawn on August 12 and 13, promising skywatchers a dazzling display with the potential to see over 100 "shooting stars" per hour. This year, the celestial event uniquely coincides with a deep partial solar eclipse, which will be visible in the UK during the early evening, creating an exceptional, albeit unrelated, stargazing opportunity. The Perseids originate from debris left by the Swift-Tuttle comet, burning up as it enters Earth's atmosphere. Optimal viewing conditions are anticipated due to a new moon, which will minimize light pollution and enhance visibility, making it one of the best nights for astronomy.

Study Links Severe COVID-19 to Reactivation of Dormant Viruses

2026-08-10 19:17:48 EDT

A new study suggests that severe COVID-19 may reactivate certain dormant viruses, such as herpes viruses like Epstein-Barr virus and cytomegalovirus, that persist in the body after previous infections. Researchers from Boston Children's Hospital and collaborators analyzed over 200,000 biological samples from 1,154 hospitalized COVID-19 patients, identifying 11 different dormant viruses that reactivated within 40 days of hospital admission. Notably, reactivation of Anelloviridae, a virus carried by most adults and traditionally considered harmless, was associated with long-term physical disability and long COVID. These findings could significantly enhance understanding of long COVID and support the development of future diagnostic tests and treatments, though further research is required to establish causality.
