English Mayors to Gain Powers to Override Local Planning Decisions

2026-08-22 23:10:51 EDT

Mayors across England are slated to receive expanded powers that will allow them to override local council decisions on substantial planning applications, specifically those involving more than 150 homes, over 15,000 square meters of commercial space, or structures exceeding 30 meters in height. This significant policy shift will grant mayors in 13 combined authority areas, including Manchester and Liverpool, similar "call-in" capabilities currently exercised by the Mayor of London. Housing minister Matthew Pennycook asserts these powers are vital for mayors to effectively deliver new homes and regeneration projects, aligning with the Labour government's ambitious goal of constructing 1.5 million homes in England by the decade's end. While the government reassures that local councils will retain authority over most applications, some critics express concern that this could foster a "two-tier system."
