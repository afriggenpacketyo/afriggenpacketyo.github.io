Bipartisan Bill Seeks to Accelerate Clinical Trials for Rare Disease Treatments

2026-10-22 06:30:01 EDT

A new bipartisan bill, the "Faster Clinical Trials Act," aims to significantly speed up the development of treatments for rare diseases by reimagining clinical trial processes. Introduced by Representatives Jake Auchincloss and Morgan Griffith, the legislation seeks to make it easier for patients to participate by bringing trials to their regular doctor's offices, rather than requiring travel to specialized sites. It also encourages the use of computer simulations in early-stage development and aims to create a network of reusable clinical trial sites to cut costs and enrollment time by at least 30%. This initiative, which includes up to $75 million in annual FDA funding, complements a recent FDA plan to be more flexible in evaluating therapies for small patient populations, offering hope for faster drug access.
