Government Proposes Relaxing Disclosure Rules for Childhood Criminal Convictions

2026-11-12 12:24:48 EST

The UK government is proposing reforms to relax the disclosure requirements for certain criminal convictions received as children, aiming to improve employment prospects for rehabilitated young people. Under the new proposals, criminal records for individuals detained under 18 for specific offenses, like affray or violent disorder, would no longer appear for life on employer background checks, instead expiring after five-and-a-half years. This initiative acknowledges that such crimes often stem from "adolescent impulsivity" rather than a persistent criminal propensity. The changes also include an 11-year disclosure period for custodial sentences under two years, promoting rehabilitation and reducing barriers to work for those who have not re-offended as adults.
