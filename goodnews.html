Acclaimed Artist Tony Berlant, Pioneer of West Coast Pop Art, Dies at 85

2026-10-28 20:04:13 EDT

Tony Berlant, a significant figure in the 1960s West Coast Pop Art movement, has died at 85 after a long battle with Alzheimer's disease. Known for his distinctive collages made from shards of printed tin and thousands of nails, Berlant's work blended assemblage, Pop Art, and abstraction, often incorporating vintage metal signs and later, personal photographs. He was also a passionate collector of ancient Southwest pottery and Paleolithic objects, co-curating museum exhibitions and co-founding the Mimbres Foundation. His art is featured in permanent collections at LACMA, the Hirshhorn Museum, and the Art Institute of Chicago, cementing his legacy in the art world as a "painterly collage artist."
