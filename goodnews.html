2026-09-15 12:27:05 EDT

The specific amount you can withdraw without incurring a surrender charge is determined by your individual annuity contract, with a common allowance being around 10% of the annuity's value annually. Exceeding this free withdrawal amount during the surrender period can result in fees. This flexibility is particularly valuable in retirement, providing access to cash for emergencies without fully liquidating the investment or resorting to high-rate borrowing, while allowing the remaining funds to continue growing or support future income payments. Atlantic Hurricane Season Sets 60-Year Record for Lack of Hurricane Activity

2026-09-15 11:44:00 EDT

The Atlantic hurricane season has established a 60-year record for the longest period without a hurricane, as none have formed since its commencement on June 1. This remarkably quiet stretch, unprecedented since modern storm tracking began in 1966, is consistent with forecasters' predictions for a season with below-normal activity. The primary driver behind this phenomenon is a particularly strong El Niño event. El Niño creates above-average sea surface temperatures in the Pacific Ocean and, conversely, increases vertical wind shear in the Atlantic, conditions that are highly unfavorable for the development and intensification of tropical systems into hurricanes, aligning with historical patterns.

Venmo, however, offers more separation, as money received can remain within your Venmo balance rather than automatically transferring to the frozen account. Nevertheless, any transfers involving the frozen bank account, whether incoming or outgoing, would still be affected.
