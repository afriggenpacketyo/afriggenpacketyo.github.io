Judge Approves $7.25 Billion Bayer Settlement for Roundup Cancer Lawsuits

2026-11-20 22:49:09 EST

A Missouri judge has formally approved a $7.25 billion settlement from Bayer, designed to resolve thousands of ongoing lawsuits alleging that its popular weedkiller, Roundup, causes non-Hodgkin lymphoma. The settlement, which the judge deemed "fair, reasonable, and adequate," will provide compensation to both current and future Roundup users diagnosed with the cancer, with individual payouts varying from $10,000 to $165,000. This substantial agreement builds upon Bayer's prior payment of approximately $10 billion in related litigation. Despite the company's continued denial of a causal link between Roundup and cancer, and a recent Supreme Court ruling favoring Bayer on federal labeling, this settlement seeks to definitively conclude years of expensive legal disputes surrounding the product's active ingredient, glyphosate.
