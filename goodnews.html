First Known Adult Tyrannosaurus Rex Footprints Discovered in North Dakota

2026-09-11 10:34:07 EDT

Scientists have announced the discovery of the first known series of footprints from an adult Tyrannosaurus rex in North Dakota's Hell Creek Formation. The 23-foot-long trackway, dating back 66.5 million years, provides a rare glimpse into the movement of a T-rex, estimating its walking speed at 3.5-4.5 mph. This significant find, documented using high-resolution 3D scanning, offers unique behavioral insights beyond what bones alone can reveal. Three of the footprints are slated for recovery and transport to the Denver Museum of Nature and Science.
