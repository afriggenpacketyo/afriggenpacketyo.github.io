Wayfair and Other Firms Fined Millions for Deceptive Sales Practices

2026-10-14 06:28:53 EDT

This article explains that factors like age, gender, and socio-economic background, rather than culture, play a more significant role in criminal behavior. Prätor emphasizes that the vast majority of both German and non-German residents do not commit serious violent crimes and advocates for increased investment in prevention and social skills development to reduce crime across all groups.
