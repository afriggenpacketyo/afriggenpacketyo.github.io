Creditors Can Raise Credit Card Interest Rates After 60-Day Payment Delinquency

2026-08-07 11:33:42 EDT

Credit card issuers can increase your interest rate after a missed payment, specifically if it's more than 60 days late, under a mechanism called a penalty APR. This increase can apply retroactively to existing balances, significantly raising costs. The CARD Act of 2009 requires issuers to disclose this penalty in terms and conditions and provide 45 days' notice before it takes effect. However, they must also review accounts every six months and typically remove the penalty APR after six consecutive on-time payments, offering a path to recovery for borrowers facing financial hardship.
