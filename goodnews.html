Australia Bans Fully AI-Generated Music from Official Charts

2026-08-25 00:54:01 EDT

Australia's official music charts will no longer allow largely or wholly AI-generated tracks to qualify, under new rules announced by the Australian Recording Industry Association (ARIA). The changes, effective August 31, distinguish between AI-generated and AI-assisted music, requiring releases to be "substantially human made" to be eligible. This decision follows a controversy involving an AI-assisted cover that topped the ARIA dance singles chart. ARIA aims to promote human artistry and prevent chart manipulation, aligning with broader initiatives by the International Federation of the Phonographic Industry.
