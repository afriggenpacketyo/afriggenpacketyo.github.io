World's Oldest Shipwreck Discovered Off Turkey's Coast

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have discovered the world's oldest known shipwreck off Kekova Island in Turkey, dating it to the Early Bronze Age, between 4,400 and 4,700 years ago. Although the ship's wooden structure has perished, more than 30 ceramic storage pots, or amphorae, were found scattered on the seabed, remarkably still containing traces of their original contents. Led by Prof. Hakan Öniz, the expedition determined the 10m trade vessel carried cargo across the Mediterranean. The distinctive shape of the amphorae, matching styles from ancient Egypt and the Middle East, allowed for precise dating, offering an unprecedented "time capsule" into ancient maritime trade.
