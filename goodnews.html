New Jersey Enacts Landmark Law Banning Surveillance Pricing to Protect Consumers

2026-07-26 11:08:40 EDT

New Jersey Governor Mikie Sherrill has signed the Fair Price Protection Act into law, making the state the third in the U.S. to prohibit businesses from utilizing shoppers’ personal data to impose individualized prices. This legislation is designed to shield consumers from "surveillance pricing," a practice where factors like online activity, location, and purchasing history arbitrarily alter prices for identical products. Governor Sherrill emphasized that the law prioritizes shoppers by ensuring fairness and safeguarding privacy. Additionally, the act institutes a one-year moratorium on new electronic shelf labels, allowing for a study into their potential impact on consumers and workers, a measure supported by labor unions.
