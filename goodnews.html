New Guidelines Endorse Hormone Therapy for Menopause and Perimenopause Symptoms

2026-10-16 11:01:00 EDT

The American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG) has updated its guidelines, now recommending hormone replacement therapy (HRT) as the first-line treatment for common menopause and perimenopause symptoms like hot flashes and night sweats. This marks the first such change in over a decade, moving away from previous non-hormonal suggestions. HRT, available in various forms, aims to alleviate symptoms caused by declining estrogen and progesterone levels, which can also impact bone density. While not suitable for all women, particularly those with a history of blood clots or breast cancer, newer research supports its safety for many patients when initiated before age 60 or within 10 years of menopause onset.
