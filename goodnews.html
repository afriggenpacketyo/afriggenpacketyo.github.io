UN Advocates for New World Map to Correct Distorted Continental Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has issued a call to replace the widely prevalent Mercator map with the Equal Earth projection, aiming to achieve a more accurate representation of continental sizes. The Mercator map, originally devised in 1569 for nautical navigation, significantly distorts landmasses, particularly exaggerating the size of regions near the poles while making continents like Africa appear disproportionately smaller. While the Equal Earth map successfully corrects these long-standing size discrepancies, it necessitates a trade-off, sacrificing the straight lines crucial for traditional navigation. This initiative highlights the inherent challenges of projecting a spherical Earth onto a two-dimensional chart and seeks to address the psychological impact of cartographic misrepresentation.
