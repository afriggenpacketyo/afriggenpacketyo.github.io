Study Links Severe COVID-19 to Reactivation of Dormant Viruses

2026-08-10 19:17:48 EDT

A new study suggests that severe COVID-19 may reactivate dormant viruses within the body, such as Epstein-Barr and cytomegalovirus, which remain after prior infections. Researchers from Boston Children's Hospital analyzed over 200,000 samples from 1,154 hospitalized COVID-19 patients, detecting 11 reactivated viruses within 40 days of admission. Reactivation of Anelloviridae, typically harmless, was associated with long-term physical disability and long COVID. The findings, published in Nature, could offer crucial insights into long COVID and support the development of future diagnostic tests and treatments, though further research is needed to establish causation.
