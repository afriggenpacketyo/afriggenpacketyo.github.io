Study Suggests Chinese Script Evolved Like a Living Organism Driven by Natural Selection

2026-09-01 00:00:05 EDT

Published in Digital Scholarship in the Humanities, the study highlights how a limited number of core components, such as 日 (sun) and 人 (human), are recurrently combined to generate new characters, with novel elements created only when existing ones were inadequate. This evolutionary pressure resulted in a system where high-frequency components became dominant, leading to simplified forms over time to ease writing, thereby reflecting a linguistic inclination towards maximizing output with minimal effort.
