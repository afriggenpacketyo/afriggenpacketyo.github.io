Understanding and Beating Jet Lag: Science-Backed Strategies

2026-08-05 19:49:46 EDT

Jet lag, the disruptive experience commonly encountered after rapid travel across multiple time zones, occurs when the body's internal clock, or circadian rhythm, struggles to adjust to the new local time. Dr. Nina Rzechorzek and Dr. Anna Edmondson of Cambridge University explain that the master clock in the brain, primarily synchronized by daily cycles of sunlight and darkness, takes several days to reset, leading to symptoms like disturbed sleep, daytime sleepiness, irritability, and poor concentration. Understanding this fundamental biological mismatch is crucial for mitigating its effects. Practical strategies such as strategically adjusting meal times and optimizing exposure to natural sunshine can significantly help realign the body's internal clock, making international travel more manageable.
