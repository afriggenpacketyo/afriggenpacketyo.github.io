UK watchdog proposes mandatory salary transparency for employees

2026-10-28 06:42:39 EDT

The UK's Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) has proposed that employees should have a legal right to know their colleagues' salaries to combat pay discrimination. The commission suggests that employers should be required to respond to salary questionnaires within a specified timeframe. Furthermore, employers should be prevented from asking about current salary during the hiring process to avoid perpetuating historical pay disparities. The EHRC argues that such measures, along with publishing salary details in job adverts, would help address gender, race, and disability pay gaps.
