MLS Finalizes New Playoff Format for 2027-28 Season

2026-11-25 14:33:24 EST

Major League Soccer (MLS) will implement a revised playoff format starting in the 2027-28 season, condensing the field to 16 teams and eliminating the wild card round. The first two rounds will consist of two-leg, home-and-home series, while the semi-finals and final will be single-game matchups hosted by the higher seed. This change aims to increase the intensity of the regular season and address criticisms of the current format, which has been seen as too forgiving in qualifying teams for the postseason.
