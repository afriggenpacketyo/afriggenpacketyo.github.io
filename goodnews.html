Understanding Eligibility and Benefits of Medicare Supplemental Insurance

2026-08-10 12:42:34 EDT

Medicare supplemental insurance, or Medigap, offers crucial additional protection for retired Americans by covering costs not included in Original Medicare, such as deductibles and coinsurance. Eligibility generally requires enrollment in both Medicare Part A and Part B, and Medigap cannot be paired with Medicare Advantage plans. A critical six-month open enrollment period, starting at age 65 with Part B enrollment, guarantees access regardless of health. While Medigap helps mitigate financial risks from frequent healthcare use or unexpected bills, it doesn't cover all services like routine dental or long-term care, and new policies exclude prescription drugs, necessitating careful consideration of options.
