Unsupervised Testosterone Therapy Risks Infertility and Health Issues

2026-08-07 04:47:00 EDT

Unsupervised testosterone therapy, a growing trend fueled by social media and unregulated providers, carries significant health risks, including potential infertility. When the body detects unusually high testosterone levels, it signals the testes to cease natural production, consequently shutting down sperm generation. While legitimate Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT) is prescribed for diagnosed hormonal deficiencies, men using it for performance enhancement without medical oversight risk severe consequences such as blood clotting disorders, cardiovascular stress, and in some cases, irreversible fertility damage. Medical professionals strongly advise addressing lifestyle factors like sleep, alcohol, and stress before considering hormonal interventions.
