2026-11-04 16:55:00 EST

The observatory, designed for a two-year mission to detect cosmic explosions like gamma-ray bursts, continued operations until last month when solar activity dragged it down. A rescue attempt by Katalyst Space earlier this year failed. Swift served as a "first responder" for other telescopes, contributing significantly to understanding various cosmic objects and events. Scientists are now working to find ways to replace its vital observational work, recognizing the inherent challenge of objects in low-Earth orbit eventually returning to Earth.
