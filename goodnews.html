Psychedelic Pop Artist Peter Max Dies at 88

2026-09-17 18:12:19 EDT

Peter Max, the iconic pop artist whose vibrant psychedelic style defined the 1960s, has died at the age of 88. Max's accessible and widely popular work, characterized by bright colors and bold, swirling lines, graced numerous items from posters and album covers to dinnerware and postage stamps. Born Peter Max Finkelstein in Berlin, his Jewish family fled Nazi Germany, and he was raised across China, Israel, and Paris before settling in New York City at 16. Despite his immense commercial success sometimes leading to less recognition among art critics, his art was featured in museum exhibitions and galleries worldwide. His son, Adam Max, remembered his father for his extraordinary creativity, warmth, and the way he perceived beauty and possibility in everything.
