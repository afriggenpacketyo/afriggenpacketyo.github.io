China Proposes Narrowing Scope of Contentious Residential Surveillance Measure

2026-11-06 23:00:09 EST

Chinese lawmakers are currently proposing amendments to the nation’s Criminal Procedure Law, notably including a plan to restrict the application of residential surveillance at a designated location (RSDL). This contentious measure, first introduced in 1996 and significantly expanded in 2012, was originally conceived as a less severe alternative to traditional detention. However, RSDL has increasingly become recognized as the harshest form of detention utilized in state security cases, including those related to terrorism. The proposed narrowing of its scope indicates a potential shift in China's approach to its controversial detention practices, aiming to mitigate concerns regarding its extensive use and implications for individual freedoms and legal due process.
