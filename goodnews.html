Pregnant women in Flint received an initial $1,500 lump sum, followed by $500 monthly until one year after childbirth, primarily allocating funds to essential needs like baby supplies, rent, and food. The study's findings suggest that this direct financial assistance likely improved access to prenatal care and reduced high-risk behaviors during pregnancy, contributing to healthier birth outcomes.
