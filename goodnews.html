The Shofar's Enduring Spiritual Significance in Rosh Hashanah Celebrations

2026-09-11 16:29:31 EDT

The shofar, an animal's horn blown like a trumpet, is the unmistakable spiritual soundtrack of Rosh Hashanah, the Jewish New Year, beginning at sunset Friday. Its use dates back to biblical times, marking significant events like the revelation of the Torah and the fall of Jericho, and serving as a primal call to attention and remembrance. Rabbinic law codified its preparation and complex blowing sequence, while its meaning has been debated for centuries, with interpretations ranging from a mother's cry to a spiritual wake-up call for introspection. The shofar's wordless sound connects individuals to their ancestors and transcends rational thought.
