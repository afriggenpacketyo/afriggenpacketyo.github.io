New Extinct Ice Age Amphibian Discovered in La Brea Tar Pits Collection

2026-08-06 22:22:43 EDT

Researchers at the La Brea Tar Pits and Museum in Los Angeles have announced the discovery of a new extinct ice age amphibian, the spadefoot toad (Spea labreae). This marks only the second ice age amphibian found in the U.S., significantly enhancing understanding of the climate at the end of the last ice age. The discovery was made by postdoctoral fellow Dr. Alberto Cruz while reviewing rarely studied amphibian and reptile collections. Amphibians are crucial climate indicators due to their sensitivity to environmental changes and limited travel. This finding underscores the ongoing scientific value of existing museum collections and the potential for further discoveries.
