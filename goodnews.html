The system requires staff to monitor and grade vital signs, with a high overall score triggering urgent attention. This initiative aims to prevent "corridor care" and save lives, particularly ahead of winter pressures on the NHS. It follows a tragic case where a two-year-old boy died after long waits in a hospital corridor.
