CDC Announces End to Record U.S. Cyclosporiasis Outbreak

2026-09-11 11:36:45 EDT

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) has declared the end of the largest-ever cyclosporiasis outbreak in the U.S., which sickened 12,833 people across 21 states. This foodborne illness, caused by the cyclospora parasite, was primarily linked to shredded iceberg lettuce supplied by Taylor Farms in Mexico. The outbreak contributed to the worst cyclosporiasis season in U.S. history, with 19,595 total cases and two deaths in Michigan. The incident raised concerns about U.S. food safety procedures, corporate consolidation in the food supply, and staffing issues at the FDA.

First Known Adult Tyrannosaurus Rex Footprints Discovered in North Dakota

2026-09-11 10:34:07 EDT

Scientists have discovered the first known series of footprints from an adult Tyrannosaurus rex in North Dakota's Hell Creek Formation, dating back 66.5 million years. The 23-foot-long trackway, consisting of four three-toed footprints, provides a rare glimpse into the behavior of these iconic dinosaurs. Researchers used 3D scanning to document the 3-foot-long prints, estimating the T. rex walked at about 3.5-4.5 mph, similar to a brisk human pace. This significant find, made by a schoolteacher and longtime museum volunteer, offers invaluable insights into the movement of one of Earth's last non-avian dinosaurs.
