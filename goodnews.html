Study Reveals How Rule-Breaking Can Be Contagious, Especially Within Political Groups

2026-11-23 01:33:00 EST

A new study from the University of Amsterdam suggests that rule-breaking can be contagious, particularly when individuals observe others from their own political affiliation engaging in such behavior. In an online experiment, participants were nearly twice as likely to break a rule after seeing someone from their political camp do so, with blatant violations having a much stronger effect than minor ones. A small percentage even escalated the rule-breaking beyond what they witnessed. Conversely, good examples of rule-following had little impact. The research highlights that while individual choices are often seen as independent, they are significantly influenced by the actions of others, challenging perceptions in individualist cultures.

Moderate Earthquake Strikes Off Southern California Coast Near El Segundo

2026-11-23 01:03:41 EST

A magnitude 3.3 earthquake was recorded Sunday evening off the coast of Southern California, near El Segundo, marking the fourth tremor in the region within four days. The epicenter was located a few miles from El Segundo, Manhattan Beach, and Redondo Beach, at a depth of 6.2 miles. Residents as far as Thousand Oaks and Long Beach reported feeling the quake, which was rated as weak on the Modified Mercalli Intensity Scale, with no damage expected.
