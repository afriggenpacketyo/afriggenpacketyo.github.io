This initiative marks a significant step in the decades-long effort to save the critically endangered species, known for its vibrant red, black, and turquoise colors, from extinction. The "head-starting" program, a collaborative effort by state and federal agencies and non-profit organizations, raises young snakes in a protected zoo environment during their most vulnerable stage of life. This approach aims to significantly increase their chances of reaching breeding age, with over 130 more snakes expected to be released in 2027 to bolster wild populations.
