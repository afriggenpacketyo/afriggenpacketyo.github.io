Archaeologists Reconstruct Parthenon's East Pediment After Centuries

2026-11-20 15:03:00 EST

Archaeologists, led by Vinzenz Brinkmann, have successfully reconstructed the 2,500-year-old Parthenon's east pediment, which depicted the birth of Athena. After decades of work and using 3D scanning technology, the team pieced together surviving fragments scattered across museums and identified Roman sarcophagi depicting sections of the pediment as crucial clues. The reconstruction is now exhibited at Frankfurt's Liebieghaus, though Greece withdrew its participation due to the ongoing dispute with the British Museum over the return of Parthenon sculptures. This breakthrough sheds new light on ancient Greek artistry.
