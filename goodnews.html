World's Oldest Shipwreck Discovered Off Turkey's Kekova Island

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have discovered the world's oldest shipwreck off Turkey's Kekova Island, dating it to between 4,400 and 4,700 years old, from the Early Bronze Age. Although the wooden structure is gone, over 30 ceramic storage pots (amphorae) were found, some still containing traces of their original cargo. The 10m-long vessel was a trade ship, and the amphorae's distinctive style helped date the wreck. This significant find offers a unique "time capsule" into ancient Mediterranean trade and seafaring, with recovered artifacts to be displayed in a new underwater archaeology museum.
