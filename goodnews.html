UN Calls for New World Map to Accurately Reflect Continent Sizes

2026-09-05 09:46:02 EDT

The United Nations has passed a resolution, 'Correct the Map,' advocating for the replacement of the widely used Mercator map with a projection that more accurately depicts the true sizes of continents. The Mercator map, created in 1569, significantly distorts landmasses, making continents near the equator appear smaller and those near the poles seem stretched. For instance, Africa is shown as similar in size to Greenland, despite being 14 times larger. The UN adopted the Equal Earth projection to address this discrepancy, recognizing the psychological and geopolitical impact of perceived size on global importance.
