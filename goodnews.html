Chinese Team Discovers World's Oldest Amber in Xinjiang Coal Seam

2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

A Chinese research team, led by Luo Cihang, has unearthed the world's oldest known amber in an ancient coal seam in Xinjiang, dating back 393 to 383 million years ago to the Middle Devonian period. This discovery, detailed in "Science Advances," pushes back the known age of chemically confirmed amber by millions of years, surpassing the previous record from the Late Carboniferous period. The paleontologists were initially skeptical when they found hundreds of microscopic pieces of the fossilized resin during their 2019 expedition. This significant finding provides new insights into ancient ecosystems and the evolution of resin-producing plants during a crucial geological era.
