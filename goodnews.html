UN Advocates for New World Map to Correct Size Discrepancies

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has called for the adoption of a new world map to address the significant size discrepancies present on the predominant Mercator projection. The Mercator map, created in 1569 for navigation, inaccurately represents landmasses, making continents like Africa appear smaller than they are relative to others. The proposed Equal Earth projection offers a more accurate representation of continental sizes but sacrifices navigational straight lines. Cartographers emphasize that accurately depicting a sphere on a flat surface is impossible, leading to inherent trade-offs in map projections and their impact on global perception.
