Japan has launched the Mars Moons Exploration (MMX) mission, aiming to retrieve a sample of moondust from Phobos, one of Mars's tiny, irregularly shaped moons, and return it to Earth by 2031. This ambitious mission seeks to unravel the mystery of how Mars's moons formed, either from an ancient impact or as captured asteroids. The spacecraft will deploy a rover to scout a landing site before collecting a small 10g sample.
