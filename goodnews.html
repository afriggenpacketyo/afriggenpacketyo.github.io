Astronomers Discover Fastest Star Orbiting Milky Way's Supermassive Black Hole

2026-08-19 11:38:57 EDT

Astronomers have discovered S301, the fastest star ever observed, orbiting Sagittarius A*, the supermassive black hole at the center of our Milky Way galaxy. Published in Nature, this research highlights the star's extreme proximity to the black hole, which allows scientists to study how gravity distorts space and time. S301 completes its highly elongated 8.7-year orbit at speeds exceeding 15,000 miles per second, nearly 8% of the speed of light. This groundbreaking discovery offers unprecedented opportunities to understand black hole mechanics, including determining Sagittarius A*'s spin, which could provide crucial insights into the formation of our galaxy.
