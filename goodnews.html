Finland Deploys World's Largest Sand Battery to Tackle Renewable Energy Intermittency

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A town in southern Finland has implemented the world's largest commercial sand battery, a 13-meter-tall facility using 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone to store 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy. Developed by Polar Night Energy, this innovative system provides combustion-free heating for much of the year, significantly reducing greenhouse gas emissions and reliance on fossil fuels. The sand battery addresses a critical challenge of renewable energy by storing excess wind and solar power as heat for extended periods, offering a scalable solution without rare earth materials. This project showcases a groundbreaking approach to sustainable energy storage.
