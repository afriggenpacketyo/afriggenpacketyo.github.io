2026-11-09 15:21:21 EST

This invention added a distinct, elastic, and vocal-like tone to lead guitar playing, profoundly transforming the sound of classic rock and funk after Jimi Hendrix popularized it. His legacy extends beyond the wah-wah, as he also contributed to other music technologies, leaving an indelible mark on modern music.
