Northwest Tribe Leads Efforts to Revive Endangered Sunflower Sea Stars

2026-08-16 05:00:00 EDT

The Samish Indian Nation, in collaboration with University of Washington researchers, is leading a vital initiative to reintroduce endangered sunflower sea stars into their traditional Pacific Northwest territories. Billions of these crucial keystone predators, essential for controlling sea urchin populations and protecting kelp forests, have been decimated by a wasting disease exacerbated by climate change-driven ocean heat waves. Researchers have successfully bred and cured the stars in a lab, with initial small-scale releases showing promising signs of survival. This significant conservation effort intertwines the tribe's deep cultural heritage with scientific preservation, aiming to restore vital marine ecosystems.
