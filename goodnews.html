California Enacts New Law to Combat Luxury Car Tax Evasion via "Montana Loophole"

2026-10-05 22:47:52 EDT

California Governor Gavin Newsom has enacted Senate Bill 1406, a new law designed to combat the "Montana loophole," a scheme used by luxury-car buyers to evade state sales taxes and vehicle registration fees. This practice involved establishing shell companies in states like Montana, which do not impose such taxes. The legislation broadens California's definition of a resident under state sales tax law, holding a company liable for state taxes if at least one member resides in California, and permits tax liability to be imposed on individual business members. This article highlights the state's proactive measures to recover an estimated $10 million annually in lost tax revenue and to prosecute those involved in such evasion schemes. Hong Kong's Diminished Role in China's Gold Trade Reflects Yuan Ambitions

2026-10-05 21:00:05 EDT

Hong Kong's once-dominant role as a crucial transit point for global gold trade destined for mainland China has significantly declined since 2014, following China's relaxation of import restrictions. In 2013, a third of the world's traded gold flowed through Hong Kong, but net gold shipments to the mainland via the city dramatically fell from 1,158 tonnes to a mere 67 tonnes by 2020. This strategic shift reflects China's deliberate move to empower more mainland banks with direct foreign gold purchasing capabilities, aligning with Beijing's broader ambition to internationalize the yuan and lessen its dependence on external financial hubs.
