India's ISRO to Launch New Satellite Enhancing Indigenous GPS Alternative, NavIC

2026-10-30 02:59:00 EDT

India's space agency, ISRO, is set to launch the NVS-03 navigation satellite to strengthen NavIC, the country's indigenous alternative to the U.S. Global Positioning System (GPS). The NVS-03, the third in its next-generation series, will be carried into orbit by the GSLV-F18 rocket from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre. This satellite incorporates an indigenous atomic clock and adds the L1 signal, aiming to provide navigation coverage across India and up to 1,500 kilometers beyond its borders. The initiative underscores India's commitment to self-reliance in space technology, despite previous challenges with atomic clocks and satellite deployments.
