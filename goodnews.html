Research Explores How Rule-Breaking Becomes Contagious, Particularly Within Social Groups

2026-11-23 01:33:00 EST

A recent University of Amsterdam study, involving an online experiment with US adults, suggests that rule-breaking behavior can be highly contagious, especially when individuals observe members of their own political group violating norms. Participants were nearly twice as likely to engage in similar rule-breaking after witnessing it, with blatant transgressions proving more influential than minor ones. The research highlights the "bad apple effect," where a few individuals prone to escalating rule violations can significantly erode group adherence to rules, while positive role models have a surprisingly limited impact on promoting compliance.
