2026 Total Solar Eclipse Offers Rare Viewing Opportunities Across Northern Hemisphere

2026-08-12 03:08:11 EDT

The only total solar eclipse of 2026 will occur on Wednesday, offering a rare celestial spectacle across parts of the Northern Hemisphere. The path of totality, where the moon completely obscures the sun, will be visible in sparsely populated northern Greenland, western Iceland, and northern Spain, the latter experiencing its first total eclipse since 1905. A partial solar eclipse, with up to 90% totality, will be visible to approximately 230 million people across major European cities like London and Paris. Viewers are reminded to use certified solar eclipse glasses for safety, removing them only during the brief two minutes of totality.
