2026-10-16 08:00:08 EDT

This remarkable find significantly challenges long-standing evolutionary theories, including those proposed by Charles Darwin, which posited that flightlessness in birds was solely a secondary adaptation occurring after ancestral species had already developed the ability to fly. The fossil offers compelling evidence that early bird evolution was far more diverse than previously understood, encompassing terrestrial, non-flying niches much earlier in avian history. This discovery suggests that the selective pressure for powered flight was not the exclusive force shaping early avialan skeletal modifications, enriching our understanding of bird evolution.
