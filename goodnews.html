While the ship's wooden structure has not survived, the ceramic storage pots it carried have been found, some still containing traces of their original contents. The trade vessel, approximately 10 meters long, was carrying cargo across the Mediterranean. The age of the wreck was determined by analyzing the pottery styles, which match those found in Egypt and the Middle East from the same period. This discovery surpasses the previous record holder, a wreck off the coast of Greece, and offers a unique glimpse into ancient maritime trade.
