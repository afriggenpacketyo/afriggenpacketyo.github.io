UK Prepares for Best Solar Eclipse Since 1999, Visible Across Europe

2026-08-11 19:17:15 EDT

The UK is set to experience its most significant solar eclipse since 1999 on Wednesday evening, with up to 96% of the Sun's disc obscured by the Moon. This astronomical event, also visible across parts of Europe including Spain, Russia, and Iceland, will peak in the UK between 18:58 and 19:15 BST. Devon, Cornwall, and western Pembrokeshire will witness the fullest coverage. Crowds are flocking to northern Spain for a total eclipse, expecting a minute and a half of darkness. Experts emphasize the importance of using certified solar glasses or pinhole viewers for safe viewing, as direct observation can cause permanent eye damage. The next comparable event for the British Isles won't occur until 2081.
