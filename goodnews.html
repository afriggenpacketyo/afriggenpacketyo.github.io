Betye Saar, Influential Assemblage Artist, Dies at 99

2026-07-27 17:30:17 EDT

Betye Saar, a renowned Los Angeles artist celebrated for her powerful assemblage works that addressed race and history in America, has died at the age of 99. Saar transformed found objects, often depicting racist stereotypes, into potent art pieces that challenged perceptions of Black identity. Her most famous work, 'The Liberation of Aunt Jemima' (1972), became an icon of the Black Arts Movement. Her art explored themes of slavery, Jim Crow, family, memory, and spirituality, influencing generations of artists. Saar's career spanned decades, with her work recognized in major museums worldwide.
