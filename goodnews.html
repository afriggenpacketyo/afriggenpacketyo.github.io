400-Year-Old Shipwreck Padlock Code Cracked by Modern Scanning Technology, Revealing 'ANCE'

2026-11-04 01:12:52 EST

Experts have successfully cracked a four-letter code, 'ANCE,' on a 400-year-old brass padlock recovered from the Ann Francis shipwreck off the Welsh coast. Discovered in 1999 by metal detectorist Peter Hughes, the lock was part of a treasure hoard from a ship caught in a 1583 storm returning from Spain. Using micro CT scanning, experts from Cardiff University and Amgueddfa Cymru solved the mystery, revealing one of 1,296 possible combinations. The padlock, found in excellent condition near Spanish silver coins, likely protected valuable items like documents or textiles, offering a unique glimpse into 16th-century maritime history and daily life.
