FTC Warns "Personalized Pricing" Based on Consumer Data May Violate Law

2026-08-20 12:54:19 EDT

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) is issuing a warning to companies that employ "personalized pricing," a practice where product costs are adjusted based on individual consumer data, indicating such methods could contravene federal law. The FTC's proposed enforcement policy asserts that consumers reasonably expect uniform pricing, and that undisclosed personalized pricing constitutes a deceptive practice. The agency cites examples like grocery stores charging more for milk to households with children or hotels increasing rates for essential travel. Consumer advocates commend the FTC's initiative to combat data-driven discrimination, while also stressing that consumers should not be solely responsible for navigating intricate disclosures.
