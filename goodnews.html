UN Adopts New World Map Reflecting Africa's True Size

2026-09-04 15:44:40 EDT

The UN General Assembly has voted to adopt a new world map, known as the Equal Earth projection, which more accurately reflects the true geographical size of continents, particularly Africa. This resolution, sponsored by Togo and backed by African Union members, aims to replace the long-used Mercator map, which is criticized for distorting sizes by making countries near the equator appear smaller while exaggerating those closer to the poles. Supporters argue that maps are not neutral and profoundly influence global perceptions and understanding of continents' standing. The United States was the sole nation to vote against the non-binding resolution, citing concerns about its underlying purpose.
