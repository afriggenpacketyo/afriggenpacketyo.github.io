Dachshunds: From Picasso's Muse to Political Symbolism in a New Exhibition

2026-10-30 03:57:00 EDT

A new exhibition at Kunsthalle Bremen, "The Dachshund. An Icon off the Leash," explores the surprisingly rich cultural history and political symbolism of the dachshund. Renowned artists like Pablo Picasso, who adopted a dachshund named Lump, have celebrated the breed in their work. Originating in Germany over 300 years ago as "badger dogs," dachshunds have evolved from bourgeois stereotypes to the 1972 Munich Olympics mascot, Waldi, symbolizing a new, democratic Germany. The article highlights how these distinctively shaped dogs have been used in propaganda, faced declining popularity during world wars, and continue to be co-opted in modern political movements, showcasing their enduring and often humorous iconic status.
