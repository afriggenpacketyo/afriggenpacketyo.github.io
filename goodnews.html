From the Civil War's "great turkey movement" to helicopter deliveries in Vietnam and Afghanistan, the holiday has been observed amidst the nation's most significant conflicts. President Lincoln established Thanksgiving as a national tradition during the Civil War, leading to massive homefront efforts to feed Union soldiers. During World War II, turkey sales were restricted for civilians to ensure military supply.
