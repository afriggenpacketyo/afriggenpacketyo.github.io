Four-Winged Dinosaur Fossil Challenges Single Origin of Flight Theory

2026-09-30 05:00:06 EDT

The discovery of a new four-winged predatory dinosaur species, Norellraptor barsboldi, measuring 57cm long, in China's Liaoning province is challenging established theories on the evolution of dinosaur flight. Belonging to the microraptorines, small feathered dinosaurs closely related to birds, this fossil provides fresh evidence that flight may have evolved independently across different dinosaur lineages rather than from a single common origin. The findings, published in Nature Communications, suggest that multiple and independent selective regimes were at play at the origin of winged taxa within Paraves, a group that includes birds and microraptorines, thereby dismissing a shared developmental pattern for all flying dinosaurs.
