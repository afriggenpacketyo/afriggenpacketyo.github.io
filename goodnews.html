Dentists Report Rising Incidence of Teeth Grinding Among Young People Amid Stress

2026-09-13 01:55:19 EDT

Dentists are observing an increase in bruxism, or teeth grinding, particularly among young people, with anecdotal evidence suggesting a rise linked to heightened stress levels in modern life. The article explains that bruxism, which can manifest as clenching, grinding, bracing, or thrusting, often results from the body's "fight or flight" response to stress, impacting the jaw and nervous system. Symptoms include jaw ache, headaches, and worn teeth, with severe cases requiring complex and costly dental work. Treatment options range from mouthguards and physiotherapy to talking therapies and lifestyle changes, with some individuals finding relief through muscle-relaxing injections like Botox.

Denisovan Fossils in China Uncover Evidence of Ancient 'Hunting Paradise'

2026-09-12 22:00:10 EDT

New research, published in Nature, details the discovery of abundant Denisovan fossils in Bianfu Cave, southwest China, significantly expanding the understanding of this extinct human group beyond Siberia. The findings reveal that Denisovans, who diverged from modern humans and Neanderthals hundreds of thousands of years ago, established a stable subsistence pattern in the region. They were highly adapted to local coniferous forests and forest-steppe environments, characterized by specialized hunting and intensive exploitation of animal resources. This discovery provides unprecedented insight into their lifestyle and environmental adaptations, portraying the area as an ancient "hunting paradise" for these archaic humans.
