UN Calls for New World Map to Accurately Represent Continent Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has passed a "Correct the Map" resolution, advocating for the replacement of the widely used Mercator map with the Equal Earth projection. The Mercator map, created in 1569, distorts the true sizes of landmasses, making continents like Africa appear smaller than they are, while regions near the poles seem stretched. Critics argue this distortion has psychological and political effects, implicitly downplaying the importance of certain regions. The Equal Earth map aims to correct area inaccuracies, though it sacrifices straight lines for navigation and true distance measurement.
