Nigerian Government Organizes Mass Wedding for 1,500 Couples to Boost Social Stability

2026-08-08 18:37:00 EDT

The Kano state government in northern Nigeria orchestrated a significant mass wedding for 1,500 couples, a "bold social intervention" designed to support low-income families and foster broader social stability within the region. Each participating bride received a dowry of 200,000 naira ($147) and comprehensive support to establish a small business, alongside essential household items like beds, mattresses, and blankets. Couples underwent thorough screening and counseling prior to the two-day event, marking the second such initiative by Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf's administration. This program directly addresses economic hardship and aims to restore hope and promote dignified family life, particularly for those impacted by ongoing security crises in Nigeria.

Newly Discovered "Coffee Frog" in Costa Rica Faces Threat from Pesticides

2026-08-08 15:48:58 EDT

A tiny, nocturnal "spring frog," measuring less than four centimeters, has been officially identified as a new species endemic to Costa Rica, thriving predominantly in coffee plantations. Initially observed in the 1960s but incorrectly classified, this unique amphibian is notable for its translucent throat, which inflates to produce short, high-pitched calls heard at night. Researchers, however, issue a stark warning: the widespread use of pesticides on these coffee plantations poses a significant and immediate threat to the species. These agrochemicals seep into the soil and aquatic systems, which are vital for the tadpoles' development, alongside other dangers like waste dumping and habitat loss.

2026-08-08 14:40:14 EDT

Kinahan, who has been identified by the U.S. government as a leader of the Kinahan organized crime cartel and sanctioned accordingly, has exhausted his legal appeals against extradition and is now expected to be flown back to Ireland.
