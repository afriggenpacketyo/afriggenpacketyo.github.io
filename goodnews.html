Scientists Discover Blood Biomarker for CTE, Offering Hope for Living Diagnosis

2026-11-13 23:00:33 EST

Scientists at Washington University School of Medicine have identified a blood biomarker, eMTBR-tau243, that shows promise for diagnosing advanced chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) in living individuals. Currently, CTE, a degenerative brain condition linked to repeated head trauma in sports, can only be definitively diagnosed post-mortem. This protein fragment, also elevated in Alzheimer's, was found in higher levels in people with confirmed CTE, correlating with disease severity. While further validation is needed, this breakthrough could enable earlier monitoring of at-risk individuals and facilitate trials for potential treatments. The discovery represents a significant step forward in addressing a major concern in sports and medicine.
