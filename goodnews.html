Ancient Cave Art Suggests First Humans in Ireland Migrated from Wales

2026-09-05 01:52:05 EDT

A new discovery of ancient cave art in south-west Ireland, dating back at least 15,000 years, suggests the first known humans to inhabit the island came from Wales. Researchers believe a "lost land bridge" existed between Pembrokeshire and Ireland during the last Ice Age, offering a viable pathway for hunter-gatherers following large herbivores. This significant finding, aided by advanced artificial intelligence technology in identifying cave sites, predates previous archaeological evidence of human presence in Ireland by approximately 5,000 years, fundamentally rewriting the timeline of early human migration in the region.
