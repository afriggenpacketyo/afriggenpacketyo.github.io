Finland Unveils World's Largest Sand Battery to Tackle Renewable Energy Intermittency

2026-07-27 04:46:51 EDT

Finland has unveiled the world's largest commercial sand battery in Pornainen, presenting an innovative solution to the intermittency challenge inherent in renewable energy. This towering 13-meter facility utilizes 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone to store 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy, capable of meeting a significant portion of the heat demand for 5,000 residents. Developed by Polar Night Energy, the project has substantially reduced greenhouse gas emissions and decreased the reliance on wood chips for heating. The technology, which stores excess renewable electricity as heat in sand for later discharge into district heating networks, is garnering international interest as a sustainable, rare-earth-free alternative, despite facing some scaling challenges.
