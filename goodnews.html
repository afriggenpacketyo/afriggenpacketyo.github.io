United Nations Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Global Representation

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map that more accurately portrays the true size of continents, particularly Africa. This resolution encourages global governments and institutions to adopt the new map, moving away from the historically distorting Mercator projection. The Mercator map, designed for navigation, significantly enlarged landmasses near the poles while shrinking regions like Africa, which is 14 times larger than Greenland.
