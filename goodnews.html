New Research Challenges 4% Retirement Rule, Suggests Partial Annuity Strategy

2026-07-29 10:03:23 EDT

New research indicates that the traditional 4% retirement withdrawal rule might be too risky for many retirees, potentially leading to them outliving their assets. The study advocates for a partial annuity strategy, which involves converting half of one's savings to an annuity upfront or gradually over time. This approach aims to balance guaranteed income with the liquidity and growth potential offered by invested portfolio assets. While full annuitization provides the highest initial income, it lacks flexibility for unforeseen needs. Experts suggest the 4% rule can serve as a starting point, but retirees may benefit from professional financial planning to adjust withdrawals based on market conditions and maximize Social Security benefits by delaying claims.
