NHS England to Offer HPV Self-Testing Kits for Cervical Screening

2026-08-24 19:27:50 EDT

NHS England is launching a new initiative to offer HPV self-testing kits to nearly 4 million women who have missed their cervical screening appointments. This program aims to increase participation in screening, as approximately one-third of eligible women currently do not attend. The self-testing kits are designed to detect high-risk HPV, which is responsible for almost all cases of cervical cancer. Early detection is crucial for effective treatment, and trials suggest this convenient option could significantly boost uptake. Women aged 24 to 64 are invited for cervical screening every five years, with the NHS striving to eliminate cervical cancer by 2040.

U.S. Restaurants Experiment with Tipping Bans to Ensure Fairer Wages

2026-08-24 19:04:17 EDT

A growing number of U.S. restaurants are eliminating traditional tipping models in favor of higher menu prices, aiming to provide more equitable and stable wages for all staff, particularly kitchen employees. Proponents argue this system addresses wage disparities, as tips often favor front-of-house staff and can be influenced by customer biases based on gender, race, or sexuality. However, the transition presents challenges, including customer resistance to higher upfront menu prices and difficulties in retaining service staff accustomed to earning significant tips. Despite "tipping fatigue" among consumers, experts believe widespread elimination of tipping is unlikely due to economic disadvantages.
