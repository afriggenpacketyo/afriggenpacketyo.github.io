Experts Question Efficacy of 'Cycle-Syncing' Workouts for All Women

2026-09-09 19:22:05 EDT

The trend of "cycle-syncing" workouts, which involves adjusting exercise routines based on menstrual cycle phases, has gained significant popularity online. However, researchers and medical experts caution that while some women experience cycle-related changes in symptoms or strength, there's no strong scientific evidence supporting a one-size-fits-all prescriptive approach for all women. Menstrual cycles are highly individual, making blanket advice difficult to apply and potentially leading to a "nocebo" effect or limiting consistent training progress. Experts recommend "cycle-awareness"—understanding one's own body patterns and making intuitive adjustments—rather than strictly following rigid, phase-based rules.
