BepiColombo Spacecraft Nears Mercury After Eight-Year, Six-Billion-Mile Journey

2026-11-20 00:59:56 EST

After an arduous eight-year, six-billion-mile journey, the BepiColombo spacecraft is set to arrive at Mercury, preparing for a perilous 11-minute orbital insertion. This mission aims to unravel major mysteries of the solar system's innermost planet, including the presence of ice in its craters and the origin of its distinctive hollows. Operating in Mercury's extreme environment, with temperatures ranging from 430C to -180C and intense solar radiation, BepiColombo will deploy two orbiters—one from ESA and one from JAXA—to conduct close-up and distant studies. Scientists hope to gain unprecedented insights into Mercury's formation, evolution, and interaction with the Sun.
