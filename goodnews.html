New 'Sponge in the Gullet' Test Recommended for Oesophageal Cancer Screening

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

New draft guidelines in England recommend offering quick "sponge in the gullet" tests to more people with persistent indigestion, to screen for oesophageal cancer. These less invasive tests, performed in GP surgeries, involve swallowing a capsule that releases a sponge to collect cells from the gullet lining for analysis. They can detect Barrett's oesophagus, a condition increasing cancer risk, and could be rolled out across England. The National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) supports this, aiming to provide peace of mind, reduce demand on hospital endoscopy services, and facilitate earlier cancer detection.
