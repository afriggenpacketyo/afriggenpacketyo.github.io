Clinical Trial Offers Hope for Rare Sun Allergy Sufferers with New Drug

2026-10-03 10:10:29 EDT

Emily Pearson, who suffered from erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP), a rare and excruciating sun allergy, has found life-changing relief through a clinical trial for the drug bitopertin. For years, even minimal sun exposure caused "insufferable pain" and severely restricted her life, making everyday outdoor activities a constant source of anxiety. Bitopertin, taken as a once-daily pill, works by targeting and lowering protoporphyrin IX levels, the naturally occurring molecule in the skin responsible for the painful reactions in EPP patients. Now in Phase 3 clinical trials, the medication has dramatically increased Pearson's sun tolerance, allowing her to pursue long-held dreams like running marathons and enjoying a newfound freedom in her daily life.
