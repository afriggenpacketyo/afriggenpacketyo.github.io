China Initiates Construction of Pioneering Supercritical CO2 and Molten Salt Energy Plant

2026-09-22 01:41:58 EDT

China has commenced construction on the world’s first utility-scale combined supercritical carbon dioxide power generation and molten salt energy storage project at the Ruitan demonstration plant in Yantai, Shandong province. Slated for operation next year, this innovative project aims to significantly reduce energy waste and improve grid stability within China's energy infrastructure. The system will utilize surplus electricity from existing coal power units during off-peak hours to heat molten salt for energy storage. Subsequently, during periods of high energy demand, this stored heat will power a supercritical unit that generates electricity using carbon dioxide instead of traditional steam, marking a crucial advancement in sustainable energy solutions and grid management.
