Lucas Museum of Narrative Art Opens in Los Angeles, Showcasing Storytelling Through Diverse Art Forms

2026-09-20 09:28:00 EDT

The newly opened Lucas Museum of Narrative Art in Los Angeles, founded by George Lucas and Mellody Hobson, aims to celebrate the power of storytelling through a vast collection of diverse artworks. While "Star Wars" memorabilia is present, it constitutes only a small portion of the museum's holdings, which primarily focus on "myths" and universal themes across 33 galleries. The museum's creation, a nearly two-decade endeavor involving significant personal investment from Lucas, was driven by a desire to honor illustrators and artists whose work often falls outside traditional art institutions. Founders hope visitors will leave inspired by the idea that "anything is possible" and encouraged to create their own "masterpieces."
