Los Angeles Moves to Regulate Unregulated Drug Phenibut

2026-08-25 19:35:22 EDT

Los Angeles City Council has unanimously voted to draft new regulations for phenibut, an unregulated "gas station drug" gaining popularity. The Cold War-era substance, a central nervous system depressant, is currently easily accessible to minors without age restrictions or warnings, despite not being federally regulated as a dietary supplement. Proposed regulations include restricting sales to individuals under 21, banning sales near schools, and mandating warning labels, addressing concerns about addiction, seizures, and other severe side effects.
