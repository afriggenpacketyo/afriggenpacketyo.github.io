Japan's uncrewed Martian Moons eXploration (MMX) mission successfully launched, aiming to be the first to return samples from a Martian moon to Earth. Scientists hope the mission will reveal insights into the formation of Mars and its moons, contributing to understanding the Solar System's origins and laying groundwork for future human exploration. 2026-10-20 03:04:02 EDT

Maximum charges for minor slip-ups will be capped at £20 on appeal, with the overall maximum remaining £100.
