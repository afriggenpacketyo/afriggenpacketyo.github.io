"Super El Niño" Forecasted to Bring Warmer, Wetter Winter Across Much of the U.S.

2026-10-28 22:20:45 EDT

National weather agencies are forecasting a "Super El Niño" to bring a warmer and wetter winter across much of the continental U.S., with above-average temperatures and precipitation expected in several regions. This natural climate pattern, originating in the tropical Pacific Ocean, significantly alters global weather, increasing risks of coastal flooding on both the Eastern and West Coasts. While these are predictions, they provide a roadmap for cities to prepare and allocate resources, potentially bringing much-needed rainfall to drought-stricken areas. However, experts caution that even heavy rains will likely not resolve long-standing water crises in regions like the Colorado River basin.

Tony Berlant, Influential West Coast Pop Artist, Dies at 85

2026-10-28 20:04:13 EDT

Tony Berlant, an influential artist recognized for his large-scale found-metal collages and a seminal figure in the West Coast Pop Art movement, has died at 85 after a long battle with Alzheimer's disease. Berlant, described as a "master of painting without paint," developed a signature style using shards of printed tin and thousands of nails, often incorporating vintage street signs or personal photographs. His career was deeply embedded in L.A.'s famous Ferus Gallery art scene, and he exhibited alongside prominent artists like Andy Warhol in early Pop Art shows. Berlant was also a passionate collector of ancient pottery and a founding member of the Mimbres Foundation. He is survived by his wife, Helen Méndez, and their daughter, comedian Kate Berlant.
