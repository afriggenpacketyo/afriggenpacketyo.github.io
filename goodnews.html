BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a joint European and Japanese endeavor, has successfully entered orbit around Mercury, marking the first visit to the planet in over a decade. After an eight-year, six-billion-mile journey, the two robotic explorers will separate next month to independently map and study Mercury's dense interior, magnetic field, and surface features. This $2 billion mission aims to unravel many mysteries of the solar system's innermost planet, which poses significant technical challenges due to extreme temperatures. Scientists eagerly anticipate new data streams beginning in the spring, comparing unprecedented measurements from both spacecraft.
