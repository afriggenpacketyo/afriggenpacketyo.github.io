Scientists Uncover Previously Unknown Immune Hubs Within Skull Bone Marrow

2026-08-24 04:17:00 EDT

Researchers at Washington University School of Medicine in St. Louis have identified novel immune "security stations" within the bone marrow of mouse skulls. These lymph node-like structures responded to brain tumors more quickly than distant lymph nodes, suggesting a direct, localized immune defense for the brain. Disrupting these structures in mice with glioblastoma accelerated tumor growth, while stimulating them enhanced tumor rejection. This discovery, published in "Nature," challenges traditional views of brain immunity and opens potential avenues for treating brain diseases without direct brain intervention, though human confirmation is still preliminary.

Three Rescued Beluga Whales Successfully Relocated to SeaWorld San Diego

2026-08-23 23:32:01 EDT

Three rescued beluga whales have safely arrived at SeaWorld San Diego after a 2,500-mile journey from Marineland in Niagara Falls, Canada, which closed in 2024. This relocation is part of an "unprecedented international whale rescue effort" to find suitable homes for 30 belugas from the former park. The whales were transported by a Qatar Airways Boeing 777 cargo plane in specialized units. SeaWorld San Diego now houses six belugas from Marineland, with teams focused on individualized care and observation to ensure their successful transition, though their public debut date remains uncertain.
