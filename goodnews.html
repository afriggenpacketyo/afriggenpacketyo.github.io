Scientists Identify Blood Biomarker for CTE, Offering Hope for Live Diagnosis

2026-11-13 23:00:33 EST

Scientists at Washington University School of Medicine have identified a blood biomarker, eMTBR-tau243, that offers significant promise for diagnosing chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) in living individuals. This discovery marks a crucial advancement, as CTE, a degenerative brain condition prevalent in contact sports, has historically only been diagnosable through post-mortem examination of brain tissue. While the test effectively distinguishes advanced CTE from healthy individuals, further validation is needed for early-stage detection. This breakthrough could revolutionize how people at risk for CTE are monitored and pave the way for clinical trials of new therapeutic drugs.
