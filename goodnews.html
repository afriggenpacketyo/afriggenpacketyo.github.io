Chinese Scientists Discover World's Oldest Amber in Xinjiang Coal Seam

2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

A Chinese research team, led by Luo Cihang, has unearthed the world's oldest known amber in an ancient coal seam in Xinjiang, dating back to the Middle Devonian period, approximately 393 to 383 million years ago. This discovery pushes back the known age of chemically confirmed amber by millions of years, surpassing the previous record from the Late Carboniferous period. Initially skeptical, palaeontologists found hundreds of microscopic amber pieces, providing unprecedented insights into ancient ecosystems and the evolution of resin-producing plants. The findings, detailed in Science Advances, mark a significant contribution to palaeontology.

Understanding Attachment Theory and Pathways to Secure Relationships

2026-07-27 20:21:34 EDT

Attachment theory, a concept suggesting early caregiver bonds shape adult relationships, has gained significant public attention for explaining relational patterns. Developed by John Bowlby, it identifies four styles: anxious, avoidant, fearful-avoidant, and secure, with secure individuals generally faring better in life. While social media popularizes simplified interpretations, experts like Dr. Pascal Vrticka caution against overgeneralization, emphasizing that attachment styles are not fixed and can evolve through new relationships and professional support. The theory helps individuals understand themselves and move forward, highlighting that insecure attachments can also be strengths and that change is possible for healthier connections.
