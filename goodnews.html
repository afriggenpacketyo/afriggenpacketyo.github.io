UN Calls for New World Map to Correct Distortions of Continent Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has passed a significant "Correct the Map" resolution, advocating for the replacement of the widely used Mercator projection with a new map, the Equal Earth projection. The Mercator map, originally created in 1569 for navigational purposes, severely distorts the true sizes of landmasses, making continents like Africa appear vastly smaller than their actual dimensions. Critics argue this cartographic distortion has profound psychological and political implications, implicitly downplaying the importance and resources of certain regions. While the Equal Earth map accurately represents landmass sizes and shapes, it necessarily sacrifices the navigational utility of straight lines, highlighting the inherent challenges of accurately mapping a three-dimensional sphere onto a two-dimensional surface.
