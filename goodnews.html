Historically Significant First Map of Mason-Dixon Line to be Auctioned

2026-10-29 07:28:22 EDT

The original hand-drawn map of the Mason-Dixon Line, a document of immense historical importance, is set to go up for auction, with an estimated value between $800,000 and $1.2 million. Created by Charles Mason and Jeremiah Dixon between 1767 and 1768, the linen-backed map initially resolved a border dispute between the then-colonies of Maryland and Pennsylvania. However, it later became the symbolic boundary between Northern free states and Southern slaveholding states during the 1820 Missouri Compromise, profoundly shaping American identity and foreshadowing the Civil War. The map, which has changed hands multiple times, including being owned by Malcolm S. Forbes, highlights a pivotal division in American history and remains a sought-after artifact.
