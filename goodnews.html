2026-11-12 12:24:48 EST

European Commission Proposes Digital Fairness Act to Combat Manipulative Online Practices

2026-11-12 12:19:00 EST

The European Commission has proposed a Digital Fairness Act (DFA) aimed at giving consumers more control over their online spending and time by targeting manipulative practices. The proposed law would require companies to remind users before free trials end and obtain explicit agreement before charging for subscriptions, making cancellations easier. It also bans "dark patterns" like false scarcity claims and restricts in-game purchases for children. While some industry groups warn of overregulation, consumer organizations and legal experts largely support the initiative, seeing it as a significant reform in consumer protection that could simplify regulations for businesses across the EU.
