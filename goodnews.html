Federal judge temporarily blocks new Postal Service rules impacting mail-in voting

2026-08-28 15:45:51 EDT

A federal judge has temporarily halted the U.S. Postal Service from enforcing new regulations concerning mail-in voting for the upcoming November midterm elections. The judge agreed with states and voting rights groups that the Postal Service likely lacks the constitutional authority to implement such rules, especially given the tight deadline before the elections. The ruling cites the irreparable harm states would face if forced to comply with a potentially unconstitutional regulation with insufficient time for implementation. The Justice Department has stated its intention to appeal the decision.
