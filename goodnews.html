Understanding and Addressing Dry Eye Syndrome, a Common Condition in Women, Especially During Midlife

2026-09-04 05:00:00 EDT

Dry eye syndrome is a chronic condition particularly common in women, with research indicating around half of women in perimenopause and beyond experience symptoms due to hormonal shifts. Optometrists explain that declining androgen levels and changes in estrogen affect the meibomian glands, which produce oil to prevent tears from evaporating too quickly. Factors like screen time, contact lens use, and certain medications can exacerbate the condition. Ignoring symptoms can lead to worsening vision, infection, and corneal scarring, making early consultation with an eye care doctor crucial for managing this condition and improving quality of life.
