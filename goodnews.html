Columbus Day / Indigenous Peoples Day: What's Open and Closed on the Federal Holiday

2026-10-12 10:23:07 EDT

Columbus Day, also widely recognized as Indigenous Peoples Day, is an official federal holiday observed on the second Monday in October, though its recognition and impact vary across the United States. On this day, federal government offices are closed, and the U.S. Postal Service does not provide regular residential or business mail delivery. However, the stock market, including both the New York Stock Exchange and the Nasdaq, remains open for its regular trading hours. The operational status of banks can differ, with some like JPMorgan Chase opening branches but processing online transactions on the preceding business day, while others such as Wells Fargo and Bank of America are closed. Major retailers, including Costco and Walmart, typically maintain their regular business hours.
