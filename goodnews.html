2026-10-10 16:57:29 EDT

This rare and widespread sighting was attributed to unusually strong solar activity, specifically coronal mass ejections (CMEs) from the Sun directed towards Earth. When these charged particles collide with atmospheric gases, they emit the vibrant colors of the aurora. The Met Office indicated that activity levels reached 600nT, making the phenomenon visible across the entire UK, a much rarer occurrence. Experts predict 2026 will be a peak year for aurora-spotting due to the Sun's natural 11-year cycle.
