Eligibility Criteria for Medicare Supplemental Insurance (Medigap) Explained

2026-08-10 12:42:34 EDT

This article outlines the eligibility requirements for Medicare supplemental insurance, also known as Medigap policies. To qualify, individuals generally need to be enrolled in Original Medicare (Parts A and B) and cannot simultaneously have a Medicare Advantage plan. A crucial factor is the Medigap Open Enrollment Period, a six-month window starting when a beneficiary turns 65 and is enrolled in Part B, during which insurers cannot deny coverage or charge more based on health status. The article also notes that rules can differ for those under 65 who qualify for Medicare due to disability, and that state laws may offer additional protections. It emphasizes that Medigap is designed to supplement Original Medicare's costs, not replace it.
