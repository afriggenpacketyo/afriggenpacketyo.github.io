The Rise of Somatic Therapy as a Solution for Modern Stress

2026-09-03 07:00:00 EDT

Somatic psychotherapy, which focuses on tuning into physical sensations to address psychological issues, is gaining significant traction as a solution for modern life's anxieties. This "bottom-up" approach, contrasting with traditional talk therapy, encourages clients to physically process "stuck energy" and "trapped emotions" often linked to trauma. While rooted in theories from the early 20th century, its popularity surged after Bessel van der Kolk's book "The Body Keeps the Score." Despite some scientific skepticism and commercialization by "wellness" ventures, many find value in its emphasis on movement, breathing, and reconnecting with the body, offering an alternative for those seeking relief from chronic stress.
