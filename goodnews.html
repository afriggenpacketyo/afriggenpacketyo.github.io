Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai Awarded Nobel Prize in Chemistry for Asymmetric Synthesis Discoveries

2026-10-07 06:05:54 EDT

Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai have been jointly awarded the Nobel Prize in chemistry for their groundbreaking work on non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis. Their discoveries, including the notable "Soai reaction," address a century-old chemical mystery concerning the spontaneous emergence of homochirality. The Nobel Committee highlighted that their chemical reactions are pivotal for designing pharmaceuticals, recognizing their significant impact on the field. Kagan is affiliated with Université Paris-Sud, while Soai, 95, is with Tokyo University of Science, sharing the 12 million Swedish kronor prize.

WHO Issues New Guidelines Warning Against Obesity Drugs for Young Children

2026-10-07 04:07:54 EDT

The World Health Organization (WHO) has released new global guidelines strongly advising against the use of obesity drugs, including GLP-1 injections like Mounjaro and Wegovy, for children under 10. While some doctors prescribe these off-label and drug companies test them in young children, WHO emphasizes focusing on healthy eating and exercise for this age group. The organization notes that obesity prevalence in children aged 5-9 has quadrupled since 1990, increasing the risk of diseases like type 2 diabetes. WHO will continue to evaluate evidence, but remains cautious about early intervention with drugs without clear long-term efficacy data.
