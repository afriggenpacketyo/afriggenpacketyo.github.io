Forecasters Predict Warmer, Wetter Winter Due to Strong El Niño

2026-10-28 22:20:45 EDT

Forecasters are anticipating a warmer and wetter winter across much of the continental United States due to a strong El Niño event, potentially one of the most significant on record. The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) predicts warmer-than-average temperatures and above-average precipitation in several regions, which could also lead to increased coastal flooding. While El Niño can influence weather patterns, experts caution that these are predictions and not guarantees. The pattern originates in the tropical Pacific Ocean and can bring beneficial rainfall to drought-stricken areas, but also increases the risk of severe storms and wildfires in other regions.
