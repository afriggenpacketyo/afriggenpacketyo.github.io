NASA Spacecraft Discovers Large Moon Crater Formed by Recent Impact

2026-09-16 17:45:43 EDT

NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) has discovered a substantial crater on the moon, larger than the Roman Colosseum, formed by an impact two years ago that initially went undetected. Measuring approximately 728 feet across, this is the largest known impact crater formed in recent times within the solar system. Researchers consider this a rare event, occurring roughly once every 132 years, providing valuable data as NASA plans for lunar bases. The impact churned lunar surface material, with ejected dust and soil thrown at a higher angle than anticipated, and revealed a cold spot around the crater. This discovery challenges previous assumptions about the rate of lunar surface turnover.
