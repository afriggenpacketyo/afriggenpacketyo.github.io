With Christmas drawing near, the United Kingdom faces a notably high probability of experiencing a "white Christmas" in 2026, given that such an event has occurred in 41 of the past 65 years. An official "white Christmas" requires merely a single snowflake to be observed falling at any Met Office weather station on December 25th. Although the last truly widespread snow event was in 2010, every year from 2020 to 2023 technically met this criterion, despite limited ground accumulation. Meteorologists are currently evaluating significant climatic factors, including a record-breaking El Niño and an unprecedented early sudden stratospheric warming event, which could collectively influence winter weather patterns towards colder and drier conditions.

2026-11-24 20:00:24 EST

Marcia Ann Gillespie, a groundbreaking journalist and editor who profoundly influenced American media, has died at the age of 82. Later, Gillespie broke barriers as the first Black editor-in-chief of Ms. magazine, even co-founding Liberty Media for Women to rescue the publication from financial distress. Her enduring legacy is defined by a career dedicated to redefining female narratives, empowering women, especially Black women, and advocating fiercely for justice and self-representation in media.
