Chile Becomes First South American Nation to Eliminate Dog-Transmitted Rabies

2026-09-14 17:49:35 EDT

Chile has been validated by the World Health Organization as the first South American country to eliminate dog-transmitted rabies to humans, marking a significant public health achievement after over five decades of sustained prevention and surveillance efforts. The last reported case of dog-to-human rabies in Chile occurred in 1972. This success is attributed to extensive pet vaccination campaigns, prompt access to post-exposure prophylaxis for exposed individuals, and robust community awareness and education initiatives. The Pan American Health Organization praised Chile for demonstrating what can be achieved through consistent investment in vaccination, surveillance, and access to care, offering valuable lessons for global rabies eradication efforts.

Legendary Fashion Designer Bob Mackie Dies at 87

2026-09-14 17:37:03 EDT

Bob Mackie, the iconic fashion designer whose exuberant style defined Hollywood glamour for decades, has died at 87 from pneumonia in Palm Springs, California. He earned nine Emmy Awards, a Tony Award, and three Oscar nominations throughout his illustrious career, and was inducted into the Television Academy's Hall of Fame.
