New NHS guidelines propose wider use of 'sponge test' for indigestion and esophageal cancer

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

Draft guidelines for the NHS in England recommend offering a quick 'sponge in the gullet' test to individuals experiencing persistent indigestion, as a potential screening method for esophageal cancer. This less invasive procedure involves swallowing a dissolving capsule containing a sponge that collects cells from the gullet lining as it is withdrawn. The test could provide peace of mind and help identify patients who require further investigation, alleviating pressure on endoscopy services. While not identifying every case, the sponge test offers a more accessible way to detect concerning changes, aiding in earlier diagnosis and treatment of potential cancers.
