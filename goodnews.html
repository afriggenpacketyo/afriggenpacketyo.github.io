Kagan and Soai Awarded Nobel Prize in Chemistry for Asymmetry Solution

2026-10-07 06:05:54 EDT

French scientist Henri B. Kagan and Japan's Kenso Soai have been awarded the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for their groundbreaking work on non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis. Their discoveries provided a solution to the long-standing chemical mystery of life's asymmetry, explaining why living systems selectively use one mirror-image version of molecules, such as amino acids.
