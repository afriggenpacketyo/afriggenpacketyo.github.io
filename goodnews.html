Powerful El Niño Leads to Record-Low Atlantic Hurricane Season Activity

2026-09-12 11:26:46 EDT

A powerful El Niño phenomenon is responsible for a record-quiet Atlantic hurricane season, with no hurricanes having formed yet, even though this is typically the peak period. This year marks the longest period without a hurricane in the satellite era, with overall storm activity registering only 7% of normal. Experts attribute this unusual calm to classic El Niño impacts, including record-strong wind shear that disrupts storm formation, dry air that starves developing systems, and downward-pushing winds that suppress growth. This occurs despite record-hot ocean waters, showcasing El Niño's dominant influence. Forecasters caution that even in a quiet season, a single catastrophic storm remains a possibility, urging continued vigilance.
