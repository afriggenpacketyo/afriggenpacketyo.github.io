United Nations Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Representation of Africa

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to more accurately represent the true proportions of continents, especially Africa. This Togo-sponsored resolution, passed with overwhelming support from 164 member states, encourages global adoption over the 16th-century Mercator projection, which distorts landmasses by enlarging regions near the poles. Advocates, including Africa No Filter, celebrated this move as crucial for correcting historical misrepresentations and shaping a more accurate understanding of the world in education and public perception. French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot stated that correcting this geographical distortion is an "act of truth."
