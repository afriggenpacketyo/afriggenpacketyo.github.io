Noah Adams, Beloved Longtime Host of NPR's All Things Considered, Dies at 84

2026-10-18 12:30:00 EDT

Noah Adams, a distinguished writer, broadcaster, and host of NPR's "All Things Considered" for 18 years, has passed away at 84. Adams was celebrated for his ability to guide listeners through major historical events and for his thoughtful, vivid features on rural America and everyday people. Colleagues like Melissa Block and Ira Glass lauded his keen observational skills and unique interviewing style, which inspired Glass's "This American Life." Adams, who authored five books, also co-created the experimental 1981 documentary "Father Cares: The Last of Jonestown," leaving a significant legacy in public radio and journalism.
