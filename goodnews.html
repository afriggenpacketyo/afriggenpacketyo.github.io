New Guidelines May Expand Access to 'Sponge in the Gullet' Tests for Oesophageal Cancer Detection

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

New draft guidelines for the NHS in England recommend offering quick "sponge in the gullet" tests to more individuals experiencing persistent indigestion, as a method to screen for oesophageal cancer. These less invasive tests, which can be performed in GP surgeries, involve swallowing a capsule that releases a sponge to collect cells for analysis, potentially detecting conditions like Barrett's oesophagus. The initiative aims to provide earlier detection and peace of mind for patients, while also alleviating the high demand on hospital endoscopy services. The article notes that while effective, no diagnostic test is foolproof, and long-term monitoring is still crucial.
