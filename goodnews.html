Eli Lilly's Combination Obesity Regimen Shows Enhanced Weight Loss in Mid-Stage Trial

2026-09-30 18:40:47 EDT

Eli Lilly announced that its experimental combination obesity regimen achieved greater weight loss than its leading drug, tirzepatide, alone in a mid-stage trial involving patients with obesity and Type 2 diabetes. The therapy, combining eloralintide (targeting amylin) with a low dose of tirzepatide, led to an average weight loss of up to 23.3%. This result raises hopes for a new blockbuster treatment, especially for those who haven't responded to current GLP-1 products. Despite higher discontinuation rates due to side effects, Lilly plans to adjust dosing for Phase 3 trials, aiming for an optimal balance of efficacy and tolerability.
