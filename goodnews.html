2026-08-31 05:00:00 EDT

A recent study highlights that millions of women are frequently misdiagnosed with recurrent urinary tract infections (UTIs) and consequently prescribed unnecessary antibiotics. Researchers discovered that a substantial majority of women presenting with classic UTI symptoms actually suffer from hormonally driven inflammation of the vulvar region or pelvic floor dysfunction, conditions that necessitate entirely different treatment approaches. The prevalent diagnostic process, often relying on initial urinalysis and immediate broad-spectrum antibiotic prescriptions, frequently overlooks these underlying, non-bacterial causes.
