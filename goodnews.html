Electric Vehicles Linked to Increased Motion Sickness in Some Passengers

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

Some passengers are reporting an increase in motion sickness when traveling in electric vehicles (EVs), a phenomenon attributed by experts to a "mismatch in sensory cues." Researchers suggest that the absence of familiar auditory cues and vibrations from a traditional engine, which typically signal acceleration, combined with the often abrupt deceleration characteristic of regenerative braking, can trigger nausea. While this issue does not affect all EV passengers, it is gaining attention, with some carmakers acknowledging the potential link.
