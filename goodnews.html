Pioneering Same-Day Metagenomic Test Detects Hidden Infections in Critically Ill Children

2026-10-15 20:34:45 EDT

A groundbreaking same-day metagenomic test is revolutionizing the detection of hidden and drug-resistant infections in critically ill children. Used at London's Great Ormond Street Hospital (GOSH), this diagnostic method sequences all genetic material in a sample, identifying bacteria, viruses, and fungi that standard tests often miss. The test proved life-saving for 10-year-old Willow Crowther, whose drug-resistant infection was identified, leading to correct antibiotic treatment.
