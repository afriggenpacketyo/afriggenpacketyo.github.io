Understanding and Managing Health Anxiety: A Personal Account and Expert Advice

2026-10-23 07:14:58 EDT

Hollie Sharman, 25, shares her debilitating experience with health anxiety, which led her to frequent hospital visits convinced she was dying, despite doctors finding no physical ailments. Her anxiety, triggered by a severe panic attack, involved constant symptom-searching online and a profound disconnection from others. Clinical psychologist Dr. Kirren Schnack defines health anxiety as a persistent worry about serious illness when none exists, characterized by "checking" behaviors like body examinations and reassurance-seeking, or "avoidance." Dr. Schnack offers coping strategies, including challenging worst-case thinking, limiting reassurance, burning off anxious energy, grounding techniques, and breathing exercises.

Chinese Scientists Reconstruct Face of Ancient Denisovan Hominids

2026-10-23 07:09:08 EDT

Chinese scientists have successfully reconstructed the face of the Denisovans, a mysterious ancient human species previously known only through DNA and fossil fragments. This groundbreaking work utilized fossils excavated from the Xujiayao archaeological site in northern China's Nihewan Basin between 1976 and 1979. The reconstruction confirms that these ancient human remains belong to the Denisovan lineage, shedding new light on their physical appearance. Denisovans are believed to have inhabited parts of Siberia, East, and Southeast Asia thousands of years ago, making this facial reconstruction a significant step in understanding human evolution.
