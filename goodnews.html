China's producer prices rise fastest in over four years, driven by fuel costs

2026-10-13 23:14:48 EDT

China's producer price index (PPI) saw its fastest growth in over four years in September, rising 5% and exceeding economist expectations. This increase is primarily attributed to higher fuel prices and increased demand for coal for winter heating. While producer prices are accelerating, consumer prices rose by a more modest 0.9%, missing expectations and indicating that domestic demand remains somewhat subdued. The higher PPI suggests a gradual shift away from deflationary pressures, though the economic recovery is described as uneven.
