Singapore inflation rises to nearly two-year high, but below expectations

2026-08-24 01:24:43 EDT

Singapore's inflation rate accelerated to its highest level in nearly two years in July, reaching 2.2% year-on-year, primarily driven by increased energy prices linked to the Iran war. Despite the rise, the figure fell slightly short of economists' expectations of 2.3%. The Monetary Authority of Singapore and the Ministry of Trade and Industry attributed the increase to higher electricity and gas charges, as well as transportation fares, influenced by global energy costs and anticipated increases in imported food prices. Core inflation, excluding private transport and accommodation, also saw a rise. Singapore has implemented economic support packages in response to the ongoing conflict.

SeaWorld describes this as an "unprecedented international whale rescue effort," with the goal of ensuring the welfare of these animals.
