Critically Endangered Vallarta Mud Turtles Seized at Amsterdam's Schiphol Airport

2026-11-27 10:48:00 EST

Dutch authorities have seized 26 critically endangered Vallarta mud turtles found in a passenger's luggage at Amsterdam's Schiphol Airport, en route to Hong Kong. These tiny freshwater turtles, endemic to a small region in Mexico and only formally discovered in 2018, are highly rare with an estimated wild population of only about 300. The turtles were found wrapped in duct tape inside a box and a sock, but all survived. The suspected poaching of these animals could reduce their wild population by nearly 10%, highlighting the severe threat posed by illegal wildlife trade to this species. Authorities are urging the public to report any suspected instances of illegal trade.
