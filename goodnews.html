Linda Mason, Pioneering CBS News Journalist, Dies at 84

2026-10-29 18:55:24 EDT

Linda Mason, a highly respected and pioneering journalist who dedicated 47 years to CBS News, has passed away peacefully at the age of 84, surrounded by her family. Starting her career as a radio desk assistant in 1966, she made history as the first female producer of the 'CBS Evening News,' working alongside legendary anchors Walter Cronkite and Dan Rather. Mason held numerous high-profile roles, including executive producer of 'CBS Sunday Morning' and head of CBS News' standards department. Her distinguished career garnered her 13 Emmy Awards, three Peabody Awards, and an Edward R. Murrow Award, solidifying her legacy as a trailblazing figure in journalism.
