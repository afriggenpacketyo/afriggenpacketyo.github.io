Criminal gangs had dumped nearly 40,000 tonnes of waste, piled up to 15 feet high, in 2023, making it the first supersite in England to receive central government funding for its clear-up. While campaigners welcome the completion, concerns remain about criminal gangs returning. The Environment Agency has extended access restrictions and dug ditches, implementing a joint "trigger plan" with police to prevent future dumping. Conservationists note it will take decades for ancient woodland species to recover, though new habitats may emerge.
