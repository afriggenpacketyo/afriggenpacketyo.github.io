2026-10-13 05:00:00 EDT

Seabirds deposit nutrient-rich guano on land, which then washes into the ocean, fertilizing the entire marine food web and benefiting plankton, fish, manta rays, and corals. Conservation efforts are now focused on restoring seabird populations by removing invasive species and non-native vegetation, aiming to reestablish these natural functions. This approach provides vulnerable coral reefs with a crucial advantage to adapt and recover from rising ocean temperatures and climate change impacts.

2026-10-13 02:00:08 EDT

A collaborative astronomy team from China and the U.S. may have identified the remnant of a cosmic "gold factory" within the Milky Way: a mysterious glow of high-energy light. Scientists hypothesize this glow is the aftermath of a violent collision between two orbiting neutron stars, an event powerful enough to briefly outshine Jupiter. Such a cataclysmic smash-up would have ejected atoms and a flood of neutrons, which then fused to form heavy elements like gold, platinum, and uranium, before the remnants collapsed into a black hole. The small, bright cloud suggests this rare event occurred less than a millennium ago.
