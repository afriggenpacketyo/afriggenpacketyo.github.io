Understanding "Hang-xiety": The Real Link Between Hangovers and Anxiety

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

"Hang-xiety," the anxiety that often accompanies a hangover, is a recognized phenomenon rooted in how alcohol affects the brain and nervous system. Alcohol initially enhances GABA, a calming neurotransmitter, and suppresses glutamate, which is involved in alertness, leading to relaxation. However, as alcohol leaves the system, the brain overcompensates, swinging towards increased alertness and arousal, triggering anxiety. Studies indicate that not everyone experiences hang-xiety, with existing psychological traits like shyness potentially playing a significant role. Experts advise against using alcohol to manage stress and suggest reframing these anxious thoughts as a temporary physiological response rather than a social catastrophe.
