UK Government Outlines Timetable for Dismantling Leasehold System in England and Wales

2026-11-19 12:49:48 EST

The UK government has announced a timetable to dismantle the leasehold system in England and Wales, aiming for most new flats to be sold as commonhold by around 2029, with a ban on new leasehold flats expected by 2031. Under the new plans, commonhold will allow residents to collectively own and manage their buildings, eliminating ground rents. A £250 cap on ground rents is anticipated in 2028, reducing to a "peppercorn" rate after 40 years. This reform, introduced through the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill, seeks to align England and Wales with other countries and make it easier for existing leaseholders to convert to commonhold, despite concerns from freeholder associations about property rights.
