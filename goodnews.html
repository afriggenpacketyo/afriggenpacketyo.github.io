NASA's Swift Observatory Expected to Re-enter Earth's Atmosphere This Week

While most of the spacecraft is expected to burn up, a small number of dense pieces, including tungsten components and mirror parts, could survive re-entry and potentially impact the ocean, though certain landmasses are within the re-entry zone.
