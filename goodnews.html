BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a collaborative European and Japanese endeavor, has successfully entered orbit around Mercury after an arduous eight-year cruise through the inner solar system. This significant achievement marks the first time in over a decade that a spacecraft has visited the sun's closest planet, and only the third mission ever to reach it. The two linked robotic explorers will soon separate to independently map and study Mercury's dense interior, magnetic field, and various surface features from distinct altitudes. This $2 billion mission is poised to provide unprecedented measurements and unravel long-standing mysteries of the scorching planet, offering invaluable scientific insights.
