2026-09-06 04:47:12 EDT

Positioned 36,000km above the equator, the LMI captures 500 frames per second, processing immense data to pinpoint individual lightning strokes, a key indicator of severe storms. This real-time intelligence, integrated with other meteorological data, allows forecasters to precisely track regional rainfall and weather changes, which is critical for anticipating flash floods and landslides. The joint operation highlights significant technological cooperation between China and Nepal in disaster monitoring and response.
