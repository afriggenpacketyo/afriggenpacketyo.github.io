Australia's Music Chart Bans Fully AI-Generated Tracks

2026-08-25 01:52:00 EDT

Australia's official music charts, overseen by the Australian Recording Industry Association (ARIA), will no longer allow wholly AI-generated music to qualify for charting, effective August 31. This new rule distinguishes between music primarily created by artificial intelligence and "AI-assisted" music that remains substantially human-made. Recordings using generative AI can still be eligible if human creativity is central and there are no concerns about stream manipulation. This move aligns with broader industry principles from the International Federation of the Phonographic Industry, aiming to support human artistry while adapting to technological advancements and ensuring chart integrity.
