2026-09-19 04:55:00 EDT

A US federal appeals court has largely upheld a ruling that blocks the Trump administration's policy of rapidly deporting migrants to third countries without adequate due process. The First Circuit Court of Appeals panel mandated that migrants must receive effective notice of their intended destination and a meaningful opportunity to challenge removal if they fear persecution or torture. This decision challenges a Department of Homeland Security policy that relied on diplomatic assurances, which critics argue denied protections to vulnerable individuals. The ruling comes amidst reports of deported individuals facing arbitrary detention and abuse, and the administration is expected to appeal to the Supreme Court.
