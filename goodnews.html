Second Group of Native Sea Turtle Hatchlings Released in California

2026-11-16 08:00:00 EST

A second group of 21 olive ridley sea turtle hatchlings was successfully released in Huntington Beach, California, following the emergence of 54 hatchlings two days prior from a Seal Beach nest. These turtles originated from eggs laid on Huntington City Beach on September 19, which, along with the Seal Beach nest, were transferred to the Aquarium of the Pacific to protect them from high tides. These are the first known sea turtle nests on the U.S. West Coast, with olive ridleys typically found in tropical regions far south of California. An extreme marine heat wave and strong El Niño conditions are believed to have made Southern California waters suitable for nesting, marking a significant and unusual ecological event.
