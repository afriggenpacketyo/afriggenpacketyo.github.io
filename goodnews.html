Researchers Explore Drug-Based Prevention for Childhood Trauma in Mice

2026-08-14 02:22:00 EDT

Scientists in Munich and Stockholm are researching whether childhood trauma can be prevented with a drug, not just managed. Their study on mice found that early hardship led to lasting social disadvantages, even without visible illness. Administering a compound called SAFit2, which blocks a stress-regulating protein, to mothers during stressful periods prevented these effects in their offspring. While promising for prevention, the drug is still experimental and has not been tested for reversing existing trauma in humans, with clinical application potentially a decade away.
