Connecticut Bakers Revive Election Day Joy with Annual Cake Bake-Off

2026-11-03 17:28:33 EST

Historically, massive, alcohol-soaked fruit cakes were baked to celebrate the rare ability to elect officials. Today, professional bakers create non-partisan, election-themed masterpieces, with this year's winning entry being a patriotic, multi-layered cake featuring lemon blueberry and spiced sponge flavors. The event aims to inject joy and community spirit back into the civic process, fostering a celebratory approach to American democracy through culinary creativity.

Rare Sea Turtle Nest Discovered in Southern California, Third This Year

2026-11-03 17:04:07 EST

A third rare olive ridley sea turtle nest has been discovered on San Buenaventura State Beach in Ventura, marking an unprecedented event for Southern California and the U.S. West Coast. This occurrence, along with two previous nests found in Seal Beach and Huntington Beach, is attributed to extreme marine heat waves and a strong El Niño, which have elevated local water temperatures. Federal biologists had previously relocated the earlier clutches to the Aquarium of the Pacific to protect them from high tides. A decision on the relocation of the Ventura eggs is currently pending.
