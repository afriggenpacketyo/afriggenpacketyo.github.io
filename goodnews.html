Macaques Exhibit Increased Germaphobia After Viral Outbreak, Study Finds

2026-10-23 10:16:10 EDT

A new study indicates that macaques, much like humans during the early stages of the COVID-19 pandemic, exhibit increased germophobic behaviors after experiencing a viral outbreak. Researchers observed macaques at a Texas sanctuary that had been affected by an HIV-like virus, SRV. The study found that monkeys who had lived through the outbreak were significantly less likely to consume a grape placed on epoxy-encased macaque feces (47%) compared to a control group unaware of the outbreak (79%). This article highlights that these findings suggest a heightened sensitivity to disgust, which is considered an evolutionary mechanism to protect against contamination. The increased caution, even for non-fecal transmission, reflects a general avoidance strategy in an uncertain world.
