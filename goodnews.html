Influential Assemblage Artist Betye Saar Dies at 99

2026-07-27 17:30:17 EDT

Betye Saar, the celebrated Los Angeles assemblage artist who profoundly influenced contemporary art by transforming bigoted objects into powerful statements on race and American history, has died at the age of 99. Her passing, just days before her 100th birthday, marks the end of an extraordinary career. Saar's iconic 1972 work, "The Liberation of Aunt Jemima," which recontextualized a stereotypical mammy figure with a rifle, became a pivotal piece in the Black Arts Movement and the Black women's movement. Her extensive body of work, found in over 80 international museums, consistently explored themes of memory, magic, and spirituality, and she remained creatively active into her late nineties.
