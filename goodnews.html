Chinese Scientists Achieve 84% Efficacy in Cancer Treatment Using Waste Water Technology

2026-10-17 04:00:09 EDT

Chinese scientists have reported a significant breakthrough in cancer treatment, achieving 84% efficacy in a landmark trial for advanced, metastatic cancer using an innovative approach inspired by industrial waste water technology. The therapy involves a nano-carbon iron injection, named CNSI-Fe, which harnesses the element iron's capacity to react with hydrogen peroxide and produce hydroxyl radicals. These radicals are known for their ability to degrade various organic and toxic molecules, effectively targeting malignant tumors. Initial results from this trial showed the injection successfully halted tumor growth in 16 patients and caused shrinkage in two, offering new hope for individuals with limited therapeutic options. A larger Phase II trial involving 52 patients is now in progress to further evaluate this promising treatment.
