Research Explores Why Electric Vehicles May Induce Motion Sickness in Passengers

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

A growing number of passengers are reporting heightened motion sickness when traveling in electric vehicles (EVs), a phenomenon that researchers attribute to a sensory mismatch. Experts suggest that the quiet operation of EVs, lacking the familiar auditory cues and vibrations of traditional combustion engines, can disrupt the inner ear's balance system, which typically anticipates vehicle movement. Furthermore, the regenerative braking system, if not applied smoothly, can cause abrupt decelerations that induce a "rocking" sensation. While this issue is not universal, it is drawing attention from both academics and EV manufacturers, who are exploring solutions such as smoother acceleration software and adaptive braking systems.
