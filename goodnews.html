New Drug Transforms Life for Woman with Rare Sunlight Allergy, Erythropoietic Protoporphyria

2026-10-03 08:00:16 EDT

Emily Pearson, 25, suffered from Erythropoietic Protoporphyria (EPP), a rare and agonizing skin disorder causing severe pain from sun exposure, which profoundly limited her life for years. She volunteered for a clinical trial for bitopertin, a groundbreaking once-daily pill designed to lower protoporphyrin IX levels in the skin, which are responsible for the painful reactions. The drug dramatically increased her sun tolerance, enabling her to achieve her dream of running marathons and enjoying outdoor activities without debilitating pain. Bitopertin is currently in Phase 3 clinical trials, offering significant hope for thousands of others living with EPP, despite the FDA's request for additional data before expedited approval.
