Ukraine and Sudan Immigrants Retain Deportation Protections Amid DHS Delays

2026-10-18 13:44:00 EDT

Immigrants from Ukraine and Sudan enrolled in the Temporary Protected Status (TPS) policy will retain their deportation protections for now, despite their scheduled expiration, as the Department of Homeland Security delays final decisions. DHS stated that nationals of Ukraine and Sudan enrolled in TPS will "retain protection" until new announcements are made, providing a temporary reprieve for tens of thousands. This decision contrasts sharply with the Trump administration's broader posture, which has revoked TPS for over a dozen countries, affecting more than 1 million immigrants over the past year and a half. The TPS program offers temporary deportation reprieves and work permits to individuals from nations facing armed conflict or other crises.
