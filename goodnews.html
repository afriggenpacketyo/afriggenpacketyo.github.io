However, the court unanimously ruled that India's grounds were unjustified, affirming that the treaty remains fully in force and that India is bound by its obligations, particularly concerning the design and operation of hydroelectric projects on the western rivers. Pakistan welcomed the PCA's order, having previously accused India of attempting to "weaponize" the precious water resource.
