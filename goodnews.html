Amateur Archaeologist Discovers Germany's Largest Roman Coin Hoard

2026-09-22 13:32:00 EDT

An amateur archaeologist in Germany, Oliver Riedl, discovered a massive trove of 934 Roman silver coins, known as "denarii," dating back to the 2nd century AD near Cologne. This hoard, now on display at the LVR-LandesMuseum Bonn, is considered the largest from the era of Roman Emperor Hadrian ever found in Germany. Regional archaeological authorities speculate the coins were buried for safekeeping, though the owner and reasons for its non-recovery remain unknown. The discovery provides significant historical insight into Roman presence and economy in the region, representing about six years' disposable income for an ordinary legionary, enriching our understanding of ancient times.
