The cloak, initially gifted to Dr. George Cleghorn in 1900 and subsequently loaned to the Pitt Rivers Museum in 1913, embodies profound cultural heritage for the Māori people. Its return was made possible through the dedicated efforts of the Homebound Project, a charitable organization focused on cultural restitution, which articulated the deep emotional resonance of the artifact by stating it was "crying to come home." This event underscores the growing global movement towards recognizing and honoring indigenous cultural patrimony.

2026-10-10 14:22:29 EDT

Earhart and her navigator, Fred Noonan, disappeared in 1937 during their attempt to circumnavigate the globe, leaving behind one of aviation's most enduring mysteries. The team is specifically investigating the "Taraia Object," a structure observed in both recent satellite imagery and historical aerial photographs from 1938, which they believe could be the long-sought wreckage of her Electra twin-engine plane. This ambitious endeavor seeks to bring closure to a nearly century-old enigma.
