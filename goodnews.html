Understanding Stroke Warning Signs: The Importance of the BE FAST Acronym

2026-11-01 17:22:12 EST

The American Stroke Association advocates the BE FAST acronym to help people identify symptoms and act promptly, which significantly improves chances of survival and full recovery. BE FAST stands for Balance loss, Eye changes, Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, and Time to call 911. A stroke occurs when blood flow to or within the brain is interrupted, causing potential permanent damage; immediate medical attention is crucial to minimize long-term effects and improve patient outcomes.
