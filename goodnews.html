Understanding Why Some Election Races Take Longer to Call

2026-11-04 10:44:31 EST

Closely contested races, combined with factors like ranked-choice voting in states like Alaska and the processing of mail-in ballots, inherently extend the counting period. Mail ballots, which require signature verification and are often counted after polls close, can cause apparent shifts in results, a phenomenon sometimes called the 'blue wave' effect. Experts caution against misinformation from candidates who prematurely claim fraud, emphasizing that winners rarely complain about the process.
