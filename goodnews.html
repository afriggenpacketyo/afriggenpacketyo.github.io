Uruguay's National Integrated Care System Becomes Global Model for Public Care Infrastructure

2026-11-20 06:00:00 EST

Uruguay has established the National Integrated Care System (SNIC), a pioneering model that legally recognizes care for children, seniors, and people with disabilities as a right. This comprehensive system provides early childhood care, subsidized home assistants, and day centers, treating care as public infrastructure rather than a privilege. Decades of feminist organizing and a sympathetic government led to its 2015 enactment, making Uruguay the first Latin American country to implement such a national system. SNIC is viewed as both a matter of rights and an economic investment, enabling women's formal employment and creating paid care jobs, though funding challenges persist.
