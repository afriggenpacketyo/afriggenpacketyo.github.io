Fridge-Free Vaccines Successfully Trialled, Promising Global Health Impact

2026-08-05 19:14:22 EDT

UK scientists have successfully trialled 'fridge-free' vaccines in patients for the first time, a significant breakthrough poised to dramatically reduce global vaccine waste. Currently, approximately half of all vaccines are discarded annually, primarily due to failures in maintaining the strict cold chain required for storage and transportation. Stablepharma's innovative technology converts liquid vaccines, such as tetanus-diphtheria, into a dry powder using trehalose, a sugar found in resurrection plants, enabling them to remain viable at room temperature for up to a year. Early-stage studies demonstrated that this 'fridge-free' version provided immune responses equivalent to conventional vaccines and was safe and well-tolerated. This innovation holds immense promise for improving access to life-saving immunizations in remote and challenging regions worldwide.
