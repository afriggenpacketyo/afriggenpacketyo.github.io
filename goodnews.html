Historic First Map of Mason-Dixon Line to be Auctioned, Symbolizing U.S. North-South Divide

2026-10-29 07:28:22 EDT

The inaugural hand-drawn map of the Mason-Dixon Line, a document of immense historical significance that came to symbolize the profound division between the U.S. free and slave states, is slated for auction. Drafted by Charles Mason and Jeremiah Dixon between 1767 and 1768, this 76-inch-long linen-backed chart initially served to resolve a territorial dispute between the colonies of Maryland and Pennsylvania. Its subsequent application in the 1820 Missouri Compromise solidified its role as the de facto boundary for human bondage, crystallizing the fundamental ideological schisms that eventually erupted into the Civil War. Expected to command between $800,000 and $1.2 million, this rare artifact offers a direct, tangible connection to America's intricate and often fraught historical narrative.
