Ankle strengthening exercises recommended to prevent common injuries

2026-11-15 08:28:10 EST

Ankle injuries are common, accounting for 5% of A&E visits in the UK, but can be largely avoided with proper strengthening and awareness. Performance physiotherapist Genevieve Tawiah emphasizes "brain to body awareness," encouraging individuals to sense ankle movements without looking. Exercises like pressing the big toe into the floor to engage the foot's arch and performing toe "abduction" are recommended to build this awareness. Additionally, choosing appropriate footwear that provides adequate space and support is crucial. Tawiah advises against consistently wearing shoes that are too narrow and suggests that while barefoot shoes can be beneficial, a baseline of foot and ankle strength is necessary first. Different activities require different shoe types, with flatter shoes for squats and cushioned trainers for running.
