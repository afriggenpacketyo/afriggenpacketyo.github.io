Understanding Potential Premium Increases for Long-Term Care Insurance

2026-07-28 12:52:52 EDT

Long-term care insurance premiums can indeed increase after a policy is purchased, but not typically due to individual aging or health changes once the policy is in force. Instead, insurers may seek state approval to raise rates for an entire class of policyholders if the block of policies is underperforming financially, often due to underestimated claims or increased life expectancy. Historically, some insurers faced these challenges, leading to past premium hikes, though newer policies often use more conservative assumptions. To mitigate this risk, consumers are advised to purchase coverage earlier, choose appropriate benefit levels, shop around with multiple insurers, and consider hybrid policies.
