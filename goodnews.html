Genetic and Lifestyle Factors Influence Caffeine Sensitivity

2026-09-16 20:09:43 EDT

The varying effects of coffee on individuals, from heightened alertness to minimal impact, are influenced by a combination of genetic and lifestyle factors. Dr. Marilyn Cornelis explains that the speed at which caffeine is metabolized by a liver enzyme, which can take six to 20 hours, is a key determinant. Smoking can accelerate this process by up to 65%, while certain medications and pregnancy can prolong caffeine's presence in the body. Regular coffee drinkers also develop a tolerance, making them less sensitive to its effects. Sleep experts recommend avoiding caffeine several hours before bed, as it can impair both the ability to fall asleep and sleep quality.
