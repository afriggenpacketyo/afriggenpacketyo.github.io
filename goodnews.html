ODI Cricket Rule Change Revitalizes Reverse Swing for Bowlers

2026-10-19 01:27:56 EDT

A rule change in July 2025, allowing captains to choose which of two white balls to use after 34 overs, has led to a "reverse swing renaissance" in One Day International (ODI) cricket. This modification helps older, scruffier balls move in unexpected directions, benefiting pace bowlers who now find significantly more swing in the final overs. Consequently, pacers' economy rates and bowling averages have improved, making it harder for batters to score quickly at the death. While England has notably benefited, the impact varies globally, with less pronounced effects in Australia and South Africa, potentially altering future World Cup strategies.
