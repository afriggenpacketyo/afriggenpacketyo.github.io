Agrivoltaics: Integrating Solar Panels with Agriculture to Boost Crop Yields and Energy Efficiency

2026-09-25 05:31:00 EDT

Agrivoltaics, the practice of co-locating solar panels with agriculture, is gaining traction for its dual benefits. This innovative approach allows crops to grow or livestock to graze under solar panels, providing shade, diverting water, and creating cooler, damper conditions that can boost crop yields. Additionally, cooler panels operate more efficiently, generating more electricity. While the practice offers significant potential for climate mitigation and adaptation, adoption has been slow due to varying effectiveness in different climates and higher initial costs for solar producers and farmers.
