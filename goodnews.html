2026-09-08 17:27:28 EDT

This initiative marks a return to a pre-9/11 practice, aiming to enhance the travel experience by enabling easier welcomes, send-offs, and opportunities for friends to meet during layovers or enjoy airport amenities. To participate, individuals must apply online one to three days in advance for approval, receiving a pass valid for a single calendar day. Currently implemented at 13 US airports, including major hubs like Los Angeles International and Dallas-Fort Worth, the program is slated for broader expansion in the coming months.
