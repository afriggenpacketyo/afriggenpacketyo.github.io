Second Wave of Olive Ridley Sea Turtle Hatchlings Emerge in Southern California

2026-11-16 08:00:00 EST

A second group of 21 olive ridley sea turtle hatchlings successfully emerged and were subsequently released in Huntington Beach, California, on Sunday, marking another historic moment after the first wave just days prior. These hatchlings originated from eggs laid on Huntington City Beach in September, which, alongside a separate nest from Seal Beach, were carefully transferred to the Aquarium of the Pacific to safeguard them from potential high tides. This significant event represents the first known sea turtle nests recorded on the U.S. West Coast, as olive ridleys typically nest considerably further south. Scientists attribute these rare nesting occurrences to an extreme marine heat wave and robust El Niño conditions, which have elevated local water temperatures.
