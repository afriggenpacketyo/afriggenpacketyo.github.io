Understanding Teenagers' Use of Therapy-Related Language

2026-11-12 05:16:54 EST

Some parents are finding it challenging to understand the "therapy speak" their teenagers use, such as terms like "triggering" or "gaslighting," which are often employed to describe everyday emotional experiences. Mental health experts suggest this linguistic shift is influenced by social media and a desire for young people to feel heard, as they may perceive their feelings are not taken seriously by adults. While overuse of these terms can be detrimental, parents are advised to listen actively and use ordinary emotional language to bridge the communication gap with their teens. Techniques like reflecting back their feelings or sharing similar personal experiences can encourage more open dialogue.

His victory, achieved by a narrow margin, signifies a significant shift in the deeply conservative state, where Republicans have held power for decades. Colom's win contributes to the Democrats' strengthened majority in the Senate. His campaign focused on engaging a diverse electorate, and his success is attributed to strong grassroots organizing, particularly within the Black community.
