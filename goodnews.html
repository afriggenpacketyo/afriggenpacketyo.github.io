Study Finds NHS Breast Cancer Screening Criteria Miss High-Risk Younger Women

2026-08-03 21:08:13 EDT

A new study warns that current NHS guidelines for breast cancer screening in women under 50 are too narrow, potentially missing up to 95% of those at higher risk. The National Institute of Health and Care Excellence (Nice) criteria primarily focus on inherited genes, overlooking other significant factors like lifestyle. Researchers from Cambridge University and the Institute of Cancer Research developed a new risk assessment tool, Boadicea, which incorporates family history, lifestyle, reproductive history, and genetic information. This tool identified eight times more women under 50 who developed breast cancer, suggesting a more comprehensive approach could significantly improve early detection and save lives, especially since most younger women with breast cancer have no family history.
