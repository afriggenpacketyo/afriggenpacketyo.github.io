Prediction Markets See Volume Boost from "Combo Contracts" Amid Reporting Scrutiny

2026-10-06 14:46:16 EDT

Prediction markets like Kalshi and Polymarket are experiencing soaring notional volumes, largely driven by the increasing popularity of "combo contracts," which are multi-leg bets similar to sports parlays. While these combos account for over 50% of notional volume, particularly during the NFL season, they represent a much smaller share of actual transactions. This discrepancy arises because the Commodity Futures Trading Commission's reporting rules measure each side of a trade as $1 of notional volume, allowing low-cash, high-payout combos to inflate headline figures significantly. Critics argue this metric can mislead investors, making platforms appear to grow faster than they are, prompting suggestions to focus on "taker volume" for a more accurate reflection of activity.
