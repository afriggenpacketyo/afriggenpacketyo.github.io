Chinese Fossil Challenges Evolutionary Theory on Early Bird Flightlessness

2026-10-16 08:00:08 EDT

Chinese scientists have discovered a 150 million-year-old fossil of the earliest known flightless bird, Yuezhuornis yangyuanensis, in Fujian province, providing strong evidence that not all early birds developed the ability to fly. This finding challenges long-held assumptions, including those by Charles Darwin, that flightlessness in birds only emerged as a secondary adaptation after species had already mastered flight. The discovery suggests that early bird evolution diversified into various terrestrial, non-flying niches much earlier than previously understood, indicating that selection for powered flight was not the sole driver of early avialan skeletal modifications.
