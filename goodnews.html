Researchers Discover Two New Species of Sea Spiders Along the Canadian Coastline in Salish Sea

2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

Researchers at the University of British Columbia have discovered two new species of sea spiders in the shallow waters of the Salish Sea, marking the first such find in over a century. These fascinating marine arthropods, measuring about a centimeter across, were collected off Vancouver and Quadra islands. One species, Callipallene pilosuspedes, features distinctive red eyes and unique grooming limbs, while Tanystylum kiixin, named after an ancient Indigenous village, has shorter, stockier legs. These slow-moving creatures, which feed parasitically on hydroids, lack venom and primarily rely on camouflage for survival in their underwater habitats.
