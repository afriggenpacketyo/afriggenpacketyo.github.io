Named Yakamys sirindhornae in honor of Thailand's Princess Sirindhorn, this "giant rat" was among the largest known from the Paleogene period. Researchers from Thailand, France, and Canada used detailed analysis of two fossilized teeth found in a coal mine in 2004 to identify the species, suggesting ancient rodents may have evolved in the region before spreading globally.
