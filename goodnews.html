UK Government Considers Reducing National Insurance for Young Workers to Boost Employment

2026-11-15 17:30:03 EST

A new report suggests the UK government should gradually reduce National Insurance contributions for businesses employing individuals under 25 to stimulate job creation for young people. The proposal, which would be implemented over five years starting with the retail and hospitality sectors, aims to address youth unemployment. The report also recommends changes to benefits for young people who are not in employment, education, or training, known as 'Neets.' While some businesses express the difficulty of affording to hire young people due to employment costs, the government is also extending its subsidized jobs guarantee scheme. The plan seeks to balance the cost of employment with the need to provide opportunities for the youth.
