Gene Therapies Offer Cure for Sickle Cell Disease, But Access Remains Limited

2026-10-16 10:43:09 EDT

Recent FDA approvals of Casgevy and Lyfgenia, the first gene therapies for severe sickle cell disease, offer a potential cure for a debilitating genetic disorder that disproportionately affects non-Hispanic Black individuals. These breakthroughs promise to free thousands from painful crises and improve life expectancy, marking a new era in treatment. However, significant barriers to access persist, including high costs ranging from $2.2 million to $3.1 million, a complex year-long treatment process, and a scarcity of specialized care centers and qualified providers. Efforts are underway, like the CMS Cell and Gene Therapy Access Model, to address cost disparities and expand the care network, but patient uptake remains slow.
