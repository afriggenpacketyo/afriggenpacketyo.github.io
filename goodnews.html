The telescope will search for Type 1A supernovas to better comprehend the universe's expansion and discover numerous exoplanets using gravitational microlensing. Utilizing repurposed technology from a surveillance satellite, Roman will conduct large-scale sky surveys much faster than previous observatories, making its data accessible to all. Astronomers anticipate Roman will either confirm or challenge existing cosmological models, potentially reshaping our view of the cosmos.
