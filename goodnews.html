Northern Lights Visible Across UK, Reaching England's South Coast

2026-10-10 16:57:29 EDT

The Northern Lights, or Aurora Borealis, captivated residents across the UK, appearing as far south as England's south coast on Friday and Saturday evenings in a rare and widespread display. This natural phenomenon, typically seen closer to the Arctic Circle, was attributed to heightened solar activity, specifically powerful coronal mass ejections (CMEs) from the Sun directed towards Earth. When these charged particles collide with atmospheric oxygen and nitrogen, they emit the stunning colored light. The Met Office has indicated that further sightings may be possible during clear skies until Tuesday, providing additional opportunities for the public to witness this extraordinary celestial event.
