Understanding England's 9-1 Numerical Grading System for GCSE Exams

2026-08-20 01:33:02 EDT

England's GCSE exams now utilize a numerical grading system from 9 to 1, having replaced the former A-to-U letter scale as part of a curriculum overhaul initiated in 2014. The highest grade is 9, while 1 is the lowest, with U denoting "ungraded." Although not a direct conversion, grade 7 aligns with the bottom of an A, and grade 4 with a C, which is considered a standard pass. Many sixth forms, however, require a minimum of 5s or 6s for entry into further education. This system, fully implemented by 2020, aims to provide clearer differentiation among high-achieving students and distinguish the new, more challenging qualifications.
