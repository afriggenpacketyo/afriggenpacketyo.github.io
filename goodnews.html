Experts Confirm 'Hang-xiety' as a Real Phenomenon Following Alcohol Consumption

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

Experts confirm that 'hang-xiety,' the anxiety accompanying a hangover, is a genuine phenomenon, rooted in how alcohol affects the brain. Professor Celia Morgan explains that alcohol alters brain chemicals like GABA and glutamate, leading to an over-alert and aroused nervous system as it wears off, triggering anxiety. Research indicates that not everyone experiences hang-xiety, and its intensity doesn't directly correlate with the amount of alcohol consumed, with existing psychological traits, particularly shyness, playing a significant role. Reframing these anxious thoughts as a temporary physiological state rather than a social catastrophe can help alleviate its effects.
