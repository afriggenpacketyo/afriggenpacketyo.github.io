China Develops High-Accuracy, Comfortable Brain-Reading Device for Real-World Use

2026-11-08 21:00:33 EST

Chinese engineers have successfully developed a novel brain-reading device that integrates gradient electrodes into a commercial electroencephalogram (EEG) cap, achieving a remarkable 94% mind-typing accuracy. This innovative design overcomes the traditional trade-offs between uniformly soft and rigid electrodes, providing enhanced comfort and stability. The device demonstrated its capability by enabling eight hours of stable EEG brain recordings during a human subject's daily activities, and 14 hours while at rest. This significant breakthrough addresses key barriers to the practical, real-world deployment of non-invasive Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs), promising advancements in assistive technologies.
