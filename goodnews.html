These programs typically involve temporarily lowering interest rates, waiving certain fees, or placing the balance on a structured repayment plan with a smaller required monthly payment. For example, a $10,000 balance at a 22.15% APR could see payments drop by approximately 32% to 41% with a suitable hardship arrangement.
