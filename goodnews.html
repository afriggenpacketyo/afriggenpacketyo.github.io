Voyager 1 Reaches New Milestone, One-Light Day from Earth

2026-11-15 17:41:22 EST

NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft is set to achieve a new milestone, reaching a distance of one-light day from Earth, equivalent to over 16 billion miles. Launched in 1977, the probe has traveled for nearly 50 years, conducting flybys of Jupiter and Saturn, and discovering Jupiter's faint ring system. It became the most distant human-made object in 1998 and has been operating outside the solar system's heliosphere since 2012. NASA is celebrating this symbolic achievement with a public "Shine Bright for Voyager's Light-Day" event, encouraging people to send its call sign in Morse code.
