Rare Nanotyrannus dinosaur skeleton sells for $47.3 million at auction

2026-10-27 17:40:13 EDT

A rare skeleton of a Nanotyrannus, a dinosaur closely related to the Tyrannosaurus rex, has sold for $47.3 million at a Sotheby's auction in New York, making it one of the most expensive fossils ever sold. The skeleton, nicknamed "Jodi," was discovered in 2019 and was estimated to sell for $6 million to $9 million. The final price significantly exceeded expectations, making it the second most valuable dinosaur ever auctioned. "Jodi" is one of only three substantial Nanotyrannus skeletons known to science and the first to be offered publicly since the genus was confirmed. While the sale is a record-breaking event for paleontology, it has drawn criticism from some scientists who argue that such high prices could divert funds from museum operations and limit public and scientific access to significant fossils. The buyer's identity has not yet been publicly disclosed.
