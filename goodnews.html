Nine New Deep-Sea Sponge Species Discovered Off Alaska Coast

2026-09-14 07:30:56 EDT

Scientists have announced the discovery of nine new deep-sea sponge species off the coast of Alaska, offering a crucial glimpse into the region's vast and largely unexplored marine biodiversity. These specimens, collected during NOAA Fisheries' bottom-trawl surveys in the Gulf of Alaska and the Aleutian Islands, elevate the total known sponge species in the area to 232. Sponges, ancient and ecologically vital, create complex seafloor habitats essential for commercially important fish and crabs. However, these delicate ecosystems are now threatened by proposed deep-sea mining expansions, advocated by the Trump administration for critical minerals, and ongoing bottom trawling, prompting environmental groups to voice strong opposition.
