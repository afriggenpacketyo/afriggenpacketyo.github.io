Agrivoltaics, the practice of combining solar energy infrastructure with agriculture, is gaining traction for its dual benefits to crops, livestock, and energy production. While still in its infancy, with less than 1% of global solar capacity, the potential is enormous, especially in addressing climate change challenges. This innovative approach offers a unique win-win for climate mitigation and adaptation, providing farmers with potential dual-income streams and fostering community support for solar development.
