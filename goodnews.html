Mondelez International Reduces Cadbury Chocolate Ingredient Lead Time by Opening Malaysian Facility

2026-09-14 00:25:17 EDT

American snack giant Mondelez International is significantly streamlining its supply chain for Cadbury chocolate by establishing a new facility in Shah Alam, Malaysia. This $22 million plant will locally produce chocolate crumb, a key ingredient, eliminating the need for imports from Australia and South Africa. This strategic move is expected to cut lead times by at least two months and reduce import and transportation costs, supporting anticipated volume growth across Southeast Asia. The Shah Alam hub is Mondelez's sole manufacturing center for Cadbury in Southeast Asia, producing over 130 varieties of chocolate annually. The company is also leveraging its Southeast Asian manufacturing network for other markets and to address global supply disruptions.
