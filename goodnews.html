Defunct SpaceX Rocket Booster on Collision Course with the Moon

2026-08-04 18:28:42 EDT

A defunct SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket booster, weighing 8,800 pounds, is expected to collide with the moon early Wednesday at 5,400 mph, creating a crater estimated to be 60-100 feet wide and 12-16 feet deep. The booster, which launched lunar landers in January 2025, has been in orbit since. While posing no danger to Earth, this rare man-made lunar impact provides a unique scientific observation opportunity. NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter will capture before-and-after images, allowing scientists to study ejecta plumes and refine models for future exploration missions, potentially revealing insights into the moon's geology.
