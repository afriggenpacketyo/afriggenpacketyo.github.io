Avoiding Common Fan Mistakes Crucial for Health During Soaring Heat Wave Temperatures

2026-07-31 19:57:16 EDT

As temperatures are projected to soar over 95 degrees this weekend, a common "heat wave mistake" of using electric fans in such extreme heat could significantly harm one's health, according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and climate experts. Above 90-95 degrees, fans can paradoxically act like convection ovens, circulating already hot air and potentially raising body temperature, thereby accelerating the risks of dehydration and heat exhaustion. While fans remain effective for nighttime ventilation when temperatures drop, continuous use in daytime high heat is strongly cautioned. Experts advise strategic cooling methods and emphasize the CDC's conservative 90-degree cutoff is crucial for ensuring broad public safety.
