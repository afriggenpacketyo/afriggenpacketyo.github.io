Chinese Scientists Reconstruct Denisovan Face from Ancient Fossils

2026-10-23 07:09:08 EDT

Chinese scientists have successfully reconstructed the face of Denisovans, an enigmatic species of ancient humans previously known primarily through DNA and fragmented fossils. This significant breakthrough was achieved using fossils excavated from China's Xujiayao archaeological site between 1976 and 1979. The reconstruction not only confirms the remains belong to the Denisovan lineage but also offers humanity its first visual representation of what these hominids, believed to have inhabited parts of Siberia and East and Southeast Asia thousands of years ago, might have looked like. This scientific advancement deepens our understanding of human evolutionary history.
