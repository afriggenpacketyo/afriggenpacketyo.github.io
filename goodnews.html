Rare Hammerhead Shark Sighting Surprises Fishermen at Malibu Pier

2026-08-15 16:24:01 EDT

A rare hammerhead shark was spotted near Malibu Pier, surprising fishermen and signaling unusually warm ocean temperatures in Southern California. Fisherman Ariel Kohanchi captured video of the distinct shark, which typically prefers warmer waters, marking the first local sighting in over a decade. Experts attribute this occurrence to a strong El Niño cycle and climate change, creating favorable conditions for warm-water species to migrate north. While generally harmless to humans, hammerhead populations are in severe decline globally due to overfishing, making such sightings a special, albeit concerning, event for marine biologists.

Liechtenstein Amends Royal Succession Laws to Permit Female Monarchs

2026-08-15 15:41:57 EDT

Liechtenstein has announced a change in its royal succession rules, now allowing women to ascend the throne, shifting from male primogeniture to absolute primogeniture. This historic declaration, made on the country's national day, means the first-born child, regardless of sex, will become heir. The change will apply to descendants of Hereditary Prince Alois's children, not affecting his own position as current heir. Prince Alois stated this amendment ensures the principality and Princely House are led by the best-prepared individual. Liechtenstein, a small European nation, now aligns with other monarchies like Britain, which made similar changes in 2011.
