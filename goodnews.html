Pig kidney functions in human for record 271 days, offering transplant hope

2026-09-03 18:31:23 EDT

The patient, Tim Andrews, described the transplant as a "bridge" that spared him from lengthy dialysis treatments. Doctors at Mass General Brigham hospital believe this xenotransplantation success demonstrates the potential for using genetically modified pig organs to address the critical shortage of human kidneys. While the pig organ eventually failed and was removed, it provided valuable time before a human donor was found. This development is a major step forward in the research to overcome organ scarcity.
