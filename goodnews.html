First Adult Tyrannosaurus Rex Trackway Discovered in North Dakota

2026-09-10 10:44:43 EDT

Paleontologists have discovered the first known trackway of a full-grown Tyrannosaurus rex in North Dakota's badlands, a significant find detailed in the Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology. Consisting of four connected footprints, each roughly three feet long, the trackway provides unprecedented insight into the animal's movement, estimating it walked at 3.5 to 4.5 mph despite its seven-ton weight. This discovery, made by an amateur fossil hunter, is crucial as only two isolated adult T. rex footprints had been confirmed globally before. Three of the footprints are slated for airlift to the Denver Museum of Nature & Science, offering a rare glimpse into the behavior of a T. rex just before the dinosaur extinction event.
