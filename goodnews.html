Mesh Networks Offer Potential Solution to Government Internet Blackouts

2026-07-25 07:08:00 EDT

In response to increasing government-directed internet shutdowns in authoritarian states like Iran and Russia, "LoRa mesh networks" are emerging as a potential communication solution. These radio-powered networks use small, inexpensive devices to transmit encrypted text messages over several kilometers, bypassing traditional internet infrastructure and making censorship difficult. While limited to small data packets, their self-repairing nature and ability to operate without a central node offer a resilient communication channel. The technology has demonstrated real-world effectiveness in Ukraine, offering a lifeline for secure information exchange in crisis zones despite risks like signal triangulation and legal prohibitions in some countries.

Dean Martin and Jerry Lewis's Anarchic Comedy Legacy Celebrated 80 Years On

2026-07-25 06:00:00 EDT

Eighty years after their 1946 debut, the comedic partnership of Dean Martin and Jerry Lewis is celebrated for its groundbreaking, anarchic style that revitalized American humor post-Depression. Unlike scripted duos, their unpredictable blend of pratfalls, ad-libs, and fourth-wall demolition captivated audiences, influencing future generations of comedians and shows like "Saturday Night Live." Their "Commedia dell’arte 2.0" act, featuring the suave crooner and the gawky buddy, became a cultural phenomenon across radio, television, and film, symbolizing the 1950s. Their final performance in 1956 marked the end of an era that forever changed the landscape of American comedy.
