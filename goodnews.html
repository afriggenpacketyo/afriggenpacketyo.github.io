Northern Ireland Implements New Graduated Driver Licensing Laws for Young Drivers

2026-10-01 02:21:06 EDT

Northern Ireland has become the first UK region to introduce Graduated Driver Licensing (GDL) laws for learner and newly qualified drivers, primarily targeting 17 to 23-year-olds, to reduce fatal road crashes. The new regulations mandate a minimum six-month driving experience for learners before a practical test and restrict young passengers for new drivers under 24 at night. While police and driving instructors laud the measures as life-saving, some rural youth express frustration over extended waiting times and limited public transport access. The reforms aim to shift focus from merely passing a test to becoming a confident and competent driver, with practical tests under the new system beginning in April 2027.
