Scientists Reveal Unprecedented Details of Sun's Dynamic Surface

2026-08-05 20:55:02 EDT

Scientists have achieved an unprecedented view of the sun's surface, capturing the most detailed images ever using the National Science Foundation's Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope in Hawaii. These remarkable images reveal a dynamic and previously unseen solar landscape, characterized by strange feathery patterns and ripples of instability. Researchers identified these as Kelvin-Helmholtz instability (KHI), a fluid dynamics phenomenon observed on Earth and other planets, but never before at such a fine scale on the sun. This discovery, published in the journal Nature, is hailed as a significant advancement in understanding solar and stellar plasma dynamics, promising improved predictions of coronal mass ejections that can disrupt Earth's communications.
