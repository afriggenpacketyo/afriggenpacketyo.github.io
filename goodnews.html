2026-10-23 08:23:32 EDT

Researchers are conducting excavations to document the wreck, investigate its galley, and understand how it sank. Theories suggest it may have been sunk during a battle in 1501 or due to a fire on board. The discovery offers unique historical and archaeological insights into naval technology and life at sea during that period.

Denmark to Ban Mink Farming by August 2029 in Government Reversal

2026-10-23 08:17:45 EDT

Denmark will cease all mink farming by August 2029, as the government has decided not to pursue an exemption to an EU-wide ban on the practice. Denmark, once a leading global producer of mink fur, has seen its industry decline due to international bans, reduced demand, and a previous COVID-19 outbreak in its mink population. The ban will be implemented with a two-year transition period. The Danish Nature Minister stated the decision prioritizes animal welfare, arguing that the industry's continuation should not be based solely on its history but also on societal and economic sense. This move aligns with growing global concerns about animal welfare in the fashion industry.
