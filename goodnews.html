UK Government Outlines Plan to Dismantle Leasehold System

2026-11-19 12:49:48 EST

The UK government has set a timetable to dismantle the leasehold system in England and Wales, aiming for most new flats to be sold as commonhold by around 2029, followed by a ban on new leasehold flats by 2031. This reform will see residents collectively own and manage their buildings, eliminating ground rents, which will be capped at £250 in 2028 and then reduced to a "peppercorn" rate after 40 years. The Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill, introduced to Parliament, also simplifies the conversion process for existing leaseholders. This move seeks to align England and Wales with other countries where shared ownership models are standard, despite opposition from freehold associations.
