Glueballs are theoretical particles made entirely of gluons, which are fundamental force carriers that "glue" quarks together to form larger particles like protons and neutrons, the building blocks of all matter. Scientists have predicted their existence for 50 years, as gluons uniquely attract each other while carrying the strong force, unlike other force-carrying particles. This finding provides crucial empirical evidence supporting a long-standing hypothesis in particle physics, marking a significant advancement in our understanding of the universe's fundamental forces.
