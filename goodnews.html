United Nations Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Representation of Continents

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to more accurately represent the true proportions of continents, especially Africa. This landmark resolution, sponsored by Togo and supported by the African Union, passed with 164 member states voting in favor, despite the U.S. voting against it and six other countries abstaining. The initiative encourages governments and institutions globally to replace the traditional Mercator map, which has historically distorted landmasses by enlarging regions near the poles while shrinking Africa and South America. Advocates and geographers stress that maps profoundly shape global understanding, making this correction a significant step towards truth and equitable representation.
