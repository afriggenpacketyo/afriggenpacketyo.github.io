Japan Launches MMX Mission to Retrieve Sample from Mars' Moon Phobos

2026-10-19 15:45:46 EDT

Japan has launched the Mars Moons Exploration (MMX) mission, aiming to be the first to return a sample from Mars or its moons to Earth. The spacecraft is set to arrive at Phobos in August 2027, where it will deploy a rover to scout a landing site before collecting approximately 10g of moon dust. This ambitious mission, led by the Japanese Space Agency, seeks to solve the mystery of how Mars's moons formed. Scientists are also hopeful the sample may contain fragments of Mars itself, offering direct insights into the Red Planet's composition and history.
