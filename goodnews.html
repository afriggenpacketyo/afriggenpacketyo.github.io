West Coast Pop Artist Tony Berlant Dies at 85

2026-10-28 20:04:13 EDT

Tony Berlant, a significant figure in the 1960s West Coast Pop Art movement, has died at 85 after a long battle with Alzheimer's disease. He was renowned for his distinctive large-scale collages crafted from shards of printed tin, fastened with thousands of nails, and also created unique metal cube sculptures described as quilt-like. Berlant's innovative work, often incorporating vintage street signs and personal photographs, blended elements of assemblage, Pop Art, and abstraction. A founding member of the Mimbres Foundation, his art is featured in major museum collections, including LACMA and the Art Institute of Chicago, cementing his lasting legacy in the art world.
