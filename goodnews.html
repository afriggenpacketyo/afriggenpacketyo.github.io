England and Wales to Abolish Leasehold System, Introduce Commonhold and Cap Ground Rents

2026-11-19 04:26:59 EST

England and Wales are set to introduce new laws to abolish the leasehold system for flats, replacing it with commonhold where residents collectively own and manage their buildings. Housing Minister Matthew Pennycook described the current system as an "anachronism" that "blights lives." The proposed Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill will also cap ground rents for existing leaseholders at £250 annually from 2028, eventually reducing them to a "peppercorn" rate after 40 years. While advocates celebrate the move to align with other countries, the Residential Freehold Association criticizes it as an "unprecedented abuse" of rights, fearing billions will be wiped from pension fund values.
