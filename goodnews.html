Clinical Trial Offers Hope for Woman with Rare Sun Allergy

2026-10-03 08:00:16 EDT

Emily Pearson, who suffered from erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP), a rare sun allergy causing "insufferable pain" from minimal sun exposure, found life-changing relief through a clinical trial for the drug bitopertin. The medication, taken daily, significantly lowers protoporphyrin IX levels in the skin, which are responsible for the painful reactions in EPP patients. Pearson's participation in Phase 2 of the trial allowed her to greatly increase her sun tolerance, enabling her to achieve her dream of running marathons and enjoy outdoor activities without severe consequences. Bitopertin is now in Phase 3 trials, offering significant hope for others living with this debilitating condition.
