Understanding Debt Account Transfers: When Collectors Can Sell Your Debt and Your Rights

Debt collectors can legally sell your account to other collection agencies or debt buyers, particularly if they own the debt themselves rather than working on commission for the original creditor. This means an account can change hands multiple times, but your legal obligations and protections under the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act remain intact. If a new collector contacts you, it's crucial to request written validation of the debt, confirm their authority to collect, and compare it with your records. For unmanageable debts, exploring options like debt relief companies, debt management programs, or hardship programs with your original creditor may be more effective than a balance transfer.
