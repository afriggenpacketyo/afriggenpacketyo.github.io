NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft reaches a light-day distance from Earth

2026-11-16 12:24:00 EST

This journey, undertaken at an incredible speed, has taken nearly 50 years. Voyager 1 became the first human-made object to reach interstellar space in 2012 and continues its mission, sending back valuable data from unexplored regions. The spacecraft carries the Golden Record, a collection of sounds and images representing Earth for potential extraterrestrial life. Its ongoing voyage highlights humanity's enduring quest for exploration and understanding of the cosmos.
