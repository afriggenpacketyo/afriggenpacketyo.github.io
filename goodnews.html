2026-10-11 08:48:01 EDT

Psychologist Mark Travers, Ph.D., specializing in couples therapy, champions a communication technique called "partial yield" as the most effective strategy for rapidly de-escalating arguments between partners. This practice involves partners conceding the specific piece of the argument for which they are genuinely responsible, without necessarily agreeing to the entire position, using straightforward phrases like "You're right, that part's on me." Travers explains that this approach effectively releases pent-up tension by acknowledging the partner's grievance before they feel compelled to forcefully extract it. By taking specific and early accountability, couples can prevent conflicts from spiraling into broader accusations, thus maintaining focus on collaborative problem-solving rather than engaging in personal attacks.
