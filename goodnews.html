Brazil reports lowest Amazon deforestation rate in nearly 40 years

2026-10-16 18:30:00 EDT

Brazil has recorded its lowest level of Amazon rainforest deforestation in nearly four decades, with 3,335 square kilometers cleared between August 2025 and July 2026. This figure represents a significant 42% decrease from the previous year. Environment Minister Joao Paulo Capobianco hailed the drop as a 'historic achievement,' and President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva reiterated the goal of achieving zero illegal deforestation by 2030. Climate advocacy groups credited the government's sustained commitment to environmental protection, including enhanced monitoring and penalties, for this positive development, placing Brazil at the forefront of forest protection efforts.
