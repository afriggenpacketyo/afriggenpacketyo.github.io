Federal Protections Generally Shield 401(k) Accounts from Ordinary Debt Collectors

2026-08-17 11:09:26 EDT

Most employer-sponsored 401(k) plans are protected by ERISA, generally preventing ordinary debt collectors and private creditors from garnishing funds. This means typical consumer debts like credit card balances cannot be directly seized from a qualified 401(k), even with a court judgment. However, these protections are not absolute; exceptions include federal tax debts and obligations arising from qualified domestic relations orders, such as child support or alimony. Withdrawing funds from a 401(k) can also remove these federal protections, making it a risky move that could trigger taxes and penalties. (not investment advice)

Homeowners Weigh HELOCs Versus Home Equity Loans Amidst Rising Interest Rates

2026-08-17 09:52:16 EDT

Homeowners are evaluating whether Home Equity Lines of Credit (HELOCs) or home equity loans are better options for accessing cash amidst high inflation and rising interest rates. HELOCs offer revolving credit with variable rates, typically lower initially, and flexibility for ongoing needs. Home equity loans provide a lump sum with fixed rates, offering payment stability against potential Fed rate increases. Experts suggest fixed-rate loans are preferable in a rising rate environment, while HELOCs suit falling rates. Cash-out refinancing is another alternative, but only advisable if it secures a similar or lower mortgage rate. (not investment advice)
