Urban Trees Crucial for Cooler Cities, Face Climate Challenges

2026-08-25 05:39:00 EDT

Large, leafy trees are increasingly vital for urban environments, acting as natural air conditioners, reducing pollution, storing carbon, and enhancing residents' well-being, but they face significant challenges in a warming climate. These urban trees can lower perceived temperatures by 10 to 12 degrees Celsius through evapotranspiration and providing shade. Experts stress the importance of preserving older, mature trees, as replacing their extensive benefits requires hundreds of saplings and decades of growth. Implementing effective watering techniques and strategically selecting diverse, drought-tolerant species are crucial for sustainable urban forestry planning, as cities and scientists collaborate to adapt green infrastructure for the future.
