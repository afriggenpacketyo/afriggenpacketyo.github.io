Created by Charles Mason and Jeremiah Dixon between 1767 and 1768, the line initially resolved a border dispute between Maryland and Pennsylvania. It later became the symbolic boundary between free and slave states, influencing the Missouri Compromise and the lead-up to the Civil War. The map, previously owned by Malcolm S.
