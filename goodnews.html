Bank of England Unveils Animals for Redesigned Banknotes

2026-10-07 19:01:30 EDT

The Bank of England has announced that four animals – the Atlantic puffin, barn owl, buff-tailed bumblebee, and European hedgehog – will feature on redesigned banknotes, replacing notable historical figures. These creatures were selected from a public vote and expert panel, with the puffin chosen to represent marine life and coastal heritage. The buff-tailed bumblebee was a clear winner in public nominations, receiving significantly more votes than other contenders. Each animal will be the central image on a £5, £10, £20, or £50 note, with the monarch remaining on the other side. The design, testing, and printing process for these new high-security notes is expected to take years.
