2026-08-03 19:20:54 EDT

Treasury Secretary Bessent has also suggested Japan utilize the Fed's Foreign and International Monetary Authorities (FIMA) Repo Facility more, allowing foreign central banks to lend Treasurys rather than sell them, easing fiscal pressures.
