US Office Market Experiences Scarcity Amid Rising Demand and Shrinking Supply

2026-10-14 08:24:05 EDT

The U.S. office market is undergoing an unexpected resurgence, leading to a scarcity of premium space. The sector recorded its fifth consecutive quarter of positive net absorption, with occupiers adding 6 million square feet in Q3, reversing a previous downsizing trend. Simultaneously, supply is shrinking rapidly, with 9.6 million square feet removed through conversions or redevelopments, and only 3.5 million square feet delivered. This "flight to scarcity" is driving up effective rents for trophy and second-generation spaces, but construction remains low due to a lack of equity capital.
