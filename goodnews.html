Choosing Between Home Equity Loan and HELOC for Credit Card Debt Consolidation

Homeowners seeking to pay off high-interest credit card debt can leverage their home equity through either a home equity loan or a Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC). A home equity loan provides a lump sum with a fixed rate and predictable payments, ideal for a fixed debt amount and those prioritizing payment certainty. Conversely, a HELOC offers a revolving line of credit with variable rates and borrowing flexibility during its draw period, suitable for evolving debt strategies. While both offer lower rates than credit cards, they convert unsecured debt to debt secured by one's home, requiring careful consideration of financial discipline and market conditions.
