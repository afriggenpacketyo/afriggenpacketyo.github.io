Potential for Increased Living Donor Liver Transplants in the US

2026-08-24 07:00:00 EDT

The U.S. has a significant opportunity to increase living donor liver transplants, mirroring the success seen in kidney donation. While living donors currently account for only about 6% of liver transplants, compared to 24% for kidneys, countries like India heavily rely on living donors. The U.S. has the potential supply of livers but lacks the necessary infrastructure, including a national registry and standardized protocols, to scale up these procedures. Advances in organ preservation technology, universal donor protections, and broader surgical training could help overcome barriers. Increased living donor liver transplants could significantly reduce the organ waitlist and save countless lives, especially for patients with end-stage liver failure who have no alternative treatments.

Taxing Deceased Billionaires Could Raise Significant Revenue, Analysts Say

2026-08-24 06:00:00 EDT

A less-discussed populist tax policy suggests taxing the investment earnings of deceased billionaires could raise substantial revenue and address a loophole in the tax code. The "stepped-up basis" rule allows heirs to inherit assets without paying capital gains taxes on their appreciated value, costing the Treasury billions annually. Closing this loophole, either through "carryover basis" or by treating death as a taxable event, could generate significant funds. While concerns exist about forcing the sale of family businesses, installment payment options could mitigate this. This policy is seen as a more logistically feasible way to "tax the hell out of" the wealthy compared to annual wealth taxes.
