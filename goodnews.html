Influential Assemblage Artist Betye Saar Dies at 99

2026-07-27 17:30:17 EDT

Renowned Los Angeles assemblage artist Betye Saar has passed away at the age of 99, shortly before her centennial birthday. Saar was widely celebrated for her pioneering work that repurposed bigoted bric-à-brac, including Sambo dolls and Aunt Jemima figures, transforming them into potent symbols of Black empowerment and resilience. Her iconic 1972 piece, "The Liberation of Aunt Jemima," became a pivotal work in the Black Arts Movement, profoundly influencing subsequent generations of artists and contributing to the eventual retirement of the Aunt Jemima brand. Saar's art consistently explored themes of race, memory, and spirituality, utilizing found objects to forge visceral connections to history.
