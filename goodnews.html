Electric Vehicles Linked to Increased Motion Sickness in Some Passengers

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

A growing number of passengers are reporting increased motion sickness when traveling in electric vehicles (EVs), a phenomenon experts attribute to a "sensory cue mismatch." Unlike traditional cars, EVs lack the familiar engine sounds and vibrations that help the brain anticipate movement, leading to disorientation. Regenerative braking, which can cause abrupt decelerations, also contributes to the issue. While not universal, this problem is acknowledged by some EV manufacturers. Strategies to mitigate sickness include looking out the front window, sitting in the front passenger seat, and taking motion sickness medication.
