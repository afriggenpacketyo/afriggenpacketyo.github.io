UN Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Representation of Africa's Size

2026-09-06 08:30:50 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has endorsed the Equal Earth map projection, encouraging its adoption to more accurately represent continent sizes, particularly Africa. Sponsored by Togo and supported by the African Union, the resolution passed with 164 votes, challenging the traditional Mercator map which distorts landmasses by enlarging regions near the poles. The Mercator projection makes Greenland appear similar in size to Africa, whereas the Equal Earth map correctly shows Africa as 14 times larger. This move aims to reshape global understanding and perception, acknowledging that maps influence education and collective views.
