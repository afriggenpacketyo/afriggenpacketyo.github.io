Fossilized Teeth Reveal 66-Pound "Giant Rat" Roamed Ancient Thailand

2026-11-05 07:11:38 EST

Scientists have identified a previously unknown species of ancient rodent, dubbed a "giant rat," weighing approximately 66 pounds, that inhabited southern Thailand 33 to 35 million years ago. The discovery, based on a fossilized molar and incisor, reveals one of the largest known rodents from the Paleogene period. Found in a Krabi coal mine in 2004, the teeth were analyzed decades later by an international research team, leading to the identification of *Yakamys sirindhornae*, named in honor of Thailand's Princess Sirindhorn. This finding offers new insights into early rodent evolution and the diversity of ancient life, despite the animal's exact appearance and behavior remaining unknown.
