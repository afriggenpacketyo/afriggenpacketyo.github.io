El Niño Phenomenon Significantly Dampens Atlantic Hurricane Activity in 2026

2026-09-23 05:26:00 EDT

The current El Niño event is a dominant factor in the unusually quiet 2026 Atlantic hurricane season, marking the first time since satellite tracking began in 1966 that no hurricanes have formed by late September. El Niño typically increases wind shear in the Atlantic, disrupting storm formation. Conversely, the Pacific region is experiencing an above-average hurricane season due to warmer waters and reduced wind shear. While the Atlantic season has been calm, meteorologists caution that major hurricanes could still emerge before the season concludes in November.
