Former US Senator Nancy Kassebaum Baker Dies at 94

2026-08-22 16:40:38 EDT

Nancy Kassebaum Baker, a former US Senator from Kansas and a pioneering figure in American politics, has passed away at the age of 94. She made history as the first woman elected to the Senate without her husband having previously held a congressional seat, serving three terms from 1978 to 1997. Kassebaum Baker was renowned for her independent and moderate Republican approach, frequently collaborating across party lines, exemplified by the co-authored 1996 health insurance law. Later in life, she increasingly diverged from the Republican Party's direction, endorsing Democratic candidates and notably advocating for Donald Trump's impeachment in 2021.
