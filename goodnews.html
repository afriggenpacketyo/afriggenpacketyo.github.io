2026-11-13 23:00:33 EST

Scientists have made a significant breakthrough by identifying a blood biomarker, eMTBR-tau243, that can detect advanced chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) in living patients. Currently, CTE, a degenerative brain condition caused by repeated head trauma common in contact sports, can only be definitively diagnosed post-mortem. While the test accurately distinguishes advanced CTE from healthy individuals, it is not yet validated for early-stage detection. This discovery opens new avenues for monitoring at-risk individuals and developing drug trials, potentially transforming the diagnosis and treatment landscape for this prevalent disease.
