World's Oldest Shipwreck Discovered Off Turkey, Preserving Ancient Trade Secrets

2026-10-14 13:44:53 EDT

Archaeologists have discovered the world's oldest known shipwreck off the coast of Turkey, dating back 4,400 to 4,700 years. While the ship's structure has not survived, its cargo of amphorae (clay jars) is remarkably preserved, some still containing traces of their ancient contents. The nearly 33-foot-long trade ship, believed to have transported goods across the Mediterranean, offers insights into ancient trading connections and seafarer life. The discovery predates the previous oldest shipwreck by over 2,000 years.
