TikTok to Pay $400 Million in Landmark Child Privacy Settlement

2026-08-21 18:00:23 EDT

TikTok and its parent company ByteDance have agreed to pay $400 million to the U.S. government, settling a lawsuit alleging violations of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The 2024 suit claimed the platform collected vast amounts of data from users under 13 without parental consent. This significant settlement, one of the largest for COPPA violations, follows TikTok's implementation of substantial changes to its ownership, privacy practices, and age-related controls for young users. The agreement includes an immediate $300 million payment, with an additional $100 million contingent on vacating a prior consent decree with the Federal Trade Commission, highlighting enhanced protections for American families.
