Earliest Polar Vortex Reversal on Record Raises Questions for UK and North American Winter Weather

2026-11-13 11:42:18 EST

The polar vortex high above the Arctic has experienced its earliest recorded "major sudden stratospheric warming" (SSW), with winds reversing direction. This unprecedented autumn event, where westerly winds shift to easterly, typically increases the chance of colder, drier, and potentially snowy weather in the UK and North America weeks later. However, experts note that predicting exact impacts is uncertain due to the early timing and interaction with other climate patterns like the strong El Niño. While past SSW events have been linked to severe winter storms, the current warmth in the Arctic may mute immediate effects.
