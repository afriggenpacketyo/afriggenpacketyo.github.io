U.S. Gasoline Prices Fall Below $4 a Gallon, First Time Since July

2026-11-10 04:16:24 EST

U.S. gasoline prices have fallen below $4 a gallon for the first time in nearly four months, with the national average recorded at $3.9788. This significant drop is largely attributed to the seasonal shift from more expensive summer-blend fuels to cheaper winter blends, which are less costly to produce and required outside the June to mid-September period. A concurrent decrease in demand, as people undertake fewer road trips and vacations during winter, further contributes to the price reduction. Additionally, declining global oil costs, influenced by hopes for a potential deal between the U.S. and Iran to de-escalate their conflict, have played a role in this positive trend for consumers.

While the EV market is booming, electricity currently covers only 20% of global final energy use, needing to reach over half by 2050 to prevent runaway heating. Stephanie Case Becomes First Woman to Complete 100-Mile Antarctic Ice Ultra

2026-11-10 01:20:04 EST

Ultra-runner Stephanie Case has achieved a historic feat, becoming the first woman to successfully complete the extreme 100-mile Antarctic Ice Ultra, finishing second overall. She navigated 38 loops of a 4.2km course in the Schirmacher Oasis, battling severe winds and temperatures plummeting to -35C, completing the race in 17 hours, 31 minutes, and 39 seconds. Case highlighted the challenge as much about survival as performance due to the brutal environment. This accomplishment, following her viral moment last year for breastfeeding during an ultramarathon, serves to defy societal expectations and powerfully demonstrate that "motherhood is not the finish line," particularly in a continent historically described as "womanless."
