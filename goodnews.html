Community Shield Victory Shows Little Correlation with Premier League Title Success

2026-08-16 01:29:55 EDT

A statistical analysis reveals that winning the Community Shield, the traditional curtain-raiser for the English football season, shows surprisingly little correlation with subsequent Premier League title success. Since the Premier League's inception in 1992-93, only eight of 34 Community Shield winners have gone on to lift the league trophy, with Manchester City in 2019 being the most recent, and only one of the past 15 winners achieving this feat. Conversely, teams that lost the Community Shield have won more league titles in the same season, totaling ten. This article highlights that nearly half the time, the eventual Premier League champions weren't even involved in the Community Shield match, suggesting the trophy holds minimal predictive power for the season ahead.
