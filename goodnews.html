2026-09-18 12:32:08 EDT

Two-year-old conjoined twins, Hiyab and Wuhbto, born with the extremely rare craniopagus condition, have been successfully separated after a complex 25-hour surgery at SSM Health Cardinal Glennon Children's Hospital in St. Louis. The girls, who were joined at the head facing opposite directions, traveled from Ethiopia for the procedure. A team of nearly 30 medical specialists spent almost a year planning the high-stakes operation, delicately separating their shared blood vessels and fused brain areas. This medical triumph allows the girls to see each other face to face for the first time, with further reconstructive surgeries planned before their anticipated return home to their family.
