California Environmental Legislator Byron Sher Dies at 98

2026-09-14 16:15:00 EDT

Byron Sher, a former Stanford law professor and California legislator, has died at 98, leaving behind a significant legacy in environmental law. Known for his cerebral and understated approach, Sher authored landmark legislation that addressed greenhouse gas emissions, promoted recycling, and combated water and air pollution. His 1988 bill was the first in any state to incorporate "global warming" into law, laying the foundation for future climate change policies. Sher's work continues to impact current environmental issues, such as the proposed Shasta Dam expansion, which his wild rivers legislation helps protect. He was respected across party lines for his integrity and dedication to environmental protection.
