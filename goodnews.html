This article highlights "Passivhaus" standard homes, an international building concept designed to maintain a constant indoor temperature by effectively sealing against the elements and consuming up to 90% less energy. An architect's retrofitted 1960s home, for example, stayed 10C cooler than outside on a 35C day.
