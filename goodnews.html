Ancient Rose-Colored Martian Meteorite Discovered in Sahara Desert Offers Insights into Planet's Early History

2026-10-30 11:09:15 EDT

A rose-colored meteorite, designated Teghaza 001 and found in the Sahara Desert in 2022, has been confirmed by NASA as a 4.1-billion-year-old fragment of Mars. Weighing nearly two pounds, this specimen is one of the three most ancient Martian meteorites discovered on Earth, offering a unique "Rosetta Stone" into the planet's primordial crust and formation. Scientists utilized a pioneering age-dating method to analyze its volcanic minerals, including zircon crystals, which match samples observed by the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter. The analysis revealed the rock was altered by Martian water about 4.1 billion years ago, indicating a potentially more dynamic early water cycle on Mars than previously believed.
