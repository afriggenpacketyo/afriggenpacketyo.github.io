Spacecraft Arrive in Orbit Around Mercury After Eight-Year Journey

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

Two spacecraft, one European and one Japanese, have successfully entered orbit around Mercury after an eight-year journey through the inner solar system. The mission, named BepiColombo, utilized a series of gravity-assist flybys of Earth, Venus, and Mercury to slow down and achieve orbit. This marks the first time in over a decade that a spacecraft has visited Mercury, the smallest and innermost planet of our solar system. The two probes will separate next month to conduct detailed mapping and studies of Mercury's surface, magnetic field, and internal structure. Scientists anticipate the mission will provide unprecedented data on the planet's extreme conditions and its geological history. The challenges of operating in Mercury's intense heat and radiation are significant for the mission's longevity.
