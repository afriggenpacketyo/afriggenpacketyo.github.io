Breakthroughs in investigative genetic genealogy in 2015 led to a DNA match for Jerry Westrom, a seemingly ordinary local businessman. Despite his initial denials, Westrom's footprints were definitively identified, culminating in his 2022 conviction and life sentence, finally delivering justice to Childs' grieving family.
