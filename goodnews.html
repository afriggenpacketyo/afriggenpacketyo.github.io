California Governor Signs 'Ghost Ticket' Ban, but Notes Concerns Over Exemptions

2026-09-28 15:02:49 EDT

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed a bipartisan bill, AB-1349, that prohibits the sale of "ghost tickets" or speculative tickets in the live-event resale market. The law prevents sellers from advertising or listing tickets they do not yet possess, unless authorized by the event presenter. It also targets software used to circumvent ticket limits and misleading resale websites. While Newsom supported the bill's intent to curb unfair practices, he expressed reservations about certain carve-outs that may unfairly exempt some ticket sellers, particularly resale marketplaces like StubHub, which lobbied heavily against the bill. He has urged the bill's author to refine these provisions. Critics argue the final version disproportionately burdens smaller venues while benefiting large platforms.

Senate to Vote on College Sports Bill Regulating NIL Deals

2026-09-28 13:17:43 EDT

The Senate is set to vote on the Protect College Sports Act, a bill aimed at establishing a national framework for Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL) deals in college sports. The legislation would codify athletes' rights to earn NIL money while granting the NCAA, conferences, and schools an antitrust exemption to enforce rules. Co-led by Senators Ted Cruz and Maria Cantwell, the bill proposes tighter transfer rules, a five-year eligibility cap, and increased revenue-sharing for athletes, including provisions for women's and Olympic sports. Earlier attempts to pass similar legislation faced opposition from the Congressional Black Caucus and other groups concerned about potential exploitation of athletes. Detractors argue the bill favors major athletic institutions and could perpetuate an exploitative system.
