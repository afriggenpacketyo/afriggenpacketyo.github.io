Voyager 1 Nears One Light-Day Distance from Earth

2026-11-17 17:40:00 EST

The Voyager 1 spacecraft is nearing a remarkable milestone, poised to reach a distance of 16 billion miles from Earth, which equates to one light-day. Launched in 1977, this 49-year-old probe initially explored Jupiter and Saturn before venturing into interstellar space in 2012. It continues its active NASA mission, measuring cosmic gases and magnetic fields beyond our solar system's protective bubble. Famously, Voyager 1 carries the Golden Record, a curated collection of images, sounds, and greetings intended as a self-portrait of human civilization for any passing aliens. Despite its power expected to deplete by 2030, the spacecraft will continue its silent journey for billions of years.
