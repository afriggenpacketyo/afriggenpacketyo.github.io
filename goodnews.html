Industry Pushes to Change FHA Mortgage Insurance Rules

The mortgage industry is advocating for changes to Federal Housing Administration (FHA) loan rules, specifically regarding mortgage insurance. A recent report to Congress indicates the FHA's insurance fund is financially strong, prompting calls for improved affordability for borrowers. Current FHA rules require borrowers to pay mortgage insurance for extended periods, unlike conventional loans where it can be removed once sufficient equity is built. Proposals include reducing mortgage insurance rates and implementing automatic cancellation of premiums once the loan balance reaches 78% of the original value. These changes aim to alleviate financial burdens on FHA borrowers, particularly first-time homebuyers.
