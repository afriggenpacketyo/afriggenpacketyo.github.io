China inaugurates world's first combined supercritical CO2 and molten salt energy plant

2026-09-22 01:41:58 EDT

China has commenced construction on the world's first utility-scale power plant that integrates supercritical carbon dioxide generation with molten salt energy storage. This innovative project aims to enhance grid stability and reduce energy waste. During periods of low energy demand, surplus electricity from existing coal units will be used to heat molten salt. This stored heat will then power a supercritical unit that utilizes carbon dioxide instead of steam to produce electricity during peak demand hours. The initial phase of the demonstration project, located in Shandong province, will feature a 50-megawatt supercritical unit and a 100-megawatt molten salt energy storage system, with operation expected next year.

Autumn equinox to mark the start of astronomical fall with nearly equal day and night

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

Astronomical autumn begins this week with the autumn equinox occurring in the early hours of Wednesday, September 23rd. This precise moment signifies the Sun crossing the celestial equator, resulting in roughly equal lengths of day and night worldwide. While the equinox itself is not visible, it holds cultural and spiritual significance for many. The term "equinox" derives from Latin for "equal night." Due to atmospheric refraction, daylight hours extend slightly beyond the actual moment of equal light and darkness, with the "equilux" occurring a few days later. The change in daylight hours is more pronounced the further north one travels.
