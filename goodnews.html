UK Faces Increased Likelihood of a White Christmas This Year

2026-11-24 20:03:24 EST

The UK has an increased likelihood of experiencing a white Christmas this year, with meteorological factors pointing towards colder and drier-than-average conditions for the winter months. Historically, 41 out of the last 65 Christmases have been officially white, defined by a single snowflake falling at a Met Office station. While the last widespread white Christmas was in 2010, every year from 2020 to 2023 saw snow recorded somewhere. Key influences include a record-breaking El Niño approaching its peak and an early sudden stratospheric warming event, both of which can subtly impact UK weather patterns, increasing the chances of festive snow.

Marcia Ann Gillespie, Pioneering Black Editor of Essence and Ms. Magazines, Dies at 82

2026-11-24 20:00:24 EST

Marcia Ann Gillespie, a trailblazing journalist and editor who championed Black women's voices and feminist ideals, has died at the age of 82. She played a pivotal role in establishing Essence magazine in the 1970s, becoming its editor-in-chief at 26 and boosting its circulation to 750,000. Later, she made history as the first Black editor-in-chief of Ms. magazine, co-founding Liberty Media for Women to save it from financial collapse. Gillespie dedicated her career to redefining female identity and fostering conversations about race and gender, leaving an indelible mark on American journalism and activism.
