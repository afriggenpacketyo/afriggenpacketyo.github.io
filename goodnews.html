Scientists Discover Two New Species of Sea Spiders in Canadian Salish Sea

2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

Researchers in Canada have discovered two new species of sea spiders in the shallow waters of the Salish Sea, marking the first such find in over a century in the area. These marine arthropods, measuring about a centimeter, were collected off Vancouver and Quadra islands. One species, Callipallene pilosuspedes, features red eyes and unique grooming limbs, while Tanystylum kiixin, named after an Indigenous village, has shorter, stockier legs. Unlike land spiders, these slow-moving creatures are parasites that feed on hydroids and rely on camouflage, with males responsible for egg care.
