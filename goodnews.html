Ancient Rose-Colored Meteorite from Sahara Confirmed as 4.1 Billion-Year-Old Martian Fragment

2026-10-30 11:09:15 EDT

A rose-colored meteorite, named Teghaza 001, discovered in the Sahara Desert in 2022, has been identified by NASA as a 4.1-billion-year-old piece of Mars. This nearly two-pound rock is one of the three most ancient Martian meteorites found on Earth, offering a rare glimpse into the planet's early crust and formation. Scientists confirmed its Martian origin by comparing its chemical and mineral makeup to data from NASA missions, calling it a "Rosetta Stone for Mars." Further analysis revealed the rock was altered by Martian water about 4.1 billion years ago, suggesting a more dynamic early water cycle than previously understood and potentially holding clues about the Martian atmosphere.
