Scientific analysis revealed its unique composition of volcanic minerals, including zircon crystals, which closely match samples observed in Mars' southern highlands. Crucially, the article indicates the rock was significantly altered by Martian water approximately 4.1 billion years ago, suggesting a more dynamic early water cycle than previously understood and offering vital clues about the planet's atmospheric evolution.
