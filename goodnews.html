2026-10-21 02:17:15 EDT

A groundbreaking study challenges the long-held belief that the thousands of life-size Terracotta Warriors were exclusively built by adult men. New quantitative biological evidence, derived from batch fingerprints, indicates that a substantial portion of the ancient army was crafted by adult women and minors. Published in the Journal of Cultural Heritage, these findings provide the first biological proof of a diverse workforce involved in the monumental project near one of China's oldest cities. This discovery significantly redefines our understanding of labor practices and social structures in ancient China, offering fresh perspectives on a monumental historical achievement.
