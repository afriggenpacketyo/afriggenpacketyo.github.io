UN General Assembly Adopts Resolution for New World Map Reflecting Africa's True Size

2026-09-04 20:53:11 EDT

The UN General Assembly has voted to replace the traditional Mercator world map with one that more accurately reflects Africa's true size, adopting the "Correct the Map" resolution. Sponsored by Togo and supported by 164 nations, the non-binding vote encourages the use of equal-area projections like Equal Earth, which correct the Mercator map's distortion that makes countries near the equator appear smaller. Critics argue the Mercator map, designed in 1569 for navigation, minimizes the development needs of equatorial countries. The United States was the sole nation to vote against the resolution, criticizing it as a distraction from genuine international issues.
