Understanding Eligibility for Medicare Supplemental (Medigap) Insurance

2026-08-10 12:42:34 EDT

Medicare supplemental insurance, commonly known as Medigap, is designed to help cover various out-of-pocket expenses that Original Medicare does not fully address, such as deductibles and coinsurance. To qualify for a Medigap policy, individuals must typically be enrolled in both Medicare Part A and Part B, and it cannot be used in conjunction with Medicare Advantage plans. A critical six-month open enrollment period begins when a person turns 65 and enrolls in Part B, offering crucial protections against denial or higher premiums due to pre-existing health conditions.
