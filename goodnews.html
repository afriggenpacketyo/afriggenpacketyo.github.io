2026-11-26 09:55:16 EST

UK net migration has significantly fallen to 77,000 in the year ending June, reaching its lowest level since 1997, excluding the unique circumstances of the pandemic, as reported by the Office for National Statistics (ONS). Prime Minister Andy Burnham asserted that these figures demonstrate "a sufficient degree of control" over the migration system, aligning with public expectations.
