Fluck, who partnered with his childhood friend Roger Law under 'Luck and Flaw,' revolutionized caricaturing with a distinctive, sharp edge that evolved into the celebrated TV series. "Spitting Image" ran from 1984 to 1996, captivating audiences with its often controversial puppet depictions of prominent political figures, celebrities, and members of the royal family, and garnered several prestigious awards, including Baftas and Emmys. His family honored his legacy, stating the show's spirit was "epoch defining" and highlighted his preference for a private life away from fame.
