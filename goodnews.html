United Nations Calls for Adoption of New World Map to Accurately Represent Continents

2026-09-05 09:46:02 EDT

The United Nations has passed a resolution, "Correct the Map," advocating for the replacement of the predominant Mercator world map with a more accurate representation of continental sizes. The Mercator projection, created in 1569, distorts landmasses, making continents like Africa appear significantly smaller than their true size, while exaggerating those near the poles. The UN has adopted the Equal Earth projection, which better reflects actual landmass proportions, despite having its own drawbacks. Critics argue that the psychological effect of distorted maps implicitly downplays the importance of regions like Africa, influencing political and economic perceptions globally.
