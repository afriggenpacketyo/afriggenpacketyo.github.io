Understanding 'Hang-xiety': The Anxiety That Can Follow a Hangover

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

Hang-xiety, the anxiety experienced after a hangover, is a real phenomenon explained by alcohol's effects on the brain's chemical messengers, GABA and glutamate. As alcohol wears off, the nervous system can become over-aroused, triggering anxiety. Studies indicate that a significant percentage of students report experiencing hang-xiety, and it appears to be more pronounced in individuals who are naturally shy. Experts advise against using alcohol to manage stress and suggest reframing anxious thoughts as temporary after-effects rather than indications of social catastrophe.
