Medieval Convent Discovered Beneath Danish Soccer Field

2026-09-07 08:00:07 EDT

Archaeologists in Roskilde, Denmark, have unearthed a remarkably well-preserved medieval convent, St. Clare's, dating back to the 13th century. The discovery was made during a ground-penetrating radar survey for a planned underground parking facility. Traces of rooms, columns, cloisters, and a church have been identified, with the eastern wing being exceptionally intact. This find is significant as St. Clare's was previously believed to be lost and not protected as an ancient monument, offering a rare glimpse into monastic life in medieval Roskilde. Researchers anticipate finding numerous artifacts from daily life, religious objects, and potentially even human remains, which could shed light on the nuns and the convent's international connections. The discovery also highlights the effectiveness of geophysical surveys in archaeological exploration.
