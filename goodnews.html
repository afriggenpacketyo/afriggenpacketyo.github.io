How and When to Observe the Leonid Meteor Shower in November 2026

2026-11-06 21:03:28 EST

The Leonid meteor shower is set to be visible across the UK throughout November, with its peak on the night of November 17, potentially offering 10-15 meteors per hour. Composed of dust particles from Comet 55P/Tempel-Tuttle, these streaks of light are among the fastest in the sky, entering Earth's atmosphere at 44 miles per second. Experts advise finding a safe, dark location away from light pollution and allowing eyes to adapt for 20-30 minutes for the best viewing experience. Clear skies are essential, and viewing conditions around the peak are expected to be favorable as the Moon will be below the horizon, enhancing visibility.
