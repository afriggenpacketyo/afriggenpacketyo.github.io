Nobel Prize in Economics awarded for work on 'sticky prices' and macroeconomic stabilization

2026-10-13 06:30:00 EDT

The Nobel Prize in Economics has been awarded to Robert Hall and Michael Woodford for their research on the microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilization, particularly their work on 'sticky prices.' This concept explains how prices and wages can remain fixed even as economic conditions change, influencing economic downturns and inflation. Their contributions helped reshape macroeconomic theory and inform policy responses to recessions, revitalizing Keynesian economics with a focus on individual decision-making and market rigidities.
