"Hang-xiety," the Anxiety Accompanying Hangovers, Confirmed as a Real Phenomenon

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

"Hang-xiety," the anxiety experienced during a hangover, is a real phenomenon, according to experts. Alcohol affects brain chemicals like GABA and glutamate, leading to relaxation during drinking, but a rebound effect as it wears off can trigger heightened alertness and anxiety. Studies show that not everyone experiences hang-xiety, with existing psychological traits, particularly shyness, playing a significant role. Shy individuals, who might feel less anxious while drinking, often face more elevated anxiety markers the next day. Reframing these thoughts as a temporary state can help manage the dread.
