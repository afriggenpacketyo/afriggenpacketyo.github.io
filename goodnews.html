Mesh Networks Offer Potential Solution to State-Imposed Internet Blackouts

2026-07-25 07:08:00 EDT

"LoRa mesh networks" are being explored as a crucial tool to circumvent state-imposed internet blackouts, particularly in authoritarian regimes. This technology utilizes small, inexpensive radio modules to create decentralized, self-repairing communication networks, transmitting encrypted data packets over several kilometers. Unlike the traditional internet, these networks are immune to central shutdowns and government surveillance, offering a secure communication channel. While primarily limited to short text messages, the system has demonstrated its effectiveness in real-world crisis situations, notably in Ukraine, providing an alternative for essential communication.

Finland Pioneers World's Largest Sand Battery for Renewable Energy Storage

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in southern Finland is leading the way in renewable energy storage with the world's largest commercial sand battery, effectively tackling the intermittency of clean energy.
