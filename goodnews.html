UN Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Global Representation

2026-09-06 08:30:50 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map that more accurately depicts the true size of continents, particularly Africa. Sponsored by Togo and supported by 164 member states, the resolution encourages replacing the traditional Mercator map, which distorts landmasses by enlarging regions near the poles. The Mercator projection makes Africa appear similar in size to Greenland, while the Equal Earth projection reveals Africa is 14 times larger. This move aims to correct historical misrepresentations and influence collective perceptions of the world.
