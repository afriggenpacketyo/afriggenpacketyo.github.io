UN General Assembly Backs New World Map Showing Africa's True Relative Size

2026-09-04 18:26:35 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has overwhelmingly supported a resolution advocating for the wider use of equal-area map projections, such as Equal Earth, which more accurately depict the true relative size of continents, particularly Africa. This non-binding "Correct the Map" resolution encourages a departure from the traditional Mercator projection, known for distorting sizes and making northern regions appear disproportionately large. Supporters argue that this change will challenge longstanding misconceptions about Africa's scale and importance, fostering a fairer visual representation of the world. The United States was the sole country to vote against the initiative.
