Using Electric Fans in Extreme Heat Can Be Dangerous, Experts Warn

2026-07-31 19:57:16 EDT

As Southern California anticipates a brutal heat wave, health experts warn against a common mistake: using electric fans when temperatures exceed 90-95 degrees Fahrenheit. According to the CDC and UCLA experts, fans can act like convection ovens, circulating hot air and potentially raising body temperature, increasing risks of dehydration and heat exhaustion. While fans can be beneficial at night for ventilation when temperatures drop, during peak daytime heat, they can be harmful, especially for vulnerable groups. Strategies like nighttime ventilation, daytime shading, and increased water intake are recommended to stay cool without air conditioning during extreme heat.
