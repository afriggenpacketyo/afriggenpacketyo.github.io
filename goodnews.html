Japan Launches Ambitious Robotic Mission to Explore Martian Moons and Return Samples

2026-10-19 17:42:23 EDT

Japan has launched the ambitious Martian Moons Exploration (MMX) mission, a $350 million endeavor to study Mars' two moons, Phobos and Deimos, in unprecedented detail. The mission's primary goal is to determine their origin—whether they were formed from a giant impact or are captured asteroids—by deploying a small rover on Phobos and subsequently collecting soil samples for return to Earth by 2031. The MMX spacecraft will spend three years orbiting Mars, conducting detailed observations of the moons' composition and assessing Phobos's potential as a future staging base for astronauts. This pioneering mission marks the first time a spacecraft will return to Earth from the Mars system, promising significant scientific breakthroughs.
