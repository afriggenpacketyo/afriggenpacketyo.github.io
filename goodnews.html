Osteoporosis Medications Show Link to Reduced Alzheimer's and Dementia Risk

2026-09-16 17:13:24 EDT

A large observational study involving over 121,000 older adults in Hong Kong suggests that nitrogen-containing bisphosphonates (NBP), common osteoporosis drugs, are associated with a significantly lower risk of Alzheimer's disease and related dementias. Compared to non-users, NBP users had a 16% reduced risk, rising to 24% against those on other osteoporosis medications. While these findings highlight a potential "bone-brain axis" and the importance of bone health for dementia prevention, researchers emphasize that the observational design means it does not prove causation. Further clinical trials are necessary to confirm this benefit and determine if these drugs could be prescribed specifically for dementia prevention.
