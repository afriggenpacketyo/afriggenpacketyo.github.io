The United Nations has called for the adoption of a new world map, the Equal Earth projection, to more accurately represent the true sizes of continents. The traditional Mercator map, created in 1569, significantly distorts landmasses, making continents like Africa appear much smaller than their actual size. Critics argue this distortion has psychological and political implications, implicitly downplaying the importance of certain regions. While the Equal Earth map addresses area accuracy, it sacrifices straight lines and true distance measurement, highlighting the inherent challenges of mapping a sphere onto a flat surface.
