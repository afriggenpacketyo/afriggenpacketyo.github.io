Avoiding a Common Heatwave Mistake: When Electric Fans Become Dangerous

2026-07-31 19:57:16 EDT

During extreme heat waves, particularly when temperatures soar above 90-95 degrees Fahrenheit, using electric fans can paradoxically increase body temperature and pose significant health risks, according to the CDC and climate experts. Fans essentially function as convection ovens, circulating hot air and thereby accelerating dehydration and the onset of heat exhaustion, especially for vulnerable individuals. UCLA experts emphasize that while fans are effective for nighttime ventilation, they become dangerous once the ambient air temperature exceeds skin temperature. Therefore, strategic cooling methods, including thorough nighttime ventilation, diligent daytime shading, and substantially increased water intake, are crucial for staying safe in soaring temperatures, particularly for those without air conditioning.
