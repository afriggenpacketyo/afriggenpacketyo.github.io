California Scientist Discovers Exceptionally Preserved 10,000-Year-Old Mastodon Tooth

2026-09-12 16:34:58 EDT

A California geomorphologist, Brigid Lynch, has discovered an exceptionally well-preserved 10,000-year-old Pacific mastodon molar in San Mateo County. Paleontologists hail it as the most complete and spectacular mastodon molar found in the area, offering valuable insights into the Ice Age saga. Pacific mastodons, ancient relatives of elephants, went extinct over 10,000 years ago due to climate change and overhunting. The fossilized tooth has been donated to Stanford University for carbon dating and further research, which will aid in understanding past biodiversity and informing future ecosystem preservation efforts.
