Ancient Shipwreck Discovered Off Turkish Coast, Dated Over 4,400 Years Old

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have announced the discovery of the world's oldest shipwreck off Turkey's Kekova Island, dating it to the Early Bronze Age, approximately 4,400 to 4,700 years ago. Although the ship's wooden structure has not survived the millennia, over 30 ceramic storage pots, or amphorae, were found remarkably intact on the seabed, some still containing traces of their original contents. Led by Prof. Hakan Öniz, the expedition determined the 10-meter vessel was a trade ship, with the pottery styles matching those from ancient Egypt and the Middle East of the same period. This significant "time capsule" discovery, now undergoing careful conservation, offers unprecedented insights into early Mediterranean trade routes and ancient life.
