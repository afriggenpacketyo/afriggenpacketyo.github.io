First Adult Tyrannosaurus Rex Trackway Discovered in North Dakota

2026-09-10 11:38:43 EDT

Paleontologists have achieved a groundbreaking discovery in North Dakota's badlands: the first known trackway left by a full-grown Tyrannosaurus rex. This remarkable 23-foot-long sequence of four connected footprints offers unprecedented insights into the locomotion and behavior of the massive predator, revealing it walked at an estimated 3.5 to 4.5 mph. Prior to this find, only two isolated adult T. rex footprints had ever been confirmed globally, providing limited information on movement. The tracks, dating back approximately 66.5 million years, offer a unique glimpse into the final moments of these iconic dinosaurs before their extinction, with three of the invaluable footprints slated for transport to the Denver Museum of Nature & Science.
