FDA Identifies Potential Source of Cyclosporiasis Outbreak Linked to Lettuce

2026-10-02 16:23:37 EDT

The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has identified a potential source for the cyclosporiasis outbreak tied to lettuce over the summer, collecting samples from Taylor Farms de Mexico facilities. Two samples tested positive for the Cyclospora parasite, one from outgoing wastewater and another from a drainage ditch of an iceberg lettuce grower. Although conclusive evidence on contamination is pending, the positive samples reinforce epidemiological and traceback data linking the outbreak to recalled shredded iceberg lettuce from Taylor Farms de Mexico. The investigation is now closed, with officials confident all contaminated lettuce is off the market after infecting over 12,800 people and causing two deaths.

Hawaii's Historic Hōlei Sea Arch Collapses into Pacific Ocean

2026-10-02 16:16:35 EDT

Hawaii's famed Hōlei sea arch, a centuries-old landmark standing approximately 90 feet high in Volcanoes National Park, has collapsed into the Pacific Ocean following a series of rough storms. Naturally formed around 550 years ago through differential erosion of ancient lava flows, the arch's exact collapse timing is unknown but was confirmed between September 23 and 27, after Tropical Storm Nolo. The National Park Service had noted the arch's instability for years, exacerbated by tropical systems and volcanic activity, making its eventual collapse anticipated. This natural event, while expected, marks a significant change to Hawaii's dramatic southern coastline, reminding observers of the dynamic forces shaping the islands.
