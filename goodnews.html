Chinese Team Discovers World's Oldest Amber in Xinjiang

2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

A team from the Chinese Academy of Sciences’ Nanjing Institute of Geology and Palaeontology has unearthed the world’s oldest known amber in an ancient Xinjiang coal seam. Dating back approximately 393 million to 383 million years to the Middle Devonian period, the discovery predates the previous record holder by millions of years. Researchers initially doubted their findings due to the amber's extreme age, but chemical analysis confirmed its authenticity. This significant paleontological find offers new insights into ancient ecosystems and the Earth's history, pushing back the timeline for known amber preservation and expanding scientific understanding.
