2026-09-10 11:38:43 EDT

This significant find, consisting of four connected footprints, provides unprecedented insights into the locomotion and behavior of the massive dinosaur. Researchers estimate the T. rex was walking at approximately 3.5 to 4.5 mph, despite its immense weight. The tracks date back to around 66.5 million years ago, near the end of the Cretaceous period. 2026-09-10 11:05:59 EDT

Experts suggest several strategies, including directly asking card issuers for a lower APR, which typically does not affect credit if no hard inquiry is involved. Enrolling in hardship programs can also lower rates but may impact credit utilization. Balance transfer cards offer introductory low APRs, but applying for a new card involves a hard inquiry, though paying down the balance can improve credit utilization long-term. Debt management programs negotiated by credit counseling agencies can also lower rates, though they may require closing accounts. Careful consideration of each option's impact is crucial.
