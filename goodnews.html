First Adult Tyrannosaurus Rex Trackway Discovered in North Dakota

2026-09-10 11:38:43 EDT

Paleontologists have announced a groundbreaking discovery in North Dakota's badlands: the first-ever known trackway made by a full-grown Tyrannosaurus rex. This remarkable 23-foot-long trackway comprises four connected, three-toed footprints, each roughly 3 feet in length, offering unprecedented insights into the locomotion of an adult T. rex. Prior to this find, only two isolated adult T. rex footprints had been confirmed worldwide. Researchers utilized the spacing between the prints to estimate the seven-ton dinosaur walked at a pace of 3.5 to 4.5 mph. Dating back approximately 66.5 million years, this discovery provides a rare behavioral snapshot of these iconic predators.
