California to Implement New License Plate Sequence for First Time Since 1980s

2026-09-11 18:59:37 EDT

California is changing its license plate sequence for the first time since the 1980s due to running out of available combinations. The current format, which has been in use for 46 years, consists of one number, three letters, and three numbers. The state's Department of Motor Vehicles has confirmed that the last plate of the old sequence has been produced, and a new configuration, starting with three numerals, three letters, and one numeral, is being rolled out. This new system is expected to last approximately 40 years, with each configuration offering over 100 million unique combinations.
