The Brain's Default Mode Network: Why Boredom is Essential for Cognitive Function

2026-11-11 01:57:00 EST

This network helps individuals make sense of their experiences and form a coherent sense of self. While modern lifestyles, particularly smartphone use, may reduce opportunities for DMN activation, research suggests problematic usage, rather than scrolling itself, impacts mental health. The DMN can also contribute to negative states like rumination in depression and is affected by Alzheimer's disease. Protecting the DMN involves prioritizing sleep, managing stress, and embracing moments of quiet reflection.
