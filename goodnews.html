NASA's Roman Space Telescope poised for launch, promising new cosmic insights

Its advanced wide-field camera will capture vast amounts of data, enabling unprecedented studies of dark matter, dark energy, and the discovery of exoplanets. Roman is expected to downlink significantly more data than Hubble over its five-year mission, potentially resolving discrepancies in current cosmological models, such as the 'Hubble Tension.' The telescope will also employ a coronagraph to detect exoplanets and analyze their atmospheres, potentially discovering thousands of new worlds. Its launch is scheduled for Sunday from the Kennedy Space Center.
