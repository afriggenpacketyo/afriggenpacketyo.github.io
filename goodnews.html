2026-11-13 00:18:22 EST

Over 1.4 million California residents who purchased non-season tickets online for Knott’s Berry Farm, Knott’s Soak City, or California’s Great America between June 28, 2020, and July 1, 2024, are eligible for an $8 refund. This eligibility is part of an $11 million class-action lawsuit settlement against Six Flags Entertainment Corp., which acquired the parks' former operator, Cedar Fair. The lawsuit alleged "drip pricing," where a $9.99 processing fee was only disclosed at checkout, constituting a "junk fee." Eligible customers can submit an online claim for a cash refund by December 22, 2026, or automatically receive an $8 credit for Six Flags' website if no claim is made, following the implementation of a state law banning such hidden fees.
