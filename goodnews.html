2026-09-16 20:09:43 EDT

Factors such as smoking, certain antibiotics, and contraceptive pills can also significantly alter how long caffeine remains active in the system. Sleep researchers generally advise avoiding caffeine for at least six hours before bedtime, but stress the importance of individual sensitivity.
