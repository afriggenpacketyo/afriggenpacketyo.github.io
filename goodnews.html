Renowned Nanotechnology Expert Antonio Facchetti Joins HKUST

2026-10-11 00:00:13 EDT

Antonio Facchetti, a distinguished materials scientist and chemist, has joined the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) as a chair professor in its new Department of Materials. Formerly with the Georgia Institute of Technology, Facchetti was elected to the US National Academy of Engineering last year for his significant contributions to commercially viable electronic materials and devices. His extensive research focuses on semiconductors, dielectrics for thin-film transistors, sensors, batteries, and photovoltaics. As the chief technology officer and co-founder of Flexterra Corporation, he also specializes in flexible transistors for e-Paper and OLED displays, bringing invaluable expertise to HKUST's burgeoning nanotechnology and materials science programs.
