NASA's Swift Observatory Reenters Earth's Atmosphere After Two Decades

2026-11-04 16:55:03 EST

NASA's Swift Observatory, a 3,200-pound space telescope, reentered Earth's atmosphere over North Sumatra, Indonesia, marking the end of its 22-year mission. Launched in 2004, the observatory, initially designed for a two-year mission, far exceeded expectations by detecting thousands of gamma-ray bursts and exploding stars, serving as a crucial "first responder" for other major telescopes. A rescue mission to reposition the telescope failed earlier in the year, and its systems were shut down in late October due to its low orbit. While much of the instrument burned up upon reentry, with NASA assessing the risk of harm as unlikely, scientists are now focused on replacing the invaluable data and insights Swift provided to astronomy.
