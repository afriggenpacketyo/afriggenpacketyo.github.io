Study Reveals No Financial Penalty for Companies Maintaining DEI Policies Despite Trump Administration Pressure

2026-08-18 13:52:05 EDT

A new study indicates that S&P 500 companies maintaining their Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) policies during the second Trump administration incurred no financial penalties, performing comparably to firms that scaled back such efforts. The research, by Jacob Grumbach of UC Berkeley, analyzed financial performance before and after President Trump's 2025 executive order aimed at cutting federal DEI programs and encouraging private sector adherence. The findings suggest that firms have considerable leeway to resist political pressure to reduce diversity initiatives, as consumer support or backlash for DEI efforts did not significantly impact revenue or stock market returns, with notable exceptions like Bud Light.

Apple Revises European App Store Fees to Address Digital Markets Act Compliance

2026-08-18 13:16:49 EDT

Apple has announced a new fee structure for third-party app stores in the European Union, aiming to resolve conflicts with regulators stemming from the Digital Markets Act. Under the revised policy, apps distributed through alternative stores or the web will face a 5% "Core Technology Commission," while apps using Apple's payment system will pay 26% and those with their own systems 20%. This overhaul replaces a more complex previous proposal and takes effect October 1. The changes reflect Apple's ongoing global challenges to its App Store model, which critics argue operates as a monopoly, but Apple maintains its system enhances trust and safety.
