Saint, a delicate and porcelain beauty, won an Academy Award for her debut film performance alongside Marlon Brando in "On the Waterfront." Despite her early success, she intentionally limited her roles after marrying director Jeffrey Hayden in 1951 to prioritize raising her children. She later returned to television, earning an Emmy in 1990, leaving behind an enduring legacy in film and television spanning eight decades.
