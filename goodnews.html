2026-11-15 17:41:22 EST

Launched in 1977, the spacecraft has journeyed for nearly 50 years, discovering Jupiter's faint ring system and detailing Saturn's rings. It crossed into interstellar space in 2012 and continues to chart magnetic fields and plasma waves beyond the heliosphere. NASA is inviting the public to celebrate this achievement by blinking Voyager's call sign in Morse code, acknowledging its enduring mission and the Golden Record it carries.
