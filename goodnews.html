Understanding and Treating Adult Acne: Causes and Solutions

2026-10-12 21:44:16 EDT

Adult acne is a prevalent condition that can significantly impact self-esteem, affecting individuals well beyond their teenage years, often appearing on the face, chest, shoulders, and back. Dermatologists categorize persistent breakouts with discoloration or texture changes as a medical condition, distinguishing it from occasional pimples. Causes are multifaceted, including genetics, hormones, environment, and diet, with hormonal acne frequently manifesting along the jawline and chin in older adults and women. Treatment options range from over-the-counter creams with benzoyl peroxide or retinoids to prescription medications like antibiotics or isotretinoin for more severe cases. Experts emphasize the importance of not picking at pimples and seeking professional help for effective, personalized treatment plans.
