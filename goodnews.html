'Hang-xiety': The Real Anxiety That Can Accompany a Hangover

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

Hang-xiety, the anxiety experienced after drinking alcohol, is a real phenomenon explained by the way alcohol affects the brain's chemical messengers, GABA and glutamate. While alcohol initially enhances GABA's calming effects, its withdrawal can lead to increased alertness and arousal, triggering anxiety. Studies indicate that a significant percentage of people experience hangover anxiety, with shy individuals potentially being more susceptible. Experts advise against using alcohol to manage stress and suggest reframing anxious thoughts as temporary states rather than dire social catastrophes.
