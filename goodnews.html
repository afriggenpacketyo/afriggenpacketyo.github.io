2026-09-15 10:42:00 EDT

Researchers in Spain have unearthed the first skeletal evidence of the Diplodocus dinosaur outside of North America, finding various vertebrae and chevron bones dating back approximately 150 million years. This discovery at the La Tejeria fossil site in eastern Spain challenges previous understanding, suggesting that land-based dinosaurs could cross vast distances between continents during the Late Jurassic period. The find provides strong evidence for episodic dispersal events and faunal exchanges across the proto-North Atlantic Ocean, reinforcing theories of ancient land bridges connecting Iberia to what is now Canada.
