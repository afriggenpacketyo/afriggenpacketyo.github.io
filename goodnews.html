Japan Launches MMX Mission to Retrieve Sample from Mars' Moon Phobos

2026-10-19 15:45:46 EDT

Japan has launched the Mars Moons eXploration (MMX) mission, aiming to collect and return a sample of moondust from Mars' moon Phobos to Earth by 2031. This ambitious mission, led by the Japanese Space Agency, seeks to unravel the mystery of how Mars' moons formed, with two competing theories involving either an impact event or captured asteroids. The spacecraft will deploy a small rover to scout a landing site before collecting a 10g sample, which scientists hope will also contain grains of Mars itself. This endeavor follows the effective cancellation of a NASA/ESA mission to return Martian samples, positioning MMX as a crucial step in planetary science.
