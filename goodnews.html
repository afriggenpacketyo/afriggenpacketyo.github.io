Understanding Travel Insurance Coverage for Weather-Related Flight Disruptions

2026-11-11 08:17:51 EST

This article clarifies the extent to which travel insurance covers flight delays and cancellations specifically caused by severe weather, stressing that the scope of coverage can vary significantly between policies. Trip delay benefits may reimburse reasonable expenses such as meals, lodging, and transportation incurred while waiting for travel to resume, provided minimum delay requirements are satisfied. Conversely, trip cancellation or interruption coverage safeguards prepaid, nonrefundable costs if severe weather prevents or disrupts the broader trip, aligning with the policy's definition of a covered event. Travelers must differentiate these insurance benefits from airline refunds, which are generally mandated by the US Department of Transportation for cancellations. The article advises careful review of policy terms, timing, and documentation for effective claims. (not investment advice)
