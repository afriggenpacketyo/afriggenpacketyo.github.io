England and Wales to Abolish 'Feudal' Leasehold System for New Flats

2026-11-18 21:02:23 EST

The UK government plans to introduce new laws to scrap the "feudal" leasehold system for most new flats in England and Wales, replacing it with commonhold, where residents collectively own and manage their buildings. Housing Minister Matthew Pennycook described the current system as "blighting lives," with leaseholders often facing escalating ground rents and high service charges. The proposed bill will cap ground rent at £250, reducing it to a "peppercorn" rate after 40 years, and aims to make it easier for existing leaseholders to convert to commonhold. While proponents hail the reform, freeholders argue the policy is "unjust" and could significantly impact investment markets.
