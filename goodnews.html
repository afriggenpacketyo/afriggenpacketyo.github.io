Understanding Individual Differences in Caffeine Sensitivity and Its Effects

2026-09-16 20:09:43 EDT

This article delves into the fascinating reasons behind varying individual sensitivities to caffeine, explaining why some people experience a strong "buzz" while others remain largely unaffected. A key determinant is the body's metabolic rate, specifically how quickly a liver enzyme breaks down and flushes out caffeine, a process that can range from six to 20 hours and is influenced by genetics. Additional factors include smoking, which accelerates caffeine metabolism, and certain prescription medications, such as antibiotics or contraceptive pills, which can conversely extend its presence in the body. Sleep researchers generally advise avoiding caffeine for at least six hours before bedtime, as it can significantly impact sleep quality, though moderate intake is linked to positive health outcomes.
