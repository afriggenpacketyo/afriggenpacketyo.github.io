Surgeon Performs First U.S. Telerobotic Prostatectomy from 35 Miles Away

2026-10-27 12:54:45 EDT

A surgeon in Florida has successfully performed the first telerobotic prostatectomy between two U.S. hospitals, removing a cancer patient's prostate from 35 miles away. The procedure, part of an investigational clinical study, allowed the surgeon to operate remotely using robotic instruments. While the surgery was successful and the patient is recovering well, researchers are still evaluating the safety, reliability, and cybersecurity of telesurgery. This marks an expansion of the study into U.S. hospitals after previous procedures between Florida and Angola, aiming to assess if telesurgery can reduce geographical barriers to specialized medical care.
