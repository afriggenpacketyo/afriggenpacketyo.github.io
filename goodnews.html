This mission aims to explore the planet's extreme environment, characterized by drastic temperature fluctuations and intense solar radiation. Scientists hope to solve long-standing mysteries about Mercury, such as the presence of ice in its craters and the origin of its surface hollows. The spacecraft, a joint mission between the European Space Agency and the Japanese Space Agency, utilized a complex trajectory involving multiple planetary flybys to slow down sufficiently for orbit insertion.
