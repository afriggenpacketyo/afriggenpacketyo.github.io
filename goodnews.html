Prime Minister Burnham scraps controversial plans to limit jury trials

2026-10-03 23:07:30 EDT

Prime Minister Andy Burnham has withdrawn controversial proposals that would have significantly curtailed jury trials in England and Wales. The original plans, introduced by the Ministry of Justice, aimed to allow judges to make sole decisions in cases carrying sentences of less than three years, with jury trials reserved for only the most serious offenses. The decision to scrap the plans followed strong opposition from criminal lawyers and a "strength of feeling" expressed by stakeholders. While the government plans to proceed with some reforms to protect victims and witnesses, the core right to trial by jury for a broader range of offenses will be maintained, a move welcomed by legal professionals.
