Dentists are observing an increase in bruxism, or teeth grinding, particularly among young people, attributing it to rising stress levels. The condition, affecting nearly one in four people, often goes unnoticed until symptoms like jaw ache, headaches, or tooth damage appear. Psychotherapists link grinding to the body's "fight or flight" response to trauma and stress, releasing tension overnight. While mouthguards can protect teeth, experts emphasize addressing underlying causes, including serious conditions like sleep apnoea, and promoting lifestyle changes. Some treatments, like Botox injections, offer relief, but a multidisciplinary approach is crucial for long-term management.

10,000-Year-Old Mastodon Tooth Discovered by Scientist in California

2026-09-12 16:34:58 EDT

A California geomorphologist, Brigid Lynch, discovered a remarkably preserved 10,000-year-old adult Pacific mastodon molar in San Mateo County, a significant paleontological find. Paleontologists confirmed the discovery, calling it the "most complete mastodon molar" from the area, providing a crucial piece to the Ice Age puzzle. Pacific mastodons, ancient relatives of elephants, were prevalent in the American West and went extinct over 10,000 years ago due to climate change and overhunting. The fossilized tooth has been donated to Stanford University for carbon dating and research, offering insights into past biodiversity and ecosystems.
