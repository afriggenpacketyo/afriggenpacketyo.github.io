The dachshund's evolution from badger hunter to cultural icon

2026-10-30 03:57:00 EDT

The dachshund, originally bred in Germany over 300 years ago to hunt badgers, has evolved into a significant cultural icon. Its distinctive silhouette has inspired numerous artists, including Pablo Picasso and Andy Warhol, who have featured the breed in their work. Historically, the dachshund has also held political symbolism, even being used as a national representation in propaganda during World War I. More recently, the breed's cheerful disposition led to its selection as the mascot for the 1972 Munich Olympics, embodying a new, more open image for West Germany. Despite attempts by extremist groups to co-opt its image, the dachshund largely remains a symbol of friendliness and joy.
