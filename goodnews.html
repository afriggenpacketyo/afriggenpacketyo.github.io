Māori Kiwi Feather Cloak Returns to New Zealand from British Museum

2026-10-10 15:37:20 EDT

A significant cultural artifact, a Māori kiwi feather cloak known as a kahu kiwi, has been repatriated to New Zealand from the Pitt Rivers Museum in Oxford, UK. The cloak, originally a farewell gift from Māori leaders to Dr. George Cleghorn in 1900, was loaned to the museum in 1913. A ceremony marked its return, funded by The Homebound Project charity, which emphasized the cloak's spiritual desire to return home. This repatriation underscores ongoing efforts to return indigenous cultural heritage items to their communities of origin.
