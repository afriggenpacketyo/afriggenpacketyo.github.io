Study Suggests Rule-Breaking Behavior Can Be Contagious, Especially Among Political Affiliates

2026-11-23 01:33:00 EST

A University of Amsterdam study indicates that rule-breaking can be contagious, particularly when individuals observe someone from their own political group violating rules. In an online experiment, participants were nearly twice as likely to cheat after seeing an in-group member do so, with a small percentage even escalating the rule-breaking. Conversely, positive examples had little effect on behavior. While the study had no real-world consequences, researchers suggest its findings could relate to phenomena like speeding or violent protests, challenging the notion of purely individual decision-making and highlighting the significant influence of social observation on adherence to norms.
