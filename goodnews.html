Fossilized teeth reveal giant rodent weighing 66 pounds once lived in Thailand

2026-11-05 07:11:38 EST

Scientists have identified a previously unknown species of giant rodent, estimated to weigh around 66 pounds, that roamed what is now southern Thailand between 33 and 35 million years ago. The discovery, based on two fossilized teeth, offers new insights into the evolution of early rodents and their immense sizes during the Paleogene period. Named Yakamys sirindhornae in honor of Thailand's Princess Sirindhorn, this prehistoric animal belonged to the extinct Ischyromyidae family. While its appearance and diet remain unknown due to limited fossil evidence, the find highlights the past diversity of life in the region.
