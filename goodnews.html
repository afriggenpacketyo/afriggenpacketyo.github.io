Exploring 'Hang-xiety': The Real Phenomenon of Anxiety After Alcohol Consumption

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

"Hang-xiety," the colloquial term for anxiety experienced during a hangover, is a recognized and real phenomenon, according to psychopharmacology experts. Professor Celia Morgan explains that alcohol's effects on brain chemicals like GABA and glutamate initially induce relaxation but cause a rebound effect of increased alertness and arousal as it leaves the system, triggering anxiety. A 2023 study involving over 5,000 students revealed that 18.3% reported symptoms of hang-xiety, with shy individuals showing a stronger effect. Experts recommend reframing these thoughts as a temporary state rather than evidence of social missteps. Alcohol Change UK advises against using alcohol to cope with stress and encourages seeking professional help if difficulties persist.
