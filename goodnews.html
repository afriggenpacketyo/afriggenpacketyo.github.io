UN adopts new map projection that more accurately depicts Africa's size

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has endorsed a new world map projection, the Equal Earth projection, which more accurately represents the size of continents, particularly Africa. Sponsored by Togo and supported by the African Union, the resolution passed with 164 member states voting in favor. This new projection contrasts with the traditional Mercator map, which significantly distorts landmasses, making Africa appear much smaller relative to Greenland. The UN encourages the adoption of the Equal Earth projection to improve global understanding and perceptions.
