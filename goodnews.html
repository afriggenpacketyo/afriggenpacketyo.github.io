England Unveils £39 Billion Plan to Build 70,000 Social and Affordable Homes

2026-08-24 17:58:41 EDT

The UK government has launched a £39 billion initiative to build 70,000 social and affordable homes across England over the next decade, marking the initial phase of a larger commitment to construct 300,000 units. This plan aims to tackle the nation's severe housing crisis, with a significant portion of the new homes allocated for social rent, offering costs approximately half of market rates. While housing charities acknowledge this as a "significant improvement," they emphasize that the proposed number of homes remains insufficient to meet the urgent demand, as 1.34 million households are currently on social housing waiting lists and homelessness figures continue to rise to record levels.

Although the tariff rate is exceptionally high, it applies to a limited range of Canadian exports, affecting roughly 5% of their total shipments to the U.S. Economists explain that while American businesses and consumers ultimately bear the cost of tariffs, companies are often hesitant to immediately transfer these costs to consumers, especially given the uncertainty surrounding how long the Section 338 levies will remain in effect.
