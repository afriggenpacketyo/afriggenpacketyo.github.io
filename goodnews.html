Chinese Scientists Achieve Record-Low Atom Temperatures on Tiangong Space Station

2026-11-05 14:00:07 EST

Chinese scientists aboard the Tiangong space station have achieved a groundbreaking feat, chilling thousands of rubidium atoms to less than 10 trillionths of a degree above absolute zero. This temperature is four times colder than previous records set in a German drop tower and surpasses anything reached by NASA's Cold Atom Lab on the International Space Station. The atoms were maintained at this ultra-low temperature for seven seconds, significantly longer than previous attempts. This scientific breakthrough paves the way for developing ultra-precise space sensors capable of finding hidden minerals, tracking climate change, and testing Einstein’s theory of gravity.
