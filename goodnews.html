Community Shield Victory Rarely Predicts Premier League Title Success, Statistics Show

2026-08-16 01:29:55 EDT

An analysis of Premier League history reveals that winning the Community Shield, an annual curtain-raiser contested by English champions Arsenal and FA Cup winners Manchester City, rarely correlates with subsequent Premier League title success. Since the league's inception in 1992-93, only eight of 34 Community Shield winners have gone on to lift the Premier League trophy in the same season. Interestingly, teams that lost the Community Shield have secured the league title more frequently, with 10 instances. Furthermore, nearly half of all Premier League champions did not even participate in the Community Shield, suggesting its limited predictive power for the season ahead.
