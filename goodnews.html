Understanding and Managing Evening Snacking Habits

2026-11-05 19:42:05 EST

Evening cravings are common due to naturally peaking appetite hormones and the brain's reward system favoring calorie-dense foods, even when full. Factors like non-nutritious earlier meals, fear of nighttime hunger, and habitual associations (e.g., snacking while watching TV) contribute to this behavior. To manage it, experts suggest identifying triggers, making earlier meals more filling, changing routines, and planning healthy, moderate snacks.
