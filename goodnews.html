Greece Enacts Social Media Ban for Children Under 15, Citing Mental Health Concerns

2026-10-23 05:04:00 EDT

Greece's parliament has passed a bill banning social media use for minors under 15, set to take effect on January 1, 2027. The measure, primarily supported by the New Democracy party, aims to combat the negative impacts of "uncontrolled internet use" on children's mental health, sleep, attention spans, and physical activity. Minister Dimitris Papastergiou defended the move as "necessary, effective, proportionate," and EU-compliant, emphasizing the government's duty to protect childhood. Greece joins a growing number of countries, including Australia, in implementing stricter age verification standards, with social media platforms now required to determine user age, despite opposition parties largely voting against or abstaining from the bill.
