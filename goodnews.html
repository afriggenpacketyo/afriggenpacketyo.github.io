Leonid Meteor Shower Set to Dazzle UK Skygazers This November

2026-11-06 21:03:28 EST

The Leonid meteor shower is expected to be visible across the UK throughout November, with its peak on the night of November 17, offering up to 10-15 meteors per hour. Composed of dust particles from Comet 55P/Tempel-Tuttle, these meteors are the fastest in the sky, entering Earth's atmosphere at high speeds. Experts recommend finding a dark location away from light pollution and allowing eyes to adapt for 20-30 minutes for the best viewing experience. Clear skies are essential, and while early weekend forecasts show some cloud, better opportunities are expected later in the month around the peak, especially with the moon below the horizon.
