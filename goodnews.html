New early warning system to identify dangerously ill children in English hospitals

2026-11-01 19:51:24 EST

A new National Paediatric Early Warning System (PEWS) is being introduced in English hospital emergency departments to help staff identify critically ill children. The system requires staff to record vital signs and escalate care if a child's overall score is high or if concerns are raised by parents or carers. This initiative aims to prevent "corridor care" and improve patient outcomes, especially during winter pressures on the NHS. The introduction follows a tragic case where a child died after spending hours in a hospital corridor. A similar system for adults, including those with sepsis, is already in use. Martha's Rule, granting patients and families the right to request a rapid review, will also be expanded to all A&E departments.
