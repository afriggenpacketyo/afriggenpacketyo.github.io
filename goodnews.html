While El Niño increases the chance of wet, mild, and stormy weather for autumn, its late-winter impact on cold and snow, potentially influenced by the polar vortex, is uncertain. A strong polar vortex usually brings mild, wet Atlantic air, while disruptions can lead to colder winds. However, past El Niño winters have varied, and current forecasts do not indicate significant cold or snow in the near future.
