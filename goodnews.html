Agrivoltaics: Integrating Solar Panels and Agriculture for Dual Benefits

2026-09-27 00:45:00 EDT

Agrivoltaics, an emerging strategy, involves co-locating solar panels with agriculture, allowing crops to grow or livestock to graze beneath them. This innovative approach offers mutual benefits: panels provide shade and divert water to crops, potentially boosting yields, while cooler conditions under the panels increase their electricity efficiency. Sheep, in particular, are proving ideal for grazing, fertilizing soil and reducing herbicide needs. Although currently less than 1% of global solar capacity, agrivoltaics presents a unique climate solution, simultaneously mitigating emissions and adapting to a warming planet by providing dual income streams for farmers.
