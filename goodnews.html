ESA's Hera Mission Arrives at Asteroid to Assess Planetary Defense Impact

2026-11-25 01:00:36 EST

The European Space Agency's (ESA) Hera mission has successfully arrived at the Dimorphos asteroid to meticulously investigate the damage caused by NASA's 2022 DART spacecraft impact. DART deliberately collided with Dimorphos as an unprecedented test of planetary defense, successfully altering the asteroid's orbit. Hera will now spend the next year studying Dimorphos, and its larger companion Didymos, utilizing advanced instruments and small satellites to precisely measure the asteroid's mass and the impact crater's characteristics. This crucial data will significantly enhance scientists' ability to predict and execute future asteroid deflection missions, bolstering Earth's preparedness against potential cosmic threats.
