Knott’s Berry Farm and Great America Ticket Holders Eligible for Class-Action Refund

2026-11-13 00:18:22 EST

Over a million theme park goers who purchased tickets to Knott’s Berry Farm and Great America may be eligible for an $8 refund from an $11-million class-action settlement. The lawsuit alleged that Six Flags Entertainment Corp., which now owns the parks, engaged in "drip pricing" by not disclosing a $9.99 processing fee until late in the purchase process. California residents who bought non-season tickets online between June 28, 2020, and July 1, 2024, with a California ZIP code, and were charged this fee, are eligible. Claims can be submitted online by December 22, 2026, or an $8 credit will be issued automatically.
