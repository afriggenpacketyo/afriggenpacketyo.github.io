Understanding HSA-Eligible Health Plans for 2027: Tax Benefits Versus High Deductibles

2026-11-05 12:41:16 EST

As workers choose their 2027 health insurance coverage, millions face the critical decision of selecting an HSA-eligible plan, balancing significant tax savings against potentially higher deductibles. These plans offer a "triple tax advantage": contributions are made before taxes, earnings within the account are not taxed, and withdrawals for qualified medical expenses are tax-free. Contributions made through an employer's Section 125 cafeteria plan also typically avoid Social Security and Medicare taxes, enhancing their efficiency. However, HSA-eligible plans usually come with high deductibles, requiring individuals to carefully assess their expected healthcare needs and ensure they have sufficient cash reserves to cover potential out-of-pocket costs before reaching their deductible.
