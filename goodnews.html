Aerospace Firm to Pay Up to $100 Million to Garden Grove Residents After Chemical Leak

2026-08-24 18:36:01 EDT

GKN Aerospace, a prominent international manufacturer of airplane parts, has agreed to pay up to $100 million to residents affected by a chemical leak in Garden Grove. Over 50,000 residents were forced to evacuate when a tank containing 7,000 gallons of toxic methyl methacrylate dangerously overheated due to a cooling system failure. This settlement, reached with the Orange County district attorney, will provide reimbursement for expenses such as hotel stays, meals, and lost wages. The company has decommissioned the problematic tank, and work at its Garden Grove plant remains on hold, contingent upon full compliance with stringent safety standards and a court-approved safety plan.
