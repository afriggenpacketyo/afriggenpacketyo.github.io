Scientists Reconstruct Face of Mysterious Ancient Denisovans

2026-10-23 07:09:08 EDT

Chinese scientists have achieved a significant milestone by reconstructing the facial features of Denisovans, a mysterious species of ancient humans whose existence was previously known mainly through DNA evidence and fragmented fossils. This innovative work relied on fossils excavated from the Xujiayao archaeological site in northern China, dating back to the late 1970s. Confirming these ancient human remains as belonging to the Denisovan lineage allowed researchers to create a visual representation of these hominids, who are thought to have lived across parts of Siberia, East, and Southeast Asia thousands of years ago. This breakthrough offers invaluable insights into human evolutionary history.
