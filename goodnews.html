England and Wales to Abolish 'Feudal' Leasehold System for New Flats

2026-11-18 21:02:23 EST

The UK government plans to introduce new legislation to scrap the leasehold system for most new flats in England and Wales, replacing it with commonhold ownership. Housing minister Matthew Pennycook described the current system as "blighting lives" and an "anachronism in the twenty-first century." The proposed Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill will also cap ground rent for existing leaseholders at £250, eventually reducing it to a "peppercorn" rate after 40 years. This reform aims to align England and Wales with international models where residents collectively own and manage their buildings, despite some freeholder opposition.
