NHS launches world's largest trial of AI for breast cancer screening

2026-11-22 19:49:22 EST

The NHS is launching the world's largest trial, inviting nearly 700,000 women across the UK to assess the effectiveness of artificial intelligence in breast cancer screening. The trial aims to determine if AI can improve early cancer detection and reduce the workload of clinical staff without leading to unnecessary over-diagnosis. Currently, two human experts review each mammogram, but in this trial, one human reader will be replaced by AI. Researchers are focused on ensuring the technology is safe and effective, as breast cancer is the most common cancer among women in the UK.
