BepiColombo Mission Successfully Enters Orbit Around Planet Mercury

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a collaborative effort between the European and Japanese space agencies, successfully achieved orbit around Mercury after an extensive eight-year cruise through the inner solar system. This significant $2 billion endeavor marks only the third time a spacecraft has visited Mercury, and the first in over a decade, following NASA's Mariner 10 and Messenger missions. The mission comprises two robotic explorers that will soon separate to map and study the scorching planet from varying altitudes. Scientists eagerly await the new data, expected to stream down in the spring, which will provide unprecedented insights into Mercury's dense interior, magnetic field, and diverse surface features under extreme conditions.
