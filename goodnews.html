Her condition previously confined her indoors and made simple activities agonizing. Bitopertin, taken as a daily pill, functions by lowering the levels of a specific molecule in the skin that is activated by sunlight, which causes the painful reactions in EPP patients. Data from clinical trials show the medication significantly improved sunlight tolerance and had no notable safety concerns. Now, in Phase 3 trials, this drug has enabled Pearson to achieve her dream of running marathons and live a life free from constant anxiety about sun exposure.
