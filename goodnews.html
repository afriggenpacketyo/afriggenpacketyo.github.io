Understanding Whether Debt Collectors Can Add Fees After Purchasing Your Debt

2026-07-30 10:34:19 EDT

Many borrowers encounter increased balances when their debt is sold to collectors, leading to questions about added fees. While debt collectors cannot arbitrarily invent new charges, they can collect amounts authorized by the original agreement or state law, such as accrued interest or court costs. Borrowers should request an itemized breakdown of charges to verify legitimacy. If the balance appears incorrect, exploring debt relief strategies, including negotiating a settlement for less than the full amount, can be beneficial. Understanding these rights helps protect consumers from unauthorized fees and manage financial hardship effectively.
