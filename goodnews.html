These crossings, which have been the primary method for illegal entry since 2020, involve an average of 68 people per boat, more than double the 2021 figure, increasing risks. While nearly all arrivals claim asylum, the number of small boat arrivals constitutes about 5% of total UK immigration. The NCA emphasizes the impact of interventions on reducing these dangerous journeys, which have claimed at least 84 lives in 2024.
