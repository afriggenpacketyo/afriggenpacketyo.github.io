Understanding the 2026 Autumn Equinox and its Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The autumn equinox, signaling the start of astronomical autumn, is scheduled for 01:06 BST on 23 September 2026. This celestial event marks the precise moment the Sun crosses the celestial equator, leading to roughly equal durations of day and night worldwide. Although the equinox is not visibly observable, it carries significant cultural and spiritual meaning for many, inspiring gatherings at ancient sites like Stonehenge to witness the sunrise. Interestingly, the actual "equilux," where day and night are exactly equal, typically occurs a few days after the astronomical equinox due to the Earth's atmospheric light refraction.
