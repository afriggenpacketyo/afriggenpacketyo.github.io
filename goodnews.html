2026-10-01 21:00:07 EDT

SpaceX Launches Google AI Chips for Space-Based Data Center Initiative

2026-10-01 20:06:02 EDT

Alphabet successfully launched its homegrown AI chips into orbit via a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket, marking a significant step towards realizing space-based data centers. This mission, Transporter-18, deployed Planet Labs satellites equipped with Google's tensor processing units (TPUs) as part of Project Suncatcher, an initiative to develop reliable, solar-powered AI computing infrastructure in space. The goal is to leverage near-constant sunlight in low Earth orbit for continuous AI workloads, offering "infinite real estate" away from terrestrial data center challenges. This collaboration between Alphabet and SpaceX, a major investor, highlights the evolving frontier of AI infrastructure, despite existing challenges like launch costs, extreme conditions, and orbital debris.

SpaceX Crew 13 Docks with ISS, Marking Start of Six-Month Mission

2026-10-01 19:28:40 EDT

Washington D.C. Enacts Law Banning Dog Breed and Weight Restrictions for Landlords

2026-10-01 18:34:15 EDT

Washington D.C. has enacted "Roscoe's Law," which took effect Thursday, banning landlords from refusing tenants' dogs based on breed, size, or weight, and prohibiting related special charges. Animal welfare organizations applauded the legislation, arguing that breed bans unfairly penalize responsible owners and that aggressive behavior is linked more to owner management than breed. The law also caps pet security deposits at 15% of monthly rent and pet rent at 1%, with other non-refundable fees prohibited. While some landlord associations express concern over potential damage costs, advocates believe this measure will reduce barriers to pet ownership and keep families together, fostering a more inclusive housing environment for pet owners.
