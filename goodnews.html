2026-07-26 11:08:40 EDT

New Jersey Governor Mikie Sherrill has signed the Fair Price Protection Act into law, establishing her state as the third in the U.S. to prohibit businesses from leveraging consumers' personal information to charge different prices for identical products. This landmark legislation is designed to shield shoppers from "surveillance pricing," a practice where data such as online activity, geographical location, and purchasing history can arbitrarily influence individualized pricing. Governor Sherrill underscored that businesses should compete by offering superior prices, rather than by devising new methods to exploit personal data. Additionally, the act imposes a one-year moratorium on the rollout of new electronic shelf labels, allowing time for a study into their broader impact, a measure supported by labor unions to protect both shoppers and grocery jobs.
