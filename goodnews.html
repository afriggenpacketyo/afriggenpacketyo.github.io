Strong El Niño Leads to Record-Low Atlantic Hurricane Activity

2026-09-12 11:26:46 EDT

The Atlantic hurricane season is experiencing record-low activity, attributed to a powerful El Niño phenomenon that creates unfavorable conditions for storm formation. This year has seen no Atlantic hurricanes and only five weak tropical storms, marking the longest period without a hurricane in the satellite era, with overall activity at a post-1950 record low. Experts explain that El Niño generates a "triple whammy" of strong wind shear, dry air, and downward-pushing winds that effectively dismantle developing tropical cyclones. While this pattern significantly dampens Atlantic activity, it concurrently fuels increased tropical cyclone activity in the Pacific, with regions like Hawaii already experiencing substantial impacts from more frequent and intense storms.
