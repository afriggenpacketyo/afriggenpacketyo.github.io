Researchers found that individuals who drank their beverages "very hot" (65°C or above) had a threefold higher risk compared to those who drank them warm. The study, involving nearly a million people, suggests that the heat can damage the lining of the food pipe. While the overall risk of this cancer remains low, experts advise waiting a few minutes for hot drinks to cool before consuming them.
