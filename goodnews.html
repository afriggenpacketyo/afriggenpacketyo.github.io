Chinese Scientists Discover World's Oldest Amber in Xinjiang

2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

A team of Chinese palaeontologists has unearthed the world's oldest chemically confirmed amber, dating back an astonishing 393 to 383 million years to the Middle Devonian period. Discovered in a coal seam in Xinjiang, these hundreds of microscopic amber pieces significantly predate the previously oldest known amber by approximately 70 million years. The findings, detailed in the peer-reviewed journal Science Advances, initially met with skepticism due to their extraordinary age. This groundbreaking discovery provides unprecedented new insights into ancient ecosystems and the early evolution of resin-producing plants during a critical geological era.
