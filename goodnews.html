Dry Eyes: A Common, Often Overlooked Condition in Women, Especially During Midlife

2026-09-04 05:00:00 EDT

Dry eyes are a remarkably common and often underestimated chronic condition, predominantly affecting women during midlife and menopause, with studies showing its prevalence doubles in women over 50 compared to men. This condition occurs when the eyes fail to produce enough tears or when tears evaporate too rapidly, frequently stemming from dysfunction in the meibomian glands, which regulate protective oil production. Hormonal fluctuations, particularly declining androgen and estrogen levels, are key contributing factors. Eye care professionals emphasize the importance of addressing symptoms like irritation, fluctuating vision, or redness, as untreated dry eyes can progress to severe corneal damage. Various treatments and lifestyle adjustments are available to manage and alleviate symptoms.
