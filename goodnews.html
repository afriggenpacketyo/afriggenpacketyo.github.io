EPP results from an excess of protoporphyrin IX in the skin, which is activated by sunlight. Bitopertin, taken daily, lowers these levels, increasing sun tolerance. Pearson, who previously limited outdoor activities and struggled with pain, was able to run her first marathon and spend a week at her family's cabin without adverse reactions after participating in the trial. The drug is currently in Phase 3 clinical trials, with data expected later this year. This treatment represents a life-changing development for individuals with EPP.
