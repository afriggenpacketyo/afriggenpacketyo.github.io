Tax Complexities Lurk for Investors in Popular Covered Call ETFs

2026-10-19 08:58:03 EDT

Covered call ETFs, popular for generating monthly income, present complex tax implications that can surprise investors. Distributions from these funds, which can include dividends, capital gains, or non-dividend distributions (return of capital), are not all taxed equally. Return of capital distributions, while seemingly favorable initially, reduce an investment's cost basis, leading to deferred but potentially higher capital gains taxes upon sale. Financial advisors recommend using tax-deferred accounts for these ETFs and urge investors to scrutinize tax-cost ratios and net asset value growth to avoid unexpected tax hits and ensure sustainable returns (not investment advice).
