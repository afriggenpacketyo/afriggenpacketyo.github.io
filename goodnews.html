Google DeepMind's model notably outperformed conventional forecasts in predicting Hurricane Isaias's path. Research Explores How Disgust Sensitivity Influences Political Beliefs

2026-10-26 04:13:00 EDT

Psychologists are investigating the complex relationship between disgust sensitivity and political attitudes, particularly social conservatism. Studies suggest a link, with some theories proposing that aversion to pathogens correlates with a preference for traditional social rules. The article distinguishes three types of disgust—pathogen, sexual, and moral—and notes that liberals and conservatives often find different things revolting. While physical reactions to disgusting images haven't shown clear political divides, questionnaires indicate conservatives report higher general disgust.
