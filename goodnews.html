Experts Explain Why Children's Lying is a Sign of Healthy Development

2026-11-18 19:12:57 EST

Children's propensity to tell fibs, such as blaming a dog for crayon marks or denying forbidden activities, is considered an important and healthy part of their mental development, according to clinical psychologists. Studies indicate that children can exhibit forms of deception before age one, evolving into more proficient and creative lies by age three as their cognitive abilities grow. This behavior is a crucial sign of developing 'theory of mind,' where children learn to understand that others possess separate thoughts and feelings distinct from their own. Experts suggest that parents can effectively encourage honesty by creating a safe, non-judgmental environment where children feel secure enough to tell the truth without fear of severe disappointment or punishment.
