Eligibility for Medicare Supplemental Insurance Explained

2026-08-10 12:42:34 EDT

Medicare supplemental insurance, or Medigap, helps cover out-of-pocket costs not covered by Original Medicare. To qualify, individuals generally need to be enrolled in both Medicare Part A and Part B and cannot have a Medicare Advantage plan. Medigap policies are designed to supplement Original Medicare, not replace it. Eligibility and enrollment rules, particularly the six-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period, are crucial. For those under 65, state-specific protections may apply, as federal law does not universally require insurers to sell Medigap to younger beneficiaries.
