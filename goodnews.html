World's oldest amber discovered in ancient Xinjiang coal seam by Chinese researchers

2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

Chinese researchers have unearthed what is believed to be the world's oldest amber, dating back approximately 393 to 383 million years to the Middle Devonian period. The microscopic amber pieces were found preserved within a unique coal seam in Xinjiang. Previously, the oldest confirmed amber was dated to the Late Carboniferous period, around 320 million years ago. This discovery, detailed in the journal Science Advances, significantly pushes back the timeline for the existence of amber and provides new insights into ancient geological and biological history.
