Ancient Egyptian tomb of judge and father, dating back 4,300 years, discovered near Cairo

2026-09-09 07:21:58 EDT

An Egyptian-Spanish archaeological mission has unearthed a remarkably well-preserved tomb dating back 4,300 years in Saqqara, near Cairo. The tomb belonged to Yunmen, an ancient judge, and his father, Ity, a senior scribe. Yunmen's role involved listening to subjects' appeals, aligning with the ancient Egyptian concept of Ma'at (truth and justice). The tomb's chapel features finely carved reliefs and paintings that have retained their original colors, depicting scenes of daily life, offerings, and funeral rites, offering significant insights into Egypt's Fifth Dynasty.
