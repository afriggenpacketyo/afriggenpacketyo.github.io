Understanding Potential Premium Increases for Long-Term Care Insurance Policies

2026-07-28 12:52:52 EDT

Long-term care insurance premiums can indeed increase after a policy is purchased, but not because of an individual's personal aging, health deterioration, or claims history. Instead, insurers can request state approval to raise premiums for an entire class of similar policyholders, a practice rooted in past underestimations of future claims and life expectancy. While current policies generally use more conservative pricing models, the possibility of future increases remains. To manage costs, consumers are advised to purchase coverage earlier in life, select benefit levels tailored to their needs, compare offerings from multiple insurers, and explore hybrid policies that integrate long-term care with life insurance for enhanced predictability (not investment advice).
