Agrivoltaics: Combining Solar Energy and Agriculture for Dual Benefits

2026-09-25 05:31:00 EDT

Agrivoltaics, the practice of growing crops or grazing livestock beneath solar panels, is a burgeoning strategy offering dual benefits for agriculture and renewable energy. This article highlights how panels provide shade for animals and crops, divert water, and create cooler, damper microclimates that can boost yields, especially for shade-tolerant varieties. Cooler panels also produce electricity more efficiently. While promising for climate mitigation and adaptation, adoption has been slow due to high initial costs and varying effectiveness across different climates. The approach helps address land-use conflicts between farming and solar development, offering a dual-income stream for farmers.
