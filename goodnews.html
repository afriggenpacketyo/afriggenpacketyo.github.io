UN Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Representation of Africa's Size

2026-09-06 08:30:50 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to represent continents in their true proportions, particularly Africa. This resolution, sponsored by Togo and supported by the African Union, aims to replace the traditional Mercator map, which significantly distorts landmasses by enlarging regions near the poles while shrinking Africa and South America. The Mercator projection, useful for 16th-century navigation, incorrectly depicts Greenland and Africa as similar in size, whereas Africa is actually 14 times larger. This move encourages global institutions to adopt more accurate cartography, recognizing how maps influence collective perceptions and education.
