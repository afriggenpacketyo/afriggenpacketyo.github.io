European Space Agency Launches Two Satellites to Enhance Climate Change Monitoring

2026-09-15 03:28:00 EDT

The European Space Agency (ESA) successfully launched two advanced climate observation satellites, Sentinel-3C and Flex, from its Kourou space center in French Guiana. The Flex satellite is designed to monitor the faint fluorescence emitted by plants during photosynthesis, providing crucial data on plant health and the impact of heat waves and climate change. Sentinel-3C will gather extensive data on the marine environment, including temperature and ocean currents, and monitor land use to optimize agricultural yields. These missions are deemed "essential" by the ESA for understanding and addressing ongoing climate change.
