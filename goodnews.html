Northern Lights Dazzle UK, Visible as Far South as England's South Coast

This rare and widespread display was a result of increased solar activity, specifically powerful coronal mass ejections (CMEs) directed towards Earth. These streams of charged particles, when captured by the Earth's magnetic field and directed into the atmosphere, collide with oxygen and nitrogen to create the vibrant colored light. The Met Office anticipates potential further sightings under clear skies until Tuesday, offering more opportunities for this natural phenomenon.
