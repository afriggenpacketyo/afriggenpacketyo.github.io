2026-09-15 03:01:23 EDT

The UK state pension is projected to rise by £488 annually next April, reaching £13,036.40, based on new official figures. This increase is determined by the triple lock guarantee, which ensures the pension rises by the highest of average wage growth, inflation, or 2.5%. Average wage growth, including bonuses, slowed to 3.9% between May and July, making it the likely basis for the increase. The triple lock policy faces increasing scrutiny due to the anticipated rise in pensioner numbers and its growing expense. Chinese Plant Extracts Critical Rare Metals from Lithium Waste in World First

2026-09-15 00:00:05 EDT

China has successfully launched a 500-tonne production line to extract critical rare metals, rubidium and caesium, from lithium waste. This demonstration line in Jiangxi province marks a world first for continuous tower extraction and separation of low-grade rubidium and caesium at this scale. The Institute of Process Engineering under the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS) confirmed stable production. These rare metals are essential for advanced technologies across various frontier fields, including aerospace, quantum computing, biomedicine, and new energy, highlighting a significant technological advancement for China's industrial capabilities and resource recovery efforts.
