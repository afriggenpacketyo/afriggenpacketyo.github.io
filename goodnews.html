BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit After Eight-Year Journey

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a joint European and Japanese endeavor, successfully entered orbit around Mercury after an eight-year cruise through the inner solar system. This marks only the third spacecraft to visit the sun's closest planet and the first in over a decade. The two robotic explorers will separate next month to map and study Mercury's dense interior, magnetic field, and surface features from different altitudes. Scientists anticipate unprecedented data in the spring, offering new insights into one of the solar system's least explored planets despite the extreme temperatures challenging its systems.
