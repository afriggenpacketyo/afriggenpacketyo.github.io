Health Warning: Using Electric Fans in Extreme Heat Can Be Dangerous

2026-07-31 19:57:16 EDT

Public health experts are issuing a critical warning that using electric fans during extreme heat, especially when temperatures soar above 95 degrees Fahrenheit, can paradoxically be dangerous and exacerbate health risks. The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) advises against using fans when temperatures exceed 90 degrees, explaining that fans can circulate hot air akin to a convection oven, thereby increasing body temperature and accelerating the risk of dehydration and heat exhaustion. While fans can be beneficial during cooler nighttime hours or when temperatures are below 95 degrees, individuals lacking air conditioning are strongly urged to prioritize natural ventilation, seek shade, and significantly increase their water intake to stay safe.

The crucial breakthrough occurred two years ago when researchers surveying Cornish maerl beds successfully located sufficient material, enabling comprehensive DNA sequencing and confirmation.
