Conservationists Launch Major Effort to Save Critically Endangered Glutinous Snail in Wales

2026-09-12 02:09:37 EDT

Conservationists are undertaking a vital mission to save the glutinous snail, one of Europe's rarest species, found exclusively in Wales' Llyn Tegid. Dubbed as precious as an "Egyptian artefact," this tiny mollusc is being bred in "ark populations" by British zoos, utilizing creatures initially reared in a conservationist's back garden. The snails, crucial indicators of healthy aquatic environments, have faced extinction in England due to pollution and habitat loss. This collaborative effort aims to research their ecology, husbandry, and genetics, with hopes for reintroduction within three years, ensuring the species' long-term survival.
