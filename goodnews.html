Physicist Francis Halzen Awarded Nobel Prize for Neutrino Observatory Work

2026-10-06 07:24:19 EDT

Belgian-born physicist Prof. Francis Halzen has won the Nobel Prize in Physics for his groundbreaking work on the IceCube Neutrino Observatory at the South Pole. Halzen led the development of this unique observatory, which uses a cubic kilometer of Antarctic ice fitted with light sensors to detect elusive neutrinos. These "ghost particles" carry crucial information about violent, high-energy processes in distant galaxies, opening a new window into astronomy. The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences lauded Halzen's vision and scientific leadership, acknowledging his tenacity in realizing a project that many initially doubted would succeed, thereby enabling unprecedented cosmic discoveries.
