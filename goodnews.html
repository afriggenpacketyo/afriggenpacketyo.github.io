Understanding Individual Differences in Caffeine Sensitivity and Metabolism

2026-09-16 20:09:43 EDT

The varied impact of coffee on individuals, from providing a strong "buzz" to having little noticeable effect, is primarily determined by how efficiently caffeine is metabolized in the body. Dr. Marilyn Cornelis explains that a specific liver enzyme breaks down caffeine, a process that can span anywhere from six to 20 hours, influenced by individual genetic factors. Additionally, lifestyle choices like smoking can significantly accelerate caffeine breakdown, while certain medications, including some antibiotics and contraceptive pills, may prolong its effects. Sleep researchers commonly advise avoiding caffeine at least six hours before bedtime, though personal sensitivity should guide consumption for optimal sleep quality.
