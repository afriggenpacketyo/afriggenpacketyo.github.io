New NHS Guidelines May Expand Use of 'Sponge Test' for Esophageal Cancer

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

Draft guidelines for the NHS in England suggest that individuals experiencing persistent and severe indigestion should be offered quick "sponge in the gullet" tests to screen for esophageal cancer. These tests, which are less invasive than hospital endoscopies, involve swallowing a capsule containing a sponge that collects cells as it's withdrawn. The sponge test can help detect Barrett's esophagus, a condition that increases cancer risk. While already available in some areas, the new recommendations aim for wider implementation, potentially providing more patients with peace of mind and freeing up endoscopy resources. NICE emphasizes that while effective, the test is not foolproof and long-term monitoring may still be necessary.
