UN Promotes 'Equal Earth' Map to Correct Mercator Projection's Size Distortions

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The UN has endorsed the "Equal Earth" projection as a more accurate representation of global landmass sizes, aiming to correct the distortions inherent in the widely used Mercator map. The Mercator map, created in 1569 for navigation, significantly distorts the perceived size of continents, making Africa appear similar in size to Greenland, when it is actually 14 times larger. Critics argue this distortion can psychologically influence perceptions of importance. The "Equal Earth" map, developed in 2018, offers a more proportional representation but sacrifices the navigational accuracy of Mercator. This initiative highlights the ongoing debate about how maps shape our understanding of the world and the importance of accurate geographical representation.
