2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in southern Finland is utilizing the world's largest commercial sand battery to address the intermittency challenge of renewable energy. The 13-meter-tall facility, filled with 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone, stores 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy, providing significant heating capacity for residents. Developed by Polar Night Energy, the project has reportedly slashed greenhouse gas emissions from the local heating network by nearly 70%. This innovative electrothermal storage system charges when electricity is cheap and discharges heat when needed, offering a promising, material-efficient solution for long-duration energy storage.
