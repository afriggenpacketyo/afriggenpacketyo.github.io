Germany Enforces Nationwide Alcohol Ban at All Train Stations

2026-10-15 06:14:00 EDT

A nationwide alcohol ban has officially come into force at all 5,700 German train stations, including platforms, aiming to enhance safety, cleanliness, and overall atmosphere. While pubs and restaurants within stations can still serve alcohol, and consumption remains legal on many long-distance trains, the immediate station areas are now booze-free zones. The measure follows incidents of violence, including assaults on railway staff, and pilot projects at 40 stations that reported positive outcomes. However, a federal group for the homeless criticized the ban, arguing it displaces vulnerable individuals who often use stations for shelter and makes them harder for social workers to locate.
