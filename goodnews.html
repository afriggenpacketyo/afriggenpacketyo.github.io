Research Confirms 'Hang-xiety' as a Real Phenomenon Linked to Alcohol's Brain Effects

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

"Hang-xiety," the anxiety accompanying a hangover, is a real phenomenon, according to psychopharmacology professor Celia Morgan. Alcohol's effects on brain chemicals GABA and glutamate can cause a rebound of alertness and arousal as it wears off, triggering anxiety. A 2023 study found 18.3% of students reported hang-xiety symptoms, with shy individuals particularly affected. Experts advise against using alcohol to manage stress and suggest reframing hang-xiety as a temporary state rather than a social catastrophe.
