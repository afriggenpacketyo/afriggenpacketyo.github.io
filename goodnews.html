First Adult Tyrannosaurus Rex Trackway Discovered in North Dakota

2026-09-10 11:38:43 EDT

Paleontologists have made a groundbreaking discovery in North Dakota's badlands: the first-ever trackway of a full-grown Tyrannosaurus rex. The 23-foot-long trackway consists of four connected, three-toed footprints, each roughly three feet long, providing rare insight into the dinosaur's movement. Researchers estimate the T. rex was walking at 3.5 to 4.5 mph despite its immense weight. This discovery, made by an amateur fossil hunter in 2025, is significant because previous finds were limited to only two isolated adult T. rex footprints globally, offering an unprecedented glimpse into the behavior of these ancient predators.
