New Guidelines Endorse Hormone Therapy for Menopause Symptoms

2026-10-16 11:01:00 EDT

The American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG) has released significant new guidelines, marking the first update in over a decade, which now recommend hormone replacement therapy (HRT) as a first-line treatment for common menopause and perimenopause symptoms such as hot flashes and night sweats. This pivotal shift acknowledges that the majority of menopause symptoms are directly caused by steep drops in estrogen and progesterone levels. HRT, available in various forms like pills, patches, or vaginal rings, is increasingly shown by newer research to be safe and beneficial for many patients, particularly when initiated before age 60 or within 10 years of menopause onset. The updated guidance aims to empower women and ensure they do not have to "suffer through the symptoms without help."
