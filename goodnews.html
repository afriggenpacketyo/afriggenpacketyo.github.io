Chinese Astronomers Discover Planets That Defied Destruction by Red Giant Stars

2026-10-28 10:00:10 EDT

Chinese astronomers have made a groundbreaking discovery, identifying three solar systems where Jupiter-like planets have inexplicably survived their host stars' expansion into red giants. Typically, when sun-like stars age, they swell into red giants, a process that usually results in the engulfment and destruction of their nearest planets. Using NASA’s TESS space telescope, a team led by Qian Shengbang from Yunnan University observed these three planets orbiting aging stars that had expanded past them before subsequently shrinking back. This puzzling survival challenges existing scientific models of planetary evolution and could significantly alter our understanding of the distant future of Earth and its potential fate within our own solar system.
