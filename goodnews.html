He follows Hiram Revels, who served from 1870 to 1871 as the first Black person in the chamber, and Blanche Bruce, who served a full term from 1875 to 1881. Both Revels and Bruce served during the Reconstruction era, advocating for racial equality before the implementation of repressive Jim Crow laws enforced racial segregation across the Southern states.
