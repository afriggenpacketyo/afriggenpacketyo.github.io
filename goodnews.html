United Nations Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Global Representation

2026-09-06 08:30:50 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map that more accurately represents the true size of continents, particularly Africa. This resolution, sponsored by Togo and backed by the African Union, encourages global institutions to replace the traditional Mercator map, which distorts landmasses by enlarging regions near the poles. The Mercator projection, designed for navigation, makes Greenland appear similar in size to Africa, whereas Africa is actually 14 times larger. This move aims to correct long-standing misperceptions influenced by cartography.
