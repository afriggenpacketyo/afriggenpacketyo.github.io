Erectile Dysfunction Drug Tadalafil Linked to Increased Glaucoma Risk

2026-08-23 17:19:53 EDT

New research suggests that tadalafil, a medication commonly used for erectile dysfunction and enlarged prostate symptoms, may be associated with an increased risk of developing glaucoma. The study, which analyzed health records of thousands of men, found that tadalafil users were 22% more likely to develop glaucoma and 33% more likely to develop the most common type, primary open-angle glaucoma. While the study identified a link, it could not prove causation. Experts advise long-term users, especially those with existing risk factors, to undergo regular eye monitoring.
