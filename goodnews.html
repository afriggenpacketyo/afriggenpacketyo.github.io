Chinese scientists have achieved a significant breakthrough by successfully building and testing the world’s first practical submarine solar farm. This innovative perovskite-based photovoltaic system operated effectively at a depth of 10 meters (33 feet) in the open sea, successfully charging batteries and powering an LED panel. The achievement, by a team from Yunnan University, overcomes a long-standing barrier in underwater energy harvesting: the rapid fading of sunlight and the unsuitability of conventional silicon solar cells for the narrow spectrum that remains.
