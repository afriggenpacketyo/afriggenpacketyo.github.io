UK Net Migration Reaches Lowest Level Since 1997

2026-11-26 09:55:16 EST

The United Kingdom's net migration has significantly decreased to 77,000 in the year ending June, marking the lowest figure recorded this century, excluding the period of pandemic-related travel restrictions. This represents a dramatic 92% reduction from the record high of 944,000 in March 2023, according to Office for National Statistics (ONS) figures. The substantial drop is attributed to stricter visa rules targeting non-European workers and students, alongside a decrease in asylum claims, policies initially introduced by the previous Conservative government and continued by Labour since July 2024. Prime Minister Andy Burnham stated these figures demonstrate "a sufficient degree of control" over the migration system.
