UN Adopts New World Map to Accurately Represent Africa's Size

2026-09-04 15:44:40 EDT

The UN General Assembly has passed a resolution to adopt a new world map, aiming to more accurately represent the true scale of continents, particularly Africa. The "Correct the Map" initiative, championed by Togo and backed by 164 nations, seeks to replace the widely used 16th-century Mercator projection, which is criticized for significantly distorting landmasses and diminishing the perceived size of equatorial regions. The United States was the only country to vote against this non-binding resolution, with six abstentions. Proponents emphasize that maps shape global perceptions and that this adjustment is a crucial step towards "cognitive justice" by correcting historical geographical misrepresentations.
