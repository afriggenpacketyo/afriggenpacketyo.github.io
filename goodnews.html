New Species of Wild Cat Discovered in Bolivia

2026-09-18 07:36:00 EDT

This small feline, characterized by its spotted coat, was initially mistaken for a domestic cat by locals. Its scientific name, Leopardus tilcayo, is inspired by the local nickname 'tilcayo.' The discovery is considered remarkable, as no new wild cat species had been described since 1923. Research on the species, published in Current Biology, involved whole genome sequencing of two individuals. The cat is currently classified as vulnerable to extinction.
