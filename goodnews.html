UN Calls for New World Map to Correct Distortions of Continent Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has passed a "Correct the Map" resolution, advocating for the replacement of the widely used Mercator map with a more accurate representation of continental sizes, such as the Equal Earth projection. The Mercator map, created in 1569 for navigation, severely distorts landmasses, making countries near the poles appear much larger than they are, and those near the equator, like Africa, seem disproportionately smaller. Critics argue this distortion has psychological and political implications, implicitly downplaying the importance of certain regions. While the Equal Earth map addresses size accuracy, it sacrifices the straight lines crucial for navigation, illustrating the inherent challenges of projecting a sphere onto a flat surface.
