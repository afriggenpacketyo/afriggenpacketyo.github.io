New 'Sponge in the Gullet' Test Recommended for Oesophageal Cancer Screening

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

New draft guidelines recommend offering quick "sponge in the gullet" tests to more people with persistent, bad indigestion to screen for oesophageal cancer. These less invasive tests, which can be performed in GP surgeries in minutes, involve swallowing a dissolving capsule that releases a tiny sponge to collect cells for analysis. The aim is to detect Barrett's oesophagus, a condition that increases cancer risk, more efficiently than traditional hospital endoscopy checks, which are in high demand. This initiative, if rolled out across England, could provide peace of mind to many patients and help identify those needing further tests and treatment earlier, improving cancer detection rates.
