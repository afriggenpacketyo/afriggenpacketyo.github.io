BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit Despite Glitch

2026-11-24 10:39:22 EST

The BepiColombo spacecraft, a joint mission by the European Space Agency and Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, has successfully entered Mercury's orbit after an eight-year journey. Despite a technical glitch during the critical orbital maneuver that sent the spacecraft into safe mode, engineers confirmed its successful lock into orbit after six hours of analysis. BepiColombo will now perform further thruster burns over several weeks to precisely adjust its position before releasing two science orbiters in December. These orbiters will then begin ambitious scientific observations of Mercury's interior, surface, and surrounding space by April 2027.
