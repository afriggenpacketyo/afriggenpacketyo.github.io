This occurs when their internal biological clocks become desynchronized with the sun's position, leading to minor yet measurable navigational errors of up to seven degrees. The findings highlight the universal nature of circadian rhythm disruptions, extending beyond humans to the avian world and impacting crucial migratory behaviors.
