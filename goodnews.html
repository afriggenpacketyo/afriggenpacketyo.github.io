While one nighttime trip is often considered normal, two or more awakenings during an eight-hour sleep period, or a noticeable change in pattern, should prompt medical consultation. Contributing factors range from lifestyle choices like alcohol and caffeine intake to medications, and more serious conditions such as diabetes, an enlarged prostate, an overactive bladder, or obstructive sleep apnea. Healthcare professionals recommend addressing these concerns early, adjusting fluid intake before bed, and implementing safety measures to prevent potential fall-related injuries, particularly for older individuals.

Conversely, the Eastern and Central Pacific regions are experiencing heightened tropical cyclone activity.
