Liechtenstein Changes Royal Succession Rules to Allow Women to Ascend the Throne

2026-08-15 15:41:57 EDT

Liechtenstein has announced a significant change to its royal succession rules, now allowing women to ascend the throne, shifting from male primogeniture to absolute primogeniture. This means that in the future, the first-born child, regardless of sex, will become the heir to the throne. The change, declared on the national day of the central European country with a population of just 44,000, will not affect the current heir, Hereditary Prince Alois, but will apply to the descendants of his four children. Prince Alois stated the amendment aims to ensure the principality and Princely House are led by the person "best prepared for this responsibility," aligning with similar changes seen in other European monarchies like Britain in 2011.
