2026-10-23 07:14:58 EDT

Her condition, triggered by a panic attack, involved constant symptom-searching online and isolation from loved ones. Clinical psychologist Dr. Kirren Schnack defines health anxiety as a persistent worry about serious illness when none exists, characterized by checking behaviors or avoidance. Dr. Schnack advises challenging worst-case thinking, limiting reassurance-seeking, burning off anxious energy, grounding techniques, and breathing exercises to manage symptoms, while still advocating for medical checks for new or persistent symptoms.

Chinese Scientists Reconstruct Face of Ancient Denisovans from Fossil Fragments

2026-10-23 07:09:08 EDT

Chinese scientists have successfully reconstructed the face of Denisovans, an enigmatic species of ancient humans previously known primarily through DNA and fragmented fossils. The reconstruction is based on fossils excavated from the Xujiayao archaeological site in northern China's Nihewan Basin between 1976 and 1979. This breakthrough confirms the remains belong to the Denisovan lineage, providing the first visual representation of these hominids believed to have inhabited parts of Siberia, East, and Southeast Asia thousands of years ago. This work offers crucial insights into human evolution.
