ESA's Hera Spacecraft Arrives to Investigate Asteroid Collision Site

2026-11-25 01:00:36 EST

The European Space Agency's (ESA) Hera spacecraft has arrived at the Didymos-Dimorphos asteroid system to investigate the aftermath of NASA's 2022 DART mission, which deliberately crashed into the smaller asteroid, Dimorphos. DART successfully altered Dimorphos's orbit, reducing it by 33 minutes, in the first-ever test of planetary defense against potential Earth-bound space rocks. Hera will spend a year meticulously studying the impact site, aiming to measure Dimorphos's mass and assess the precise crater or reshaping caused by the collision. This mission is crucial for understanding how to effectively deflect hazardous asteroids, providing vital data for future planetary protection strategies to safeguard Earth.
