2026-08-18 03:03:22 EDT

Over 8,000 customers were affected by disruptions attributed to prolonged hot weather, high demand, pipe bursts, and power issues. While most areas, including Herne Bay, have seen restoration, about 400 properties in Benenden, Cranbrook, and Rolvenden still face low pressure or no water.
