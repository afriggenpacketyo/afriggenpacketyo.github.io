The American Stroke Association advocates the easily remembered BE FAST acronym: B for Balance loss, E for Eye changes, F for Face drooping, A for Arm weakness, S for Speech difficulty, and T for Time to call 911. Murphy's experience, reporting arm numbness, directly illustrates the "A" in BE FAST, and his quick action to seek care underscores the "T."
