Experts Question Efficacy of Popular 'Cycle-Syncing' Workout Trend

2026-09-09 19:22:05 EDT

The online trend of "cycle-syncing" workouts, which suggests adjusting exercise routines based on specific menstrual cycle phases, is gaining significant popularity but lacks robust scientific evidence. Researchers like Kelly McNulty emphasize that while some women do experience cycle-related changes in symptoms or perceived strength, there is currently no research supporting a universal, prescriptive phase-based approach to exercise. Experts caution against rigid plans due to the highly individual nature of menstrual cycles and the potential for a "nocebo" effect, where negative expectations could inadvertently hinder performance. Instead, they advocate for "cycle-awareness," encouraging women to intuitively adapt workouts based on their unique bodily sensations and energy levels rather than strictly adhering to generalized, unproven rules.
