If a referee signals for physio treatment for a goalkeeper, requiring a stoppage in play, the team's coach must nominate an outfield player to leave the pitch for one minute after play resumes. If no player is chosen within ten seconds, the captain must depart. This proactive measure is designed to deter teams from disrupting opposition momentum by feigning goalkeeper injuries, particularly in critical game situations, and ensures fair play. The first game to feature this trial will be a Carabao Cup preliminary round tie.
