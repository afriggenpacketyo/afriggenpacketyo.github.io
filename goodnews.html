Australia Implements Landmark Protections for Gig Delivery Workers

2026-08-12 02:05:00 EDT

Australia's industrial relations tribunal has approved new rules ensuring gig food and grocery delivery workers receive at least A$31.30 (€19.15, $22.11) an hour and accident insurance. This hourly rate surpasses Australia's minimum wage and is hailed by the Transport Workers Union as a "world-leading set of standards" for the gig economy. The order, effective August 17, also mandates personal accident insurance for engaged time, building on previous legislation that granted gig workers more negotiating rights. This move is expected to impact approximately 250,000 workers and aligns with a new global treaty from the UN's International Labour Organization to protect gig workers.
