Chinese Scientists Achieve 84% Efficacy in Cancer Treatment Trial Using Nano-Carbon Iron Injection

2026-10-17 04:00:09 EDT

Chinese scientists have reported significant success in a landmark trial for advanced, metastatic cancer, achieving 84% efficacy by halting tumor growth in 16 patients and inducing shrinkage in two. The innovative treatment, a nano-carbon iron injection called CNSI-Fe, draws inspiration from industrial waste water treatment principles. This method utilizes iron to react with hydrogen peroxide, generating hydroxyl radicals that effectively degrade toxic molecules in tumors. A promising Phase II trial is now underway with 52 additional patients, offering new hope for those with critically limited therapeutic options against aggressive cancers.
