Study Reveals How Fatty Diets Increase Colorectal Cancer Risk Through Gut Bacteria

2026-08-17 02:49:00 EDT

German researchers have uncovered a causal link between Western high-fat diets and an increased risk of colorectal cancer, particularly in adults under 50. The study, published in the journal *Gut*, demonstrates how fatty foods sustain specific gut bacteria that produce deoxycholic acid (DCA), a secondary bile acid promoting tumor growth. Experiments in mice confirmed that DCA-producing bacteria increase cell division, raising the likelihood of cancer development. This foundational research, which also found higher prevalence of these bacteria in human colorectal cancer patients, emphasizes the critical role of diet in gut health and opens avenues for potential treatments like microbiome transplants, alongside lifestyle changes for prevention.
