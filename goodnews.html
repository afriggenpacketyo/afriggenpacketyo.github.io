New 'Sponge in the Gullet' Test Recommended for Oesophageal Cancer Screening in England

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

New draft guidelines for NHS England are recommending the broader offering of quick "sponge in the gullet" tests to individuals experiencing bad and persistent indigestion, with the aim of screening for oesophageal cancer. This innovative and less invasive method involves patients swallowing a dissolving capsule that releases a tiny sponge to collect cells from the gullet lining as it is gently retrieved. These tests can be conveniently carried out in GP surgeries, offering a significant alternative to more complex hospital endoscopy checks. Voyager 1 Reaches Historic Milestone, One Light-Day Away from Earth

2026-11-15 17:41:22 EST

NASA's iconic Voyager 1 spacecraft is poised to achieve a remarkable milestone, reaching a distance of one light-day away from Earth, which equates to over 16 billion miles. Launched on September 5, 1977, Voyager 1 has spent nearly 50 years traversing the cosmos, making significant discoveries such as Jupiter's faint ring system and capturing detailed photos of Saturn's rings and moons. It surpassed Pioneer 10 in 1998 to become the most distant human-made object and entered interstellar space in 2012. NASA is commemorating this extraordinary occasion by inviting the public to participate in a symbolic "Shine Bright for Voyager's Light-Day" event, acknowledging its ongoing mission to chart magnetic fields and plasma waves beyond our solar system.
