Such cataclysmic events are known to forge the universe's heaviest elements, including gold, platinum, and uranium, by releasing a flood of neutrons that atoms absorb. The blast is estimated to have occurred less than 1,000 years ago, as indicated by the small and bright nature of the glowing cloud, signifying a relatively young cosmic remnant.
