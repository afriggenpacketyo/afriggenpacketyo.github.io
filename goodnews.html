Study Suggests Chinese Script Evolution Mirrors Natural Selection

2026-09-01 00:00:05 EDT

A new study by researchers from Beijing Language and Culture University and Shanghai Normal University proposes that Chinese characters have evolved similarly to living organisms, driven by natural selection. The research, published in Digital Scholarship in the Humanities, reveals that a small number of core components are repeatedly combined to form new characters, with entirely new elements invented when existing ones were insufficient. This evolutionary pressure led to a system where high-frequency components dominate, reflecting a linguistic principle of economy. The findings offer unique insights into the long-term development and simplification of Chinese writing.
