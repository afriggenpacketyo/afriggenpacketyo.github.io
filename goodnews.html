Chinese Fossil Discovery Challenges Theories on Early Bird Flight Evolution

2026-10-16 08:00:08 EDT

Chinese scientists have unearthed a 150 million-year-old fossil of Yuezhuornis yangyuanensis in Fujian province, identified as the earliest known flightless bird. This discovery challenges long-held evolutionary assumptions, including those by Charles Darwin, that flightlessness in birds only emerged as a secondary adaptation after initial species had mastered flight. The fossil suggests that early bird adaptation branched into diverse, terrestrial, non-flying niches much earlier than previously believed. Published in Nature, this finding provides compelling evidence that the evolutionary path of early avialans was more varied, with selection for powered flight not being the sole driver of skeletal modifications.
