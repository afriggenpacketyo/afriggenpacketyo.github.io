Conservationists Launch Collaborative Effort to Save Critically Endangered Glutinous Snail in Wales

2026-09-12 02:09:37 EDT

Conservationists are undertaking a vital mission to save the glutinous snail, one of Europe's rarest species, found in only one UK lake, Llyn Tegid in Wales. Deemed as precious as "Egyptian artefacts," these tiny molluscs, which indicate healthy aquatic environments, have been driven to extinction across England due to pollution and habitat loss. Through the dedicated efforts of conservationist Ian Hughes, who successfully bred them in his garden, "ark populations" are now being established in British zoos. This initiative, part of a £60 million government-backed scheme, aims to eventually reintroduce the snails into the wild and safeguard their future amidst climate change threats.
