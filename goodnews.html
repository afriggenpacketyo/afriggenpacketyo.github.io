Archaeologists Discover World's Oldest Shipwreck Off Turkey's Kekova Island

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have unearthed the world's oldest known shipwreck off Turkey's Kekova Island, dating it to the Early Bronze Age, between 4,400 and 4,700 years old. Although the wooden hull has disintegrated, over 30 ceramic storage pots, or amphorae, were found scattered on the seabed, some still containing viscous residue. The 10-meter trade vessel's age was determined by comparing the distinctive two-handled, flat-based pottery styles to artifacts from Egypt and the Middle East. Analysis is now underway to identify the original cargo, which could include olive oil, wine, or tree resins.
