Attachment Theory Gains Popularity as a Framework for Understanding Relationships

2026-07-27 20:21:34 EDT

Attachment theory, developed by British psychiatrist John Bowlby, has gained significant traction in popular culture and social media as a framework for understanding relationships. It suggests early caregiver bonds shape adult relationship expectations, leading to four styles: anxious, avoidant, fearful-avoidant, and secure. While widely used for self-awareness and relationship navigation, experts caution against overgeneralization and the misconception that attachment styles are fixed. Research indicates change is possible, with secure attachment being the most stable and beneficial for overall well-being.
