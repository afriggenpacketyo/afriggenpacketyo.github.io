T-levels Explained: Vocational Qualifications for 16-19 Year Olds in England

2026-08-20 05:26:19 EDT

T-levels are vocational qualifications designed for 16-to-19-year-olds in England, offering an alternative to A-levels with a focus on practical skills and industry experience. The two-year program combines classroom learning with a substantial work placement of at least 315 hours. Grades range from a pass to a distinction*, with the latter equivalent to three A*s at A-level, providing UCAS points for university entry. Despite increasing student numbers, retention rates are lower than A-levels, and some courses have faced delays or cancellations.
