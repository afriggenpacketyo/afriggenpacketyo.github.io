'Hang-xiety' Identified as a Real Phenomenon, Linked to Alcohol's Brain Effects

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

The phenomenon known as 'hang-xiety'—the anxiety that often accompanies a hangover—is a real and scientifically recognized condition. Experts explain that alcohol's impact on brain chemicals like GABA and glutamate, which regulate nerve activity, can lead to a rebound effect of heightened alertness and arousal as alcohol wears off. Studies suggest that not everyone experiences hang-xiety, and its severity isn't always correlated with consumption, with existing psychological traits like shyness potentially playing a significant role in its occurrence. Understanding this temporary state can help individuals manage the dread without assuming a social catastrophe.
