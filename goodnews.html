Study Links High-Fat 'Western Diets' to Increased Colorectal Cancer Risk

2026-08-17 02:49:00 EDT

New German research reveals that high-fat "Western diets" can significantly increase the risk of colorectal cancer, particularly in adults under 50. The study, published in the journal Gut, demonstrates how such diets sustain gut bacteria that produce deoxycholic acid (DCA), a secondary bile acid promoting tumor growth. This groundbreaking research establishes a causal link, moving beyond mere correlation, and highlights the impact of diet on human gut health. Understanding this mechanism opens avenues for potential treatments like microbiome transplants and strongly emphasizes lifestyle changes, including reducing fatty foods, to prevent this increasingly prevalent and deadly cancer.
