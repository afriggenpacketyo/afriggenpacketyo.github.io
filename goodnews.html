Chinese Scientists Create Unbreakable Diamond with Enhanced Toughness

2026-08-11 00:00:09 EDT

Chinese scientists have developed a revolutionary diamond material that is six times tougher than conventional diamonds, making it virtually unbreakable even under hard impact. This breakthrough, published in Nature Synthesis, overcomes the inherent brittleness of natural diamonds while maintaining their legendary hardness. The team achieved this by integrating multi-walled carbon nanotubes—ultra-strong carbon fiber cords—into the diamond's internal structure. The resulting material is even tougher than tungsten alloys used in armor-piercing ammunition, opening new possibilities for industrial applications requiring extreme durability.
