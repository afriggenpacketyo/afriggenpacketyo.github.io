Historic Debut: 54 Rare Olive Ridley Sea Turtle Hatchlings Emerge in Southern California

2026-11-14 16:54:52 EST

Fifty-four rare olive ridley sea turtle hatchlings have made a historic debut in Southern California, marking the first native-born sea turtles ever recorded in the region. These tiny turtles emerged from eggs that were carefully relocated to the Aquarium of the Pacific after being laid on Seal Beach, a highly unusual occurrence attributed to an extreme marine heat wave and strong El Niño. Aquarium staff and federal biologists successfully transferred the hatchlings, who then instinctually scooted into the sea. While a third nest in Ventura was unfortunately destroyed by high tides, this successful hatching represents a remarkable and hopeful milestone for conservation efforts.
