Hypermobility Spectrum Disorder: A 'Bendy Disease' Causing Widespread Undiagnosed Pain

2026-08-27 19:13:52 EDT

Hypermobility Spectrum Disorder (HSD), a connective tissue disorder characterized by greater-than-normal joint range of motion, affects hundreds of thousands in the UK, yet many individuals face significant diagnostic delays. Sufferers often experience years of unexplained pain, fatigue, and digestive issues before receiving a diagnosis, as exemplified by Vivienne Duval who discovered her condition at 58. The disorder, sometimes linked to hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (hEDS) and neurodiversity, lacks a dedicated clinical guideline, contributing to a "postcode lottery" for assessment. Experts emphasize the urgent need for increased awareness, research, and integrated care to improve diagnosis and support for those impacted by this often-misunderstood condition.
