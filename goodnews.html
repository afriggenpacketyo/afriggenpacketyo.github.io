2026-10-05 21:00:05 EDT

Hong Kong's historical dominance in global gold trade, once handling a third of the world's bullion and serving as a key conduit to mainland China, has significantly diminished. This shift began in 2014 when China relaxed restrictions, allowing more mainland banks to directly import foreign gold, thereby reducing Hong Kong's transit role. Net gold flows to the mainland via Hong Kong plummeted from 1,158 tonnes in 2013 to a nadir of 67 tonnes in 2020. This evolution reflects China's broader ambition to internationalize the yuan and exert greater direct control over its financial markets, reshaping the region's economic dynamics and trade relationships.
