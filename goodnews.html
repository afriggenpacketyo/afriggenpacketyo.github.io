UN General Assembly Adopts Resolution for New World Map Reflecting Africa's True Size

2026-09-04 20:53:11 EDT

The UN General Assembly has voted to adopt a new world map that more accurately reflects the size of Africa, replacing the traditional Mercator projection. The "Correct the Map" resolution, sponsored by Togo and supported by 164 nations, aims to address the Mercator map's distortion, which makes Africa appear similar in size to Greenland despite being 14 times larger. Critics argue this distortion minimizes the development needs of countries near the equator. The non-binding resolution encourages wider use of equal-area projections like Equal Earth, promoting a fairer visual representation of continents.
