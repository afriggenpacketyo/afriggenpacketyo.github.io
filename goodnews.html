Euclid Telescope Detects Universe's Most Distant Quasar, Dún Aonghas

2026-11-24 17:34:00 EST

The European Space Agency's Euclid telescope has achieved a groundbreaking discovery, identifying Dún Aonghas, the most distant and oldest quasar ever observed, which formed just 630 million years after the Big Bang. This extremely bright galactic core, powered by a supermassive black hole, provides invaluable insights into the early universe's formation and the rapid growth of black holes to enormous sizes. The findings, published in Astronomy and Astrophysics, support theories that supermassive black holes developed remarkably quickly from large gas clouds. Researchers are now combining data from various telescopes to further characterize this significant cosmic object.
