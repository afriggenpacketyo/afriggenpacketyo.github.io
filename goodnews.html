Two New Species of Sea Spiders Discovered Off Canadian Coastline

2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

Researchers in Canada have announced the discovery of two new species of sea spiders in the shallow waters of the Salish Sea, located between Washington State and British Columbia. These newly identified marine arthropods, measuring approximately a centimeter across, represent the first sea spiders found in this region in over a century. Lead researcher Cormac Toler-Scott explained that while related to land spiders, these creatures are slow-moving, transparent, and lack venom or fangs. The species, named Callipallene pilosuspedes and Tanystylum kiixin, exhibit distinct characteristics such as self-grooming limbs or a reliance on camouflage, and feed parasitically on hydroids, underscoring the rich, unexplored biodiversity of the area.
