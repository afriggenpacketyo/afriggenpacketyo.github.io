2026-08-27 19:13:52 EDT

Hypermobility Spectrum Disorder (HSD) is a little-known connective tissue disorder characterized by joints with an excessive range of motion, leading to chronic pain, fatigue, and digestive issues for many individuals. Like Vivienne Duval, who was diagnosed late in life, many sufferers face significant delays in receiving a diagnosis, with average wait times reaching 19 to 21.7 years in the UK. The disorder is complex to diagnose, partly due to the lack of a dedicated clinical guideline from the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence. Research suggests HSD disproportionately affects women, and many diagnosed individuals report a lack of adequate support and understanding from healthcare professionals, often relying on social media for information. The condition can also be linked to neurodevelopmental conditions like autism and ADHD, highlighting its multifaceted impact on individuals' lives.
