Understanding Health Anxiety: Symptoms, Impact, and Coping Strategies

2026-10-22 19:20:31 EDT

Hollie Sharman's personal experience of frequently believing she was dying due to severe chest pain, despite numerous negative medical tests, vividly illustrates the debilitating effects of health anxiety. Clinical psychologist Dr. Kirren Schnack defines health anxiety as a persistent worry about being or becoming seriously ill when no medical condition is present. Key indicators include obsessive body checking, frequent online symptom research, and constant reassurance-seeking from friends or family. Conversely, some individuals may exhibit avoidance behaviors, such as shunning medical appointments due to fear of diagnosis. Dr. Schnack offers practical coping strategies, including challenging worst-case thinking, setting limits on reassurance-seeking, engaging in physical activity to burn anxious energy, employing grounding techniques, and practicing specific breathing exercises.
