Historic Hatching of 54 Olive Ridley Sea Turtles in Southern California

2026-11-14 16:54:52 EST

In a historic first for California, 54 rare olive ridley sea turtle hatchlings successfully emerged from eggs laid on Seal Beach, marking the state's inaugural native-born sea turtles. These eggs, originally laid in September, were carefully relocated to the Aquarium of the Pacific for incubation after forecasts predicted high tides would swamp their natural nests. All 54 tiny turtles were safely transferred to the sea, a significant conservation milestone. While a third nest in Ventura was unfortunately destroyed by high tides, the successful Orange County hatching is attributed to an extreme marine heat wave and a strong El Niño, which have elevated Southern California water temperatures to resemble the turtles' usual tropical nesting grounds.
