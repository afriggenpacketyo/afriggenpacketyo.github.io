Investors in Covered Call ETFs Face Potential Tax Surprises on Monthly Income Distributions

2026-10-19 08:58:03 EDT

Covered call ETFs, favored for their monthly income generation, can harbor hidden tax complexities for investors. While they provide attractive yields, distributions can comprise dividends, capital gains, or non-dividend distributions, often termed "return of capital." Critically, return of capital payments are tax-deferred but reduce the investment's cost basis, potentially leading to a larger capital gains tax liability upon selling shares. Financial advisors recommend holding these ETFs in tax-deferred accounts like IRAs and meticulously examining a fund's tax-cost ratio and Net Asset Value (NAV) growth to avoid unexpected tax burdens and ensure sustainable income.
