Historic Black-Owned Threatt Filling Station on Route 66 Undergoes Restoration

2026-11-24 05:00:00 EST

During the Jim Crow Era, the Threatt Filling Station in Luther, Oklahoma, stood as a rare haven for Black travelers on Route 66, offering fuel, food, and lodging when most establishments denied them service. Founded in 1915 by Allen Threatt Sr., the station became a vital community hub in a region marked by "sundown towns." After closing in the 1970s, the derelict sandstone building was listed as an endangered historic place in 2021. Now, Threatt's grandchildren are leading a restoration effort, transforming the site into a historical landmark with plans for an RV park and restaurant, honoring their family's legacy of welcome and resilience.
