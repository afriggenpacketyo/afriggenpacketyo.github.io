BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Orbits Mercury Despite Technical Glitch

2026-11-24 12:34:32 EST

The BepiColombo spacecraft, a joint European and Japanese mission, has successfully entered Mercury's orbit after an eight-year journey, despite encountering a technical glitch. The maneuver was interrupted by a software issue, causing the spacecraft to enter safe mode and complete only 77% of its intended burn. However, mission control confirmed BepiColombo had still locked into orbit, and it will now make small thruster adjustments over weeks to reach its optimum position. The spacecraft is set to release two science orbiters on December 9, with full science operations expected to begin in April 2027 to study Mercury's interior, surface, and surrounding space.
