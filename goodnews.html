UN Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Representation of Continents

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection as a new world map, a significant step towards more accurately representing the true sizes of continents, especially Africa. This resolution, sponsored by Togo and backed by the African Union, encourages governments and institutions globally to transition from the traditional Mercator map. The Mercator projection, commonly used since the 16th century, is known for distorting landmasses by vastly enlarging regions near the poles while shrinking Africa and South America. The Equal Earth projection, following Earth's curvature, corrects this, for example, showing Africa as 14 times larger than Greenland, aiming to foster a more accurate global understanding.
