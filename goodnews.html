Archaeologists Reconstruct East Pediment of the Parthenon After Decades of Work

2026-11-20 15:03:00 EST

An international team of archaeologists, led by Vinzenz Brinkmann, believes they have solved the long-standing 'Parthenon puzzle' by reconstructing the east pediment's original arrangement. Utilizing 3D scanning and printing technology, researchers pieced together surviving fragments and compared them with Roman sarcophagi depicting Athena's birth. This breakthrough allows for a complete visualization of the ancient sculpture, which was previously fragmented and scattered across museums. The exhibition at Frankfurt's Liebieghaus sculpture collection showcases this reconstruction, though it highlights the ongoing dispute over the Parthenon Marbles held by the British Museum.
