New Same-Day Test Revolutionizes Infection Detection in Critically Ill Children

2026-10-15 20:34:45 EDT

A pioneering same-day metagenomic test is transforming the detection of hidden and drug-resistant infections in critically ill children at London's Great Ormond Street Hospital (GOSH). This advanced diagnostic method sequences all genetic material from a single respiratory sample, identifying bacteria, viruses, and fungi that standard tests often miss. The technology proved life-saving for 10-year-old Willow Crowther, whose drug-resistant bacterial infection was swiftly identified, leading to correct antibiotic treatment. The study, published in the Lancet Microbe, revealed the test influenced prescribing decisions in a quarter of cases and detected 50 organisms missed by routine diagnostics, positioning it as a powerful tool against antimicrobial resistance and potential future pandemics.
