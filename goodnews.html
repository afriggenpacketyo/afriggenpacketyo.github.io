Noah Adams, Revered Host of NPR's 'All Things Considered,' Dies at 84

2026-10-24 20:58:47 EDT

Noah Adams, the revered and longtime host of NPR's "All Things Considered," has passed away at the age of 84 due to complications from a benign brain tumor. Adams was celebrated for his gentle voice, genuine curiosity, and ability to connect with everyday people, hosting the weekday edition of the program for 18 years. A college dropout, he was inspired by the show to pursue public radio, joining NPR in 1975. Colleagues like Melissa Block and Susan Stamberg lauded his authentic interviewing style, craftsmanship, and the profound sincerity he brought to his work. Adams' legacy includes impactful radio documentaries, memorable interviews, and several books, leaving an indelible mark on American radio journalism.
