Chinese Scientists Achieve 84% Efficacy in Cancer Treatment Trial Using Waste Water Technology

2026-10-17 04:00:09 EDT

Chinese scientists have announced a landmark achievement in cancer treatment, reporting 84% efficacy in a trial utilizing a novel nano-carbon iron injection, CNSI-Fe, whose concept originated from industrial waste water treatment. This therapeutic agent combines iron with a nano-carbon injection, designed to generate hydroxyl radicals that degrade malignant tumors, similar to how they break down toxic molecules in waste water. In initial findings, the injection successfully halted tumor growth in 16 patients and induced shrinkage in two. A Phase II trial is currently underway with 52 additional patients, offering a promising and unconventional new avenue for those with advanced, metastatic cancer who face critically limited treatment options.
