Spanish scientists discover first evidence of Diplodocus dinosaur in Europe

2026-09-15 10:42:00 EDT

Researchers in Spain have unearthed the first skeletal evidence of the Diplodocus dinosaur outside of North America, discovering vertebrae and chevron bones dating back approximately 150 million years. The find at the La Tejeria fossil site in eastern Spain is considered one of the most complete European diplodocid skeletons found to date. This discovery provides strong support for theories of continental faunal exchange between North America and Europe during the Late Jurassic period. Scientists suggest that temporary land bridges or sea regressions across the proto-Atlantic Ocean may have facilitated such dinosaur migrations. The find adds significant insight into prehistoric ecosystems and the movement of species across ancient continents.
