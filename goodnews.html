NHS Offers HPV Self-Testing Kits to Women in England Missing Cervical Screening

2026-08-24 19:27:50 EDT

The NHS in England is now offering HPV self-testing kits to women who have missed cervical screening appointments, aiming to increase uptake. Nearly 4 million women will be invited to order a free kit for home use. This initiative seeks to detect the high-risk HPV virus, which causes most cervical cancers, addressing reasons like embarrassment or lack of time that prevent women from attending appointments. The self-testing kits are seen as a potential game-changer in making cervical cancer screening more accessible and in the NHS's goal to eliminate cervical cancer by 2040.
