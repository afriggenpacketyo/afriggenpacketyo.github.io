World's Oldest Shipwreck Discovered Off Turkish Coast, Revealing Ancient Trade Secrets

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have discovered the world's oldest shipwreck off Turkey's Kekova Island, dating it to the Early Bronze Age, between 4,400 and 4,700 years ago. Although the wooden structure is gone, over 30 ceramic amphorae were found on the seabed, some still containing traces of their original cargo, likely olive oil, wine, or tree resins. Led by Prof. Hakan Öniz, the expedition determined the 10m trade vessel's age by analyzing the distinctive pottery styles, matching them to contemporary finds in Egypt and the Middle East. The artifacts will be displayed in a new Mediterranean Underwater Archaeology Museum in Antalya, marking a significant find that surpasses previous records and offers unique insights into ancient Mediterranean trade and seafaring life.
