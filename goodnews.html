China Begins Construction of World's First Supercritical CO2 and Molten Salt Energy Plant

2026-09-22 01:41:58 EDT

China has broken ground on a groundbreaking energy project in Shandong province, marking the world's first utility-scale combined supercritical carbon dioxide power generation and molten salt energy storage plant. This Ruitan demonstration project, slated for operation next year, is designed to significantly reduce energy waste and improve grid stability. It innovatively uses surplus electricity from existing coal power units to heat molten salt when energy demand is low. During peak consumption, the stored thermal energy will then power a supercritical unit that employs carbon dioxide, rather than steam, to produce electricity efficiently.
