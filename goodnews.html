Understanding Your Rights: Debt Collectors Cannot Legally Re-Age Old Debts

2026-08-06 10:42:23 EDT

This article clarifies that debt collectors are generally prohibited by law from "re-aging" old debts, which involves improperly changing the delinquency date to keep an account on a credit report longer. Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, negative information tied to a debt must typically fall off after seven years, irrespective of how many times the debt is sold. The Fair Debt Collection Practices Act also forbids misrepresenting a debt's legal status, including treating time-barred debts as enforceable. Consumers are advised to check for mismatched dates on credit reports and to dispute any re-aged debts, as even small payments can restart the statute of limitations in some states.
