August's Sturgeon Moon Features Partial Lunar Eclipse, Capturing Reddish Hues

2026-08-28 04:27:50 EDT

August's full Moon, traditionally known as the Sturgeon Moon, illuminated the night sky over the UK, accompanied by a captivating partial lunar eclipse. This astronomical event saw the Earth positioned between the Sun and the Moon, causing a segment of the Moon to enter the Earth's darkest shadow, the umbra. The Moon's distinctive reddish hue was a result of sunlight passing through Earth's atmosphere, where shorter blue wavelengths scattered more easily, allowing longer red wavelengths to reflect off the Moon's surface and be visible from Earth. This phenomenon provided stunning photographic opportunities for sky-watchers, celebrating a natural spectacle that connects to historical naming conventions for full moons.
