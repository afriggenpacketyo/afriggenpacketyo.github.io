During the December 7, 1941, attack, Johnson, then 21, was a fireman below deck on the USS St. Louis, which remarkably emerged "unscathed." He often stated he never saw the Japanese fighters and considered his actions simply doing his job, not heroic, finding his greatest pride in his family. His death leaves only 11 known Pearl Harbor survivors, all centenarians, representing a dwindling link to a pivotal moment in history.
