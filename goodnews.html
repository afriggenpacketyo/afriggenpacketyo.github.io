Water supply restored to London Gatwick Airport after major outage caused by power failure

2026-07-26 14:50:47 EDT

Gatwick Airport has seen its water supply gradually return after a major outage that disrupted services for passengers. The disruption, caused by a power failure at a water treatment works, led to closed restaurants and bars, and long queues for toilet facilities. SES Water, the supplier, confirmed the power issue was resolved, but acknowledged that restoring the network would take time. The airport apologized for the inconvenience and stated that no flights were canceled due to the issues. Passengers were advised not to arrive early for flights as facilities were brought back online.
