2026-09-30 05:00:06 EDT

This microraptorine species, closely related to birds, provides fresh evidence suggesting that flight evolved independently across different dinosaur lineages rather than from a single common ancestor. Researchers believe these findings dismiss a shared developmental pattern for flying dinosaurs, instead supporting multiple and independent selective regimes in the evolution of winged taxa within the Paraves group. The findings, published in the peer-reviewed journal Nature Communications, offer significant new insights into avian evolution.
