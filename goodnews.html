Study Suggests Erectile Dysfunction Drug Cialis Could Aid Muscle Regeneration

2026-10-13 15:54:48 EDT

New research from Italy indicates a potential link between the erectile dysfunction drug Cialis (tadalafil) and muscle regeneration, based on a seven-day study on mouse muscle cells. The drug was found to increase the number of muscle precursor cells, which are crucial for early muscle formation, and enhance their survival, also showing signs of promoting muscle repair by encouraging growth and reducing cell death. While these findings are encouraging for future therapeutic strategies in muscle recovery, the study's authors and independent experts caution that it does not yet prove increased muscle size or function in humans.
