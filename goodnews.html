UN General Assembly Endorses New World Map Reflecting Africa's Accurate Size

2026-09-04 18:26:35 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has overwhelmingly adopted a resolution to replace the traditional Mercator world map with one that more accurately reflects the true relative size of continents, particularly Africa. The "Correct the Map" resolution, sponsored by Togo and backed by the African Union, passed with 164 votes, with only the United States voting against it. Critics have long argued that the 16th-century Mercator projection distorts sizes, making regions near the poles appear larger while minimizing Africa. This non-binding vote encourages wider use of equal-area projections like Equal Earth, aiming to challenge misconceptions and promote a fairer visual representation of the world.
