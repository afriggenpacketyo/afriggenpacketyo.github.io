Report calls for 'fundamental overhaul' of PIP assessments to better support disabled individuals

2026-11-22 23:01:13 EST

A review into the Personal Independence Payment (Pip) system has concluded that a "fundamental overhaul" of the assessment process is necessary to better recognize the varying costs and needs of disabled individuals. The review, led by Sir Stephen Timms, found that the current system is "no longer fit for purpose" due to societal shifts and changes in the nature of disability, particularly mental health and neurodiverse conditions. Recommendations include a "fast track" process for those with the most severe or lifelong conditions to reduce the burden of repeated assessments. The review aims to create a more personalized and flexible system that supports disabled people's participation in everyday life, including work, rather than acting as a barrier.
