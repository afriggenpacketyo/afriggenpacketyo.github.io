He also critiques modern technologies and capitalist economies for exploiting our evolutionary programming.

Nobel Prize in Chemistry awarded for solving life's molecular asymmetry mystery

2026-10-07 07:51:52 EDT

The Nobel Prize in Chemistry has been awarded to Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai for their work on molecular asymmetry, explaining how life exclusively utilizes one form of a molecule over its mirror image. This phenomenon, known as chirality, is crucial in drug development, as different molecular forms can have vastly different effects on the body. Kagan and Soai developed methods to design chemical reactions that selectively produce the desired molecular form, a feat previously thought impossible in nature. Their research has significant implications for creating more effective and safer medicines.
