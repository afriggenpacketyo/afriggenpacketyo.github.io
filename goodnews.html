Supreme Court to Review 150-Year-Old Federal Ban on Home Whiskey Distilling

2026-10-21 18:18:47 EDT

The U.S. Supreme Court has agreed to review a 150-year-old federal law that prohibits home distilling of whiskey, a practice that currently carries penalties of up to five years in prison. This Reconstruction-era restriction, originally aimed at combating liquor tax evasion, is being challenged by individuals like Ohio brewery owner John Ream. The case questions whether Congress's taxing authority extends to prohibiting an otherwise lawful activity within one's home. Proponents argue the ban sets a troubling precedent for other private activities and that home distilling has deep American roots, with challengers willing to pay applicable taxes.
