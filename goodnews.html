New Species of Sea Spiders Discovered in Canada's Salish Sea

2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

Researchers at the University of British Columbia have discovered two new species of sea spiders in the shallow waters of the Salish Sea, marking the first such find in over a century. These marine arthropods, measuring about a centimeter across, exhibit unique characteristics like red eyes and specialized limbs for grooming in one species, Callipallene pilosuspedes. The other, Tanystylum kiixin, is named after a nearby Indigenous village. Lead researcher Cormac Toler-Scott noted their parasitic feeding habits on hydroids and their reliance on camouflage for survival. The discovery highlights the ocean's biodiversity and the potential for more finds.
