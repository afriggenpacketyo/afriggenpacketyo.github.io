Los Angeles City Council Approves Enhanced Scheduling Protections for Fast-Food Workers

The Los Angeles City Council has approved new scheduling protections and mandatory in-person labor rights training for fast-food workers, expanding the existing Fair Work Week ordinance. This measure requires businesses to provide work schedules at least two weeks in advance and guarantee a minimum of 10 hours of rest between shifts, aiming to improve workers' financial stability and personal planning. Labor groups strongly advocated for the ordinance, stressing the importance of empowering workers, many of whom are women and immigrants, to understand their rights.
