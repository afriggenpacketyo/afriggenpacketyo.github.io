Scott Colom Joins Hiram Revels and Blanche Bruce as Historic Black Senators from Mississippi

2026-11-12 09:12:55 EST

With his victory in Mississippi, Democrat Scott Colom becomes the state's third Black U.S. Senator, following in the footsteps of Hiram Revels and Blanche Bruce. Revels, who served from 1870 to 1871, was the first Black person elected to the Senate, while Bruce served a full term from 1875 to 1881. Both served before the era of Jim Crow laws enforced racial segregation. Colom's election is a historic achievement in the deeply conservative state, marking the first time Mississippi has sent a Black senator to Washington in over 150 years.
