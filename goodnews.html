Experts Explain Jet Lag and Offer Strategies for Adjusting Body Clocks

2026-08-05 19:49:46 EDT

Jet lag, a pervasive issue for travelers, arises when the body's internal circadian rhythms fail to synchronize with a new time zone after rapid travel. Experts from Cambridge University, Dr. Nina Rzechorzek and Dr. Anna Edmondson, explain that this biological misalignment causes a range of symptoms including disturbed sleep, daytime fatigue, irritability, and impaired concentration. The body's master clock, which is primarily regulated by daily cycles of light and darkness, requires several days to adapt to altered environmental cues. Understanding these fundamental mechanisms is key to developing effective strategies, such as adjusting sunlight exposure and meal times, to help individuals overcome the disruptive effects of jet lag and improve their travel experience.
