2026-09-19 05:01:00 EDT

Archaeologists and the Quinhagak community are undertaking an urgent emergency dig to salvage as many artifacts as possible, including hand-carved animals and masks, before the impending fall storm season. This collaborative effort highlights the critical race against time to preserve invaluable cultural heritage and ancestral resilience.

Understanding the Federal Reserve's Interest Rate and Its Economic Impact

2026-09-19 05:00:00 EDT

This article demystifies the Federal Reserve's pivotal role in managing the US economy, primarily through its interest rate adjustments, aiming for stable prices and maximum employment. It clarifies that when inflation is high, like currently, the Fed raises rates to curb spending by increasing borrowing costs for mortgages, credit cards, and business loans, effectively applying a brake to the economy. Conversely, rates are cut to stimulate activity. The recent quarter-point hike, the first this year, is a step to combat persistent inflation, with potential further increases expected to achieve the central bank's long-term goal of price stability, even if it means short-term pain for households.
