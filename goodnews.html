FAA Mandates Inspections for Boeing 737 Max Jets Due to Crack Concerns

2026-08-06 19:53:28 EDT

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) has ordered inspections of hundreds of Boeing 737 Max jets after cracks were discovered in structural reinforcement parts of some older generation Boeing planes. The directive applies to 471 planes in the U.S. and 1,429 worldwide, covering certain 737 Max 8, 737 Max 9, and 737-200 models. While no cracks have been found specifically in Max jets yet, the order establishes a timeline for mandatory inspections to preemptively address potential safety issues in the bear strap area near doorways and emergency exits, where stress from takeoffs and landings can cause cracking. Boeing initiated these inspections for its Next Generation planes in 2019 and extended them to the 737 Max fleet in 2024 due to similar design and build processes.
