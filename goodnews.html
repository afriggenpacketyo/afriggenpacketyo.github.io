Researchers analyzed death certificates and found statistically significant higher mortality rates linked to breast cancer, central nervous system cancers, prostate cancers, and melanoma among these professions. This increased risk is attributed to exposure to cosmic radiation at higher altitudes. The study's findings could prompt discussions about earlier or more frequent cancer screenings for airline personnel. While the risk increase is noted for the groups as a whole, individual risk levels may vary.
