Wayward SpaceX Rocket Booster Expected to Impact Moon, Creating a Significant Crater

2026-08-04 17:43:42 EDT

An 8,800-pound SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket booster is projected to collide with the moon at 5,400 mph, creating a crater estimated to be 65-100 feet wide and 16 feet deep. The booster, which launched lunar landers in January 2025, has been in orbit since and is now on an unintended collision course due to solar activity and gravity. NASA confirmed the impact poses no danger to Earth and plans to use the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter for before-and-after photos, hoping to gain valuable insights into lunar geology and refine space object tracking techniques. This rare man-made lunar impact will provide unique scientific observation opportunities.
