While the total number of official reports also saw a slight decrease, from 8,195 in 2024 to 7,998 in 2025, the reporting rate itself increased to 33%. The Pentagon considers this rise in reporting a positive sign, indicating greater trust and encouraging victims to seek restorative care and accountability.
