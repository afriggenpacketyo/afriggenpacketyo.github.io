World's Oldest Shipwreck Discovered Off Turkish Coast, Dating Back 4,700 Years

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have discovered the world's oldest shipwreck off Turkey's Kekova Island, dating back 4,400 to 4,700 years to the Early Bronze Age, concurrent with the Great Pyramid's construction. Although the wooden structure has perished, over 30 ceramic storage pots, or amphorae, were found on the seabed, remarkably still containing traces of their original cargo. Researchers dated the wreck by analyzing the distinctive shape of the amphorae, matching them to pottery styles from that period. This "time capsule" offers invaluable insights into ancient Mediterranean trade, with ongoing analysis to identify the specific contents, potentially olive oil, wine, or tree resins.
