Green growth proposes economic expansion alongside significantly reduced emissions and resource use through sustainable technologies, a phenomenon observed in some developed economies like the EU. However, its long-term feasibility for complete decoupling remains uncertain due to the "rebound effect" and the complexities of decarbonizing heavy industries. Alternatively, degrowth advocates for slower or even negative economic growth in richer nations to allow ecosystems to recover, prioritizing human well-being over GDP, though its practical implementation for entire modern economies is yet unproven and could trigger social upheaval.
