Trip delay coverage may reimburse reasonable expenses like meals, lodging, and transportation incurred while waiting for travel to resume, typically after a specified minimum delay period. Trip cancellation or interruption benefits, meanwhile, protect prepaid, nonrefundable costs when severe weather derails the broader trip.
