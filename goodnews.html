Launched in 2022, HEART dispatches mental health clinicians and peer support specialists to non-violent calls like trespassing or panhandling, traditionally handled by police. Studies indicate the program has increased public trust, reduced arrests, and rarely requires police backup. This initiative, inspired by the 2020 killing of George Floyd, aims to free up police resources and provide more appropriate support for individuals in crisis, with a long-term goal of addressing systemic issues like homelessness.
