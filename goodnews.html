2026-08-01 04:49:00 EDT

Germany is considering significant reforms to its "minijob" system, a popular form of part-time employment that allows individuals to earn up to €603 monthly without social insurance or income tax. Proposals include raising the flat tax rate for employers and eliminating the opt-out for pension contributions, effectively ending minijobs in their current form. Supporters argue this would strengthen social security, reduce old-age poverty, and address gender inequality. However, business representatives warn against abolishing minijobs, citing their importance for flexibility and tapping into additional labor potential.
