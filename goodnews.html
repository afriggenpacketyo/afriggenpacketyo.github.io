Singapore Develops World's Most Accurate Clock, Outperforming U.S. and China

2026-10-02 04:00:11 EDT

Singaporean scientists have developed the world's most accurate clock, utilizing a single ion of lutetium, a rare earth metal element. This groundbreaking clock boasts an astounding precision, losing only one second every 260 billion years, making it nearly 20 times more accurate than the estimated age of our universe. The achievement significantly surpasses previous records held by calcium-ion clocks developed by Chinese Academy of Sciences teams and aluminum-ion clocks unveiled by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology. This scientific advancement represents a major leap in metrology, with profound potential applications in fundamental physics research, advanced navigation systems, and ultra-precise timing technologies.
