UN Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Representation of Continents

2026-09-06 08:30:50 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to more accurately represent the true proportions of continents, with a particular focus on Africa. This resolution, sponsored by Togo and overwhelmingly supported by 164 member states, encourages governments and institutions worldwide to adopt it, moving away from the traditional Mercator map. The Mercator projection, commonly used since the 16th century, is criticized for significantly distorting landmasses by enlarging polar regions like Greenland and shrinking continents such as Africa and South America. The UN's decision aims to correct these historical misrepresentations, fostering a more accurate global understanding.
