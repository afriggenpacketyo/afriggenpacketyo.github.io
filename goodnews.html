California Law Shields Immigrant Aid Workers' Home Addresses from Public Records

2026-08-22 21:37:25 EDT

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed Assembly Bill 2624 into law, establishing a state address confidentiality program for workers and volunteers who provide legal, humanitarian, and other services to immigrants. This legislation allows these individuals to shield their home addresses from public records and makes it illegal to post their personal information online with the specific intent to incite imminent great bodily harm against them. The program, set to open in October 2027, requires applicants to register with the secretary of state and document a threat within the past year. Assemblymember Mia Bonta, who authored the bill, stated it is fundamentally about protecting immigrant service providers from fear and rejecting anti-immigrant rhetoric, despite concerns from some critics about potential restrictions on citizen journalism.
