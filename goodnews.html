The revised policy introduces a 5% "Core Technology Commission" for apps distributed through third-party app stores or the web. For apps utilizing Apple's payment system, a 26% commission applies, while those using their own payment processing will pay 20%, and apps linking to external websites for purchases will be charged 15%. This updated system, which takes effect on October 1st, replaces a more intricate prior proposal and aims to address criticisms regarding Apple's alleged monopoly over iPhone software distribution, while the company continues to highlight its focus on security and user trust.
