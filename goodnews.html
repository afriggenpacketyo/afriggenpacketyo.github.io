Critically Endangered Afia Birago's Puddle Frogs Bred for First Time at London Zoo

2026-10-15 01:03:03 EDT

London Zoo has achieved a significant conservation milestone by breeding 15 critically endangered Afia Birago's puddle frogs, marking the first time this species has been successfully bred in captivity. These tiny froglets, about 5mm long, originate from Ghana's Atewa Hills Forest Reserve, where their habitat is severely threatened by mining. Twenty adult frogs were brought to London in September 2025 to develop breeding techniques, which will now be shared with conservationists in Ghana to establish a wild-release population. This success follows the zoo's earlier achievement in breeding the Atewa slippery frog, another critically endangered species from the same region.
