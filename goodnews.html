While these funds offer attractive yields by selling call options, their distributions can include dividends, capital gains, or non-dividend distributions (return of capital), which reduces the investment's cost basis and defers taxes until sale. Financial advisors recommend using tax-deferred accounts like IRAs for these ETFs to avoid immediate tax hits.
