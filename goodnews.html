UN General Assembly Votes to Adopt New World Map Reflecting Africa's True Size

2026-09-04 20:53:11 EDT

The UN General Assembly has voted to adopt a new world map, sponsored by Togo and backed by the African Union, that more accurately reflects Africa's true size. The "Correct the Map" resolution, supported by 164 nations, aims to replace the 16th-century Mercator map, which significantly distorts landmasses, making Africa appear similar in size to Greenland despite being 14 times larger. Critics argue the Mercator projection minimizes the development needs and potential of equatorial countries, influencing global perceptions. While the UN vote is non-binding, it signifies a global shift towards a more equitable and accurate representation of continents, influencing maps used by institutions worldwide.
