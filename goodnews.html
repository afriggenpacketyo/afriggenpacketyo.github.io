Mesh Networks Emerge as a Potential Solution to Counter Government Internet Shutdowns

2026-07-25 07:08:00 EDT

"LoRa mesh networks" are emerging as a promising solution to counter government-imposed internet shutdowns, a tactic increasingly used by authoritarian regimes to suppress dissent and control information flow. These radio-powered networks, comprised of interconnected transmitting devices, facilitate encrypted, untraceable communication over several kilometers without relying on central internet infrastructure. Successfully demonstrated in Ukraine, they offer resilience against censorship and surveillance by bypassing traditional firewalls and legal mandates on providers. However, limitations include small message sizes, the potential for transmitter tracking, and the critical need for widespread participation to establish an effective network, presenting challenges in rapid deployment during crises.
