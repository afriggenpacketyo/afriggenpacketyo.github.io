First Series of Adult Tyrannosaurus Rex Footprints Discovered in North Dakota

2026-09-11 10:34:07 EDT

A groundbreaking discovery has been made in North Dakota's Hell Creek Formation: the first known series of footprints from an adult Tyrannosaurus rex. This 23-foot-long trackway, consisting of four distinct three-toed prints, offers an unprecedented look into the locomotion and behavior of a T. rex approximately 66.5 million years ago. Researchers from the Denver Museum of Nature and Science and Liverpool John Moores University utilized high-resolution 3D scanning to document and reproduce the footprints, estimating the massive predator walked at a brisk 3.5-4.5 mph. This significant finding provides invaluable data that complements skeletal records, allowing scientists to literally follow an animal as it moved across its prehistoric environment, enriching our understanding of the most famous dinosaur.
