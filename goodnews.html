Chinese Yuan Gains Traction as a Major Currency in Africa

2026-08-16 02:00:20 EDT

The Chinese yuan is increasingly becoming a significant currency in Africa, as more financial institutions integrate with China’s Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS). Libya's central bank is set to join CIPS, facilitating direct yuan payments for trade and potentially issuing yuan-denominated panda bonds for reconstruction efforts. Other African lenders, including the African Export-Import Bank and South Africa’s Standard Bank, have also connected to the system. Zambia has begun collecting taxes from Chinese mining firms in yuan, channeling the currency back to Beijing for imports and loan servicing.
