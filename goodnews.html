New Guidelines May Expand Access to 'Sponge' Tests for Oesophageal Cancer Detection

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

New draft guidelines for the NHS in England recommend offering quick "sponge in the gullet" tests to more people experiencing persistent indigestion, as a means to screen for oesophageal cancer. These less invasive tests, performed in GP surgeries, involve swallowing a capsule that releases a sponge to collect cells for analysis, offering an alternative to hospital endoscopies. The initiative aims to detect Barrett's oesophagus, a condition increasing cancer risk, more efficiently, thereby providing peace of mind and freeing up endoscopy resources. While not infallible, these tests are a welcome advancement in early cancer detection.
