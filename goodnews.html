Daylight Saving Time Ends Tonight, Americans 'Fall Back' One Hour

2026-10-31 15:00:23 EDT

Daylight Saving Time concludes tonight, November 1, 2026, at 2 a.m. local time, as most Americans "fall back" an hour, gaining an extra hour of sleep. This annual change shifts daylight from the morning to the evening during warmer months, returning to standard time for shorter days. The U.S. first adopted it in 1918 during World War I to conserve fuel, with states later given the option to observe it. Legislation for year-round daylight saving, the Sunshine Protection Act, passed the House but faces hurdles in the Senate, with President Trump supporting it despite past failed attempts due to concerns over late winter sunrises. The change can disrupt sleep schedules, especially for families with young children.
