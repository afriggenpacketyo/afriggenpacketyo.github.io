CBD is categorized as a "novel food" in the EU, requiring a thorough safety assessment and market authorization before it can be legally sold as a food ingredient, a status it has not yet achieved. This ruling specifically impacts CBD-based or CBD-enriched foods lacking EU market authorization, distinguishing them from other cannabis-derived products like hemp seeds, which can still be certified organic.
