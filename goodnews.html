Why Caffeine Affects People Differently: Genetics, Lifestyle Play Key Roles

2026-09-16 20:09:43 EDT

Individual responses to caffeine vary significantly due to a combination of genetic factors, lifestyle choices, and medication use. Caffeine metabolism, influenced by liver enzymes, can take between six to 20 hours depending on the person. Smoking can accelerate caffeine breakdown, while certain medications and pregnancy can slow it down. Tolerance develops with regular consumption, making occasional drinkers more sensitive. Experts advise avoiding caffeine for at least six hours before bed to ensure quality sleep, though individual sensitivity dictates personalized limits.
