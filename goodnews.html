Mesh Networks Offer Potential Solution to State-Imposed Internet Blackouts

2026-07-25 07:08:00 EDT

LoRa mesh networks, using small radio modules to transmit encrypted data over license-free frequencies, are emerging as a potential solution to government-directed internet shutdowns in authoritarian states. These self-healing networks bypass traditional internet infrastructure, making censorship and surveillance difficult, as demonstrated by their use in Ukraine. While effective for small text messages, they cannot handle larger data like images. However, risks include the ability for authorities to triangulate transmitter locations and the illegality of unauthorized radio equipment in some countries. The technology's effectiveness relies on widespread participation, posing a challenge in rapid crisis deployment.
