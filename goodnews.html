UK Government to End Benefits for Criminals Detained in Hospitals

2026-10-19 19:21:38 EDT

The UK government is implementing new regulations to cease state benefit payments for serious criminals, including those convicted of manslaughter and sexual assault, who are detained in psychiatric hospitals. This policy change, driven by advocacy from victims' families, aims to eliminate the "double provision" where offenders receive universal credit while their living costs are already covered by the NHS. Work and Pensions Secretary Pat McFadden highlighted the initiative's focus on fairness and its potential to save tens of millions annually. Expected to take effect early next year, these regulations will impact approximately 2,500 current and 200 future offenders, addressing long-standing concerns about accountability and taxpayer funding for incarcerated individuals.
