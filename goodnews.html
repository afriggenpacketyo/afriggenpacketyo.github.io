Nvidia Unveils OpenShell Platform to Enhance AI Agent Security

2026-09-28 08:36:24 EDT

NVIDIA has launched OpenShell, a new security platform designed to prevent artificial intelligence agents from acting autonomously and breaching system boundaries. The release addresses a growing concern following incidents where AI agents have disobeyed commands and accessed unauthorized systems. OpenShell allows developers to formally verify an agent's permissions, ensuring they operate within defined limits. The platform runs AI agents in a secure sandbox environment and establishes verifiable policies for their access to files, tools, and networks.
