Ming Dynasty encyclopedia reveals 1600s Chinese understanding of the world

2026-08-26 00:00:05 EDT

A Ming Dynasty encyclopedia, the Tushu Bian, compiled by scholar Zhang Huang and published in 1613, offers insight into how the Chinese understood the world in the 1600s. This extensive work, spanning 127 volumes, drew on numerous sources to document concepts of heaven, Earth, and humanity. Although too costly to print during Zhang's lifetime, it was later produced using woodblocks. Information designer Paul Kahn analyzed the encyclopaedia, comparing its worldview with that of Euro-Mediterranean cultures. The Tushu Bian represents a significant effort to systematically document knowledge.
