Study Reveals Chinese Characters Evolved Like Organisms Through Natural Selection

2026-09-01 00:00:05 EDT

Published in "Digital Scholarship in the Humanities," the research highlights that a small number of core components, such as 日 (sun) and 人 (human), are repeatedly combined to form new characters, with entirely new elements invented when existing ones were insufficient. This evolutionary process, influenced by usage frequency, led to a simplification from complex to modern forms, reflecting a linguistic principle of achieving greater output with less effort.
