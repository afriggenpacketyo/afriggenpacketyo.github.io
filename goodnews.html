Noah Adams, Longtime Host of NPR's All Things Considered, Dies at 84

2026-10-18 12:30:00 EDT

Noah Adams, a distinguished writer, broadcaster, and storyteller who notably hosted NPR's "All Things Considered" for 18 years, has passed away at the age of 84. Adams was widely celebrated for his ability to guide listeners through major historical events while also excelling in "softer features" that vividly captured the essence of places and people with simple, evocative language. He was renowned for his deep curiosity and unique talent for connecting with individuals, profoundly influencing future public radio figures such as Ira Glass. Adams also authored five books and produced the groundbreaking 1981 experimental radio documentary "Father Cares: The Last of Jonestown," leaving an indelible mark on the landscape of public radio journalism.
