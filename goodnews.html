Understanding the 2026 Autumn Equinox and Its Astronomical Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The autumn equinox, marking the start of astronomical autumn, will occur on September 23 at 01:06 BST. This precise moment signifies when the Sun crosses the celestial equator, resulting in roughly equal day and night lengths globally. Although the equinox itself is not visible, it holds cultural and spiritual importance for some, with gatherings at sites like Stonehenge. Due to light refraction, the actual "equilux," when day and night are truly equal, happens a few days later in the UK, depending on latitude.
