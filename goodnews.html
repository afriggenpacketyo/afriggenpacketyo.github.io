UN General Assembly Votes to Adopt New World Map Reflecting Africa's True Size

2026-09-04 15:44:40 EDT

The UN General Assembly has overwhelmingly voted to adopt a new world map, sponsored by Togo and backed by African Union members, that more accurately represents Africa's true size. This "Correct the Map" resolution, supported by 164 nations, aims to replace the traditional Mercator map, which has historically distorted landmasses, making Africa appear disproportionately small compared to regions like Greenland. Critics argue the Mercator projection minimizes the development needs and potential of countries near the equator. This non-binding vote is a significant symbolic step towards correcting historical cartographical misrepresentations and promoting a fairer global perspective.
