Finland Pioneers World's Largest Sand Battery for Renewable Energy Storage

2026-07-27 04:46:51 EDT

A small town in southern Finland is utilizing the world's largest commercial sand battery to tackle the intermittency challenge of renewable energy. This innovative 13-meter-tall facility stores 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy using 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone, providing nearly a month of summer heat or a week of winter heat for 5,000 residents. Developed by Polar Night Energy, the project has significantly reduced greenhouse gas emissions and wood chip use, offering combustion-free heating for most of the year. This article highlights the potential of sand batteries to provide long-term, material-efficient energy storage, crucial for the global energy transition.
