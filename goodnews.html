Using Electric Fans in Extreme Heat Can Be Dangerous, Health Experts Warn

2026-07-31 19:57:16 EDT

Health experts are issuing urgent warnings that using electric fans in extreme heat, particularly above 90-95 degrees Fahrenheit, can be counterproductive and potentially dangerous. According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, fans can act like convection ovens, circulating hot air and inadvertently raising body temperature, thereby increasing the risk of dehydration and heat exhaustion. UCLA experts David Eisenman and V. Kelly Turner strongly advise against using fans during peak daytime heat, especially in dry conditions prevalent in areas like Los Angeles. Instead, they recommend strategic nighttime ventilation and daytime shading, while emphasizing significantly increased water intake if fans are utilized at night, to effectively manage soaring temperatures.
