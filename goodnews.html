UN Endorses New Equal Earth Map Projection for More Accurate Global Representation

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection as a new world map, aiming to represent continents more accurately, particularly Africa, which is significantly distorted on the traditional Mercator map. Sponsored by Togo and backed by members of the African Union, the resolution passed with 164 member states voting in favor, with only the US voting against it. The Mercator projection, designed for 16th-century navigation, greatly enlarges regions near the poles while shrinking Africa and South America. The UN encourages governments and institutions worldwide to adopt the Equal Earth projection to foster a more truthful understanding of global geography and challenge historical misconceptions about landmass proportions.
