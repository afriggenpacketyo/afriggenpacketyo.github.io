Middle East Oil Exports Rebound to Pre-War Levels Despite Continued Hormuz Attacks

2026-10-05 01:35:00 EDT

Middle East crude oil exports, excluding Iran, have surprisingly recovered to pre-war levels by late September, defying continued attacks on vessels in the Strait of Hormuz. Shipping data from Kpler indicates exports surpassed 18 million barrels per day on several days, reaching up to 22.5 million bpd. This resilience was achieved by utilizing alternative routes and ship-to-ship transfers, with about 40% of crude bypassing Hormuz, compared to 17% before the conflict. Saudi Arabia increased Red Sea exports, and the UAE used its Fujairah pipeline, demonstrating robust adaptability in regional oil supply chains amidst persistent security threats and geopolitical instability.

Italian Friars Utilize AI and Advanced Tech for Basilica of Saint Francis Restoration

2026-10-04 20:05:48 EDT

Chief restorer Sergio Fusetti, unable to place over 80,000 fragments, now relies on AI to solve this complex jigsaw puzzle. A technology company, Haltadefinizione, is creating the highest-resolution 3D blueprint of the basilica, a "digital twin," using hundreds of thousands of photos. This advanced technology will also analyze cracks and discoloration, make art more accessible through virtual reality, and provide a crucial roadmap for future preservation.
