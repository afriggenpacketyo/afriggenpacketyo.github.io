New TSA Program Allows PreCheck Members Ticket-Free Access to Airport Gates

2026-09-08 17:27:28 EDT

The Transportation Security Administration (TSA) has launched a new program, 'Gateside by TSA PreCheck,' allowing eligible PreCheck members to pass through security and access airport gates without a plane ticket. This initiative revives a practice largely discontinued after 9/11, enabling friends and relatives to see off travelers or meet them during layovers, and enjoy airside amenities. The free program requires online application and approval one to three days prior to a visit, valid for one calendar day.
