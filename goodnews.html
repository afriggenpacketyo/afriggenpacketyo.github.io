UN Endorses New Equal Earth Map for Accurate Global Landmass Representation

2026-09-06 08:30:50 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to more accurately depict the true geographical sizes of continents, with a particular focus on Africa. This resolution, spearheaded by Togo and supported by an overwhelming 164 member states, encourages global governments and educational institutions to transition from the traditional Mercator map, which has been prevalent since the 16th century. The Mercator projection is known for its significant distortions, enlarging regions closer to the poles while inaccurately diminishing the apparent size of continents like Africa and South America. This landmark vote represents a crucial symbolic stride towards rectifying historical cartographic biases and promoting a more precise and equitable global geographical understanding.
