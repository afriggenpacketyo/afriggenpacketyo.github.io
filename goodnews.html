2026-10-16 18:30:00 EDT

Environment Minister Joao Paulo Capobianco lauded the "stratospheric drop," while President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva reaffirmed the nation's goal of achieving zero illegal deforestation by 2030. Climate advocacy groups credit this "historic achievement" to the government's sustained commitment, enhanced monitoring, and more stringent penalties against illegal logging activities.
