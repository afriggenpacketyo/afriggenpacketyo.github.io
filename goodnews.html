"Gung-ho," or "gong he" (工合) in pinyin, is an abbreviation for "gongye hezuoshe" (工业合作社), meaning "industrial cooperatives." These were grassroots labor units established in China in 1938 to support the war effort against Japan, illustrating a historical linguistic connection between the two nations.
