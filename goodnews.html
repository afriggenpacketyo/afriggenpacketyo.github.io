Housing minister Matthew Pennycook strongly condemned the current leasehold system, labeling it as "blighting lives" and an "anachronism in the twenty-first century." The proposed Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill also includes provisions to cap ground rent for existing leaseholders at £250, which will eventually be reduced to a symbolic "peppercorn" rate after 40 years, directly addressing widespread complaints and aiming to align the UK with international housing models.

Experts Explain Why Lying is a Key Part of Children's Mental Development
