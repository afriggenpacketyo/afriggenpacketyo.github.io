European Space Agency Deploys Satellites to Monitor Climate Change and Plant Health

2026-09-15 03:28:00 EDT

The European Space Agency (ESA) successfully launched two new climate observation satellites, Sentinel-3C and Flex, into orbit from French Guiana. The Flex satellite is specifically designed to provide crucial data on the impact of heatwaves on plant life by monitoring the very faint fluorescence emitted during photosynthesis, helping scientists assess plant health and ecosystem productivity. Meanwhile, Sentinel-3C, a component of the EU’s Copernicus space program, will gather extensive data from the marine environment, including temperature, wave height, and ocean currents, and also monitor land use to optimize agricultural yields. These essential missions are expected to significantly enhance Europe's capacity to monitor and respond to the accelerating challenges posed by climate change.
