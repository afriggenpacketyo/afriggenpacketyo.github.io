This harmful marine organism smothers native species and clogs infrastructure. Working with her owner, marine ecologist Dr. Texa Sim, Sgiath has located hundreds of colonies, demonstrating the potential of canine scent detection in marine environments. Her exceptional problem-solving skills and rapid learning have exceeded initial project expectations.
