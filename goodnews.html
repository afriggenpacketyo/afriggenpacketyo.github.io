University of North Carolina Settles Federal Probes on Antisemitism and Race-Based Admissions

2026-10-20 00:43:57 EDT

The University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill has reached a voluntary settlement with the Trump administration, resolving 18 federal investigations into alleged campus antisemitism and race discrimination. The agreement, effective until 2028, requires UNC to appoint a "Merit & Civil Rights Administrator," establish a "Title VI Coordinator," and implement mandatory antisemitism training for students, faculty, and staff. UNC will also adopt a "color-blind admissions system," prohibiting race as a factor in admissions or student benefits, while still allowing consideration of personal adversity. This resolution allows UNC to maintain federal funding and concludes an 18-month Justice Department compliance review, setting a precedent for other universities facing similar federal scrutiny.
