2026-10-14 09:55:06 EDT

Over 75 million Americans will receive a 3.6% boost to their monthly Social Security benefits in 2027, the largest annual cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) in four years. This increase, designed to offset higher inflation driven by soaring fuel prices due to the Iran war, will raise the average payment by about $75 to $2,170 monthly. However, critics argue that the COLA, based on the CPI-W, may not fully reflect the actual living costs for seniors, particularly healthcare expenses, and may not restore purchasing power lost from previous lagging adjustments. Cruise Passenger Rescued in Ionian Sea After 20 Hours Adrift

2026-10-14 09:51:55 EDT

A 44-year-old Salvadorian woman was miraculously rescued from the Ionian Sea after enduring 20 hours adrift, following her alleged jump from the Royal Caribbean cruise ship Odyssey of the Seas. The incident, which occurred southwest of the Greek island of Zakynthos, triggered an extensive search and rescue operation involving two coastguard boats, a navy helicopter, and the active participation of the cruise ship itself. A passing freighter ultimately located the woman, fully conscious and secured in a life ring, before she was picked up by a Greek coastguard patrol boat. She was subsequently transported to a hospital for comprehensive health tests, including a psychiatric evaluation, after informing rescuers she had "wanted to disembark."
