2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

Sponsored by Togo and supported by the African Union, the resolution encourages the adoption of this map over the traditional Mercator projection, which distorts landmasses. The Equal Earth projection shows Africa as significantly larger than Greenland, unlike the Mercator map where they appear similar in size.
