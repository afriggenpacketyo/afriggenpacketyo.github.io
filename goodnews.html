ESA's Hera Spacecraft Arrives to Investigate Asteroid Collision Site

2026-11-25 01:00:36 EST

The European Space Agency's Hera spacecraft has reached the Didymos asteroid system to assess the impact of NASA's 2022 DART mission, which deliberately crashed into the smaller moonlet, Dimorphos. DART successfully altered Dimorphos's orbit, reducing its period by 33 minutes, in the first test of planetary defense. Hera will spend a year studying the "crash scene," aiming to measure Dimorphos's mass and the crater's characteristics to better understand how to deflect potentially hazardous space rocks in the future. This mission is crucial for developing strategies to protect Earth from asteroid impacts and prevent global devastation.
