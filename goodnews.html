Vietnam Secures Free Trade Agreement with European Free Trade Association

2026-10-18 05:19:00 EDT

Vietnam has signed a free trade agreement with the European Free Trade Association (EFTA), comprising Norway, Switzerland, Iceland, and Liechtenstein. The deal, which resumed talks last year after an interruption, will waive or reduce customs duties on industrial, fish, and agricultural products. Notably, around half of Norway's exports to Vietnam will become tariff-free immediately, with over 98% achieving this status within seven years. This agreement aims to strengthen the rules-based trading system, diversify Vietnam's import and export markets, and deepen its integration into global value chains, fostering economic growth for the Southeast Asian nation.
