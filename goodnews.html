Magnitude 3.2 Earthquake Rattles Southern Los Angeles Area

2026-09-06 17:46:28 EDT

A magnitude 3.2 earthquake struck near Compton early Sunday morning, causing light shaking across the southern Los Angeles area. The earthquake occurred at 12:13 a.m. with its epicenter located about three miles south-southeast of Compton. Shaking was reported as far north as Santa Clarita. The epicenter is situated over the Newport-Inglewood Fault Zone, a significant urban fault line in Southern California. The USGS reported that nearly 1,100 people felt the tremor. Fortunately, no damage or accidents were reported as a result of the earthquake, which occurred at a depth of approximately eight miles. The Newport-Inglewood Fault is known for its seismic activity, having been the site of the devastating 1933 Long Beach earthquake.
