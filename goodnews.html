Remarkable mammal fossil discovery sheds light on early evolution

2026-10-07 15:59:16 EDT

Paleontologists have unearthed a remarkably preserved fossil of an ancient mammal, Megacauda sungei, dating back approximately 165 million years to the middle Jurassic period. The chihuahua-sized creature exhibits a unique combination of features, including a platypus-like hindquarters suggesting a semi-aquatic lifestyle and a predator-like head with sharp teeth. Crucially, the fossil provides some of the oldest evidence of mammals capable of chewing food before swallowing and suckling young. This discovery significantly pushes back the timeline for these fundamental mammalian traits, offering invaluable insights into early mammalian evolution.
