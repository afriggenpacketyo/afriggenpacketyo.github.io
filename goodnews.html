Watchdog Bans Voy Adverts for Misleading Low Testosterone Claims

2026-11-17 20:11:07 EST

The Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) has banned five adverts by online healthcare provider Voy for making misleading claims linked to low testosterone in men. The adverts, which included posters and paid-for Facebook ads, were found to exaggerate the connection between common symptoms like irritability, fatigue, and low sex drive with low testosterone. Furthermore, the ASA deemed Voy's claim that "low testosterone sneaks up on 1 in 4 men over 30" as misleading and unsupported by evidence. Voy, which sells at-home testosterone tests and treatments, has withdrawn the adverts, with this ruling forming part of a wider ASA investigation into men's health advertising practices.

UK Launches Major Study on Social Media Ban's Impact on Youth Mental Health

2026-11-17 19:49:31 EST
