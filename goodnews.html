2026-08-03 04:00:11 EDT

Chinese scientists have successfully developed natural rubber from a dandelion-like weed known as the rubber dandelion (Taraxacum kok-saghyz), a significant step towards reducing China's heavy reliance on imported rubber. Researchers in Xinjiang harvested one tonne of dried roots from a test field, yielding 40kg of natural rubber. This breakthrough offers a domestic alternative to traditional rubber sources, which are predominantly imported from Southeast Asia. The development could bolster China's economic self-sufficiency in a critical industrial material, marking a notable advancement in agricultural science and resource management.
