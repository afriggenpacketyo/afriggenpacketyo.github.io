Understanding Your Rights: Debt Collectors Cannot Legally Re-Age Old Debts

2026-08-06 10:42:23 EDT

This article clarifies that debt collectors are generally prohibited by law from "re-aging" old debts, which means improperly changing the reporting timeline to extend their presence on a credit report. Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, negative information typically must fall off after seven years from the original delinquency date, regardless of debt sales. The Fair Debt Collection Practices Act also prevents misrepresenting a debt's legal status. Consumers are advised to check credit reports for discrepancies, dispute inaccuracies, and be wary of requests for small payments that could restart the statute of limitations.
