Debate Over World Map Projections Highlights Discrepancies in Size Representation

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The UN is advocating for the replacement of the predominant Mercator map projection with one that more accurately reflects the true size of continents, particularly Africa. The Mercator map, created for navigation, significantly distorts the size of landmasses, making Africa appear similar in size to Greenland when it is fourteen times larger. The Equal Earth projection aims to address this distortion by presenting landmasses more accurately, but it sacrifices the straight lines crucial for navigation. Cartographers emphasize that no flat map can perfectly represent a sphere, leading to trade-offs between preserving area, shape, distance, and direction.
