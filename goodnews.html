2026-10-16 21:53:34 EDT

This weather pattern has resulted in warmer-than-normal conditions and significantly reduced rainfall across much of England and Wales, exacerbating drought concerns in southern regions. However, the Met Office predicts a shift towards a wetter and windier late autumn and early winter, influenced by a strengthening El Niño in the Pacific.
