Experts propose a new antiwar agenda for a multipolar world order

2026-07-29 06:00:30 EDT

In response to the perceived decline of the American-led world order, scholars Aslı Bâli and Samuel Moyn advocate for a new antiwar agenda suited to an inevitably plural and rivalrous world. They argue against reviving the flawed "rules-based international order" and instead propose four commitments: strengthening limits on self-defense, reaffirming the inadmissibility of annexation, prohibiting proxy wars, and restricting arms sales that abet aggression. This "thin" bargain, based on mutual restraint and enlightened self-interest, aims to prevent coercion from becoming unconstrained. The authors suggest this approach offers a more stable path than a hierarchy that no longer commands belief.

California incentivizes electric car adoption with new rebate program amid high gas prices

2026-07-29 06:00:00 EDT

California is launching a new instant rebate program, "MyFirstEV," to encourage the adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) amidst persistently high gas prices, exacerbated by the war in Iran. The program offers $3,500 for new EVs and $1,750 for used EVs, aiming to make EVs more accessible to low and moderate-income residents. While the state has seen a strong EV share in new car sales, supply chain issues and higher upfront costs remain barriers. Governor Newsom positions the initiative as a way to offer financial relief to families facing volatile gas prices, contrasting it with federal policies. The program is funded by a $135 million state allocation matched by automakers.
