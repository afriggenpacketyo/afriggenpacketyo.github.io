2026-09-16 17:45:43 EDT

Measuring 728 feet across and 141 feet deep, this is the solar system's biggest known impact crater formed recently, an event estimated to occur only once every 132 years. The discovery provides invaluable data as NASA plans a moon base for astronauts.
