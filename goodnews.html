This technique involves genuinely conceding the specific piece of an argument for which one holds responsibility, without necessarily agreeing with the partner's entire stance. Using phrases such as "You're right, that part's on me" can almost immediately diffuse tension by validating the partner's feelings and preventing the argument from broadening into endless grievances. The article stresses the importance of being specific, avoiding conditional "but" statements, and offering these concessions early, even when one believes they are mostly in the right, thereby fostering more constructive communication and problem-solving in relationships.
