These findings provide unprecedented insights into Denisovan life, revealing a stable subsistence pattern highly adapted to local resources. The evidence suggests they were specialized hunters, intensively exploiting animals within coniferous forests and forest-steppe environments. This significant discovery enhances scientists' understanding of Denisovans, an extinct human group that diverged from modern humans and Neanderthals hundreds of thousands of years ago.
