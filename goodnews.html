BE FAST: Understanding Stroke Warning Signs for Rapid Response

2026-11-01 17:22:12 EST

Following Senator Chris Murphy's recent stroke, the American Stroke Association is emphasizing the "BE FAST" acronym to help people quickly recognize critical stroke warning signs. BE FAST stands for Balance loss, Eye changes, Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, and most importantly, Time to call 911 immediately. Prompt medical attention is absolutely crucial for survival and minimizing permanent brain damage, as a stroke occurs when blood flow to or within the brain is interrupted, depriving tissue of oxygen. Senator Murphy's excellent prognosis after acting quickly underscores the vital importance of recognizing these symptoms and seeking care without delay to improve outcomes.
