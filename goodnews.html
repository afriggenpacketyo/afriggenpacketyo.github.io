Understanding If and When Old Debt Can Reappear on Your Credit Report

2026-08-17 11:57:27 EDT

Borrowers are questioning if old debt can reappear on credit reports, as lenders are keeping charged-off accounts longer. Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, most negative information remains for about seven years, tied to the original delinquency date, not when a debt collector acquires it. "Re-aging" a debt by changing this date is generally prohibited. It is crucial for consumers to distinguish between credit-reporting time limits and statutes of limitations for lawsuits, as making a payment on old debt can restart the latter. If old debt reappears, verify details and dispute inaccuracies, or consider debt relief options for legitimate balances. (not investment advice)
