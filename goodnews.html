This discovery challenges the long-held assumption that the region's ancient inhabitants were primarily Eurasian nomads migrating from the steppes. Located in the Hami oasis, the site, dating back 3,600 years to China's Shang dynasty, now shows a previously unknown, genetically mixed population. This research provides significant new insights into the complex demographic history and interactions between different cultural groups in the eastern Tianshan region during that period.
