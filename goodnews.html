BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a joint European and Japanese endeavor, successfully deployed a pair of robotic spacecraft into orbit around Mercury after an eight-year journey. This marks the first visit to the solar system's innermost planet in over a decade, aiming to unravel its many mysteries. The $2 billion mission, named after Italian mathematician Giuseppe “Bepi” Colombo, will see the two craft separate next month to map and study Mercury from different altitudes. Despite the extreme temperatures and technical challenges, scientists anticipate unprecedented data from simultaneous measurements, enhancing our understanding of this least-explored inner planet.
