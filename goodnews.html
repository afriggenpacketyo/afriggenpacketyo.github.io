Archaeologists Discover World's Oldest Shipwreck Off Turkish Coast

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Off the coast of Kekova Island in Turkey, archaeologists have unearthed what is now recognized as the world's oldest shipwreck, dating back to the Early Bronze Age, approximately 4,400 to 4,700 years ago. While the ship's wooden components have long disintegrated, more than 30 ceramic storage pots, known as amphorae, were discovered on the seafloor, some remarkably preserving traces of their original contents. Believed to be a 10-meter trade vessel, its age was determined by comparing the distinctive style of the amphorae to similar pottery from ancient Egypt and the Middle East. This extraordinary "time capsule" provides invaluable insights into ancient Mediterranean maritime trade and the lives of its sailors.
