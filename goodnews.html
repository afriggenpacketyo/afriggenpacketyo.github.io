Conservationists Launch Effort to Save Critically Endangered Glutinous Snail in Wales

2026-09-12 02:09:37 EDT

Conservationists are embarking on a crucial mission to save the glutinous snail, one of Europe's rarest species, found exclusively in a single lake in the UK. This tiny mollusc, described as being as precious as an "Egyptian artefact," is now the focus of a collaborative effort by several British zoos to establish "ark populations." These captive breeding programs utilize creatures initially bred in a Ceredigion back garden, a significant breakthrough as their eggs had never been knowingly observed before. The initiative aims to protect the snail, which has vanished from England due to pollution and habitat loss, as a vital first step towards its eventual reintroduction into the wild.
