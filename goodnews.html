UN Approves New World Map Projection Highlighting Africa's True Size

2026-09-04 19:34:00 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has approved a resolution advocating for the Equal Earth cartographic projection, which more accurately depicts the relative sizes of continental landmasses. This new map aims to correct significant distortions found in the traditional Mercator projection, which historically makes regions near the poles, like Greenland, appear disproportionately large compared to areas near the equator, such as the African continent. Spearheaded by several African countries, the resolution emphasizes that maps profoundly shape global understanding and should avoid perpetuating inaccurate representations. While the resolution is not legally binding, it encourages widespread adoption, though the United States was the sole country to vote against the measure.
