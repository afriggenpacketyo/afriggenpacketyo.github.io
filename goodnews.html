India's ISRO to Launch New Satellite Enhancing Indigenous GPS Alternative, NavIC

2026-10-30 02:59:00 EDT

India's space agency, ISRO, is preparing to launch the NVS-03 navigation satellite, aimed at bolstering the country's indigenous GPS alternative, NavIC. The satellite, the third in ISRO's next-generation series, will be carried into orbit by the GSLV-F18 rocket from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota. This initiative seeks to reduce India's reliance on foreign navigation systems like the US-owned GPS, providing navigation coverage across India and up to 1,500 kilometers beyond its borders. The NVS-03 features an indigenous atomic clock and adds the L1 signal to existing L5 and S-band signals, representing a significant step despite previous challenges with the NavIC program.
