US-China Decoupling Could Benefit Other Countries, China Trade War Game Suggests

2026-09-12 08:00:05 EDT

Chinese researchers have developed a war game scenario suggesting that a complete economic decoupling between the US and China could, surprisingly, generate dividends for most other nations. The study, published in the South China Journal of Economics, modeled this outcome, indicating potential welfare gains for economies not directly involved in the decoupling. However, the simulations included a crucial caveat: any initial spillover benefits would prove fragile. If these beneficiary economies were to subsequently sever their own economic ties with China, their initial welfare gains would rapidly transform into significant losses, highlighting the delicate balance of global economic relationships.
