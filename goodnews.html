Scientists Discover First Skeletal Evidence of Diplodocus Dinosaur in Europe

2026-09-15 10:42:00 EDT

Researchers in Spain have announced a groundbreaking discovery: the first skeletal evidence of the Diplodocus dinosaur found outside of North America, unearthing various vertebrae and chevron bones at the rich La Tejeria fossil site in eastern Spain. Dating back approximately 150 million years, this find challenges previous understanding that the iconic long-necked herbivore was exclusive to North America, where its remains had only been recovered until now. The discovery strongly supports theories of ephemeral land bridges or episodic sea regressions that allowed land-based dinosaurs to cross vast distances between separating continents during the Late Jurassic period. This article details the significant paleontological breakthrough, reinforcing the idea of faunal exchanges between North America and Europe.
