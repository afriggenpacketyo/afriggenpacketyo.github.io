2026-09-21 18:38:34 EDT

The acting director of the National Park Service, Jessica Bowron, described Yellowstone's wild bison recovery as an 'incredible conservation story.' A century ago, these majestic animals were nearly extinct, but today thousands thrive within the park, necessitating a shift from mere survival to effective herd management. Through the Bison Conservation Transfer Program, over 600 bison have been relocated to 29 tribes across 13 states since 2019, fostering new conservation herds. This remarkable turnaround demonstrates decades of scientific work and crucial cooperation among various partners, ensuring a vibrant future for the species.
