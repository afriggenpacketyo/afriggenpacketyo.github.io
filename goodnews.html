2026-11-23 08:17:15 EST

This article explains how to determine a good Certificate of Deposit (CD) rate, emphasizing that it involves more than just the stated interest rate, but also term length, early withdrawal penalties, and the minimum deposit. A good CD rate typically doubles the national average for its term, and ideally surpasses high-yield savings account rates, as it requires locking up funds. Investors should compare Annual Percentage Yield (APY), which includes compounding interest, and carefully factor in potential penalties for withdrawing money before the term is up.
