2026-09-06 10:19:00 EDT

The small Canadian town of Gander, Newfoundland, is remembered for its extraordinary generosity and community spirit after the 9/11 attacks, when it unexpectedly hosted 7,000 stranded air travelers. With U.S. airspace closed, 38 international flights diverted to Gander, effectively doubling its population overnight. Locals, including former Mayor Claude Elliott and volunteer Beulah Cooper, swiftly mobilized to provide essential food, shelter, and comfort to the "plane people." This remarkable act of compassion inspired the acclaimed musical "Come From Away," and Gander continues to draw visitors eager to experience its unique story of human kindness during a global crisis.
