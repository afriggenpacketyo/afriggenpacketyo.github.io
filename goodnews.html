These hormones appear to regulate the coral's immune system, preventing it from expending excessive energy and improving its chances of survival during marine heat waves. This finding offers a promising avenue for developing strategies to prevent coral bleaching, a phenomenon that threatens reef ecosystems globally. Specialist's Guide to Managing Migraine Pain, Particularly in Women

2026-11-11 07:00:11 EST

Migraines affect 37 million Americans, with women being three times more likely to experience them than men. Dr. Jessica Ailani, a neurologist specializing in headache disorders, explains that migraines are more than just headaches, often involving multiple phases with various symptoms like irritability, nausea, and visual disturbances. The condition is believed to be primarily genetic, involving abnormalities in proteins that cause inflammation and network errors in the brain. Hormonal fluctuations, particularly in women during puberty and perimenopause, play a significant role, while testosterone may offer some protection. Advances in treatment include Botox injections and CGRP inhibitors, which block a protein involved in migraine pain. Ailani emphasizes the importance of self-care for women, urging them to "put yourself first."
