Understanding When Debt Collectors Can Sell Your Account to Another Agency

2026-07-28 11:12:52 EDT

This article explains that debt collectors can often legally sell your account to another collection agency or debt buyer, especially if they have purchased the debt outright from the original creditor. There are typically no limits to how many times a single debt can change hands, which can be frustrating for borrowers. If your debt is transferred, your legal obligations generally remain the same, but the new owner must be able to demonstrate their legal authority to collect. Borrowers are advised to ask for written validation of the debt, confirm the company's authority, and review their records, as this process can sometimes create opportunities for more favorable settlement negotiations.
