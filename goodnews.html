New Study Reveals Manx Shearwaters Experience "Flap Lag" During Long Flights

2026-10-29 14:00:00 EDT

New research published in the journal Science indicates that Manx shearwaters, a type of seabird, experience a form of "jet lag" or "flap lag" during their long flights across the Atlantic. The study, utilizing 20 years of GPS tracking data, found that these birds become slightly disoriented, veering off course by a few miles, due to their internal clocks falling out of sync with the sun's position. This disorientation occurs as they rush back to their nests after foraging hundreds of miles away. The findings highlight the universal nature of circadian rhythms and navigation challenges across species, even for creatures known for their impressive migratory abilities.
