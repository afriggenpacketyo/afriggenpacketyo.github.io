"Super El Niño" Predicted to Bring Warmer, Wetter Winter Across U.S.

2026-10-28 22:20:45 EDT

National weather agencies are forecasting a significantly warmer and wetter winter across large parts of the continental U.S., attributed to a powerful "Super El Niño" climate pattern. Originating in the tropical Pacific, this phenomenon is expected to alter global weather, bringing above-average temperatures and precipitation to various regions, alongside elevated risks of coastal flooding. While El Niño may alleviate drought conditions in some areas, it also carries potential hazards such as strengthened nor'easters and an increased likelihood of tornadoes in the South. Forecasters stress that these are probabilistic predictions, serving as a critical roadmap for communities to prepare and mitigate potential impacts.
