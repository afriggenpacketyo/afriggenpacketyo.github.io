Spironolactone Shows Promise in Lowering Blood Pressure for Stroke Survivors

2026-11-15 17:20:45 EST

A new study, the REDUCE trial, suggests that adding spironolactone, a widely available and low-cost blood pressure medication, significantly aids stroke survivors in reaching critical blood pressure targets. Participants receiving spironolactone experienced an average reduction of nearly eight points in their top blood pressure number within three months, with 52% achieving the recommended goal of less than 130/80, compared to only 30% in the usual care group. While these findings are encouraging for preventing future strokes, experts emphasize the need for larger and longer studies to directly confirm the medication's impact on preventing subsequent stroke events. Patients are encouraged to discuss this option with their clinicians.
