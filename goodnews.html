Federal Judge Deems Trump Administration's Voter Roll Collection Policy Unlawful

2026-10-09 16:08:10 EDT

A federal judge has ruled unlawful the U.S. Justice Department's policy of collecting unredacted state voter rolls for comparison against a federal immigration database. U.S. District Judge Sparkle Sooknanan stated the policy, enacted to comply with President Trump's election integrity executive order, violated privacy laws and risked disenfranchising citizens, citing instances of incorrect removals. The ruling emphasized that Congress granted states, not the federal government, authority over voter roll maintenance, asserting the policy improperly attempted to decide who votes. This decision marks a significant setback for the administration's efforts to ramp up non-citizen voting investigations ahead of the midterm elections, protecting voter privacy and state autonomy.
