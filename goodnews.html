Kagan and Soai Awarded Nobel Prize in Chemistry for Solving Chirality Mystery

2026-10-07 07:51:52 EDT

French scientist Henri B. Kagan and Japan's Kenso Soai have been awarded the Nobel Prize in Chemistry for their groundbreaking work on chirality, solving the mystery of why nature exclusively produces one mirror image of a molecule. Their research explains how living organisms function with only one form of molecules, such as amino acids, which exist in "left-handed" and "right-handed" versions. Kagan and Soai developed methods to design chemical reactions that predominantly produce the desired molecular mirror image, a crucial advancement for drug development where different versions can have vastly different biological effects.
