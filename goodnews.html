Understanding the 2026 Autumn Equinox and Its Global Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The astronomical autumn equinox is set to occur on September 23rd at 01:06 BST, precisely when the Sun crosses the celestial equator. This moment signifies that neither hemisphere is tilted towards or away from the Sun, resulting in approximately equal day and night lengths worldwide. Due to light refraction in the atmosphere, the visible "equilux" of truly equal light typically happens a few days after the equinox in the UK. This event carries significant cultural and spiritual importance for various communities, with many people gathering at historical sites such as Stonehenge to mark the occasion and witness the sunrise.
