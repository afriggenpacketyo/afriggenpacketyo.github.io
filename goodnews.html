2026-08-03 21:08:13 EDT

Current guidelines, set by Nice, primarily focus on inherited genes, neglecting other crucial factors like lifestyle, reproductive history, and broader genetic information. A new risk assessment tool, Boadicea, identified eight times more women in this age group who developed breast cancer.
