Experts Explore Reasons Behind Increased Motion Sickness in Electric Vehicles

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

An increasing number of passengers are reporting heightened motion sickness when traveling in electric vehicles (EVs), a phenomenon explored in this article. Experts attribute this to a "mismatch in sensory cues," where the absence of traditional engine sounds and vibrations, combined with the often-abrupt deceleration of regenerative braking, disrupts the body's perception of movement. While not all EV passengers experience this, the issue is gaining recognition, with some carmakers acknowledging the problem. Researchers and manufacturers are now exploring solutions like smoother acceleration software, subtle seat vibrations, and adaptive braking to mitigate these effects as EVs become more prevalent.
