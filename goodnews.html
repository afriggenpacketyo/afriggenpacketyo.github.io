New Sea Spider Species Identified in Canadian Waters After Century-Long Gap

2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

Researchers at the University of British Columbia have announced the discovery of two new species of sea spiders in the shallow waters of the Salish Sea, an area between Washington State and British Columbia. This marks the first such finding in over a century for the region, highlighting the ongoing potential for marine biodiversity discoveries. The newly identified species, Callipallene pilosuspedes and Tanystylum kiixin, exhibit distinct characteristics, including varying leg structures and self-grooming abilities. Lead researcher Cormac Toler-Scott noted that these marine arthropods, while related to land spiders, are slow-moving, transparent, and feed parasitically on hydroids. The team plans additional dives, expecting to uncover more species in the future.

California Enacts Stricter Industrial Safety Laws Following Boyle Heights Warehouse Fire

2026-09-27 16:30:58 EDT

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed new legislation aimed at strengthening industrial safety rules, prompted by a massive fire at a Lineage cold storage facility in Los Angeles' Boyle Heights neighborhood last June. The incident caused significant environmental and health issues for local residents, including noxious smoke and pest infestations. Senate Bill 716 increases fines for health and safety violations to a maximum of $50,000 per infraction, a substantial rise from previous limits. Additionally, Assembly Bill 817 requires new cold storage facilities to establish contingency funds or secure insurance for emergency community needs. These laws, which will become statewide by July 2028, aim to enhance corporate accountability and protect communities from industrial disasters.
