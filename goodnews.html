Historic SS United States Deliberately Sunk to Become World's Largest Artificial Reef

2026-11-12 11:20:04 EST

The iconic retired ocean liner, SS United States, has been deliberately sunk in the Gulf of Mexico, marking its final voyage to become the world's largest artificial reef, now resting 180 feet beneath the surface. The nearly 1,000-foot vessel, which famously shattered the trans-Atlantic speed record in 1952, will now serve as a vibrant deep-sea habitat for marine life and an intriguing site for divers. Susan Gibbs, whose grandfather designed the ship, expressed a mix of grief and gratitude for its new, environmentally beneficial purpose. The SS United States Conservancy plans to establish a museum in Florida to honor the ship's storied legacy.

2026-11-12 11:13:00 EST

Hungary's new government, under Prime Minister Peter Magyar, has announced significant reforms, including easing abortion rules and committing to ratify the Istanbul Convention on violence against women. The controversial "heartbeat regulation," which previously mandated women listen to fetal vital signs before an abortion, is set to be abolished. Additionally, emergency contraceptive pills will now be available without a prescription, improving access to reproductive healthcare. These changes represent a notable departure from the previous conservative policies and aim to enhance protections for women, criminalizing the misuse of intimate images and strengthening support for victims of domestic violence.
