Research Confirms 'Hang-xiety' as a Genuine Phenomenon Accompanying Hangovers

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

“Hang-xiety,” the pervasive feeling of dread and anxiety that often accompanies a hangover, is scientifically recognized as a genuine phenomenon. Experts explain that alcohol disrupts the brain's chemical messengers, GABA and glutamate, leading to an initial sense of relaxation but a subsequent rebound of heightened alertness and arousal as the alcohol metabolizes. Research, including a 2023 study of over 5,000 students, indicated that 18.3% reported hangover-induced anxiety, with shy individuals experiencing particularly elevated markers. Understanding this as a temporary physiological state, rather than proof of social catastrophe, can help individuals manage its power.
