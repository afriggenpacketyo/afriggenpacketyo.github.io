Senator Hawley Introduces Bill to Regulate AI-Powered Surveillance Cameras

2026-09-30 06:59:00 EDT

Senator Josh Hawley has introduced the 'Stop Flock Abuse Act,' legislation designed to establish guardrails for AI-powered surveillance technologies like Flock cameras. The bill aims to impose stricter limits on data retention, security, and search functions, prohibiting local governments from selling or sharing vehicle data and requiring data deletion within 10 days unless a criminal investigation is active. It also bans facial recognition technology within these networks and mandates written approval for searches, with audit logs to track activity. This move comes amid growing privacy concerns and a Senate Judiciary subcommittee hearing where Flock executives declined to testify, highlighting the urgent need for federal oversight of these widespread surveillance systems.
