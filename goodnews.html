BepiColombo Mission Successfully Places Twin Spacecraft in Mercury Orbit

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a joint European and Japanese endeavor, successfully placed two robotic spacecraft into orbit around Mercury, marking the planet's first visitor in over a decade. After an eight-year, 6-billion-mile journey through the inner solar system, the conjoined spacecraft will separate next month to map and study Mercury's dense interior, magnetic field, and surface features from different altitudes. This $2 billion mission, named after Italian mathematician Giuseppe "Bepi" Colombo, aims to unravel the mysteries of the sun's closest and least-explored planet. Scientists eagerly await new data in the spring, despite the extreme temperatures of over 800 degrees Fahrenheit posing significant technical challenges for the mission's electronics.
