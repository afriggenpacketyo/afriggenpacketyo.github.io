Historical Context: Mississippi's Previous Black US Senators Before Scott Colom's Victory

2026-11-12 09:12:55 EST

Scott Colom's recent victory makes him Mississippi's first Black US Senator in over 150 years and only the third in the state's history, a significant achievement in the deeply conservative state. This historic win follows the terms of Hiram Revels and Blanche Bruce, who served during the Reconstruction era. Revels was the first Black person in the US Senate, serving from 1870-1871 and advocating for racial equality. Bruce, born into slavery, became the first Black person to serve a full Senate term from 1875-1881. Their tenures preceded the oppressive Jim Crow laws, providing crucial historical context to Colom's modern-day electoral success.

Understanding Pet Insurance: Deductibles, Payouts, Waiting Periods, and Exclusions

Comparing options and understanding these details are vital for effective pet healthcare financial planning.
