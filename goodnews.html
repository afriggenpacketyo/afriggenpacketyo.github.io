European Space Agency Deploys Two Satellites for Enhanced Climate Monitoring

2026-09-15 03:28:00 EDT

The European Space Agency (ESA) has successfully launched two new satellites, Sentinel-3C and Flex, from French Guiana, aiming to significantly enhance climate change monitoring. The Flex satellite, a €350 million mission, will provide vital data on heatwave impacts by observing faint fluorescence from plants during photosynthesis, indicating vegetation health and environmental stress. Concurrently, Sentinel-3C will gather extensive marine environment data, such as temperature and ocean currents, while also monitoring land use to optimize yields and avert food crises. ESA officials emphasize these missions are essential for comprehending and tackling the pressing realities of climate change.

China Pioneers Extraction of Critical Rare Metals from Lithium Waste

2026-09-15 00:00:05 EDT

China has launched the world's first 500-tonne production line capable of continuously extracting rubidium and caesium from lithium waste. This demonstration line, located in Jiangxi province, has achieved stable production, according to the Institute of Process Engineering under the Chinese Academy of Sciences. Rubidium and caesium are critical rare metals essential for advanced technologies, including aerospace, quantum computing, biomedicine, and new energy. This breakthrough represents a significant advancement in resource recovery and sustainable practices for high-tech industries, potentially reducing reliance on primary mining for these vital elements.
