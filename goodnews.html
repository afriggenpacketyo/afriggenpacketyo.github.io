Investors Face Hidden Tax Complexities with Covered Call ETFs

2026-10-18 09:10:33 EDT

Covered call ETFs, popular for generating monthly cash flow, present hidden tax complexities that can surprise investors. While these funds hold equities and sell call options for income, their distributions can comprise dividends, capital gains, or non-dividend distributions (return of capital). Return of capital distributions, while tax-deferred, reduce an investment's cost basis, potentially increasing capital gains tax liability upon sale. Financial advisors recommend using tax-deferred accounts like traditional IRAs for these ETFs to mitigate immediate tax hits. Investors are urged to examine a fund's tax-cost ratio and net asset value growth to fully understand after-tax returns, as the income isn't "magic." (not investment advice)
