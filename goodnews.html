Archaeologists Claim to Have Solved Parthenon East Pediment Reconstruction Puzzle

2026-11-20 15:03:00 EST

Archaeologists believe they have successfully reconstructed the lost east pediment of the ancient Parthenon temple in Athens, which depicted the birth of Athena. After decades of research and utilizing modern technology like 3D scanners, Vinzenz Brinkmann and his team have pieced together surviving fragments and Roman copies of the sculptures. The exhibition 'Athena, the Stage is Yours: The Parthenon Puzzle' showcases this reconstruction. The project faced controversy regarding the loan of fragments, highlighting the ongoing dispute between Greece and Britain over the Parthenon sculptures held by the British Museum. The reconstruction provides a detailed visual of the pediment's original arrangement and artistry.

New York City Lawmakers Propose Ban on Boiling Lobsters Alive

2026-11-20 13:49:03 EST

New York City lawmakers are proposing legislation to ban restaurants from boiling lobsters, crabs, and other crustaceans alive. The proposed ban, sponsored by Bronx Democrat Oswald Feliz and Harvey Epstein, cites scientific studies indicating that crustaceans experience significant pain when boiled without prior stunning. Several European countries and the UK have already implemented similar bans. The legislation suggests methods like chilling lobsters in a freezer or packing them in ice before humane killing. Proponents argue that boiling live animals constitutes animal cruelty, questioning the notion that lobsters do not feel pain.
