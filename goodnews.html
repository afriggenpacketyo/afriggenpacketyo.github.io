2026-11-10 08:00:28 EST

Unlike traditional optical fibers where signals travel through solid glass at two-thirds the speed of light in a vacuum, this new design replaces the core with air, allowing for much faster light propagation. This innovation is particularly impactful for vast data exchanges between geographically distant computing centers, where current delays are substantial.
