Ukrainian and Sudanese Immigrants to Retain Deportation Protections in U.S.

2026-10-18 13:44:00 EDT

The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has announced that tens of thousands of Ukrainian and Sudanese immigrants enrolled in the Temporary Protected Status (TPS) policy will provisionally retain their deportation protections, despite their programs being officially slated to expire. This decision, while not yet a final determination, stands in stark contrast to the Trump administration's broader, aggressive stance against TPS, which has previously led to the revocation of protections for over a million immigrants from more than a dozen other countries. TPS, a humanitarian immigration program established by Congress in 1990, allows the U.S. government to offer temporary deportation relief and work permits to nationals from countries facing armed conflict, environmental disasters, or other severe crises. This extension provides a crucial period of continued stability for these vulnerable communities.
