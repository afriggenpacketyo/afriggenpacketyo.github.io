Evaluating Pet Insurance Premiums: When to Stay or Switch Insurers

2026-11-20 08:17:00 EST

Rising pet insurance premiums can prompt owners to reconsider their policies. However, switching insurers requires careful evaluation beyond just the cost. Pre-existing conditions are a major factor, as new policies may not cover them, potentially leading to higher out-of-pocket expenses if the pet has chronic health issues. It's crucial to compare not just premiums but also deductibles, reimbursement percentages, annual limits, and exclusions. Reviewing waiting periods and considering the pet's age and medical history are also vital. Before switching, owners should explore options with their current insurer to adjust coverage levels, as an established policy may offer benefits difficult to replace.
