Constructors overcame complex geological challenges, including high water pressure and alternating rock layers, using a specialized boring machine named Changjiang. LoRa Mesh Networks Offer Potential Solution to Government Internet Blackouts

2026-07-25 07:08:00 EDT

LoRa mesh networks are emerging as a potential solution to government-imposed internet blackouts, enabling decentralized communication even when traditional internet access is cut, as demonstrated in Ukraine. These networks use small, license-free radio modules to transmit encrypted data packets over several kilometers, with each device forwarding messages to the next, creating a self-repairing system. While limited to small messages and potentially vulnerable to physical tracking, LoRa mesh networks bypass firewalls and surveillance, offering a resilient communication channel for activists and citizens in authoritarian states. This technology provides a vital tool for information flow in crisis situations.
