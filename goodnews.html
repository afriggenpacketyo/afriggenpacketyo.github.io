New UK Planning Rules Aim to Protect Pubs from Conversion to Housing or Offices

2026-08-16 11:04:13 EDT

New government planning rules in England, effective Monday, will make it harder to convert pubs into housing or offices, offering increased protections for these community assets. Under the updated National Planning Policy Framework (NPPF), developers must now demonstrate no reasonable prospect of a pub business running, including providing proof of 12 months of marketing for sale. This aims to prevent owners from deliberately diminishing trade to justify closure, protecting historically successful establishments. While hospitality groups welcome the move, they emphasize that high taxes and employment costs remain the primary drivers of pub closures, despite a recent 20% business rates cut. The NPPF also includes default approval for homes near railway stations and changes to consultation processes for housing developments.
