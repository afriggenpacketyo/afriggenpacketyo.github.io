Colorado Wildlife Overpass Drastically Reduces Vehicle-Animal Collisions

2026-10-19 19:17:12 EDT

A new report reveals that Colorado's wildlife overpass system has achieved a remarkable 91% reduction in vehicle-animal collisions within 10 months of its completion. This overpass, the largest in the US, spans six lanes of Interstate-25 and connects 39,000 acres of habitat, facilitating safe passage for elk, bears, pronghorn, and even northern leopard frogs. The $15 million project, which also includes miles of fencing and underpasses, addresses a previous average of one collision per day in the corridor. Officials note its success in accommodating large game and smaller animals, with plans for more such crossings statewide, demonstrating a highly effective and cost-efficient solution for conservation and public safety.
