Property Guardianship Offers Discounted Rent in Vacant Buildings Amid Cost of Living Crisis

2026-07-30 19:05:41 EDT

Property guardianship is gaining traction in the UK as an alternative housing solution amid the ongoing cost of living crisis, allowing individuals to live in vacant residential or commercial buildings for significantly reduced rent. Guardians typically pay 30-50% less than private tenants, sometimes with bills included, in exchange for protecting properties from squatters, vandalism, and disrepair. However, guardians hold a "licence to occupy" rather than a traditional tenancy, which affords them substantially fewer rights than private renters, including the vulnerability of receiving just 28 days' notice for eviction and no deposit protection. While this arrangement offers affordable and often unique living experiences, it is inherently precarious, with some residents also facing challenges related to communal living and antisocial behavior.
