2026-08-28 15:45:51 EDT

A federal judge has temporarily blocked the U.S. Postal Service from enforcing its new requirements for mail voting in the upcoming November midterm elections. U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani's order, issued after a lawsuit from two dozen states and voting rights groups, found that states face irreparable harm and that Congress did not grant the Postal Service authority to regulate federal elections. The new rules, which include design requirements for mail ballot envelopes and an online portal for state election officials, were deemed likely unconstitutional. The Justice Department is appealing the decision, as the dispute over mail voting restrictions continues to escalate.
