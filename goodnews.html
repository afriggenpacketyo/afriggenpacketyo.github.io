Strategies to Combat Social Anxiety and Overcome Hidden Habits

2026-09-29 20:44:50 EDT

Experts offer strategies for managing social anxiety, a condition affecting a significant portion of the population, particularly students returning to university. Key advice includes stopping obsessive self-focus, which often involves constant self-monitoring during social interactions. Instead, individuals are encouraged to focus outwardly on conversations, others, or their surroundings. "Behavioral experiments" are recommended to test fears, such as speaking up in a meeting or group, to challenge negative assumptions about social outcomes. Dropping "safety behaviors" like avoiding eye contact or rehearsing sentences is also crucial. The article emphasizes that social anxiety is a recognizable condition that can be treated, often through cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), and highlights the importance of challenging negative thought patterns and gradually increasing social engagement.
