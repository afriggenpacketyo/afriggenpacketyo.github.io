Marriage Does Not Automatically Combine Existing Debts, But Financial Planning is Crucial

2026-08-13 12:29:36 EDT

Getting married generally does not automatically merge existing individual debts, such as credit card balances or personal loans, which typically remain the sole responsibility of the original borrower. However, if spouses jointly acquire debt, both individuals are usually responsible for repayment, with significant implications for credit if payments are missed. In community property states, debts incurred during marriage may be legally considered obligations of the marital community, even if only one spouse's name appears on the account. Couples with substantial debt should proactively understand applicable state laws and explore debt relief options like consolidation or settlement to effectively manage their shared financial future (not investment advice).
