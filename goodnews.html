Blood Biomarker Discovered for CTE Diagnosis in Living Patients

2026-11-13 23:00:33 EST

Scientists have identified a blood biomarker, eMTBR-tau243, that could enable the diagnosis of advanced chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) in living individuals. Currently, CTE, a degenerative brain condition caused by repeated head trauma common in contact sports, can only be diagnosed post-mortem by examining brain tissue. This protein fragment, elevated in Alzheimer's disease and other tauopathies, was found to be higher in autopsy-confirmed CTE cases and correlated with disease severity. While not yet validated for diagnosis, this discovery could facilitate monitoring at-risk individuals and drug trials, offering a significant step forward in addressing this major concern in science and sport, where CTE has affected numerous athletes.
