Wayfair and Other Retailers Fined Millions for Deceptive Online Sales Practices

2026-10-14 06:28:53 EDT

Online furniture giant Wayfair, along with Appliances Direct and Gold's Gym, have been collectively fined £6 million by the competition watchdog for employing illegal and misleading sales tactics. Wayfair received a £4.8 million fine for false time-limited offers, using phrases like "Here Today, Gone Tomorrow" for products that remained available. Appliances Direct was fined £1.2 million for similar deceptive practices and automatically opting customers into services, while Gold's Gym was penalized for unclear membership pricing.
