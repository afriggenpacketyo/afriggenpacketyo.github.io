China Develops High-Accuracy, Comfortable Brain-Reading Device for Day-Long Use

2026-11-08 21:00:33 EST

The innovative device incorporates gradient electrodes, integrated into a commercial electroencephalogram (EEG) cap, specifically designed to overcome the traditional trade-offs found in conventional uniformly soft or rigid electrodes.
