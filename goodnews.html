England to Extend Free 24-Hour Bus Travel for Disabled People

2026-08-17 20:32:51 EDT

Disabled people in England will soon benefit from free 24-hour bus travel starting in April, a significant expansion from the current time-restricted passes. This change, which will allow disabled individuals to use their passes at any time on weekdays, aims to remove barriers to employment and social participation for an estimated one million people. Prime Minister Andy Burnham, who previously implemented a similar policy in Greater Manchester, stated it was "simply not right" that disabled people faced travel restrictions and expressed pride in extending it nationwide. The disability charity Scope lauded the move as one that would "remove barriers to everyday life," though a Conservative Party spokesman questioned its funding.
