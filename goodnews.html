India's ISRO to Launch Satellite to Enhance Indigenous Navigation System

2026-10-30 02:59:00 EDT

India's space agency, ISRO, is set to launch the NVS-03 satellite to strengthen NavIC, the country's indigenous alternative to the US Global Positioning System (GPS). The satellite, the third in ISRO's next-generation navigation series, will be carried into orbit by the GSLV-F18 rocket from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre in Sriharikota. It features an indigenous atomic clock and adds the L1 signal, aiming to provide navigation coverage across India and 1,500 kilometers beyond its borders, reducing dependence on foreign systems despite past setbacks. This launch is a critical step in India's efforts to achieve greater self-reliance in space technology and strategic capabilities.
