Cambodia Achieves Elusive HIV Target, Becoming First in Asia-Pacific Region

2026-08-06 09:13:28 EDT

Cambodia has become the first country in the Asia-Pacific region to reach the U.N.'s ambitious 95-95-95 HIV targets, meaning 95% of HIV-positive people know their status, 95% are on medication, and 95% are virally suppressed. This milestone, achieved by only 11 countries globally, demonstrates Cambodia's success in combating the epidemic, which peaked in the late 1990s. Key factors include strong political will, free medication, and innovative community-led outreach programs targeting high-risk populations. Advocates like Sophal Seum and Dalish Prum, who are living with HIV, highlight the importance of acceptance and hope in their country's journey.
