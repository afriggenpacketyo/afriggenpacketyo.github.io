Living Donor Liver Transplants Underutilized Despite Potential to Save More Lives

2026-08-24 07:00:00 EDT

Living donor liver transplants, though highly effective and widely practiced globally, remain significantly underutilized in the U.S., accounting for only 6% of liver transplants compared to 24% for kidneys. The article highlights that while kidney donation has benefited from robust infrastructure like the National Kidney Registry, a comparable national system for livers is currently lacking, impeding widespread adoption. Key challenges include a scarcity of surgeons trained in these complex procedures, non-standardized processes across transplant centers, and insufficient financial support for living donors who incur travel and recovery expenses. Implementing new organ preservation technologies and universal donor protections could bridge this critical gap, potentially saving countless more lives.

London Zoo Conducts Annual Weigh-In for Animal Health Monitoring

2026-08-24 06:24:57 EDT

London Zoo is conducting its annual weigh-in, a meticulous process of checking and measuring every animal, from the world's largest rodents to the smallest katydids, to monitor their health and well-being. Zookeepers employ various methods, including coaxing animals onto scales with strategic snacks, to accurately record their individual measurements. This vital data is then systematically entered into the Zoological Information Management System (ZIMS), a comprehensive global database. ZIMS enables animal carers and conservationists worldwide to compare crucial information on thousands of endangered species, significantly contributing to broader conservation efforts and ensuring optimal care for the zoo's diverse inhabitants.
