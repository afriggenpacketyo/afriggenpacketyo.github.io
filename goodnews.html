Certificates of Deposit (CDs) Offer Low-Risk Savings with Guaranteed Returns

2026-07-27 13:09:46 EDT

Certificates of Deposit (CDs) are savings accounts that require funds to be deposited for a fixed term in exchange for interest, often higher than standard savings accounts. CDs offer a low-risk way to earn money with guaranteed fixed interest rates, protected by FDIC or NCUA insurance. They typically provide higher returns than checking or savings accounts and offer various term lengths to suit individual goals.
