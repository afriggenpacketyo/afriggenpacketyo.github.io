California Introduces New License Plate Sequence After Exhausting Old Combinations

2026-09-11 18:59:37 EDT

California has exhausted all combinations for its current license plate sequence, which began in 1980, necessitating a new configuration. Although production of the new plates has begun, over 110,000 plates from the old "0AAA000" format are still awaiting issuance. This change, expected to last 40 years, will unfold without fanfare as existing plates are distributed.
