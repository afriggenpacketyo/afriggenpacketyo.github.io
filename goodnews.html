Leonid Meteor Shower Set to Dazzle UK Skygazers This November

2026-11-06 21:03:28 EST

The Leonid meteor shower is set to illuminate the skies across the UK throughout November, with its peak anticipated on the night of November 17, potentially offering views of 10-15 meteors per hour. These exceptionally fast streaks of light originate from dust particles shed by Comet 55P/Tempel-Tuttle as it orbits the Sun. Experts recommend finding a safe, dark location far from light pollution and allowing eyes 20-30 minutes to adapt for optimal viewing. While clear skies are crucial, the forecast suggests better opportunities in southern and western England and Wales this weekend, with ideal viewing conditions expected around the peak due to the moon being below the horizon.
