Understanding Stroke Warning Signs: The BE FAST Acronym

2026-11-01 17:22:12 EST

Senator Chris Murphy's recent hospitalization following a stroke underscores the critical importance of recognizing the immediate warning signs, which are helpfully encapsulated by the BE FAST acronym. BE FAST stands for Balance loss, Eye changes, Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, and crucially, Time to call 911. Murphy, aged 53, promptly sought medical attention after experiencing numbness in his arm, exemplifying the "T" in BE FAST. Rapid medical intervention significantly increases the chances of survival and minimizes potential permanent brain damage, as strokes occur when blood flow to the brain is interrupted, depriving tissue of vital oxygen.
