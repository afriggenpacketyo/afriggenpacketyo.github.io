Understanding Homeowners Insurance Coverage for HVAC System Replacement

2026-07-30 12:42:58 EDT

Homeowners insurance typically covers HVAC replacement only if the damage results from a covered peril, such as fire, lightning, or a windstorm, rather than gradual wear and tear or mechanical failure. During periods of extreme heat, if an HVAC unit fails due to age or excessive use, insurance generally won't cover the cost. Neglected maintenance can also lead to denied claims. The article advises reviewing policy details and considering supplemental protections like home warranties or regular maintenance to mitigate costs, as insurers often only cover sudden, accidental damage.
