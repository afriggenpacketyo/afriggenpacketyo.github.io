Experts Explain How Lying is a Normal and Important Part of Children's Mental Development

2026-11-19 05:49:21 EST

Experts suggest that children telling fibs is a normal and even important part of their mental development, indicating the growth of sophisticated cognitive skills. Clinical psychologist Dr. Jade Redfern explains that deception, evident even before age one, demonstrates a child's developing "theory of mind"—the ability to understand others have separate thoughts and feelings. This skill is crucial for fostering empathy as they grow. Children also lie for self-preservation, driven by attachment and a desire to avoid upsetting caregivers. Parents can encourage honesty by creating a safe environment where children feel secure enough to tell the truth, fostering open and curious conversations about their actions.

England and Wales to Abolish Leasehold System, Introduce Commonhold for Flats

2026-11-19 04:26:59 EST

England and Wales are set to introduce new laws to scrap the controversial leasehold system for flats, replacing it with commonhold where residents collectively own and manage their buildings. Housing Minister Matthew Pennycook described the current system as "blighting lives" and an "anachronism." The proposed Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill will also cap ground rents at £250 annually from 2028, eventually reducing them to a "peppercorn" rate after 40 years. This reform aims to align England and Wales with other countries globally that already utilize shared ownership models, addressing long-standing complaints from leaseholders.
