Scientists Discover Blood Biomarker for CTE Diagnosis in Living Patients

2026-11-13 23:00:33 EST

Scientists at Washington University School of Medicine have made a significant breakthrough by identifying a blood biomarker, eMTBR-tau243, that could enable the diagnosis of advanced chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) in living patients. Currently, CTE, a degenerative brain condition caused by repeated head trauma common in contact sports, can only be confirmed post-mortem. While the test accurately distinguished advanced CTE from healthy individuals, it could not reliably identify early-stage cases. If validated in larger studies, this discovery could revolutionize monitoring for at-risk individuals and facilitate drug trials, offering new hope for managing a condition that has impacted numerous athletes, including many NFL players.
