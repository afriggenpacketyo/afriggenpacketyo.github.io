German Court Rules Suno AI Violated Copyrights by Training on Music Without Licenses

2026-07-31 07:41:00 EDT

A German court has ruled that Suno AI, a US-based company generating songs from text prompts, violated copyrights by training its AI models on protected music without proper licenses. Initiated by Germany's licensing agency GEMA, this verdict is considered globally significant, as it is one of the first major cases to test copyright law in the context of AI music. Suno is now required to pay damages and disclose illicit revenues, though it intends to appeal. GEMA emphasized that AI systems, unlike human brains, commercially exploit vast amounts of music data without fair compensation for creators.
