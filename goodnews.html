Bare Footprints Lead to Conviction in 1993 Jeanie Childs Murder Case

2026-08-02 01:05:02 EDT

The decades-old murder of 35-year-old Jeanie Childs in Minneapolis, who was stabbed over 60 times in 1993, was ultimately resolved by a critical piece of evidence: bloody bare footprints found at the crime scene. Investigators, initially facing a cold case, leveraged advancements in DNA technology and investigative genetic genealogy in 2015 to identify Jerry Westrom. Despite Westrom's consistent denials and a lack of prior violent criminal history, forensic experts confirmed his DNA at the scene and, most significantly, that the unique friction ridge patterns from the footprints belonged to him. Westrom was convicted of first-degree murder in 2022 and sentenced to life in prison.
