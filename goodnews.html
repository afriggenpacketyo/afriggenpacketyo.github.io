FDA Advisory Committee to Vote on First Multi-Cancer Blood Test, Galleri

2026-09-23 06:00:00 EDT

An FDA advisory committee is set to vote on whether to recommend approval for Grail's Galleri blood test, the first multi-cancer screening test of its kind. The test claims to detect up to 50 forms of cancer, many of which lack routine screenings. El Nino Significantly Impacts Hurricane and Tropical Storm Activity in Atlantic

2026-09-23 05:26:00 EDT

The current strong El Nino weather pattern is significantly influencing hurricane and tropical storm activity, leading to an unusually quiet Atlantic season. As of late September, only six tropical storms had formed, with none intensifying into hurricanes, a first since 1966. El Nino's impact involves weakening trade winds and shifting jet streams, which disrupt storm formation in the Atlantic through increased wind shear. Conversely, the eastern and central Pacific Ocean experience increased cyclone activity due to warmer waters and less wind shear, as seen with Hurricane Polo. Meteorologists warn that despite the calm, a major hurricane in the Atlantic is still possible before the season concludes.
