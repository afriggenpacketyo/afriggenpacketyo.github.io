Understanding the Transferability of Debt Accounts Between Collection Agencies

2026-07-28 11:12:52 EDT

Debt collectors can legally sell delinquent accounts to other collection agencies or debt buyers, meaning the same debt can change hands multiple times. This occurs when the collection agency owns the debt outright, rather than working on commission for the original creditor. While the legal obligation to repay remains, consumers retain rights under the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act, including validating the debt and protection against deceptive practices. If debt changes hands, it's crucial to verify the new collector's authority and understand repayment options, as it may present opportunities for negotiation. (not investment advice)
