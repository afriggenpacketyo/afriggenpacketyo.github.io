Finland Unveils World's Largest Sand Battery to Tackle Renewable Energy Intermittency

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in southern Finland has deployed the world's largest commercial sand battery, a 13-meter-tall facility using 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone, to store 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy. This innovative solution, developed by Polar Night Energy, aims to provide combustion-free heating for the majority of the year, significantly reducing greenhouse gas emissions and reliance on wood chips. The technology addresses renewable energy's intermittency by heating sand with excess wind or solar power when electricity is cheap, then discharging it as heat for district networks when needed. This project demonstrates a promising, material-efficient approach to long-duration energy storage without requiring rare earth materials.
