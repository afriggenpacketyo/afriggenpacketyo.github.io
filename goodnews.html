TSA Program Allows Non-Travelers Access to Airport Security and Gates

2026-09-08 01:13:03 EDT

The Transportation Security Administration (TSA) has launched a new program called Gateside, which permits eligible TSA PreCheck members without plane tickets to pass through airport security. This initiative allows individuals to meet or see off friends and family at their gates, connect with someone during a layover, or visit airport dining and retail establishments. Currently operational at 13 U.S. airports, Gateside aims to offer a new way for non-travelers to access gate areas, which have been heavily restricted since the 9/11 attacks.
