Understanding why caffeine affects individuals differently

2026-09-16 20:09:43 EDT

The varying effects of caffeine on individuals stem from a complex interplay of genetics, metabolism, lifestyle, and tolerance. Caffeine's half-life, the time it takes for the body to eliminate half of it, can range from six to 20 hours depending on individual factors. Smoking can significantly speed up caffeine metabolism, while pregnancy and certain medications can prolong its presence in the body. Regular consumption leads to tolerance, diminishing caffeine's perceived effects. Sleep experts recommend avoiding caffeine several hours before bedtime to prevent sleep disruption. Ultimately, individual sensitivity and lifestyle choices dictate how caffeine impacts alertness and sleep patterns.
