2026-11-28 08:00:00 EST

The ceremonial keel for the aircraft carrier USS Doris Miller is scheduled to be laid next week, a significant event honoring a true Pearl Harbor hero whose extraordinary personal courage transcended the racial segregation of his era. Doris Miller, a young Black mess attendant in the then-segregated U.S. Navy, displayed immense bravery during the December 7, 1941, attack, moving wounded sailors to safety and manning an anti-aircraft gun despite having no combat training. He was subsequently awarded the Navy Cross for his actions, a commendation personally presented by the commander of the U.S. Pacific Fleet. Miller tragically died in service two years later, and naming an aircraft carrier after him serves as a powerful memorial to his heroism, reminding everyone that courage is an inherent quality not defined by rank or race.
