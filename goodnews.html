The US initially adopted daylight saving during World War I to conserve fuel, with its implementation undergoing various changes over the decades. Although legislation known as the Sunshine Protection Act, aimed at making daylight saving time permanent, passed the House in July, it still faces significant hurdles in the Senate before the current congressional session ends in January.
