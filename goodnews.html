Child Development Experts Explain the Importance of Lying in Mental Growth

2026-11-18 19:12:57 EST

Children's fibbing, such as blaming pets for drawings on walls or missing cookies, is a common and important aspect of their mental development, according to clinical psychologists. Experts explain that learning to deceive demonstrates the emergence of a "theory of mind," which is crucial for developing empathy and understanding that others possess distinct thoughts and feelings. Studies indicate that even before age one, children show signs of deception, progressing to more sophisticated forms by age three, including withholding information.
