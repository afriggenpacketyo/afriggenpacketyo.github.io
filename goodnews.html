UN Advocates for New World Map to Correct Distortions of Continent Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has advocated for replacing the widely used Mercator world map with the Equal Earth projection to provide a more accurate representation of continent sizes. The Mercator map, developed in 1569 primarily for maritime navigation, significantly distorts landmasses, notably depicting Africa as similar in size to Greenland when it is, in reality, 14 times larger. Critics contend that this long-standing distortion carries psychological implications, subtly diminishing the perceived importance of regions like Africa. While the Equal Earth map rectifies these area inaccuracies, it compromises the straight lines essential for compass navigation, underscoring the inherent challenge that no two-dimensional map can perfectly preserve all properties of a three-dimensional globe.
