2026-08-25 01:52:00 EDT

Australia's official music charts, managed by the Australian Recording Industry Association (ARIA), will no longer qualify fully AI-generated tracks under new rules effective August 31. This distinction separates music created predominantly by artificial intelligence from recordings where AI is used as a supplementary creative tool. Music that is substantially human-made and does not involve stream manipulation will remain eligible. ARIA's decision aligns with international principles aimed at preserving human artistry while acknowledging the evolving use of AI in music creation. The rules allow ARIA to remove ineligible recordings or adjust chart positions.
