2026-08-08 15:48:58 EDT

A tiny, nocturnal greenish frog, nicknamed the "coffee frog," has been identified as a new species endemic to Costa Rica, thriving in coffee plantations where it was previously misidentified. Measuring less than 4 centimeters, males of this "spring frog" species possess a distinctive translucent throat that inflates with their high-pitched calls. While these amphibians find shade, moisture, and insects within the coffee crops, their habitat is increasingly threatened by the agrochemicals used in farming. Researchers warn that pesticides can seep into aquatic systems where tadpoles develop, posing a significant risk to the species, alongside other threats like waste dumping and habitat loss.
