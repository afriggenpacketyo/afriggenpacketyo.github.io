Mesh Networks Offer Potential Solution to State-Imposed Internet Blackouts

2026-07-25 07:08:00 EDT

In response to governments using internet shutdowns as a weapon against dissent, "LoRa mesh networks" are emerging as a potential solution for communication in crisis zones. These networks use small, radio-powered devices to transmit encrypted data packets over several kilometers, creating a self-repairing system without a central node that can be shut down. While limited to smaller messages, these networks bypass firewalls and surveillance, offering a secure communication channel. Demonstrated successfully in Ukraine, this technology provides a vital tool for activists in authoritarian states, although risks like transmitter tracking and legal consequences for unauthorized equipment remain.
