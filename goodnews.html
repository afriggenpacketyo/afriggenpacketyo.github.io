UN Endorses New Map Projection for More Accurate Representation of Africa's Size

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to provide a more accurate representation of continental sizes, especially Africa. This resolution, spearheaded by Togo and supported by the African Union, encourages global governments and institutions to adopt this projection over the traditional Mercator map. The Mercator projection, commonly used since the 16th century, is criticized for distorting landmasses, making regions like Greenland appear similar in size to Africa, when Africa is actually 14 times larger. This article highlights a significant step towards correcting geographical misrepresentations and shaping a more accurate global understanding.
