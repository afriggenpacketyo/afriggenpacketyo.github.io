UK Reports Significant Drop in English Channel Small Boat Crossings, Citing Law Enforcement Efforts

2026-09-15 05:00:14 EDT

The number of individuals crossing the English Channel in small boats from France has seen a significant decrease, dropping by 43% to 17,281 in the first eight months of 2026 compared to the previous year. Law enforcement efforts are identified as a primary contributor to this reduction in crossings, which have become the most common method for detected illegal entry into the UK since 2020. While nearly all arrivals claim asylum, the average number of people per boat has more than doubled since 2021, intensifying safety risks, with at least 84 fatalities reported in 2024. Despite these numbers, small boat arrivals constitute only about 5% of total UK immigration.
