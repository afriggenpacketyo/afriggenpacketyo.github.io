Experts Explore Reasons Behind Motion Sickness in Electric Vehicles

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

Some passengers are reporting increased motion sickness when traveling in electric vehicles (EVs), a phenomenon experts attribute to specific engineering differences that disrupt sensory cues. Unlike traditional combustion engine cars, EVs lack the familiar auditory cues and vibrations from an engine that signal acceleration, creating a sensory mismatch that can trigger nausea. Regenerative braking, a process where the car slows down to recharge its battery when the accelerator is released, is another significant contributing factor, as it can induce sudden, jerky decelerations and a rocking sensation. As EV adoption rapidly grows globally, this issue is gaining attention, prompting researchers and manufacturers to explore solutions like smoother acceleration software, subtle seat vibrations, and adaptive braking to enhance overall passenger comfort.
