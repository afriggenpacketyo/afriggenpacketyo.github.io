Understanding the Taxability of Life Insurance Proceeds for Beneficiaries

2026-11-04 12:18:28 EST

Life insurance payouts are generally not taxable income for beneficiaries, but certain scenarios can trigger tax obligations. These include receiving payouts in installments where interest accrues, making cash withdrawals exceeding premiums paid, or surrendering/selling a policy for more than its cost. Additionally, employer-provided coverage over $50,000 and interest earned on policies can be taxable. Estate taxes may apply if proceeds go to an estate exceeding a $15 million valuation. Consulting a tax professional is advised to navigate these complexities and optimize tax outcomes (not investment advice).
