Ancient Bronze Sword with Sharp Edge Discovered in Italian Lake

2026-11-18 08:00:58 EST

Italian officials have announced the remarkable discovery of a 3,300-year-old bronze sword in Lake Mezzano, a volcanic lake in central Italy's Lazio region. Dating back to the 13th century B.C., this artifact was unearthed during excavations of a submerged Bronze Age village and is exceptionally well-preserved, astonishingly retaining a sharp edge after millennia. This discovery marks the third sword recovered from the Mezzano site since the 1970s, underscoring the area's profound archaeological significance. Alongside other items such as axes and arrowheads, these objects are believed to have been intentionally deposited as part of ancient ritualistic or cultic practices, offering unique insights into protohistoric communities.
