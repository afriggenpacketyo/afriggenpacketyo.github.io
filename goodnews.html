BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Orbit Around Mercury

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a collaborative European and Japanese endeavor, successfully deployed a pair of robotic spacecraft into orbit around Mercury on Saturday, concluding an eight-year cruise through the inner solar system. This achievement marks Mercury's first visitor in over a decade, with the linked explorers slated to separate next month to independently map and study the scorching planet from varying altitudes. The $2 billion mission, named after Italian mathematician Giuseppe "Bepi" Colombo, aims to unravel mysteries concerning Mercury's dense interior, exterior magnetic field, and diverse surface features like craters and volcanic plains. Despite the planet's extreme temperatures posing significant technical challenges, the mission plans to operate for at least one Earth year, providing unprecedented comparative measurements.
