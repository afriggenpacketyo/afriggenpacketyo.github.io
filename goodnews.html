2026-09-11 18:59:37 EDT

California is changing its license plate sequence for the first time in 46 years due to running out of available combinations. The current system, which began in 1980 with a '9-series' format, has been exhausted. The new sequence will feature three numerals, followed by three letters, and then one numeral, such as '000AAA0.' While the new plates have begun production, they have not yet been issued to drivers, as a substantial number of the current '0-series' plates remain to be distributed. The California Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) anticipates the new configuration will last approximately 40 years. The state's license plates are manufactured by inmates at Folsom State Prison. This change signifies a practical necessity driven by the state's vehicle registration numbers.
