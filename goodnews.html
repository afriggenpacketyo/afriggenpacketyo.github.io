Chinese Scientists Reconstruct Face of Ancient Denisovan Human Species

2026-10-23 07:09:08 EDT

Chinese scientists have achieved a significant breakthrough by reconstructing the face of the Denisovans, an enigmatic species of ancient humans. Previously, knowledge of Denisovans was limited to DNA evidence and fragmented fossils, indicating their presence in parts of Siberia and East/Southeast Asia millennia ago. This intricate reconstruction was based on fossil remains unearthed from the Xujiayao archaeological site in northern China's Nihewan Basin during excavations conducted between 1976 and 1979. The scientific work confirmed the remains' Denisovan lineage, providing humanity with its first visual understanding of this ancient hominid. This remarkable achievement marks a new chapter in understanding human evolutionary history.
