UK Trial to Utilize AI for Reducing Climate-Warming Contrails from Aircraft

2026-08-18 05:26:41 EDT

A £5 million UK trial, "Operation Blue Skies," is set to test whether artificial intelligence can help aircraft avoid forming climate-warming contrails over the North Atlantic. Contrails, or vapor trails, are icy clouds from aircraft exhausts that, in certain conditions, persist and spread, trapping heat and contributing significantly to aviation's climate impact. Google, the UK government, Met Office, and NATS are collaborating to use satellite images and flight data combined with weather patterns to forecast contrail formation zones. Air traffic controllers will then direct pilots to adjust altitude, typically by around 2,000ft, a minor change for passengers, aiming to reduce this environmental effect during selected winter periods.
