Ancient Martian Meteorite Discovered in Sahara Offers Glimpse into Early Mars

2026-10-30 11:09:15 EDT

A rose-colored meteorite, named Teghaza 001, discovered in the Sahara Desert in 2022, has been identified by NASA as a 4.1-billion-year-old fragment of Mars. This nearly two-pound rock is one of the three most ancient Martian meteorites found on Earth, providing a rare look into the planet's crust formation. Scientists used a pioneering age-dating method to analyze its unique volcanic minerals, including zircon crystals, which match samples from Mars' southern highlands. The analysis revealed that the rock was altered by Martian water around 4.1 billion years ago, suggesting a more dynamic early water cycle than previously understood.
