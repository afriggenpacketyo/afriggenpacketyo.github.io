This joint mission between the European Space Agency (ESA) and the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA) aims to unravel Mercury's mysteries, such as the presence of ice in its craters and the formation of its unique surface hollows. The spacecraft had to employ a complex trajectory, including multiple flybys of Venus and Mercury, to counteract the Sun's gravity and achieve orbit.
