BepiColombo Spacecraft Reaches Mercury Orbit Despite Glitch

2026-11-24 12:34:32 EST

The BepiColombo spacecraft, a joint mission by the European Space Agency and Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, successfully entered Mercury's orbit after an eight-year journey, overcoming a technical glitch. The maneuver was interrupted by a software issue, causing the spacecraft to enter safe mode and complete only 77% of its planned burn. Despite this, ESA scientists confirmed the craft had locked onto orbit after six hours of analysis. BepiColombo will now gradually adjust its orbit over weeks, aiming for an "optimum point" before releasing two science orbiters in December to study Mercury's interior, surface, and surrounding space.
