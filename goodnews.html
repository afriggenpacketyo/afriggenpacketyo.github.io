Appeals court rejects Trump administration's request in California voting case

2026-10-26 22:35:55 EDT

A 9th Circuit Court of Appeals panel has denied the Justice Department's request for an immediate order to segregate ballots in California potentially cast by non-citizens. The court ruled 2-1 that it lacked jurisdiction at this stage, sending the case back to the district court. Judges expressed skepticism about the government's timeline and the constitutionality of the request, citing the state's authority over election administration. The Trump administration had sought to set aside approximately 20,000 mail-in ballots.
