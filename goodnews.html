Australia's Official Music Charts Ban Wholly AI-Generated Tracks

2026-08-25 01:52:00 EDT

Australia's official music charts, overseen by the Australian Recording Industry Association (ARIA), have implemented new rules banning wholly artificial intelligence-generated music from eligibility, effective August 31. The new guidelines stipulate that recordings must be "substantially human-made" to qualify, distinguishing them from AI-assisted music where AI serves as a supporting creative tool. ARIA Chief Executive Annabelle Herd stated the move aims to promote human artistry and prevent manipulation of chart positions. Ineligible tracks face refusal, removal, or even revocation of awards, aligning with international industry principles.
