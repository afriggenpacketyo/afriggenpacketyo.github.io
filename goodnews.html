England and Wales to Abolish Leasehold System for Flats

2026-11-18 21:02:23 EST

The government in England and Wales is set to eliminate the leasehold system for most new flats, replacing it with commonhold where residents collectively own and manage their buildings. Housing Minister Matthew Pennycook described the current leasehold system as "blighting lives" and an "anachronism." This move aligns with Labour's manifesto to end the "feudal leasehold system." While freeholders express concerns about detrimental impacts on investment markets, the proposed legislation also includes capping ground rents. Many international models already utilize shared ownership and collective management systems, similar to commonhold. This reform aims to bring the property ownership system in England and Wales more in line with global standards, addressing long-standing complaints from leaseholders. The change is expected to offer greater security and control to homeowners.
