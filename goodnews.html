Leonid Meteor Shower Expected to Dazzle UK Skygazers Through November

2026-11-06 21:03:28 EST

The Leonid meteor shower is set to be visible across the UK throughout November, with its peak expected on the night of November 17. Skygazers could potentially spot 10-15 meteors per hour, which are dust particles from Comet 55P/Tempel-Tuttle entering Earth's atmosphere at speeds up to 44 miles per second, making them the fastest meteors. Experts advise finding a safe, dark location away from light pollution and allowing eyes 20-30 minutes to adjust for the best viewing experience, focusing on the constellation Leo. While early weekend forecasts suggest cloudy conditions, clearer skies are anticipated by Sunday night, offering better opportunities to witness this celestial spectacle, especially around its peak.
