Understanding Health Anxiety: Recognizing Symptoms and Coping Strategies

2026-10-23 07:14:58 EDT

Health anxiety, characterized by persistent worry about serious illness, can significantly impact an individual's life, leading to frequent medical visits and distress. Symptoms include excessive body checking, constant online symptom research, and reassurance-seeking from others. This condition can cause individuals to disconnect from social life and strain relationships. Clinical psychologists recommend challenging worst-case thinking, limiting reassurance-seeking, engaging in physical activity, and practicing grounding techniques and breathing exercises to manage health anxiety. Seeking professional help is crucial for individuals experiencing these symptoms.
