NASA's Swift Observatory Expected to Re-enter Earth's Atmosphere

2026-11-02 14:00:07 EST

NASA's aging Swift Observatory, a 1.5-ton space telescope, is projected to plunge back to Earth on Wednesday after a failed rescue effort left it in a decaying orbit. Most of the spacecraft is expected to burn up upon re-entry, but some dense tungsten and mirror components could survive, with the U.S. Space Force actively tracking its trajectory. NASA assesses the risk of harm to anyone on Earth as unlikely, approximately 1 in 2,100, anticipating any surviving wreckage will most likely fall into the ocean. The mission, launched in 2004, is being commemorated by its team for 22 years of groundbreaking observations of gamma-ray bursts and supernovas.
