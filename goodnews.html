Germany's Former "Death Strip" Transforms into Biodiversity Oasis

2026-07-31 08:33:00 EDT

Germany's former "death strip," the nearly 1,400-kilometer border zone that once divided East and West Germany, has remarkably transformed into a vibrant "green belt" and a crucial biodiversity oasis. Decades of military isolation allowed approximately 7,500 species, including many endangered insects, spiders, otters, and wildcats, to flourish, with 88% of the area now under protection. This ecological success is complemented by other innovative green initiatives across Germany, such as a citizen-science project for mapping urban trees and the repurposing of washed-up seagrass into sustainable building insulation on Poel island. These efforts showcase diverse and impactful approaches to environmental conservation.
