Port of Los Angeles achieves record for busiest month and quarter

2026-10-12 17:21:11 EDT

The Port of Los Angeles has set a new record for its busiest month ever, moving over 1 million 20-foot-equivalent container units in September. This achievement caps off the port's most successful quarter in its history, from July to September. The surge in cargo is attributed to lower tariffs, increased retailer preparations for the upcoming holiday season, and security and environmental challenges affecting the Suez and Panama canals, which are redirecting shipments to Southern California. The port's executive director, Gene Seroka, highlighted that Los Angeles remains the "fastest and most reliable route from Asia to markets across the United States." The September volume also marks the highest ever for any port in the Western Hemisphere. While imports have significantly increased, loaded exports remain softer, reflecting challenges for American farmers and manufacturers. This article underscores the port's critical role in national and global trade.
