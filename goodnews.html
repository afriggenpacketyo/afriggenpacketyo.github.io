The declaration, made on the central European country's national day, transitions the order from male primogeniture to absolute primogeniture. This means that, in the future, the first-born child, regardless of sex, will become the heir to the throne. The change will apply to the descendants of Hereditary Prince Alois and his wife, Hereditary Princess Sophie, aiming to ensure the principality is led by the most prepared individual. This move reflects a significant modernization for Liechtenstein, where the ruler holds substantial political authority.
