2026-10-22 16:49:12 EDT

This decision comes in the wake of a tragic incident where an 18-year-old tourist died after falling from a bolting horse, alongside the recent death of a carriage horse named Deniz. While animal rights organizations and many city residents applauded the ban, the approximately 200 carriage drivers voiced profound disappointment, vehemently denying allegations of animal mistreatment and expressing deep anxieties about their future employment and the well-being of their horses. Mayor Zohran Mamdani has pledged to support a transition plan for the affected workers.
