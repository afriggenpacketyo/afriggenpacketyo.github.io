Finland Pioneers Renewable Energy Storage with World's Largest Commercial Sand Battery

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in Finland has deployed the world's largest commercial sand battery, a 13-meter-tall facility storing 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy using 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone. This innovative technology addresses the intermittency of renewable energy by storing heat for days or weeks, providing combustion-free heating for much of the year and significantly cutting greenhouse gas emissions. The sand battery allows electricity procurement to be separated from heating production, utilizing low spot prices for energy. This project demonstrates a promising, rare-earth-material-free solution for energy storage that could be replicated globally.
