World's oldest amber discovered in ancient Chinese coal seam

2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

Chinese paleontologists have unearthed the world's oldest known amber, dating back approximately 393 to 383 million years to the Middle Devonian period. The microscopic amber pieces were discovered preserved within an ancient coal seam in Xinjiang. This finding significantly predates previously known amber, which dated to the Late Carboniferous period, around 320 million years ago. The discovery was detailed in the journal Science Advances and challenges existing timelines for the formation and preservation of amber.
