Expiration of Debt Judgments: What Borrowers Need to Know

2026-08-31 10:29:58 EDT

When a debt judgment expires, creditors generally lose their ability to pursue collection through legal means, such as wage garnishment or bank levies, unless the judgment has been renewed. The duration of a judgment's enforceability varies by state, with some lasting several years and others a decade or more, and renewal processes exist. It's important for borrowers to understand that expiration doesn't automatically erase the debt itself; the underlying obligation may still exist. Proactively checking court records and state laws is recommended to clarify a judgment's status, and considering debt relief options before expiration can be a strategic move to avoid further costs and collection attempts.
