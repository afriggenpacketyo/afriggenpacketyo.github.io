New 'Sponge in the Gullet' Test Recommended for Oesophageal Cancer Screening

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

New draft guidelines for NHS England propose expanding the availability of quick "sponge in the gullet" tests for individuals with persistent indigestion, to screen for oesophageal cancer. These minimally invasive tests, performable in GP surgeries, offer a more accessible alternative to traditional hospital endoscopies, which use a camera. The initiative aims to improve early detection of Barrett's oesophagus, a pre-cancerous condition, particularly given the high demand for endoscopies that leaves many at-risk patients underscreened. The test involves swallowing a capsule that deploys a sponge to collect cells from the gullet lining for analysis, promising to identify concerning changes sooner and potentially freeing up resources for earlier cancer diagnoses.
