SpaceX Successfully Launches Cargo Dragon to International Space Station

2026-10-13 11:53:00 EDT

SpaceX successfully launched an unpiloted Dragon cargo ship into orbit on Tuesday aboard a Falcon 9 rocket, destined for a rendezvous with the International Space Station (ISS) on Thursday. The mission is delivering over 6,300 pounds of vital supplies, including research materials, spare parts, and crew provisions, to the orbiting laboratory. Notably, the cargo includes the final two rollout solar array blankets (IROSA), completing a major upgrade to boost the station's power output. The spectacular pre-dawn launch from Cape Canaveral featured the Falcon 9's first stage executing a flawless return-to-launch-site landing, while its second stage propelled the Dragon into orbit, offering stunning views of the exhaust plume backlit by the rising sun.
