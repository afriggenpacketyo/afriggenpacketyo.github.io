Compiled by Neo-Confucian scholar Zhang Huang, this extensive work synthesized over 200 printed sources across decades to comprehensively document concepts of heaven, Earth, and humanity. Information designer Paul Kahn emphasized that Huang's ambitious goal was to gather all essential illustrations and texts for an educated person's complete comprehension of the world. Its eventual carving on woodblocks and printing after Zhang's death underscores its enduring historical and cultural significance.
