England and Wales to Abolish 'Feudal' Leasehold System for New Flats

2026-11-18 21:02:23 EST

England and Wales are set to introduce new laws to abolish the leasehold system for most new flats, replacing it with commonhold, where residents collectively own and manage their buildings. Housing minister Matthew Pennycook described the current system as "blighting lives" and an "anachronism." The proposed Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill will also cap ground rent for existing leaseholders at £250, eventually reducing it to a "peppercorn" rate after 40 years. While freeholders argue the policy is "unjust" and will impact investment, the reforms aim to align England and Wales with international models and offer greater rights and protections to homeowners.
