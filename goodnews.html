UN Calls for New World Map to Address Distortions in Continent Representation

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has passed a "Correct the Map" resolution, advocating for the replacement of the widely used Mercator map with a projection that more accurately reflects continent sizes. The Mercator map, designed in 1569 for navigation, distorts landmasses, making continents near the poles appear larger than they are, and those near the equator smaller. Critics argue this has psychological and political implications, implicitly downplaying regions like Africa. The newly adopted Equal Earth projection, while better representing true sizes, sacrifices the straight lines essential for navigation.
