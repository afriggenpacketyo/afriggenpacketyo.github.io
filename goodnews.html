Understanding Why VPN Usage Often Triggers Enhanced Online Security Checks

2026-10-20 15:00:34 EDT

Using a Virtual Private Network (VPN) frequently triggers additional security checks on websites for services like banking or online shopping, which can be a source of frustration for users. This phenomenon occurs because VPNs alter your visible IP address, causing your login location to appear inconsistent or shared among numerous users, thereby activating fraud detection protocols. The article clarifies that while VPNs do not interfere with standard authentication methods such as passkeys, their use of dynamic or shared IP addresses often raises red flags for security systems that monitor typical login patterns.
