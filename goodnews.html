BepiColombo Spacecraft Overcomes Glitch to Successfully Orbit Mercury After Eight-Year Journey

2026-11-24 12:34:32 EST

The BepiColombo spacecraft, a collaborative mission between the European Space Agency and Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, successfully entered Mercury's orbit after an eight-year journey, despite experiencing a technical glitch. The orbital insertion maneuver was interrupted by a software issue, causing the spacecraft to enter safe mode and complete only 77% of its intended thruster burn. Nevertheless, mission control eventually confirmed BepiColombo had achieved orbit, necessitating subsequent small thruster adjustments to optimize its position. In December, the spacecraft will deploy two science orbiters, with the European Mercury Planetary Orbiter slated to commence scientific operations by April 2027, focusing on studying Mercury's interior, surface, and surrounding environment.
