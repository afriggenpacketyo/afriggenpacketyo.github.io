Debt collectors generally cannot add new fees after buying debt, experts state

2026-07-30 10:34:19 EDT

Debt collectors typically cannot add new fees to a balance simply because they have purchased the debt. Federal law generally prohibits them from collecting amounts not authorized by the original contract or state law. While interest may accrue if permitted by the original agreement, debt buyers cannot invent new charges like administrative or processing fees. Borrowers are advised to request a breakdown of their balance to ensure all charges are legally valid and to explore debt relief options if the balance has increased unexpectedly.
