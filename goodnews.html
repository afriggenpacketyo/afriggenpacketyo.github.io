Explaining England's 9-1 GCSE Numerical Grading System

2026-08-20 01:33:02 EDT

GCSE results day in England draws attention to the 9-1 numerical grading system, which replaced the traditional A-to-U letter grades as part of a 2014 curriculum reform. Under this system, 9 is the highest grade, 1 is the lowest, and a grade of 4 is considered a standard pass. Notably, the top three numerical grades—9, 8, and 7—encompass the previous A* and A grades, while a 4 aligns with the former C grade. This updated scheme was designed to offer clearer differentiation among high-attaining students and to distinguish the more challenging new GCSE qualifications from their predecessors. Students who do not achieve at least a grade 4 in English or maths are required to continue their studies in those subjects.
