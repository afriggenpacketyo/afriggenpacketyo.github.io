Understanding Debt Collection: Can Your Account Be Sold to Another Agency?

2026-07-28 11:12:52 EDT

Debt collectors can legally sell delinquent accounts to other collection agencies or debt buyers, meaning the company contacting you for payment may change multiple times over several years. This typically occurs when the collection agency owns the debt outright, rather than merely working on commission for the original creditor. If your debt changes hands, your legal obligations generally remain, but the new owner must be able to demonstrate their legal right to collect. Borrowers are advised to request written validation of the debt, confirm the company's legal authority, and compare account information with your own records. This process can sometimes create opportunities for negotiating more favorable settlement terms, as debt buyers often acquire accounts for less than face value. (not investment advice)
