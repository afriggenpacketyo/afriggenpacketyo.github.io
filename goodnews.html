Rare 10,000-Year-Old Mastodon Tooth Discovered in California Preserve

2026-09-12 16:34:58 EDT

California geomorphologist Brigid Lynch made a significant discovery, unearthing a remarkably preserved 10,000-year-old adult Pacific mastodon molar at a San Mateo county preserve. Paleontologists have lauded the find as the most complete and spectacular mastodon tooth from the area, providing invaluable insight into the Ice Age era. Pacific mastodons, ancient relatives of modern elephants, became extinct over 10,000 years ago, primarily due to the combined pressures of climate change impacts on their habitat and human hunting. The fossilized tooth has been donated to Stanford University, where carbon dating will help determine its precise age, contributing to a deeper understanding of past biodiversity.
