2026-09-20 02:32:00 EDT

The United Nations has endorsed the "Correct the Map" resolution, spearheaded by African countries, to promote equal-area map projections that more accurately reflect the true size of continents, moving away from the distorting 16th-century Mercator projection. This initiative is part of a broader global movement to decolonize knowledge, challenge historical misperceptions about Africa, and reclaim African history and identity within educational systems. The resolution, supported by the African Union, aims to symbolically redraw maps of facts, culture, and power dynamics, fostering a stronger sense of national identity and countering biased media portrayals that negatively impact the continent economically.
