United Nations Endorses New World Map Projection to Accurately Represent Continents' Sizes

2026-09-04 18:26:35 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has overwhelmingly backed a resolution encouraging the wider use of equal-area map projections, such as Equal Earth, to more accurately represent the true relative size of continents. This move aims to correct the distortions of the traditional Mercator projection, which makes regions closer to the poles appear disproportionately larger, while Africa and other equatorial areas seem smaller. Introduced by Togo on behalf of the African Group, the "Correct the Map" resolution was adopted by 164 votes to one, with the United States being the sole dissenter. Although not legally binding, it advocates for a fairer visual representation of the world, challenging longstanding misconceptions.
