2026-11-04 23:40:00 EST

The $500 million satellite, designed for a two-year mission but operating for nearly 22, studied gamma-ray bursts, supernovae, and black holes, contributing to thousands of scientific papers. Its fall was partly due to geomagnetic storms and a private company's robotic servicing spacecraft, LINK, failing to raise its altitude.
