Chile Achieves Elimination of Dog-Transmitted Rabies to Humans

2026-09-14 17:49:35 EDT

Chile has become the first South American nation to eliminate dog-transmitted rabies in humans, a milestone achieved through over five decades of dedicated public health efforts. The World Health Organization confirmed the last reported case was in 1972, praising Chile's sustained investment in prevention and surveillance. Key initiatives included extensive pet vaccination campaigns, post-exposure prophylaxis for humans, and community awareness programs. This success offers a valuable model for other countries aiming to combat this nearly always fatal, yet preventable, disease.
