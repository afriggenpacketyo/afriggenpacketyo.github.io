Oldest Pearl Harbor Survivor, Freeman Johnson, Dies at 106

His ship, nicknamed "Lucky Lou," was the only major vessel to leave unscathed. Johnson humbly stated he wasn't a hero, merely doing his job, and found his greatest pride in his family. His passing leaves only 11 known Pearl Harbor survivors, all centenarians, marking a significant moment in historical remembrance.
