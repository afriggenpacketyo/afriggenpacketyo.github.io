Northwest Tribe Leads Efforts to Revive Endangered Sunflower Sea Stars

2026-08-16 05:00:00 EDT

The Samish Indian Nation is collaborating with University of Washington researchers to save the critically endangered sunflower sea star, a keystone species decimated by a wasting disease exacerbated by ocean heat waves. After a previous delay due to disease resurgence, a team successfully released 20 lab-raised stars into traditional Samish territory. The tribe views the stars as crucial for protecting kelp forests, which are vital for coastal ecosystems and hold cultural significance, reflecting a broader effort to restore their ancestral lands and marine environments. This ongoing project aims to understand effective reintroduction methods before larger-scale releases.
