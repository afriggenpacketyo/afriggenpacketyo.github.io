Leonid Meteor Shower Set to Grace UK Skies This November

2026-11-06 21:03:28 EST

The Leonid meteor shower is poised to offer a celestial spectacle across the UK throughout November, with its most intense period of visibility projected for the night of November 17. Enthusiastic skygazers may observe between 10 to 15 meteors per hour, originating from dust particles shed by Comet 55P/Tempel-Tuttle, which enter Earth's atmosphere at speeds up to 44 miles per second. Experts recommend seeking out a safe, dark location, far from urban light pollution, and allowing one's eyes 20 to 30 minutes to adapt to the darkness for the best viewing experience, as binoculars or telescopes are not required. This article advises that while initial weekend viewing might be partially obstructed by cloud cover, conditions are expected to improve significantly closer to the shower's peak.
