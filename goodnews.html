Archaeologists Discover World's Oldest Shipwreck Off Turkish Coast

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have discovered the world's oldest shipwreck off Kekova Island, Turkey, dating back 4,400 to 4,700 years to the Early Bronze Age. Although the wooden structure is gone, ceramic storage pots (amphorae) containing traces of their original cargo were found scattered on the seabed. Led by Prof Hakan Öniz, the expedition determined the 10m-long vessel was a trade ship. The distinctive shape of the amphorae allowed for dating, matching styles from ancient Egypt and the Middle East. The recovered artifacts will be displayed in the new Mediterranean Underwater Archaeology Museum in Antalya.
