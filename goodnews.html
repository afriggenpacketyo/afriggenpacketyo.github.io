UN Adopts New World Map to Accurately Represent Africa's Size

2026-09-04 13:26:40 EDT

The UN General Assembly has voted to adopt a new world map, replacing the traditional Mercator projection with one that more accurately reflects the true size of Africa. The "Correct the Map" resolution, sponsored by Togo and supported by 164 nations, passed with only the United States voting against it and six abstentions. Critics have long argued that the 16th-century Mercator map severely distorts landmasses, particularly making Africa appear similar in size to Greenland despite being 14 times larger. This historical misrepresentation is believed to have minimized the perceived development needs and potential of countries located near the equator.
