Understanding and Treating Dry Eyes, Especially Common in Women

2026-09-04 05:00:00 EDT

Dry eyes represent a chronic condition significantly prevalent among women, especially during perimenopause and post-menopause, and should not be disregarded due to the risk of progressing to blurry vision, infection, and corneal scarring. This article elucidates that hormonal fluctuations, particularly a decline in androgen levels, impact the meibomian glands, which are crucial for producing the oil that prevents tears from evaporating too quickly. Additional factors contributing to dry eyes include extensive screen time, contact lens wear, and various medications. Although there is no definitive cure, a broad spectrum of treatments, from lifestyle adjustments and over-the-counter remedies to prescription drugs and specialized in-office procedures, are available, underscoring the importance of professional medical consultation for symptoms affecting daily life.
