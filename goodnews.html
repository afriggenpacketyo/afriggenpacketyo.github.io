World's oldest amber discovered in ancient Chinese coal seam

2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

Chinese researchers have unearthed what is believed to be the world's oldest amber, preserved within a Middle Devonian coal seam dating back approximately 393 to 383 million years. This discovery predates previously known amber by over 50 million years. The paleontologists initially expressed skepticism upon finding microscopic amber pieces in the Xinjiang region's unique coal seam. The findings, detailed in the journal Science Advances, push back the timeline for the existence of amber significantly. This discovery offers new insights into ancient ecosystems and the early evolution of plant life.
