BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Orbit Around Mercury After Eight-Year Journey

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a collaborative European and Japanese endeavor, has successfully placed two robotic spacecraft into orbit around Mercury following an arduous eight-year, six-billion-mile cruise through the inner solar system. This achievement marks Mercury's first visitor in over a decade and only the third spacecraft ever to orbit the sun's closest planet, barely larger than Earth's moon. The $2 billion mission aims to unravel the planet's many mysteries, with the two probes slated to separate next month to map and study its dense interior, magnetic field, and complex surface features from varying altitudes. Despite facing extreme temperatures exceeding 800 degrees Fahrenheit, scientists eagerly anticipate the data, hoping to extend the mission for at least one Earth year.
