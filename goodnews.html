Understanding Dry Eyes: A Common Condition Affecting Women, Especially Post-Menopause

2026-09-04 05:00:00 EDT

Dry eye syndrome is a prevalent condition, particularly among women, often exacerbated by hormonal changes associated with perimenopause and menopause. This occurs when eyes produce insufficient tears or tears evaporate too quickly, frequently due to issues with the meibomian glands in the eyelids. Factors like increased screen time, contact lens wear, and certain medications can also contribute. Ignoring dry eye symptoms is crucial as it can lead to vision problems, increased risk of infection, and corneal damage, necessitating medical attention for effective management.
