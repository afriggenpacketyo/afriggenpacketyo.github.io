Understanding the Necessity of a Power of Attorney Alongside a Will

2026-11-24 14:02:00 EST

The article highlights that while a will is crucial for asset distribution after death, it does not grant authority for financial management during one's lifetime, especially in cases of incapacitation. A financial power of attorney, particularly a durable one, is essential to designate a trusted agent to handle bills, bank accounts, and other financial matters if you become unable to make decisions yourself. Without this document, families may face costly and delayed court authorizations to manage financial responsibilities. These two legal instruments serve distinct purposes—a will for post-mortem affairs and a power of attorney for lifetime financial protection—making both vital for a comprehensive estate plan to ensure financial affairs are handled appropriately. BepiColombo Spacecraft Overcomes Glitch to Successfully Orbit Mercury

2026-11-24 12:34:32 EST

The BepiColombo spacecraft, a joint European and Japanese mission, successfully entered Mercury's orbit after an eight-year journey, despite encountering a technical glitch during the crucial maneuver. Mission control briefly lost contact and the spacecraft entered safe mode, completing only 77% of its planned thruster burn due to a software issue involving attitude guidance. Despite this setback, ESA scientists confirmed BepiColombo had locked into orbit, taking six hours to verify. Over the coming weeks, the spacecraft will make further small burns to gradually optimize its orbit before releasing two science orbiters in December, which are expected to begin scientific operations by April 2027 to study Mercury's interior, surface, and surrounding space.
