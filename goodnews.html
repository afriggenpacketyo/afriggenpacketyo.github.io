BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Orbit Around Mercury

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a collaborative European and Japanese space endeavor, has successfully achieved orbit around Mercury following an intricate eight-year journey through the inner solar system. This significant milestone marks Mercury's first robotic visitor in over a decade and only the third spacecraft in history to reach the sun's innermost planet. The mission comprises two linked explorers designed to separate next month, allowing them to independently map and study Mercury's dense interior, magnetic field, and diverse surface features from distinct altitudes. Despite extreme temperatures and considerable engineering challenges, the mission is planned to operate for at least one Earth year, promising unprecedented comparative data.
