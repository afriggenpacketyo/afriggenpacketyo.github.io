Raising Awareness: Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs) Affects Athletes

2026-10-20 01:45:34 EDT

Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs) is a serious syndrome impacting athletes, both male and female, due to insufficient energy intake relative to exercise demands, often leading to severe health issues. Initially termed "female athlete triad," REDs was renamed to acknowledge its prevalence in men, who often suffer in silence due to stigma and less obvious symptoms like reduced testosterone. The "lighter is faster" culture in certain sports contributes to under-fueling, initially boosting performance but eventually leading to injuries like stress fractures and long-term problems such as osteoporosis. Efforts are underway to increase education and implement mandatory health checks, with many athletes like Jake Smith successfully reversing the condition and advocating for greater awareness and support.
