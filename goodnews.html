Chile's Atacama Desert Bursts into Record Floral Bloom

2026-11-08 06:00:00 EST

These rains, the heaviest since the 1950s, penetrated deep into the parched ground, awakening millions of dormant seeds and bulbs, resulting in over 200 species flowering across vast areas. This rare phenomenon, while preceded by flash floods, has transformed the desert into a patchwork of colors, attracting 15,000 tourists in recent weeks and prompting efforts to seek UNESCO World Heritage recognition. Scientists are studying this transient ecosystem, which is becoming more frequent due to climate change.
