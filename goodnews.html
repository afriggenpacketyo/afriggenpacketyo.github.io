World's Oldest Shipwreck Discovered Off Turkey's Kekova Island

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have announced the discovery of the world's oldest shipwreck off Turkey's Kekova Island, dating back an astonishing 4,400 to 4,700 years to the Early Bronze Age. Although the ship's wooden structure has not survived, over 30 ceramic storage pots, known as amphorae, were found on the seabed, with some still containing traces of their original contents. The 10-meter trade vessel's age was determined by analyzing the distinctive pottery styles, which align with artifacts from that period unearthed in Egypt and the Middle East. This remarkable find provides an unprecedented "time capsule" into ancient Mediterranean trade routes and early maritime history.
