2026-10-07 07:54:26 EDT

Their discoveries revolutionized modern medicine by enabling scientists to selectively produce desired mirror-image forms of molecules, crucial for drug design. Kagan's initial work laid the foundation, while Soai achieved the first-ever reaction to produce only one mirror image, known as the 'Soai reaction.' This achievement was lauded as a feat previously only accomplished by life itself, profoundly impacting catalyst development and pharmaceutical advancements.
