Weight-Loss Medications Influence Alcohol Consumption Habits

2026-08-08 19:30:06 EDT

A growing body of research indicates that weight-loss drugs like Wegovy, Mounjaro, and Ozempic, which contain GLP-1 mimicking compounds, are significantly altering individuals' relationships with alcohol. These medications, primarily designed to suppress appetite, also appear to affect the brain's reward response, potentially reducing cravings for substances like alcohol and nicotine. Users report varied experiences, from a complete loss of desire to drink, to feeling nauseous or getting intoxicated more quickly after consuming smaller amounts. While this side effect can be a positive bonus for some seeking to reduce alcohol intake, others, particularly younger individuals, find it negatively impacts their social lives.

Newly Discovered 'Coffee Frogs' in Costa Rica Face Pesticide Threats

2026-08-08 15:48:58 EDT

A tiny, nocturnal frog species, measuring less than four centimeters and nicknamed the 'coffee frog,' has been officially recognized in Costa Rica's central Pacific region, thriving in coffee plantations. This greenish amphibian, first observed in the 1960s but misidentified, was confirmed as a new, endemic species in 2021 through genetic and morphological studies. While the males' translucent throats inflate with their distinctive high-pitched calls among coffee shrubs, the species faces significant threats from agrochemicals used in farming. Pesticides, traveling through the air and seeping into aquatic systems where tadpoles develop, along with waste dumping and habitat loss, endanger these newly celebrated creatures.
