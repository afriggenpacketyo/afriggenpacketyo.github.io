2026-10-12 20:22:45 EDT

The Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust admitted fault and apologized, having delayed informing staff for 16 months about the dangers. This represents the largest group legal action by midwives in the UK, prompting the Royal College of Midwives to advocate for improved safety guidance across all NHS trusts.

Expedition Lands on Remote Island to Search for Amelia Earhart's Plane Wreckage

2026-10-12 18:01:43 EDT

A research team from the Archaeological Legacy Institute and Purdue University has landed on Nikumaroro Island in the Pacific Ocean to investigate potential wreckage of Amelia Earhart's vanished 1937 plane. The five-day search for the "Taraia Object," spotted in satellite imagery, faces significant challenges, including the strongest-ever El Niño climate phenomenon and the presence of large, carnivorous coconut crabs, forcing the crew to stay on their ship at night for safety. This marks the first authorized trip to the island in seven years, aiming to finally solve the nearly 90-year-old mystery of Earhart and Fred Noonan's disappearance.
