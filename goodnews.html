New Guidelines Propose Wider Use of 'Sponge' Tests for Oesophageal Cancer Detection

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

New draft guidelines for the NHS in England propose offering "sponge in the gullet" tests to more individuals suffering from persistent indigestion, providing a less invasive method for early oesophageal cancer detection. These quick tests, which can be conducted in GP surgeries, offer an alternative to traditional hospital endoscopies. The procedure involves swallowing a dissolvable capsule that releases a sponge to collect cells from the gullet lining for subsequent analysis. The National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) supports the expanded use of these tests, aiming to identify patients requiring further investigation, alleviate pressure on endoscopy services, and facilitate earlier cancer diagnoses.
