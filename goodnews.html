UN Endorses New Map Projection for More Accurate Representation of Continents

2026-09-06 08:30:50 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to more accurately represent the true proportions of continents, particularly highlighting Africa's correct size. This significant resolution, sponsored by Togo and backed by 164 member states, encourages governments and institutions worldwide to adopt it over the traditional Mercator map. The Mercator projection, historically used for navigation, is criticized for distorting landmasses by exaggerating polar regions like Greenland while diminishing the apparent size of continents such as Africa, which is actually 14 times larger than Greenland. This initiative underscores the belief that maps profoundly influence global understanding and aims to rectify long-standing geographical misrepresentations.
