Byron Sher, Environmental Law Pioneer, Dies at 98

2026-09-14 16:15:00 EDT

Byron Sher, a former Stanford law professor and influential California legislator, has passed away at 98. Sher was instrumental in authoring landmark environmental laws, including those promoting recycling, limiting environmental marketing claims, and combating pollution. He played a key role in preserving Headwaters Forest and authored legislation in 1988 that was the first to acknowledge "global warming" in state law, laying the groundwork for future climate change policies. Known for his quiet demeanor and commitment to policy, Sher's legislative work significantly shaped California's environmental landscape.
