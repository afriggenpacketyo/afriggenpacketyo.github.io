Community Shield Victory Rarely Predicts Premier League Success, Historical Data Shows

2026-08-16 01:29:55 EDT

Ahead of the Community Shield match between English champions Arsenal and FA Cup winners Manchester City, an analysis reveals that winning this curtain-raiser rarely correlates with subsequent Premier League success. Since the Premier League's inception in 1992-93, only eight of 34 Community Shield winners have gone on to claim the league title, with Manchester City in 2019 being the most recent instance. Intriguingly, teams that lose the Community Shield have historically won more league titles in the upcoming season, totaling ten. Furthermore, nearly half the time, the eventual Premier League champions were not even involved in the Community Shield match.
