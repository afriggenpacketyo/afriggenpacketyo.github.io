Using Electric Fans in Extreme Heat Can Be Dangerous, Experts Warn

2026-07-31 19:57:16 EDT

Experts are cautioning that using electric fans during extreme heat waves, especially when temperatures exceed 95 degrees Fahrenheit, can be harmful rather than helpful. According to the CDC, fans can act like convection ovens, circulating hot air and potentially raising body temperature, increasing the risk of dehydration and heat exhaustion. UCLA professors David Eisenman and V. Kelly Turner emphasize that the skin's temperature, around 95 degrees, is a critical threshold. They recommend strategic nighttime ventilation with fans when temperatures cool, combined with daytime shading and significantly increased water intake, as safer methods to combat soaring temperatures.
