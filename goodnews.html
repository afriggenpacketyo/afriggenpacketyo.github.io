Chinese-Led Team Discovers Elusive "Glueball" Particle

2026-08-08 09:00:11 EDT

A Chinese-led research team has announced the discovery of a "glueball," a rare particle made entirely of gluons, which are fundamental force carriers that "glue" quarks together into larger particles like protons and neutrons. This finding, presented at the International Conference on High Energy Physics, confirms a 50-year-old scientific prediction that gluons could bind together. Unlike photons, gluons attract each other while carrying the strong force, making their self-binding possible. The discovery, involving around 700 scientists from 15 countries, represents a significant advancement in understanding the fundamental building blocks of matter and the strong nuclear force.
