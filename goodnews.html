Germany Considers Major Reforms to 'Minijob' Employment System

2026-08-01 04:49:00 EDT

These jobs are widely used by women and students, with nearly 7 million Germans holding them, either as primary or secondary employment. The government plans to raise the flat tax rate for employers and potentially eliminate the opt-out for pension contributions, making minijobbers subject to regular social security. Proponents argue this would strengthen social security systems, reduce old-age poverty, and address gender inequality, while business representatives warn against losing crucial flexibility and additional labor potential provided by the current system.
