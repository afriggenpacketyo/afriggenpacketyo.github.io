Retirees Face Year-End Deadline for Required Minimum Distributions to Avoid IRS Penalties

2026-11-21 09:30:02 EST

Millions of American retirees must take required minimum distributions (RMDs) from their retirement accounts by December 31st each year, starting at age 73, to avoid a hefty IRS penalty. Missing this deadline can result in an excise tax of up to 25% of the unwithdrawn amount, though the penalty can be reduced or waived under certain conditions. Financial experts advise against waiting until the last minute to prevent errors. Charitable transfers, known as qualified charitable distributions (QCDs), offer a dual tax benefit by not counting as taxable income and satisfying RMDs for eligible individuals.
