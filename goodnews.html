New Guidelines Propose Wider Use of 'Sponge' Tests for Oesophageal Cancer Screening

2026-11-15 19:47:04 EST

New draft guidelines for the NHS in England recommend offering quick "sponge in the gullet" tests to more people with persistent indigestion to screen for oesophageal cancer. These less invasive tests, which can be performed in GP surgeries, involve swallowing a capsule that releases a sponge to collect cells for analysis. The National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) believes this method can help detect Barrett's oesophagus, a pre-cancerous condition, earlier, reducing reliance on more invasive hospital endoscopies and improving early detection for thousands of patients, ultimately freeing up NHS resources.
