First Adult Tyrannosaurus Rex Footprints Discovered in North Dakota

2026-09-11 10:34:07 EDT

The first known series of footprints from an adult Tyrannosaurus rex has been discovered in North Dakota's Hell Creek Formation by a team of scientists from the Denver Museum of Nature and Science and Liverpool John Moores University. The 23-foot-long trackway, consisting of four three-toed footprints, provides a rare and invaluable glimpse into the behavior of the iconic dinosaur from 66.5 million years ago. Researchers utilized high-resolution 3D scanning to document the prints, estimating the T. rex walked at approximately 3.5-4.5 mph, akin to a brisk human pace. This significant paleontological find will see three of the discovered footprints transported to the Denver museum later this year.
