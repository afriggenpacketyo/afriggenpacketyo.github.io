UN Backs New World Map to Show Africa's True Relative Size

2026-09-04 18:26:35 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has overwhelmingly approved a resolution, "Correct the Map," advocating for the wider use of equal-area map projections that accurately represent the true relative size of continents. The resolution, backed by the African Group, encourages governments and institutions to adopt maps like Equal Earth, which corrects the distortions of the traditional Mercator projection. Critics argue the Mercator map, designed for navigation, misrepresents northern regions as disproportionately large while making Africa appear smaller. The U.S. was the sole country to vote against the non-binding resolution, calling it an ideological distraction.
