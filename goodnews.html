Conservationists Launch Major Effort to Save Critically Rare Welsh Snail

2026-09-12 02:09:37 EDT

Conservationists are undertaking a significant effort to save the glutinous snail, one of Europe's rarest species, found exclusively in Wales' Llyn Tegid. Several British zoos are establishing "ark populations" using snails successfully bred in captivity by conservationist Ian Hughes, who made a breakthrough in rearing their eggs. This project aims to eventually reintroduce the tiny molluscs, which serve as vital indicators of healthy aquatic ecosystems, back into suitable wild habitats within three years. The initiative combats severe threats from water pollution and habitat loss, preserving a species deemed as precious as an "Egyptian artefact" for future generations.
