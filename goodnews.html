This celestial spectacle is formed by dust particles shed from Comet 55P/Tempel-Tuttle, with these particles entering Earth's atmosphere at an astonishing speed of up to 44 miles (71km) per second, making them the fastest meteors visible. Experts recommend finding a safe, dark location away from urban light pollution and allowing eyes to adapt for 20-30 minutes.
