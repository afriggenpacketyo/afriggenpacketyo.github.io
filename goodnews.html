World Health Organization Issues New Guidelines Against Obesity Drugs for Young Children

2026-10-07 04:07:54 EDT

New World Health Organization (WHO) guidelines strongly advise against the use of obesity drugs and GLP-1 weight-loss injections for children under 10 years old. The WHO recommends that for this age group, the focus should be on healthy eating and exercise, rather than medical interventions or surgery, although drug trials are not opposed. While most of these medications are approved for older adolescents, some are being tested in younger children. Obesity rates among children are a growing concern, with a significant increase in prevalence since 1990. The WHO's caution stems from a lack of long-term evidence regarding the safety and efficacy of these drugs in very young children.

German Government Unveils Five-Year Plan to Revamp Railroad System

2026-10-07 02:17:00 EDT

The plan outlines goals for repairs, digitization, electrification, station reconstruction, and line expansion from 2026 to 2030. It includes performance indicators to monitor progress, such as the kilometers of new tracks laid and electrification achieved. While the plan sets ambitious targets, it does not yet specify financing mechanisms, which will depend on the government budget. The Transport Ministry is working on a law to establish a dedicated railroad fund to secure long-term financing for these renovations.
