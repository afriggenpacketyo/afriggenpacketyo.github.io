UK Government to Halt Benefits for Serious Criminals in Psychiatric Hospitals

2026-10-19 19:21:38 EDT

The UK government is implementing new regulations to cease benefit payments for serious criminals, including individuals convicted of manslaughter and sexual assault, who are detained in hospitals for psychiatric treatment. This policy change, championed by victims' families, aims to eliminate the "double provision" where taxpayers fund both their NHS care and up to £800 monthly in Universal Credit. Work and Pensions Secretary Pat McFadden stated this initiative addresses a "gross unfairness" and is expected to save tens of millions of pounds annually. The new rules, set to affect approximately 2,500 current offenders and around 200 annually in the future, apply to specific Mental Health Act orders but do not include murder convictions where hospital orders are not a sentencing option.
