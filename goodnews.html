2026-10-16 08:00:08 EDT

Chinese scientists have unearthed a 150 million-year-old fossil of Yuezhuornis yangyuanensis, the earliest known flightless bird, in Fujian province, providing compelling evidence that not all early birds evolved to fly. This significant discovery challenges long-held assumptions, including those from Charles Darwin, that flightlessness in birds only emerged as a secondary adaptation after species had already mastered flight. The fossil suggests that early bird adaptation branched into diverse, terrestrial non-flying niches much earlier than previously believed. This finding published in Nature, could reshape understanding of avialan skeletal modifications and evolutionary drivers.
