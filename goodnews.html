Leonid Meteor Shower Set to Dazzle UK Skygazers This November

2026-11-06 21:03:28 EST

The Leonid meteor shower is expected to offer a celestial spectacle across the UK throughout November, peaking on the night of the 17th with up to 10-15 meteors per hour. Composed of dust particles from Comet 55P/Tempel-Tuttle, these meteors are the fastest in the sky, entering Earth's atmosphere at 44 miles per second. Experts advise finding a dark location away from light pollution, allowing eyes 20-30 minutes to adapt for optimal viewing without special equipment. While clear skies are essential, early forecasts suggest better viewing opportunities in southern and western England and Wales this weekend, with conditions expected to improve around the peak.
