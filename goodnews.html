California Approves New "Zone Zero" Fire-Safe Landscaping Rules for Fire-Prone Areas

2026-08-19 16:13:51 EDT

California's Board of Forestry and Fire Protection has unanimously approved new "Zone Zero" landscaping requirements for millions of residents living in fire-prone areas, concluding nearly six years of debate. These regulations mandate a one-foot plant-free zone directly adjacent to homes and require the first five feet around structures to be clear of combustible materials, including firewood, fallen leaves, and certain mulches. Additionally, wooden sheds and wooden fences attached to houses are prohibited within this zone. Homeowners will have up to five years to comply, while new constructions must adhere immediately. This article underscores the state's critical effort to enhance wildfire defense and improve community safety following a decade of increasingly devastating fire seasons.
