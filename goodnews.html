Singapore Develops World's Most Accurate Clock, Surpassing US and China

2026-10-02 04:00:11 EDT

Singaporean scientists have achieved a groundbreaking feat by constructing the world's most accurate clock, utilizing a single ion of lutetium. This remarkable timepiece is so precise that it would only lose one second every 260 billion years, a duration nearly 20 times the age of the universe. This accomplishment significantly surpasses previous records held by calcium-ion clocks developed in China and aluminum-ion clocks unveiled by the US National Institute of Standards and Technology, marking a major advancement in metrology.
