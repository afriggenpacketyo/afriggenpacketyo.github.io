Study Challenges Narrative on Non-German Nationals and Crime Rates

2026-10-14 04:48:00 EDT

A new research paper challenges the Alternative for Germany (AfD) party's narrative that non-German nationals are inherently more violent, by analyzing both officially reported and unreported crime data. Sociologist Susann Prätor's study found that while police statistics show non-Germans committing crimes more frequently, they are also twice as likely to be reported, creating a significant reporting bias. When adjusted for this bias, non-Germans are 1.7 times more likely to commit crimes, a figure consistent with studies on juvenile violent crime. The article highlights that factors like age, gender, and socioeconomic background, rather than cultural origin, primarily influence criminal behavior.

World's Oldest Shipwreck Discovered Off Turkey's Kekova Island

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have discovered the world's oldest shipwreck off Turkey's Kekova Island, dating it to the Early Bronze Age, between 4,400 and 4,700 years ago. Although the ship's wooden structure has not survived, over 30 ceramic storage pots, known as amphorae, were found on the seabed, remarkably some still containing traces of their original cargo. The 10-meter trade vessel, identified by its distinctive pottery styles, carried goods across the Mediterranean Sea. This significant discovery, which predates the previous record holder off Dokos, Greece, offers a unique "time capsule" into ancient maritime trade and the lives of early sailors, with artifacts slated for display in a new museum.
