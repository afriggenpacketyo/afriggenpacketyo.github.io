Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs) is a serious syndrome affecting athletes, both male and female, due to insufficient energy intake relative to exercise demands. Undiagnosed, it can lead to brittle bones, cardiovascular issues, fertility problems, and reduced performance, as exemplified by British runner Jake Smith. The condition, once known as "female athlete triad," now recognizes its prevalence in men, though stigma often delays diagnosis. Experts advocate for greater awareness, education, and mandatory health checks in sports to prevent long-term health consequences, emphasizing that proper fueling is crucial for both overall health and optimal athletic performance.
