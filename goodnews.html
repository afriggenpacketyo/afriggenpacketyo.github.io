Scientific Evidence for Mushroom Supplements' Health Benefits Remains Limited

2026-11-23 05:00:00 EST

Mushroom supplements are increasingly marketed for health benefits like improved cognition and immunity, drawing on traditional practices and emerging research. While compounds like beta-glucan and certain extracts, such as PSK from turkey tail, show promise in studies—including a meta-analysis linking PSK to reduced mortality risk in cancer patients—comprehensive clinical trials are largely insufficient. Experts, including dietitians and oncologists, stress the necessity for more robust research to establish efficacy, safe dosing, and potential side effects, especially since mushrooms can accumulate environmental elements. Currently, there isn't enough scientific evidence to support their routine clinical use, with a focus on foundational nutrition being a more immediate recommendation.
