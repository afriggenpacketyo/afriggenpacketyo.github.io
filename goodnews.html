2026-10-13 09:02:58 EDT

NASA's Perseverance rover has achieved a significant milestone, setting a new record for the longest distance driven by any vehicle on another planet. As of Sunday, the rover had traveled 28.1 miles on Mars, surpassing the previous record of 28.06 miles set by the Opportunity rover. Perseverance's advanced autonomous navigation system, AutoNav, allows it to "think while driving," enabling it to map hazards and plan its own routes efficiently. This capability has allowed it to cover considerable ground relatively quickly. The mission's scope has been extended to explore the Jezero Crater rim until the end of 2028, with scientists hoping to find traces of ancient hydrothermal systems.
