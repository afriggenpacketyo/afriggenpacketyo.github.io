Understanding Measles Immunity Amid Rising Cases: A Guide

2026-10-12 05:00:00 EDT

With measles cases surging nationwide, this article provides essential guidance on determining one's immunity status and vaccination needs. Experts emphasize that individuals born before 1957 are generally presumed immune due to widespread exposure, while those vaccinated between 1957 and 1968 might need an additional dose of the MMR vaccine due to less effective early versions. Although one MMR dose is 93% effective, a second dose is recommended for higher-risk groups, including healthcare workers, college students, international travelers, and those in areas with high measles activity. If immunization records are unavailable, a blood test can check antibody levels, but getting an additional, safe, and often free MMR vaccine is a simpler alternative.
