Northern Lights Dazzle UK, Visible as Far South as England's Coast

2026-10-10 16:57:29 EDT

Residents across the UK were captivated by a spectacular display of the Northern Lights, or Aurora Borealis, which was visible as far south as England's Devon, Hampshire, and Kent coasts. This rare and widespread sighting was attributed to significantly increased solar activity, specifically powerful coronal mass ejections (CMEs) from the Sun directed towards Earth. These streams of charged particles, when captured by Earth's magnetic field and directed into the atmosphere, collide with oxygen and nitrogen, emitting the vibrant colored light that creates the aurora. The Met Office anticipates potential continued sightings in clear skies for several more days, offering further opportunities to witness this stunning natural phenomenon.
