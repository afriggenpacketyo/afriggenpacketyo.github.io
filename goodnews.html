The move stands in contrast to the Trump administration's broader stance on TPS, which has led to the revocation of protections for over a million immigrants from various other nations. Noah Adams, Longtime Host of NPR's All Things Considered, Dies at 84

2026-10-18 12:30:00 EDT

Noah Adams, a distinguished writer, broadcaster, and beloved host of NPR's "All Things Considered" for 18 years, has died at the age of 84. Adams was celebrated for his ability to guide listeners through major historical events and for his thoughtful, vivid storytelling, particularly in features about rural America and everyday people. Colleagues remember his deep curiosity and unique interviewing style, which notably inspired "This American Life" host Ira Glass. Adams also authored five books and produced the experimental 1981 radio documentary "Father Cares: The Last of Jonestown," leaving a lasting legacy on public radio and the art of journalism.

BBC Pauses Civil Service Secondment Scheme Amid Impartiality Concerns

The BBC has paused its secondment scheme for government statisticians working with BBC Verify and will review their roles, following a report that raised concerns about impartiality. The BBC emphasized its commitment to independence and transparency, ensuring that during the review, current secondees will not be involved in news publication. This aims to uphold the highest standards for a public broadcaster.
