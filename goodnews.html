Liechtenstein has announced a change to its royal succession rules, now permitting women to ascend the throne. This declaration, made on the central European country's national day, shifts the order from male primogeniture to absolute primogeniture, meaning the first-born child, regardless of sex, will become heir. While the change will not affect the current heir, Hereditary Prince Alois, it will apply to the descendants of his four children and their future offspring. Prince Alois stated that the amendment aims to ensure the Principality and Princely House are led by the person best prepared for the responsibility, reflecting a modernizing trend in royal lines, similar to Britain's 2011 reform.
