Dachshunds: From Picasso's Muse to Political Symbolism

2026-10-30 03:57:00 EDT

The dachshund, an instantly recognizable breed, holds a surprisingly rich cultural and political history, explored in the "The Dachshund. An Icon off the Leash" exhibition. Beyond being a muse for artists like Picasso and Andy Warhol, these "badger dogs" originated in Germany and were unfortunately used in WWI propaganda to represent the nation, causing their popularity to plummet in the US. However, they later became a symbol of a new, open West Germany as the 1972 Munich Olympics mascot, Waldi. Today, they continue to be co-opted in political symbolism, from right-wing groups to counter-protest movements, yet largely retain an endearing image of cheerfulness.
