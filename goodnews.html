Covered Call ETFs Offer Income but Pose Hidden Tax Complexities for Investors

2026-10-18 09:10:33 EDT

Covered call ETFs, popular for generating monthly income by holding equities and selling call options, can hide complex tax implications for investors. While they offer attractive yields, distributions can come as dividends, capital gains, or non-dividend distributions (return of capital), which reduces cost basis and defers taxes until sale. Many investors are surprised by the magnitude of taxable income or the delayed tax liability, which can make the "after-tax net total return fall below expectations." Financial advisors recommend using tax-deferred accounts for these funds and carefully evaluating the tax-cost ratio, as well as the fund's Net Asset Value growth to sustain distributions.
