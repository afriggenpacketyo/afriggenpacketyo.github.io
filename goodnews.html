Understanding Credit Card Interest Rate Increases After Missed Payments

2026-08-07 11:33:42 EDT

This article explains that credit card creditors can indeed increase interest rates after missed payments, specifically through a penalty APR if a payment is more than 60 days late. Under the CARD Act of 2009, this increase must be disclosed beforehand and a 45-day notice provided, and it can apply retroactively to existing balances. Issuers are generally required to review these accounts every six months and remove the penalty APR after six consecutive on-time payments. The article advises consumers struggling with payments to contact their issuer for hardship programs or explore debt relief strategies like management plans or consolidation, emphasizing proactive measures to avoid escalating debt.
