Protecting Your Home Title from Fraud: Steps and Services

2026-10-03 14:30:21 EDT

Home title fraud involves criminals forging deeds or tricking owners into transferring property, which is then filed with local records. To protect against this, homeowners can check property records for free, sign up for county alerts, and consider identity theft protection services. While monitoring services alert to changes, they cannot prevent initial fraudulent filings, and "title lock" is not actual title insurance. Understanding the difference between a title (ownership) and a deed (transfer document) is crucial for effective self-protection.
