Understanding Gold IRAs: Requirements, Fees, and Investment Considerations

2026-08-14 12:57:41 EDT

Gold IRAs offer a way to diversify retirement portfolios with physical gold while retaining tax advantages, but they come with specific rules and costs. Unlike traditional IRAs, gold IRAs are self-directed accounts requiring careful selection of custodians and dealers, with strict IRS purity standards for eligible metals. Storage in an IRS-approved depository is mandatory and incurs annual fees. Investors should be aware of the layered costs, including setup fees, custodian fees, and markups on the metal itself, and treat gold IRAs as a component of a diversified strategy rather than a primary retirement vehicle.
