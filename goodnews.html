2026-08-08 15:48:58 EDT

A tiny, nocturnal tree frog, recently identified as a new species endemic to Costa Rica's central Pacific region, has been nicknamed the "coffee frog" due to its habitat. Measuring less than four centimeters, these greenish amphibians thrive and sing in coffee plantations, where they feed on insects. Although first observed in the 1960s, its unique genetic and morphological traits were only confirmed in 2021. However, the species faces significant threats from agrochemicals used in coffee farming, as pesticides can seep into aquatic systems where tadpoles develop, endangering their survival and highlighting conservation challenges.
