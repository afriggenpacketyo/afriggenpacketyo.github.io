BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Orbit Around Mercury

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a collaborative European and Japanese endeavor, has successfully entered orbit around Mercury after an eight-year cruise through the inner solar system, marking the planet's first visitor in over a decade. This $2 billion mission comprises two linked robotic explorers designed to separate next month, allowing them to map and study the scorching planet from distinct altitudes. BepiColombo is only the third spacecraft ever to visit Mercury, following NASA's Mariner 10 and Messenger. Scientists eagerly await an unprecedented influx of data to unlock the planet's mysteries, despite the immense technical challenges presented by its extreme environment.
