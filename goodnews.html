2026-10-29 20:40:30 EDT

UK researchers suggest that consuming fruit and vegetable scraps, such as peels and stalks, could significantly boost dietary fiber intake, which most adults currently lack. The "root-to-tip" eating approach includes items like watermelon rind and pineapple core, which are rich in fiber. Studies show that many discarded parts of produce contain more fiber than the commonly eaten portions. For instance, watermelon rind offers fiber comparable to wholemeal bread, and butternut squash skin and seeds provide nearly as much fiber as bran flakes. Experts emphasize that incorporating these scraps can improve gut health, reduce the risk of heart disease and bowel cancer, and also offer financial savings by reducing food waste.
