Ranked Choice Voting Explained as Two States Utilize System in Congressional Races

2026-11-03 20:46:42 EST

Ranked choice voting (RCV) is being employed in congressional and state races in Maine and Alaska, enabling voters to rank candidates in order of preference. This system aims to ensure a majority winner by reallocating votes from the lowest-ranked candidates until one surpasses the 50% threshold. Advocates highlight RCV's potential to reduce the "spoiler" effect of third-party candidates and foster more positive campaigning, as candidates seek broader appeal for second and third-choice rankings.
