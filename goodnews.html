Experts Offer Strategies to Combat Jet Lag by Resetting the Body's Internal Clock

2026-08-05 19:49:46 EDT

Jet lag, a pervasive and annoying consequence of rapid travel across multiple time zones, arises when an individual's internal body clock, or circadian rhythms, fails to quickly synchronize with the new local time. Dr. Nina Rzechorzek and Dr. Anna Edmondson from the University of Cambridge explain that the brain's master clock is predominantly regulated by daily cycles of sunlight and darkness. This fundamental mismatch between one's internal biological time and the destination's time results in common symptoms such as disturbed sleep, pervasive daytime sleepiness, diminished concentration, irritability, and digestive issues. Effective strategies to mitigate jet lag involve consciously adjusting meal times and strategically utilizing exposure to natural light to help realign the body's intricate internal systems with the new environmental cues, thereby minimizing discomfort.
