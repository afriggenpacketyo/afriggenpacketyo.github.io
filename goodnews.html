Apple Revises European App Store Fees to Comply with Digital Markets Act

2026-08-18 13:16:49 EDT

Apple has announced an overhaul of its European App Store fees, introducing new commission structures to resolve disagreements with EU regulators stemming from the Digital Markets Act. Under the revised rules, apps purchased through Apple's App Store using its payment system will incur a 26% commission, while those using their own payment processors will pay 20%. Apps linking to external websites for purchases face a 15% charge, and those distributed via third-party app stores or the web will be charged a 5% "Core Technology Commission." This article highlights Apple's adaptation to regulatory pressure, aiming to foster greater competition and choice within the app ecosystem in Europe.
