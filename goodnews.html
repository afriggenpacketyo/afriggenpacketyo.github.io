New Jersey Governor Mikie Sherrill has signed the Fair Price Protection Act into law, making the state the third in the U.S. to prohibit businesses from employing surveillance pricing. This legislation prevents retailers from using personal data, such as online activity and purchase history, to charge different prices for identical products to individual shoppers. Governor Sherrill emphasized that the law prioritizes New Jersey shoppers by safeguarding their privacy and ensuring pricing fairness. Additionally, it imposes a one-year pause on the rollout of new electronic shelf labels, allowing for a study into their potential impact on both consumers and workers.
