Third Rare Olive Ridley Sea Turtle Nest Discovered in Southern California, Prompting Relocation Concerns

2026-11-03 17:04:07 EST

A third rare olive ridley sea turtle nest has been discovered in Southern California, this time on San Buenaventura State Beach in Ventura, marking an unusual occurrence amid record ocean temperatures. This follows two previous nests found in Seal Beach and Huntington Beach in September, making it the third naturally-occurring sea turtle nest ever found on the U.S. West Coast. The extreme marine heatwave and strong El Niño conditions are believed to be pushing water temperatures closer to the turtles' typical nesting grounds. Federal biologists previously relocated the first two clutches to the Aquarium of the Pacific due to high tide threats, and a decision on relocating the Ventura eggs is pending, highlighting ongoing conservation efforts.
