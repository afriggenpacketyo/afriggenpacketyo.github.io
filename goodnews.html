Neuroscience Explains Why People Disagree on Tennis Ball Color

2026-10-05 07:00:00 EDT

A neuroscientist explains that the debate over whether tennis balls are yellow or green stems from the fact that 'there's no such thing as a color' existing independently of the brain. Bevil Conway, a neuroscientist at the National Eye Institute, clarifies that color is a generative product of the brain, dependent on an individual's visual system interacting with the world. This phenomenon, known as color constancy, means our brains make assumptions about lighting to perceive consistent object colors. The article highlights that the specific fluorescent yellow-green of tennis balls, positioned at the peak of human visual sensitivity, contributes to varying perceptions.
