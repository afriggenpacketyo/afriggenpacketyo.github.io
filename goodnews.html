Hera Spacecraft Arrives at Asteroid Crash Site to Assess Planetary Defense Impact

2026-11-25 01:00:36 EST

The European Space Agency's (ESA) Hera mission has arrived at the Didymos-Dimorphos asteroid system to investigate the aftermath of NASA's Dart spacecraft collision in 2022. Dart deliberately crashed into the smaller asteroid, Dimorphos, to test planetary defense capabilities, successfully altering its orbit. Hera will now conduct a detailed assessment of the impact site, aiming to measure Dimorphos's mass and the extent of the damage, which could range from a crater to a complete reshaping of the asteroid. This crucial data will inform future strategies for deflecting potentially hazardous space rocks heading towards Earth, enhancing humanity's preparedness for cosmic threats.
