California Supreme Court Authorizes Electronic Recording in Civil Cases Lacking Court Reporters

2026-08-10 15:13:16 EDT

The California Supreme Court has ruled to allow electronic recording in civil cases when a court reporter is unavailable and parties cannot afford private stenographers. This landmark decision aims to improve access to justice, particularly for indigent litigants, by ensuring a verbatim record of proceedings, which is crucial for appeals. Previously, a severe shortage of professional stenographers often left civil hearings unrecorded, impacting cases involving domestic violence and family disputes, with over a million hearings unrecorded in a single year. The court emphasized that a record is "presupposed under California law" and that thousands of litigants should not sacrifice their right to appeal due to staffing shortages, marking a significant step towards equitable legal access.
