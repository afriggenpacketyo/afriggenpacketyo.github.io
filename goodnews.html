FTC Orders Redfin and Zillow to Amend Anti-Competitive Rental Agreement

2026-08-24 13:22:24 EDT

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) has mandated that real estate firms Redfin and Zillow amend a commercial agreement from last year, which the agency determined significantly weakened competition in the home rental market. This settlement resolves an antitrust lawsuit alleging Zillow paid Redfin $100 million to shut down its listing services for multifamily properties and exclusively repost Zillow-provided apartment listings. The FTC requires the removal of these anti-competitive provisions and Redfin's re-entry into the market with more home listings. This action aims to restore competition, bringing down prices and benefiting both renters and property management companies.
