Research Confirms 'Hang-xiety' as a Real Phenomenon Following Alcohol Consumption

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

"Hang-xiety," the anxiety accompanying a hangover, has been confirmed as a real phenomenon by scientific research. Professor Celia Morgan of the University of Exeter explains that alcohol affects brain chemicals GABA and glutamate, leading to a rebound in alertness and arousal as it wears off, which can trigger anxiety. A 2023 study found 18.3% of students reported hang-xiety, with shy individuals experiencing stronger effects. Experts advise against using alcohol to manage stress and suggest reframing the thoughts as a temporary state. The article highlights that while the dread is real, the perceived catastrophe usually isn't.
