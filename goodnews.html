401(k) Funds Generally Protected from Ordinary Debt Collectors

2026-08-17 11:09:26 EDT

In most situations, money held within an employer-sponsored 401(k) plan is protected from garnishment by ordinary debt collectors due to the Employee Retirement Income Security Act (ERISA). This federal law generally prevents retirement benefits from being assigned or transferred to creditors. However, exceptions exist for qualified domestic relations orders (QDROs) related to spousal or child support obligations, and for federal tax debts, where the IRS has broad levy powers. It's crucial to distinguish between funds still within the 401(k) and those withdrawn, as distributed funds may lose their protections.
