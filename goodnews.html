Study Suggests Current Breast Cancer Screening Misses High-Risk Younger Women

2026-08-03 21:08:13 EDT

A new study warns that current NHS criteria for breast cancer screening in women under 50 are overly narrow, potentially failing to identify up to 95% of individuals at elevated risk for the disease. The National Institute of Health and Care Excellence (Nice) guidelines predominantly consider inherited genes, neglecting other crucial factors such as lifestyle and reproductive history. Researchers utilized a new risk assessment tool, Boadicea, which integrates a wider array of risk indicators, and found it identified eight times as many younger women who later developed breast cancer. This article emphasizes that a significant majority of breast cancer cases in women under 50 occur without a family history, highlighting the urgent need for a more holistic and inclusive screening strategy to enhance early detection and improve outcomes.

Businesses Grapple with Pricing Challenges for AI Services

2026-08-03 19:21:10 EDT

Companies developing and utilizing AI, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs) and agentic AI, are facing significant difficulties in setting prices for their services. This challenge stems from the rapidly evolving economics of "tokens"—the mathematical building blocks of AI processing—whose consumption is unpredictable and skyrocketing. While individual token costs have decreased, the sheer volume used by businesses and consumers has surged, leading to unexpected expenses. Firms like Saviynt and smartR AI note the impossibility of long-term cost models, as subtle prompt variations and the use of multiple AI agents make token usage non-deterministic. Businesses are exploring various pricing strategies, but the instability of underlying AI costs makes budgeting and consistent customer pricing a complex and unresolved issue.
