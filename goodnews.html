Understanding High-Functioning Depression and Anhedonia

2026-10-29 16:00:00 EDT

Psychiatrist Dr. Judith Joseph highlights "high-functioning depression," a condition where individuals maintain daily obligations while quietly losing the ability to feel pleasure, known as anhedonia. This "silent sister" of traditional depression often goes unnoticed, even by those experiencing it, until they reach burnout or suffer a physical breakdown. Research indicates a link between high-functioning depression, anhedonia, and trauma, with parents and caregivers showing the highest scores in a recent study. Joseph emphasizes the critical need to detect anhedonia, offering a self-assessment quiz and a "Five V's" framework for emotional well-being, as it represents an existential crisis not to be ignored.
