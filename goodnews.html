2026-10-01 18:34:15 EDT

A new law in Washington D.C., known as "Roscoe's Law," now prohibits landlords from refusing tenants' dogs based on breed, size, or weight, and bans related special charges. This legislation, which took full effect on Thursday, aims to reduce barriers to pet ownership and prevent the separation of pets from their owners, a move applauded by animal welfare organizations. Historically, certain breeds faced restrictions due to aggression concerns, but animal groups argue behavior is linked to owner management, not breed. Landlord associations, however, express concerns that current caps on pet security deposits and rent may not adequately cover potential pet-related damages.
