UK Proposes Relaxing Criminal Record Disclosure for Childhood Offenders

2026-11-12 12:24:48 EST

The UK government is proposing reforms to allow some childhood offenders to no longer declare past criminal convictions to employers, aiming to improve their employment prospects. Under the new guidelines, certain drug-related and violent crimes committed by individuals under 18 would cease to appear on background checks after five-and-a-half years, rather than for life, provided they haven't re-offended as adults. This initiative recognizes that many young people who offend do not continue a life of crime and struggle to find work due to their past. The most serious offenses, such as severe violence or sexual offenses, would remain exempt from these changes, which are part of a broader effort to support rehabilitation and reduce barriers to employment.

EU Proposes Digital Fairness Act to Combat Online Consumer Manipulation

2026-11-12 12:19:00 EST

The European Commission has proposed a Digital Fairness Act (DFA) aimed at making it harder to trick consumers online by banning manipulative marketing tactics, known as "dark patterns." This comprehensive reform seeks to give consumers greater control over their online time and spending, addressing issues like automatic subscription renewals and addictive game features. Key provisions include requiring explicit consent before charging for free trials, easier cancellation processes, and clear labeling of personalized ads. The proposal also targets features like "doomscrolling" and prohibits children from paying to win in online games. While industry groups express concerns about overregulation, proponents argue it simplifies compliance across the EU and protects consumers from exploitative practices.
