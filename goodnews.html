New Sea Spider Species Discovered Off Canadian Coastline

2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

Scientists from the University of British Columbia have announced the discovery of two novel species of sea spiders in the shallow waters of the Salish Sea, off the Canadian province of British Columbia. This represents the first such finding in the area in over a century. One newly identified species, Callipallene pilosuspedes, is characterized by its red eyes, triangular mouth, and specialized limbs used for self-grooming. The other, Tanystylum kiixin, named after a nearby Indigenous village, features shorter, stockier legs and was found covered in debris. The article explains that these marine arthropods, which are related to land spiders but are slow-moving and water-dependent, feed parasitically on other marine animals like hydroids.
