New Ice Age Amphibian Species Discovered in La Brea Tar Pits Collection

2026-08-06 22:22:43 EDT

Researchers at the La Brea Tar Pits and Museum in Los Angeles have announced a significant scientific discovery: an extinct ice age amphibian, identified as the spadefoot toad (Spea labreae). This remarkable find marks one of only two ice age amphibians ever discovered in the U.S., profoundly impacting the understanding of climate conditions at the end of the last ice age. The discovery was made by Dr. Alberto Cruz while meticulously reviewing rarely studied amphibian and reptile collections.
