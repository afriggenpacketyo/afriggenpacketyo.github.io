These marine arthropods, measuring about a centimeter, were collected near Vancouver and Quadra islands. One species, Callipallene pilosuspedes, features red eyes and unique grooming limbs, while Tanystylum kiixin, named after an Indigenous village, has shorter legs and relies on camouflage. The discovery highlights the ongoing potential for new species identification and the slow-moving, parasitic feeding habits of these fascinating creatures.
