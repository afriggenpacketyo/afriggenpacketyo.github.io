Agrivoltaics: Integrating Solar Panels with Agriculture for Dual Climate Benefits

2026-09-27 00:45:00 EDT

Agrivoltaics, an innovative strategy combining agriculture with solar energy infrastructure, is gaining traction for its dual benefits in climate mitigation and adaptation. This practice involves growing crops or grazing livestock beneath solar panels, which can provide shade for animals, protect crops from harsh elements, and even boost yields by creating cooler, damper microclimates. The panels themselves become more efficient when kept cool by underlying vegetation. While the potential is vast, with agrivoltaics on just 1% of European agricultural land potentially exceeding 2030 solar targets, adoption has been slow due to factors like cost, regional suitability, and the challenge of bridging cultural divides between farmers and solar developers.
