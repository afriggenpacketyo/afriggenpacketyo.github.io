California Bill Shields Immigrant Aid Workers' Home Addresses from Public Records

2026-08-22 21:37:25 EDT

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed Assembly Bill 2624 into law, establishing an address confidentiality program for workers and volunteers assisting immigrants. This legislation allows them to shield their home addresses from public records and criminalizes posting their personal information online with specific intent to incite imminent great bodily harm against them. The program, set to open in October 2027, requires applicants to document threats and register for a substitute address. Authored by Assemblymember Mia Bonta, the law aims to protect immigrant service providers facing intimidation and anti-immigrant rhetoric, though critics, including Assemblymember Carl DeMaio, raised concerns about potential restrictions on citizen journalists and free speech.
