Two New Species of Sea Spiders Discovered Off Canadian Coast

2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

Researchers in Canada have identified two previously unknown species of sea spiders in the shallow waters of the Salish Sea. These marine arthropods, part of the Pycnogonida class, were found between Washington State and British Columbia, marking the first discoveries in the area in over a century. The new species, Callipallene pilosuspedes and Tanystylum kiixin, exhibit unique characteristics such as red eyes and specialized limbs for grooming. Scientists note that sea spiders are distinct from their terrestrial counterparts, requiring aquatic environments and feeding on hydroids.
