Bipartisan US Senators Introduce Bill to Reform ICE Arrest and Detention Practices

2026-09-25 14:40:52 EDT

US Senators Dick Durbin and Alex Padilla have introduced the "Immigration Warrant Reform Act," a legislative proposal designed to limit warrantless or specious ICE arrests and prevent the indefinite detention of immigrants deemed non-dangerous. The bill mandates that immigration officers obtain administrative warrants detailing probable cause for detention and present them to individuals. It also requires bond hearings within 24 hours of warrantless arrests and judicial custody reviews every 60 days to ensure detainees are not held indefinitely. While aiming to prevent civil rights abuses, some advocates worry the bill still grants ICE broad discretion, particularly regarding definitions of "imminent danger."
