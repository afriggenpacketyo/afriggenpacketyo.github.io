California Proposition 5 Proposes Significant Reforms to State Recall Elections

2026-10-01 06:00:00 EDT

California's Proposition 5 proposes significant constitutional amendments to the state's recall election system, aiming to separate the vote on removing an official from the election of their replacement. Under the current system, both occur simultaneously, a process that cost over $200 million during Governor Gavin Newsom's 2021 recall challenge. If Proposition 5 passes, voters would first decide on the recall; if successful, the official would be replaced by appointment, succession, or a later special election, with the former officeholder still eligible to run again. Co-authored by a former state senator who was recalled, the measure seeks to eliminate political "gamesmanship" and reduce financial burdens.
