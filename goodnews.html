Key recommendations include a "fast track" for individuals with the most severe conditions, reducing unnecessary regular reassessments, and directing those without additional costs to appropriate support services. Magnitude 3.3 Earthquake Strikes Off Southern California Coast

2026-11-23 01:03:41 EST

A magnitude 3.3 earthquake was recorded Sunday evening off the coast of L.A. County’s South Bay, marking the fourth quake in the region in four days. The epicenter was located a few miles from El Segundo, Manhattan Beach, and Redondo Beach, with residents feeling the tremors as far as Thousand Oaks and Long Beach. The U.S. Geological Survey rated the quake as "weak," with no damage expected, and an intensity of 3 on the Modified Mercalli Scale, meaning it was noticeably felt indoors. This follows other recent quakes, including a magnitude 3.9 near Commerce and a 3.3 near San Pedro.
