Airbnb Implements New Rules Limiting Guest Cleaning Chores

2026-10-21 17:29:12 EDT

Airbnb is rolling out significant changes to its house rules, set to apply to all reservations booked from November 20 onwards, specifically limiting the cleaning chores hosts can impose on guests. Under the revised policy, guests will generally no longer be required to perform tasks like stripping beds, washing and putting away dishes, or taking trash offsite. Hosts will still be permitted to ask for simpler actions, such as placing used dishes in the sink, and are now explicitly prohibited from levying their own fines on guests. The company stated these updates are designed to make house rules clearer, more reasonable, and easier to manage, responding to community feedback about confusing and burdensome requests.
