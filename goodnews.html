2026-09-06 10:19:00 EDT

The small Canadian town of Gander, Newfoundland, is being remembered for its extraordinary acts of kindness and hospitality following the September 11, 2001, terrorist attacks. When US airspace was closed, 38 international flights were diverted to Gander, bringing 7,000 stranded passengers and crew members to a town of just 12,000 residents. Locals mobilized to provide food, shelter, and comfort, transforming schools and churches into temporary accommodations. This incredible display of human generosity, often referred to as a "9/12 musical," became the inspiration for the acclaimed Broadway musical 'Come From Away,' which has brought global attention to Gander's compassionate response.
