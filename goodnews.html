California Approves New 'Zone Zero' Fire-Safe Landscaping Rules for Millions of Homes

2026-08-19 16:13:51 EDT

California's Board of Forestry and Fire Protection has unanimously approved new 'Zone Zero' landscaping requirements for millions of homes in fire-prone areas, after nearly six years of debate. These rules mandate a one-foot vegetation-free zone immediately around homes and require the first five feet to be clear of combustible materials like firewood and certain mulches. Homeowners in State Responsibility Areas and Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones will have up to five years to comply, with new constructions adhering immediately. The regulations aim to enhance wildfire safety, building on existing defensible space and home hardening measures, following a decade of worsening wildfires.
