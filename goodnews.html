DoorDash Agrees to $131.5 Million Settlement for Underpaying New York Workers

2026-09-22 15:18:57 EDT

DoorDash has agreed to a $131.5 million settlement with New York City, resolving an investigation into the company's underpayment and delayed payments to its delivery workers. Mayor Zohran Mamdani hailed it as the city's largest labor enforcement action, accusing DoorDash of using "greedy algorithms" to circumvent a 2023 law designed to improve worker pay. The settlement includes nearly $115 million in relief for approximately 264,000 Dashers, alongside a $16.7 million civil fine. DoorDash acknowledged its errors, attributing them to technical issues and cross-city deliveries, and has committed to providing detailed pay data to the city for three years.
