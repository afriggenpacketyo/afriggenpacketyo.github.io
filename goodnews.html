Psychologist Offers Creative Strategies to Combat Performance Anxiety

2026-10-03 05:00:00 EDT

Psychologist Cindra Kamphoff, who works with top athletes, shares practical techniques from her book, "The Confidence Habit," to manage performance anxiety in high-stakes situations. One notable method, "learn, burn, return," encourages individuals to quickly process mistakes by learning from them, using a physical cue like a "pretend flush" to move on, and then rebuilding self-confidence. Other strategies include crafting "power phrases" for self-affirmation, setting tiered "gold, silver, and bronze" goals to reduce pressure, and fact-checking self-doubt with critical questions. These tools aim to help people perform at their best by transforming self-doubt into self-belief, emphasizing confidence as a learnable skill.
