Study Suggests Chinese Script Evolved Organically Through Natural Selection

2026-09-01 00:00:05 EDT

New research indicates that Chinese characters evolved in a manner akin to living organisms, driven by principles of natural selection and usage frequency. A study published in Digital Scholarship in the Humanities reveals that a small set of core components are repeatedly combined to create new characters, with new elements invented when existing ones were insufficient. Researchers propose that this evolutionary pressure led to a system favoring high-frequency components, mirroring a linguistic tendency towards efficiency. The study suggests that simplification of characters over time, from ancient forms to modern simplified versions, was partly motivated by the desire to make writing easier and was influenced by how often characters were used.
