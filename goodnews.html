Understanding England's 9-1 Numerical Grading System for GCSEs

2026-08-20 01:33:02 EDT

England's GCSEs now utilize a numerical 9-1 grading system, a significant overhaul introduced alongside curriculum reforms in 2014 and fully rolled out across all subjects by 2020. The highest grade is 9, while 1 is the lowest, with a 'U' indicating ungraded; a grade 4 is considered a standard pass. This system replaced the previous A-to-U letter scale, though the two align at certain points, such as a grade 7 corresponding to the bottom of an A, and a 4 to the bottom of a C. The new scheme aims for greater differentiation among high-achieving students and to clarify the rigor of new qualifications for employers.
