Researchers Discover Two New Species of Sea Spiders Off Canadian Coastline

2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

Researchers in Canada have announced the exciting discovery of two new species of sea spiders in the shallow waters of the Salish Sea, located between Washington State and British Columbia. This marks the first such finding in the area in over a century. These marine arthropods, approximately one centimeter across, were collected near Vancouver and Quadra islands. One species, named Callipallene pilosuspedes, is characterized by its red eyes, a triangular mouth, and specialized ovigers for self-grooming. The other, Tanystylum kiixin, named after an ancient Indigenous village, possesses shorter, stockier legs and feeds parasitically on hydroids. This significant discovery by the University of British Columbia team underscores the rich, undiscovered biodiversity along the Canadian coastline.
