Chinese Astronomers Discover Real-Life Three-Star System

2026-10-22 21:00:06 EDT

Chinese researchers, utilizing the world's largest single-dish radio telescope, FAST, have discovered an "extremely rare" three-star system. Located approximately 7,000 light-years from Earth, this system comprises two dead stars—a rapidly spinning pulsar and a white dwarf—orbiting each other every eight days, alongside a "living" star in a remarkably stable cosmic arrangement. This scientific breakthrough offers a real-life counterpart to the chaotic fictional "Three-Body Problem" depicted in Liu Cixin's renowned science fiction novel. The actual system demonstrates a far more predictable celestial dance than its literary inspiration, highlighting the universe's diverse and complex structures.
