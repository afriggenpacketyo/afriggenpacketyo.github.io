Prehistoric Mammal Fossils Discovered at Southern California Construction Site

2026-09-24 06:00:00 EDT

Construction crews in Oceanside, Southern California, have unearthed fossilized remains of a massive, hoofed mammal from approximately 40 million years ago. Discovered 12 feet below the surface during a routine water infrastructure project, the limb bones and fragments likely belong to either a Brontotheriidae ("thunder beast") or an amynodon ("swamp rhino"), both extinct families of large herbivores that roamed North America. The find, within the fossil-rich Santiago Formation, adds to Southern California's significant paleontological history, which frequently surfaces during modern development. Scientists hope to uncover teeth or a lower jaw as construction continues, which would be key to further identifying the intriguing prehistoric animal.
