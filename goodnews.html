Betye Saar, Influential Assemblage Artist Who 'Liberated' Aunt Jemima, Dies at 99

2026-07-27 17:30:17 EDT

Betye Saar, the acclaimed Los Angeles assemblage artist, has died at the age of 99, just days before her 100th birthday. Saar was celebrated for her powerful works that transformed bigoted imagery, such as Sambo dolls and Aunt Jemima figurines, into potent critiques of race in America. Her seminal 1972 piece, "The Liberation of Aunt Jemima," featuring a rifle-wielding mammy, became a symbol of the Black Arts Movement and influenced generations of artists. Saar, known for her use of found objects to explore themes of family, memory, magic, and spirituality, continued creating art well into her nineties, leaving behind a profound legacy.
