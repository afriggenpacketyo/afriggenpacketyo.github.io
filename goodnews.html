President Trump reiterates support for a united Ireland during visit

2026-09-13 14:09:56 EDT

During his visit to Ireland, U.S. President Donald Trump reiterated his support for a united Ireland, stating it would be "natural" to bring Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland together. He made these remarks while attending the Irish Open at his golf resort in Doonbeg, County Clare. Trump described his comments as "a fairly routine comment" and noted that "many people agree" with his sentiment. The Taoiseach, Micheál Martin, acknowledged Trump's perspective, stating that the president believes unity is the "logical way for the island to be configured." The president also announced the lifting of tariffs on Irish whiskey during a speech at the tournament. The visit was met with protests in Dublin and Doonbeg, with demonstrators expressing opposition to the U.S. president.

Record donations to Reform UK spark debate on political funding

2026-09-13 13:51:19 EDT

Reform UK has received two record-breaking donations totaling £72 million from cryptocurrency billionaires Christopher Harborne and Ben Delo, significantly boosting its financial war chest. This influx of funds is intended for hiring policy experts and campaign managers across target seats. The substantial donations have intensified the debate surrounding political donations in the UK, with calls for caps on overseas and domestic contributions. Labour plans to introduce a cap on donations from British citizens living overseas, and discussions are ongoing about potential caps for UK-based donors. While Reform UK asserts the donations are legal, the government is proposing changes to donation laws, including a backdated cap, which could impact the legality of these contributions. The situation raises questions about the influence of large financial contributions on the political landscape.
