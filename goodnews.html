India's central bank maintains benchmark interest rates citing moderate core inflation

2026-08-05 01:50:04 EDT

India's central bank has kept its benchmark interest rates steady at 5.25% for the fifth consecutive meeting, despite headline inflation exceeding the target. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) noted that core inflation remains moderate and is expected to decline. Governor Sanjay Malhotra indicated that future policy actions would depend on evolving growth-inflation dynamics and the need for recalibration. The bank cited uncertainties from the monsoon, geopolitics, and global trade policy as factors influencing the growth outlook. Concerns remain about food prices as a significant risk to inflation, with experts monitoring broad-based inflationary pressures.
