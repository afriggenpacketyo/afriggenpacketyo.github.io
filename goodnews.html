California Updates License Plate Sequence After Running Out of Combinations

2026-09-11 18:59:37 EDT

California has exhausted its current license plate combinations, which have been in use since 1980, necessitating a new sequence for the first time in 46 years. The previous format, consisting of one number, three letters, and three numbers, has officially concluded. The new configuration will feature three numerals, followed by three letters and then one numeral (e.g., 000AAA0), and is expected to last another 40 years. While new plates are being produced by inmates at Folsom State Prison, over 110,000 plates from the old series still remain to be issued to drivers.
