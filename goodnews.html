Homeowners Insurance and HVAC Replacement: What's Covered

2026-07-30 12:42:58 EDT

Homeowners insurance typically covers HVAC replacement only when the damage results from a sudden, accidental event listed in the policy, such as a fire or lightning strike, not from gradual wear and tear or mechanical failure. Prolonged use during extreme heat, while stressing the system, is generally not an insurable event. Maintenance-related issues and normal aging of the unit are also typically excluded. Homeowners should review their specific policy details to understand coverage limitations, as insurers may opt for repairs over full replacement if feasible. Supplemental options like home warranties may offer broader coverage for mechanical breakdowns.
