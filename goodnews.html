This article provides essential information for retirement savers considering a Gold Individual Retirement Account (IRA), especially as gold prices remain historically high amidst economic uncertainty and geopolitical tensions. A Gold IRA is a distinct, self-directed account requiring investors to choose custodians and dealers, and adhere to strict IRS purity standards for eligible metals, such as 99.5% pure gold. Unlike traditional IRAs, physical gold cannot be stored at home and must be held in an IRS-approved depository, incurring various setup, annual custodian, and storage fees.
