Explaining the Autumn Equinox and its Cultural and Astronomical Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The autumn equinox, marking the official start of astronomical autumn, is scheduled to occur in the early hours of Wednesday, September 23rd, 2026, at precisely 01:06 BST. This specific moment denotes when the Sun crosses the celestial equator, resulting in a nearly equal duration of day and night across the globe. While the equinox itself is a purely astronomical phenomenon, it holds deep cultural and spiritual significance for many, often commemorated with gatherings at ancient sites like Stonehenge. The true equality of day and night, known as the "equilux," typically follows a few days later due to atmospheric light refraction.
