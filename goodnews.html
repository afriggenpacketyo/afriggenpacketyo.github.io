Chinese Scientists Develop Self-Powered Neural Device to Aid Stroke Survivors' Mobility

2026-09-14 04:00:09 EDT

Scientists in Hong Kong and mainland China have developed a groundbreaking, lightweight, and battery-free neural device to enhance mobility for individuals with walking difficulties, particularly stroke survivors. This innovative neural prosthesis operates by harvesting energy directly from the wearer’s own movements, which then electrically stimulates their muscles. This process effectively helps to flex the ankle, thereby restoring a more natural and efficient walking pattern. Early trials have shown that the device significantly increases stride length and walking speed during both indoor treadmill and outdoor walking tests, offering substantial promise for rehabilitation.
