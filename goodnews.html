Advanced Technology Aids Restoration of Italy's Basilica of Saint Francis

2026-10-04 20:05:48 EDT

Italy's Basilica of Saint Francis of Assisi, which suffered extensive damage from 1997 earthquakes, is now undergoing a groundbreaking restoration effort utilizing advanced digital technology and artificial intelligence. Art historians and software engineers are collaborating with Franciscan friars to meticulously reassemble over 80,000 fragments and create an unprecedented high-resolution 3D digital twin of the 13th-century church. This innovative project, spearheaded by Haltadefinizione, employs gigapixel cameras, drones, and lasers to capture minute architectural and artistic details, enabling rapid analysis of structural damage and providing an exact blueprint for future repairs. Beyond preservation, the initiative also aims to enhance public accessibility to the basilica's invaluable art through immersive virtual reality experiences.
