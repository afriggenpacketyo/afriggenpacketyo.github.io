Dry Eyes, a Common Condition in Women, Should Not Be Ignored

2026-09-04 05:00:00 EDT

Dry eyes are a chronic and highly common condition, particularly affecting women in perimenopause and beyond, with about half experiencing symptoms. Hormonal changes, specifically declining androgen levels, impact the meibomian glands, leading to insufficient or poor-quality oil production that causes tears to evaporate quickly. Staring at screens, wearing contacts, and certain medications also contribute to the condition. Experts warn against ignoring symptoms, as the disease can worsen, causing blurry vision and increasing risks of infection and corneal scarring. A range of treatments and lifestyle changes can offer relief, making early consultation with an eye care doctor crucial.
