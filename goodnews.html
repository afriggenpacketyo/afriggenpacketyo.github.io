Understanding Legal Limits on Debt Collectors Re-aging Old Debts

2026-08-06 10:42:23 EDT

This article explains that debt collectors are generally prohibited by law from "re-aging" old debts, which means improperly changing the reporting timeline to make them appear newer. Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, the original date of delinquency dictates how long negative information can remain on a credit report, typically seven years, and this cannot be reset by debt transfers. The Fair Debt Collection Practices Act also forbids misrepresenting a debt's legal status. Borrowers are advised to dispute any discrepancies, as re-aged debts can negatively impact credit scores and borrowing options, and even small payments can inadvertently restart the statute of limitations in some states.
