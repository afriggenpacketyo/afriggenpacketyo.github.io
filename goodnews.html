The bone, identified as a sacrum (the triangular bone at the base of the spine), was matched through DNA analysis to a sample obtained from Raxa's son three decades prior. This poignant discovery underscores the critical importance of preserving DNA samples from family members in missing person investigations, ultimately providing answers after years of profound uncertainty.
