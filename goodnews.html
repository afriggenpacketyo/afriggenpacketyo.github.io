Wayward SpaceX Rocket Booster Set to Impact Moon at 5,400 MPH

2026-08-04 18:28:42 EDT

An 8,800-pound SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket booster, which became wayward after launching lunar landers in January 2025, is projected to impact the moon early Wednesday morning at a speed of 5,400 mph, near the Einstein and Bell craters. SpaceX confirmed it followed proper disposal regulations for the second stage, but a combination of solar activity and gravitational forces unexpectedly shifted its trajectory towards the lunar surface. The collision, which will not be visible to the naked eye, is estimated to have the force of 2.8 tons of TNT and is expected to create a crater approximately 60-100 feet wide and 16 feet deep. NASA plans to utilize its Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter to capture before-and-after images, aiming to collect valuable data and refine techniques for tracking objects in space.
