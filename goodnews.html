Agrivoltaics: Integrating Solar Panels and Agriculture for Dual Benefits

2026-09-25 05:31:00 EDT

Agrivoltaics, the practice of growing crops or grazing livestock beneath solar panels, is emerging as a beneficial strategy for both agriculture and renewable energy. This innovative approach provides shade for crops and animals, conserves water, and can even boost crop yields by creating cooler, damper microclimates. Additionally, cooler panels operate more efficiently, generating more electricity. While the potential for agrivoltaics is significant, especially in adapting to climate change, its adoption has been slow globally, representing less than 1% of the world's solar capacity. This article highlights that challenges include regional suitability, installation costs, and farmer education.
