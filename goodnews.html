Understanding and Managing Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD)

2026-10-31 05:01:00 EDT

Seasonal Affective Disorder (SAD), a condition affecting about 1 in 20 Americans, causes feelings of sluggishness, depression, and overeating during darker winter months. Psychiatrist Dr. Norman Rosenthal, who coined the term in 1984, emphasizes recognizing symptoms like a desire to hibernate and taking them seriously. Effective strategies for managing SAD include light therapy using SAD lamps, maintaining consistent eating and sleep schedules, and engaging in regular physical activity. Reducing stress through enjoyable activities or therapy is also crucial. While not a cure, these proactive measures can significantly alleviate symptoms and improve overall well-being.
