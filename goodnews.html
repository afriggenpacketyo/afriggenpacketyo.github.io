Chinese Team Discovers World's Oldest Amber in Xinjiang Coal Seam

2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

A team of Chinese palaeontologists, led by Luo Cihang from the Chinese Academy of Sciences, has unearthed the world's oldest known amber, discovered within an ancient Xinjiang coal seam. Dating back to the Middle Devonian period, approximately 393 to 383 million years ago, this remarkable discovery significantly predates the previously oldest confirmed amber by nearly 70 million years. Initially skeptical of finding such ancient samples, the researchers found hundreds of microscopic pieces, which were subsequently chemically analyzed to confirm their age. This groundbreaking find offers invaluable new insights into ancient ecosystems and the evolution of resin-producing plants.
