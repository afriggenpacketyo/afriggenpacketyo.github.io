Scientists Identify Blood Biomarker for Potential CTE Diagnosis in Living Patients

2026-11-13 23:00:33 EST

Researchers have identified a blood biomarker, eMTBR-tau243, that shows promise for diagnosing chronic traumatic encephalopathy (CTE) in living individuals. CTE, a degenerative brain disease linked to repeated head trauma, is currently only diagnosable post-mortem. The study found elevated levels of this protein fragment in individuals with confirmed CTE, and its levels correlated with disease severity. While the test is not yet validated for early-stage CTE, it could significantly aid in monitoring at-risk individuals and facilitating drug trials. This breakthrough offers hope for earlier detection and potential treatment of CTE, a condition prevalent in contact sports like American football and rugby.
