This disorientation occurs because their internal biological clocks fall out of sync with the sun's position in the sky, which they use for navigation, resulting in minor but measurable deviations from their intended course. This intriguing finding expands our understanding of animal behavior and biological rhythms, demonstrating that the challenges of navigating time zones are not unique to humans.
