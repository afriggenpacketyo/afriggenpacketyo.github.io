Researchers Identify Potential New Depression Subtype Linked to Circadian Rhythms

2026-11-07 15:28:59 EST

Scientists have identified a newly proposed subtype of depression, termed "circadian depression," characterized by specific sleep habits linked to the body's internal clock. A study involving over 11,000 Australian adults with a history of depression found approximately 23% met the criteria, which include being an evening person, experiencing significant morning fatigue, and sleeping longer during depressive episodes. Published in The British Journal of Psychiatry, this research suggests that sleep-wake disruption could be an underlying cause rather than merely a symptom of depression, potentially leading to more personalized treatment approaches.
