Wayward SpaceX Rocket Booster on Course for Uncontrolled Lunar Impact

2026-08-04 17:43:42 EDT

An 8,800-pound SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket booster is projected to collide with the moon early Wednesday at 5,400 mph, creating a new crater near the Einstein and Bell craters. This rare, uncontrolled impact by a man-made object, influenced by solar activity and gravitational forces, poses no threat to Earth. NASA and independent astronomers confirmed its trajectory, and scientists anticipate the impact will leave a crater up to 100 feet wide and 16 feet deep. The Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter will capture before-and-after images, providing valuable data to refine techniques for tracking space objects and understanding lunar geology.
