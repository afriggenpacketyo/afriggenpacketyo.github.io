Germany Debates Future of "Minijobs" Amid Calls for Reform

2026-08-01 04:49:00 EDT

Germany is considering significant reforms to its "minijob" system, a form of part-time employment allowing workers to earn up to €603 monthly without social insurance or income tax contributions. The government plans to raise the flat tax rate and potentially eliminate the opt-out option for pension contributions, effectively ending minijobs in their current form. Proponents argue this would strengthen social security, reduce old-age poverty, and address gender inequalities, as women disproportionately hold these jobs. However, business representatives warn against abolishing minijobs, citing their importance for operational flexibility and accessing additional labor potential.
