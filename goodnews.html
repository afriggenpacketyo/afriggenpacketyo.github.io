China Strengthens Infrastructure Dominance in Africa Amidst Collapsed Deals

2026-08-02 04:00:18 EDT

Scottish Photographer Explores Diverse Redhead Identity Across America

2026-08-02 03:58:51 EDT

Scottish photographer Kieran Dodds, a "proud ginger," has expanded his decade-long project documenting redheads to the United States, culminating in his new book, "Gingers of America." His curiosity was sparked by a Native American man with red hair, leading him to explore European ancestries among various tribes, revealing early Scottish and Irish intermarriages. Dodds also encountered diverse redheads in unexpected places like Birmingham, Alabama, challenging stereotypes and highlighting the unifying "golden thread" of ginger hair across America's complex history. He estimates approximately 14 million redheads reside in the U.S., a figure higher than the global average.
