UN Calls for New World Map to Accurately Represent Continent Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has passed a "Correct the Map" resolution, advocating for the replacement of the widely used Mercator projection with the Equal Earth map to more accurately depict the true sizes of continents. The Mercator map, developed in 1569 for nautical navigation, significantly distorts landmasses, making regions near the poles appear much larger than they are, while those near the equator, such as Africa, seem considerably smaller. Critics argue this long-standing distortion carries psychological and political implications, potentially downplaying the vast resources and importance of continents like Africa. While the Equal Earth map successfully addresses area accuracy, it inherently sacrifices the straight lines crucial for navigation, highlighting the enduring cartographic challenge of faithfully representing a three-dimensional globe on a two-dimensional surface.
