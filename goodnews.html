Maradona's 'Hand of God' Football Sells for £2.5 Million at Auction

2026-08-24 02:55:06 EDT

Tunisian referee Ali Bin Nasser, who officiated the match, had kept the ball for over three decades and confirmed its authenticity. New Zealand Proposes Social Media Ban for Children Under 16

2026-08-24 01:45:00 EDT

New Zealand's government is set to introduce legislation banning social media access for children under 16, following similar moves by other countries. The proposed Online Safety Bill would mandate platforms like Instagram and TikTok to verify users' ages, with non-compliance potentially incurring fines up to 10% of global revenue. Prime Minister Christopher Luxon emphasized the significant harm to children's mental health, sleep, and education as the driving force behind the ban. However, the bill's future is uncertain due to opposition from coalition partners questioning its effectiveness, despite Australia's similar ban showing limited impact on teen social media use.

Three Rescued Beluga Whales Arrive at SeaWorld San Diego

2026-08-23 23:32:01 EDT

Three rescued beluga whales have successfully completed a 2,500-mile journey from Canada to SeaWorld San Diego, following the 2024 closure of Marineland in Niagara Falls. This relocation is part of an "unprecedented international whale rescue effort" to find suitable homes for 30 belugas. The whales were transported via a Qatar Airways Boeing 777 cargo plane and will join other belugas at SeaWorld San Diego. This initiative emphasizes individualized care and observation during the transition, with teams focused on ensuring the welfare and adaptation of each animal. The park plans to assess their health before their public introduction.
