Vietnam Signs Free Trade Agreement with Four European EFTA Countries

2026-10-18 05:19:00 EDT

Vietnam has signed a free trade agreement with the European Free Trade Association (EFTA), comprising Norway, Switzerland, Iceland, and Liechtenstein, after talks that began in 2012. This landmark deal will waive or reduce customs duties on a wide range of industrial, fish, and agricultural products. Notably, approximately half of Norway's exports to Vietnam will immediately become tariff-free, with over 98% achieving this status within seven years. The agreement aims to strengthen the rules-based trading system, diversify Vietnam's import and export markets, and deepen its integration into global value chains, fostering economic growth for Southeast Asia's fastest-growing economy.
