Rwanda Genocide Accused Faces £100,000 Bail Condition in UK

2026-09-28 07:15:06 EDT

Dr. Vincent Brown, 65, accused of offenses related to the 1994 Rwanda genocide, will remain in custody unless he pays a £100,000 guarantee for conditional bail. Brown, formerly Dr. Vincent Bajinya, is the first person in the UK charged over the genocide, facing allegations of leading or encouraging the deaths of Tutsi ethnic group members. The court imposed stricter bail conditions, including electronic monitoring and daily police reporting, in addition to the increased surety. Brown denies wrongdoing. The case highlights the long reach of justice for crimes against humanity, even decades after the events, and the UK's commitment to investigating such allegations.
