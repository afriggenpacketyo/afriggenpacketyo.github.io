Japan Launches MMX Mission to Mars Moon Phobos for Sample Return

2026-10-20 03:16:00 EDT

Japan's uncrewed Martian Moons eXploration (MMX) mission successfully launched from Tanegashima Space Center, embarking on a journey to Mars' moons. Expected to reach Mars by August 2027, the mission aims to collect at least 10 grams of material from Phobos, the larger of the planet's two moons, and deploy the German-French IDEFIX rover for surface exploration. Scientists hope these samples will unlock mysteries about the formation of Mars and its moons, offering paradigm-shifting science for generations and aiding in understanding the Solar System's origins. The mission also explores using Phobos as a "natural space station" and is a collaborative effort involving US and European space agencies.
