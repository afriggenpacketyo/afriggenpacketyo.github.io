Germany Intensifies Crackdown on Small Boat Smuggling Gangs with New Law

2026-08-18 01:02:58 EDT

Germany has conducted its first raids under a new law specifically targeting gangs that facilitate small boat crossings across the English Channel, the BBC has learned. A joint operation involving the UK, France, and Germany seized over 9,000 life jackets, dozens of dinghies, engines, and pumps this spring in North Rhine-Westphalia, enough equipment to transport more than 2,000 migrants. The new law, passed last December, criminalizes migrant smuggling to the UK, closing a loophole that previously only applied to EU nations. This marks a significant step in disrupting supply chains, as gangs often store equipment in western Germany before moving it to northern France for crossings.
