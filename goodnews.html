Researchers at the La Brea Tar Pits and Museum have announced the discovery of an extinct ice age amphibian, named the spadefoot toad, or Spea labreae. This significant find marks only the second ice age amphibian ever identified in the United States, making it a rare paleontological event. Dr. Alberto Cruz made the discovery while re-examining rarely studied collections, highlighting how historical specimens can yield new insights with fresh analysis. Amphibians are crucial indicators of past climates due to their sensitivity to environmental changes, and this finding contributes greatly to understanding the region's ancient ecosystem and its evolution.
