BepiColombo Mission Successfully Enters Orbit Around Mercury After Eight-Year Journey

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a joint European and Japanese endeavor, successfully placed a pair of spacecraft into orbit around Mercury after an eight-year cruise through the inner solar system. This $2 billion mission aims to unravel the planet's mysteries, with the two robotic explorers separating next month to map and study Mercury's dense interior, magnetic field, and surface features from different altitudes. BepiColombo is only the third spacecraft to visit Mercury and the first in over a decade, following NASA's Mariner 10 and Messenger missions. The mission faces significant technical challenges due to Mercury's extreme temperatures, but scientists anticipate collecting unprecedented comparative data for at least one Earth year, marking a major scientific achievement.
