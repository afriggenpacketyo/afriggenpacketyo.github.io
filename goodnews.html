German Asylum Applications Increase Slightly but Remain Significantly Lower Than Previous Years

2026-10-04 04:29:00 EDT

Germany experienced a rise in first-time asylum applications in September compared to the preceding month, yet the overall numbers remain sharply lower than those recorded in recent years. The Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF) received 5,276 first-time applications, marking a significant 40% decrease from September 2025 and 70% from September 2024. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt credited this long-term decline to effective measures implemented by both Germany and the European Union. Conversely, subsequent asylum applications have seen an increase, partly influenced by a 2024 European Court of Justice ruling regarding Afghan women's refugee status, indicating evolving legal and humanitarian considerations.
