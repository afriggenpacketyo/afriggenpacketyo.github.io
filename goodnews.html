Leonid Meteor Shower Peaks Tuesday Night, Offering UK Stargazers Prime Viewing Opportunity

2026-11-17 04:03:08 EST

The Leonid meteor shower is set to reach its peak on Tuesday night, providing UK sky gazers with the best chance to witness the celestial event this year. Running until November 30, the shower is expected to show 10-15 meteors per hour during its peak activity into Wednesday morning. These fast-moving meteors, originating from dust particles left by Comet 55P/Tempel-Tuttle, are the fastest in the sky.
