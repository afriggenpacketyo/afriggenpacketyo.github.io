Stunning Images Capture August's Blood Moon Partial Lunar Eclipse

2026-08-28 04:27:50 EDT

The August full Moon, known as the Sturgeon Moon, graced the UK sky, offering a partial lunar eclipse that resulted in a reddish appearance. This phenomenon occurs when the Earth passes between the Sun and Moon, causing part of the Moon to enter the Earth's darkest shadow. Sunlight scattering through Earth's atmosphere allows longer red wavelengths to reach and reflect off the Moon, creating the distinctive "Blood Moon" effect. Historically, full Moons are named after natural events, with the Sturgeon Moon referencing the abundance of sturgeon fish in North American rivers during this period.
