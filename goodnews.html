While industry groups welcome the protection, they argue that high taxes and employment costs are the primary drivers of closures, warning that pubs could become empty premises if underlying economic issues are not addressed. High-Fat Western Diets Linked to Increased Colorectal Cancer Risk

2026-08-17 02:49:00 EDT

German researchers have established a causal link between high-fat, low-fiber Western diets and an increased risk of colorectal cancer, particularly in adults under 50. The study, published in the journal Gut, found that these diets sustain specific gut bacteria that produce deoxycholic acid (DCA), a secondary bile acid that promotes tumor growth. Experiments in mice demonstrated increased tumor growth and cell division in the presence of DCA-producing bacteria, with human stool samples corroborating the presence of these bacteria in cancer patients. The article emphasizes the importance of dietary choices and lifestyle factors in preventing this cancer, whose mortality rate in younger adults has been rising.
