Three Scientists Awarded Nobel Prize in Medicine for Optogenetics Research

2026-10-05 06:11:33 EDT

The Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine has been jointly awarded to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel for their groundbreaking research into optogenetics. Their pioneering work has illuminated the intricate mechanisms the brain employs to precisely control the activity of individual nerve cells through light-gated ion channels. This revolutionary method is now a fundamental tool in neuroscience laboratories worldwide, enabling scientists to unravel the complexities of neural circuits behind specific memories, feelings, and behaviors relevant to various disorders. The prestigious award, announced at the Karolinska Institute, recognizes their profound contributions, which are already being explored for clinical applications like restoring sight and represent a new era in understanding the human brain.
