New Ice Age Spadefoot Toad Species Discovered in La Brea Tar Pits

2026-08-06 22:22:43 EDT

Researchers at the La Brea Tar Pits and Museum in Los Angeles have identified a new extinct ice age amphibian, the spadefoot toad (Spea labreae), from existing collections. This discovery is significant as it represents only the second ice age amphibian species found in the U.S. The finding, made by Dr. Alberto Cruz while reviewing rarely studied specimens, helps scientists understand past climates, as amphibians are sensitive indicators of environmental change. The fragile nature of amphibian skeletons makes their preservation in the fossil record a rare occurrence, underscoring the importance of this discovery for reconstructing ancient local environments. More discoveries are expected from the ongoing renovations at the museum.
