UN Calls for New World Map to Accurately Reflect Continent Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The UN has called for the replacement of the widely used Mercator map with the Equal Earth projection, aiming for a more accurate representation of continent sizes. The Mercator map, created in 1569 for navigation, distorts landmasses, making regions near the equator appear smaller and those near the poles larger. Critics argue this distortion has psychological and political implications, implicitly downplaying continents like Africa. The Equal Earth map, adopted by the African Union, corrects area and shape but sacrifices straight lines and true distance measurement, highlighting the inherent challenges of projecting a sphere onto a flat surface.
