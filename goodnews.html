2026-10-16 11:01:00 EDT

The American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG) has updated its guidelines, now recommending hormone replacement therapy (HRT) as the first-line treatment for common menopause and perimenopause symptoms like hot flashes and night sweats. This marks a significant shift in advice after more than a decade, moving away from previous non-hormonal recommendations. HRT, available in various forms, aims to alleviate symptoms caused by declining estrogen and progesterone levels and may also help with bone density. The article emphasizes that while not suitable for everyone, particularly those with a history of certain medical conditions, HRT can be a safe and effective option for many women, especially when started before age 60 or within 10 years of menopause onset.
