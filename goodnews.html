Strong El Niño Leads to Record-Low Atlantic Hurricane Activity

2026-09-12 11:26:46 EDT

A powerful El Niño phenomenon is significantly contributing to a record-quiet Atlantic hurricane season, actively suppressing storm formation during what is typically the period of peak activity. Classic El Niño effects, including strong wind shear, dry air, and downward-pushing winds, are effectively dismantling potential tropical cyclones, even amidst unusually warm ocean waters. This has resulted in the lowest hurricane activity recorded in the satellite era. However, experts caution against complacency, recalling past catastrophic storms that formed late in quiet seasons, while noting that El Niño is concurrently fueling increased tropical cyclone activity in the Pacific.
