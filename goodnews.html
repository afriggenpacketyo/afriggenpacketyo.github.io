Conservation efforts are now focusing on restoring seabird populations, which have declined due to habitat loss and invasive species like rats, alongside traditional coral restoration projects. This integrated approach aims to enhance the resilience of vulnerable coral reefs against rising ocean temperatures and climate impacts.
