California Implements New License Plate Sequence After Exhausting Current Combinations

2026-09-11 18:59:37 EDT

California has officially run out of combinations for its current license plate sequence, which has been in continuous use since 1980, necessitating the introduction of a new format. The previous configuration, consisting of one number followed by three letters and three numbers (e.g., 1AAA000), has reached its maximum with the production of the 9ZZZ999 plate. The state's Department of Motor Vehicles has begun producing plates with the new sequence: three numerals, followed by three letters, and then one numeral (e.g., 000AAA0). While over 110,000 plates of the old format are still awaiting issuance, this change represents a significant administrative update, with the new configuration projected to last for another 40 years.
