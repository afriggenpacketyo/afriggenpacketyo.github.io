2026-09-02 00:00:08 EDT

However, these speeds are considerably slower than NASA's 2013 lunar laser communications demonstration, which reached 622 Mbps for downloads and 20 Mbps for uploads. Developed by the University of Birmingham and Nonacus, the Galeas test checks for genetic material shed by cancer cells into urine.
