Ming Dynasty Encyclopedia Reveals 17th-Century Chinese Worldview

2026-08-26 00:00:05 EDT

A monumental 127-volume encyclopedia, the Tushu Bian or Compendium of Illustrations and Texts, published in 1613, offers profound insights into how Chinese scholars understood the world during the Ming dynasty. Compiled by Neo-Confucian scholar Zhang Huang, this extensive work drew from over 200 sources to document concepts of heaven, Earth, and humanity. Information designer Paul Kahn highlighted its purpose: to gather all necessary knowledge for an educated person to comprehend their world. The compilation, too costly for Zhang Huang to print during his lifetime, was eventually published posthumously.
