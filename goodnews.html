Electric Vehicles Linked to Increased Motion Sickness for Some Passengers

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

A rising number of passengers are reporting increased motion sickness when traveling in electric vehicles (EVs), a phenomenon researchers attribute to a "mismatch in sensory cues." The lack of familiar engine sounds and vibrations, which typically signal acceleration, can disrupt the body's perception of movement, conflicting with visual input. Furthermore, the regenerative braking system in EVs, if not applied smoothly, can lead to abrupt decelerations that cause passengers to feel a "rocking back and forth" sensation. While not affecting all passengers, this issue is being acknowledged by some carmakers, prompting exploration into technological solutions like refined software and subtle sensory feedback to enhance passenger comfort and mitigate discomfort.
