The medication, taken daily, reduces protoporphyrin IX, the molecule activated by sunlight that causes painful reactions in EPP patients. Pearson's participation in Phase 2 of the trial led to a dramatic increase in her sun tolerance, allowing her to achieve her dream of running a marathon and enjoy outdoor activities without constant anxiety. Bitopertin is now in Phase 3 trials, with data expected by late 2026.
