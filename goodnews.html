Finland Pioneers World's Largest Sand Battery for Renewable Energy Storage

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in southern Finland is utilizing the world's largest commercial sand battery to address the intermittency challenge of renewable energy, storing clean energy as heat. The 13-meter-tall facility, using 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone, provides 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy, significantly reducing carbon emissions and wood chip use for local heating. Developed by Polar Night Energy, this innovative technology stores heat for days or weeks, discharging it via heat exchangers to warm water for the district heating network. The project showcases a promising, rare-earth-material-free solution for long-term energy storage, inspiring international interest despite scaling hurdles.
