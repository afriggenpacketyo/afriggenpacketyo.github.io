UK Explores Passivhaus Standard Homes to Combat Rising Temperatures

2026-08-21 19:56:48 EDT

As Britain experiences hotter summers, with over half of UK homes at risk of overheating, the Passivhaus international building standard is gaining traction as a solution. This standard focuses on designing airtight, well-insulated homes to maintain a constant indoor temperature, significantly reducing energy consumption. Architect Marion Baeli's retrofitted 1960s home, 10C cooler than outside on a 35C day, exemplifies its effectiveness, though retrofitting can be costly. While new builds incorporating Passivhaus measures are more affordable, adapting the existing housing stock remains a major challenge. Simple measures like shading windows are also advised for immediate relief.
