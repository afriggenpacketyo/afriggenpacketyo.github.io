Ancient Cave Art Discovery Links Ireland's First Humans to Wales

2026-09-05 01:52:05 EDT

A groundbreaking discovery of 15,000-year-old cave art in south-west Ireland suggests that the island's first human inhabitants likely migrated from Wales. Researchers involved in the "Lost Land Bridge Project" found eight cave sites, predating previous evidence of humans in Ireland by 5,000 years. During the last Ice Age, a land bridge connected Pembrokeshire and Rosslare, offering a pathway for hunter-gatherers to follow large herbivores across the then-forested plateau. This finding, aided by AI, redefines understanding of early human movement in north-west Europe.
