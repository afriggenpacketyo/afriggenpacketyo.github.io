2026-11-18 14:19:35 EST

This remarkable feat means radio signals now require a full 24 hours to travel between the probe and flight controllers at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory. Both Voyager 1 and its sister ship, Voyager 2, continue to beam back invaluable data from interstellar space about magnetic fields and plasma waves, even as their power gradually diminishes. The mission, a testament to human ingenuity, also carries a golden record containing a sampling of Earth's sights and sounds, a timeless message for any distant civilizations.
