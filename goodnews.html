UN General Assembly Votes to Adopt New World Map Reflecting Africa's True Size

2026-09-04 13:26:40 EDT

The UN General Assembly has voted to replace the traditional Mercator world map with a new projection that more accurately reflects the true size of Africa. The "Correct the Map" resolution, spearheaded by Togo and supported by 164 nations, passed despite a lone "no" vote from the United States and six abstentions. Critics have long argued that the Mercator map, in use since the 16th century, distorts the size of landmasses, particularly making Africa appear similar in size to Greenland, despite being 14 times larger. This change aims to address historical misrepresentations and highlight the importance of regions near the equator.
