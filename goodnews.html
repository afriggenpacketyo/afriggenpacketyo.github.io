Review Recommends Closer Link Between PIP Disability Benefit and Claimant Costs

2026-11-24 12:38:37 EST

A government-commissioned review has recommended that the Personal Independence Payment (PIP) disability benefit in England and Wales be more closely aligned with the additional costs faced by claimants. The review, led by Disability Minister Sir Stephen Timms, found the current system 'not appropriate' for individuals without extra costs. While the government has committed to some recommendations, such as fast-track payments for severe conditions, a full response is pending. The review also highlighted concerns about the 'dehumanizing' assessment process, suggesting a shift towards supporting independence rather than focusing on limitations. Some disability charities have welcomed the proposals, while also emphasizing that any changes must not financially disadvantage disabled individuals.

BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit After Eight-Year Journey Despite Glitch

2026-11-24 12:34:32 EST

The BepiColombo spacecraft has successfully entered Mercury's orbit after an eight-year journey, overcoming a technical glitch during its orbital insertion maneuver. Launched as a joint mission between the European Space Agency and the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, the spacecraft fired its thrusters to slow down and lock into orbit. Although a software issue caused the maneuver to be interrupted, leading to the spacecraft entering safe mode, mission control was able to confirm its successful orbital capture. BepiColombo will now make further adjustments to its orbit before releasing its science orbiters in December to begin studying Mercury's interior, surface, and space environment.
