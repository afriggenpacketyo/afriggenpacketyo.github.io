NASA's Voyager 1 Reaches New Milestone, One Light-Day Distance from Earth

2026-11-15 17:41:22 EST

NASA's Voyager 1 spacecraft is celebrating a new milestone, reaching a distance one light-day away from Earth, approximately 16 billion miles. Launched in 1977, the probe has traveled for nearly 50 years, conducting flybys of Jupiter and Saturn, where it discovered a faint ring system and captured detailed photos. Voyager 1, along with its twin, Voyager 2, are the only human-made objects operating outside the heliosphere, continuing their interstellar mission to measure magnetic fields, particles, and plasma waves. NASA is inviting the public to participate in a symbolic "Shine Bright for Voyager's Light-Day" celebration, using Morse code to send its call sign.
