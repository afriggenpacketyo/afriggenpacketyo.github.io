Finland Deploys World's Largest Sand Battery to Tackle Renewable Energy Intermittency

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A town in southern Finland has implemented the world's largest commercial sand battery to effectively address the intermittency of renewable energy sources. The 13-meter tall facility uses 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone to store 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy, providing substantial heat demand for local residents. Developed by Polar Night Energy, this project has significantly reduced greenhouse gas emissions and reliance on wood chips, showcasing a combustion-free heating solution for much of the year. While promising for regional heat storage and reducing dependence on rare earth materials, the technology still faces hurdles in efficiently converting heat back to electricity and its overall energy density.
