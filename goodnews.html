Dentists Note Rising Trend of Teeth Grinding Among Young People, Linked to Stress

2026-09-13 01:55:19 EDT

Dentists are reporting an anecdotal but noticeable rise in bruxism, commonly known as teeth grinding, particularly among young people, with many experts linking the condition directly to increased stress and anxiety levels in modern life. This prevalent and often intense issue can lead to excruciating jaw ache, persistent headaches, and significant long-term tooth damage, frequently going undiagnosed until severe symptoms manifest. Psychotherapists explain that the body's 'fight or flight' response to stress tenses muscles, including those in the jaw, leading to clenching and grinding. Effective treatments range from protective mouthguards and physiotherapy to talking therapies like CBT, and even muscle-relaxing Botox injections, offering relief and preventing further dental complications for sufferers.

New Denisovan Fossils in China Offer Insights into Ancient Human Hunting Practices

2026-09-12 22:00:10 EDT

New research from Bianfu Cave in southwest China has uncovered the most abundant Denisovan remains ever found outside of Siberia, significantly advancing scientific understanding of this enigmatic extinct human group. Published simultaneously in two papers in Nature, the findings, co-authored by researchers from institutions including the Chinese Academy of Sciences, reveal that Denisovans established a stable and highly adapted subsistence pattern within the region's coniferous forests and forest-steppe environments. This archaic human population demonstrated specialized hunting techniques and intensively exploited local animal resources, leading researchers to describe the area as an 'ancient hunting paradise.' These discoveries provide crucial insights into the behaviors and ecological adaptations of Denisovans, who diverged from modern humans and Neanderthals hundreds of thousands of years ago.
