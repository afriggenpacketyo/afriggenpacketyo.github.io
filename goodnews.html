Influential West Coast Pop Artist Tony Berlant Dies at 85

2026-10-28 20:04:13 EDT

Tony Berlant, a highly influential artist in the West Coast Pop Art movement of the 1960s, has died at 85 after a prolonged battle with Alzheimer’s disease. Berlant was renowned for his distinctive large-scale collages crafted from found metal shards, often incorporating vintage street signs and personal photographs, held together by thousands of nails. A key figure in L.A.'s iconic Ferus Gallery art scene, he exhibited alongside celebrated artists like Andy Warhol and Robert Rauschenberg. Beyond his artistic contributions, Berlant was also a notable collector of ancient pottery and co-curated museum exhibitions. He is survived by his wife and comedian daughter, Kate Berlant.
