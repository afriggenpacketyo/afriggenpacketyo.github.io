European Space Agency Deploys Two New Climate Monitoring Satellites

2026-09-15 03:28:00 EDT

The European Space Agency (ESA) has successfully launched two advanced climate observation satellites, Sentinel-3C and Flex, into sun-synchronous orbit from its Kourou space center. The Flex satellite, a €350 million mission, is designed to provide critical data on the impact of heat waves on plant life by monitoring the faint fluorescence emitted during photosynthesis, offering insights into plant health and ecosystem productivity under environmental stress. Concurrently, Sentinel-3C will collect comprehensive data from marine environments, including temperature and ocean currents, and monitor land use to optimize yields and prevent food crises. These missions are considered "essential" by ESA for enhancing global understanding and response to the pressing issue of climate change.
