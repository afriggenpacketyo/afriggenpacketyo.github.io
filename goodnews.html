Californians Over 80 to Permanently Opt Out of Jury Duty Starting 2028

2026-09-22 19:20:13 EDT

Beginning January 1, 2028, Californians aged 80 and older will be able to permanently opt out of jury duty without needing medical documentation, thanks to Assembly Bill 1359. The legislation cites health concerns, vulnerability to pathogens, and mobility issues common in older demographics as justification. This change expands upon existing rules that allowed individuals over 70 to be excused for medical reasons upon selection. The bill, which passed both the state Assembly and Senate unanimously, aims to streamline the process for seniors who wish to be excused. Governor Gavin Newsom signed the bill into law, recognizing the realities faced by many older adults.
