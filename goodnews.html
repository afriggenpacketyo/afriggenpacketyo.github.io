2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

Found preserved in an ancient coal seam in Xinjiang, this amber dates back to the Middle Devonian period, approximately 393 to 383 million years ago. This finding, detailed in the peer-reviewed journal *Science Advances*, significantly pushes back the record for chemically confirmed amber by over 70 million years. The researchers were initially skeptical when they encountered hundreds of microscopic pieces, as the previously oldest confirmed amber dated to the Late Carboniferous period, around 320 million years ago.

Understanding Attachment Theory and Pathways to Secure Relationships

2026-07-27 20:21:34 EDT

Attachment theory, initially developed by British psychiatrist John Bowlby in the 1950s, posits that the bonds formed with early caregivers profoundly influence our expectations and behaviors in adult relationships. The article elucidates the four primary attachment styles—anxious, avoidant, fearful-avoidant, and secure—describing how each impacts an individual's approach to intimacy and independence. Despite its surging popularity, especially on platforms like TikTok, experts warn against oversimplification and the common misconception that attachment styles are immutable. Research suggests that while secure attachment is the most stable, change is indeed possible, often occurring during life transitions, though more entrenched patterns may necessitate professional guidance, affirming that individuals can evolve beyond their initial attachment patterns.
