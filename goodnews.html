2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

These marine arthropods, measuring about a centimeter, are related to land spiders but are slow-moving and require water to survive. One species, Callipallene pilosuspedes, features red eyes and unique grooming limbs, while Tanystylum kiixin, named after an Indigenous village, has shorter, stockier legs.
