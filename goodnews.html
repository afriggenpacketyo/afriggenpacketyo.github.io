Historical Context: Mississippi's Black US Senators Before Scott Colom's Recent Win

2026-11-12 09:12:55 EST

Scott Colom's recent electoral victory in Mississippi marks a truly historic milestone, establishing him as the state's first Black US senator in over 150 years and only the third in its history. He follows the pioneering figures of Hiram Revels, who served from 1870-1871 as the first Black senator, and Blanche Bruce, who completed a full term from 1875-1881. Both Revels and Bruce served during the Reconstruction era, actively campaigning for racial equality, before the oppressive Jim Crow laws were enacted. This article provides vital historical context to Colom's groundbreaking achievement.
