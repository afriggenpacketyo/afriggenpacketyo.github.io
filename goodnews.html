California Supreme Court Allows Recording of Civil Hearings Lacking Court Reporters

2026-08-10 15:13:16 EDT

This decision aims to ensure access to judicial proceedings for indigent litigants, addressing a significant shortage of court reporters that has left over a million hearings unrecorded annually. The ruling acknowledges the importance of a verbatim record for appeals, particularly in sensitive cases like domestic violence disputes. While criminal proceedings still require official transcripts, this change is expected to improve access to justice for many.
