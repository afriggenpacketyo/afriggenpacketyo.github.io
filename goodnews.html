Orionid Meteor Shower Active Until November 7, Peaks October 21-22

2026-10-02 03:16:45 EDT

The Orionid meteor shower, celebrated for its exceptionally fast meteors and the brief luminous trails they leave behind, is currently active and visible until November 7, with its peak viewing period expected overnight from October 21 to 22. This annual celestial spectacle occurs as Earth traverses through the dusty remnants shed by Halley's Comet, a famous comet last observed from Earth in 1986 and not anticipated to reappear until 2061. Under optimal conditions, observers can anticipate witnessing approximately 10 to 20 meteors per hour during the shower's zenith. For the best viewing experience, finding a dark location away from city lights with a wide, unobstructed view of the night sky is recommended.
