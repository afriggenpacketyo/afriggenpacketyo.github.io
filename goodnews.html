Job Applicants Not Obligated to Disclose Salary History in UK Interviews

2026-10-04 19:22:07 EDT

In the UK, job applicants are not legally obligated to disclose their current salary during interviews, even though employers are within their rights to ask. This advice comes as new regulations in the EU will prevent such questions, and campaigns like #EndSalaryHistory advocate for fairer pay practices. The Fawcett Society, a gender equality group, argues that asking about salary history risks perpetuating historic pay disparities based on gender, race, and disability. Instead of revealing past earnings, experts recommend that candidates focus on their salary expectations, research the market rate for the role, and emphasize their skills, experience, and the value they can bring to the position. This empowers job seekers to negotiate a compensation package based on their qualifications rather than prior pay.
