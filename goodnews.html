Finland Pioneers World's Largest Commercial Sand Battery for Renewable Energy Storage

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in southern Finland is utilizing the world's largest commercial sand battery to address the intermittency challenge of renewable energy. The 13-meter-tall facility, using 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone, stores 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy, providing significant heat demand for 5,000 residents. Developed by Polar Night Energy, this project has drastically cut greenhouse gas emissions and reduced wood chip use for heating, offering a combustion-free solution for much of the year. The technology leverages cheap electricity to heat sand, storing energy for days or weeks, and offers a promising alternative to chemical batteries by avoiding rare earth materials.

Archaeological Discovery Challenges Origin of Silk Road Sericulture in Uzbekistan

2026-07-24 22:00:10 EDT

The findings indicate that silkworm cocoons and mulberry trees already existed in the region more than 2,000 years before the Silk Road officially opened in 138BC with Chinese diplomat Zhang Qian's envoy. This suggests a more complex and ancient history of silk production and trade in Central Asia, predating the established historical timeline for the famous trade route. The discovery re-evaluates the understanding of early global cultural and economic exchanges, indicating that knowledge and materials might have moved earlier or through different pathways.
