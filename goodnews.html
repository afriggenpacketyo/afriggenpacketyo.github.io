Homeowners Insurance Coverage for HVAC Replacement Explained

2026-07-30 12:42:58 EDT

This article clarifies when homeowners insurance typically covers HVAC replacement, emphasizing that coverage usually applies only if damage results from a "covered peril" like fire, lightning, windstorm, hail, or certain falling objects. It stresses that standard policies generally exclude damage from normal wear and tear, age, excessive use, or routine mechanical failure, which are common issues during prolonged heatwaves. The article advises homeowners that neglected maintenance, such as clogged drain lines or uncleaned filters, can lead to denied claims. Insurance may only cover repairs, not full replacement, if a component is salvageable. It also suggests considering home warranties or manufacturer warranties for additional protection against mechanical breakdowns, as well as regular maintenance.
