Article Clarifies Illegality of Debt Collectors Re-Aging Old Debts

2026-08-06 10:42:23 EDT

This article clarifies that debt collectors are generally prohibited by federal law, specifically the Fair Credit Reporting Act, from improperly "re-aging" old debts to extend their presence on credit reports. The original date of delinquency determines how long negative information can remain on a report, typically seven years, and cannot be reset by transferring the debt. Consumers are advised to verify dates on their credit reports and understand state-specific statutes of limitations. Discrepancies can be disputed with credit bureaus or directly with debt collectors.
