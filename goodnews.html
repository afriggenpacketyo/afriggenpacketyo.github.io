UK Government Outlines Plan to Abolish Leasehold System in England and Wales by 2031

2026-11-19 12:49:48 EST

The UK government has introduced a timetable to overhaul the leasehold system in England and Wales, with plans to transition most new flats to a commonhold model by 2029, effectively banning new leasehold flats by 2031. This reform aims to empower residents with collective ownership and management of their buildings, eliminating ground rents and lease expiry issues. Key measures include a £250 cap on ground rents by 2028, eventually reducing them to a nominal "peppercorn" rate after four decades. The Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill also simplifies the process for existing leaseholders to convert, requiring only 50% support. The government intends to align the UK with international norms where such shared ownership is standard.
