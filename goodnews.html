Study Warns Current Breast Cancer Screening Misses High-Risk Younger Women

2026-08-03 21:08:13 EDT

New research indicates that current NHS criteria for breast cancer screening in women under 50 are too restrictive, potentially missing up to 95% of those at higher risk. The guidelines, which primarily focus on inherited genes, overlook other significant factors like lifestyle, reproductive history, and broader genetic information. A new risk assessment tool, Boadicea, which incorporates these diverse factors, identified eight times more at-risk women in this age group who subsequently developed breast cancer. Given that breast cancer is a leading cause of death for women under 50 in the UK, a broader, more comprehensive screening approach could significantly improve early detection and outcomes.
