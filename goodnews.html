World Map Projections Re-evaluated for Accurate Representation of Landmass Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has called for a replacement of the predominant Mercator world map projection, which inaccurately represents continental sizes, particularly that of Africa. The Equal Earth projection, adopted by the African Union, aims to provide a more accurate depiction of landmasses, addressing the psychological and political implications of perceived size differences. While the Mercator map was historically useful for navigation due to its preservation of angles, it significantly distorts the size of regions further from the equator. The Equal Earth map, though better for area representation, sacrifices the straight-line navigation capabilities of Mercator. The article discusses the inherent trade-offs in map projections, emphasizing that no two-dimensional representation can perfectly capture a sphere's surface.
