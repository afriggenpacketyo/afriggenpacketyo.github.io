Scientists in Bolivia have identified a new species of wild cat, locally known as "tilcayo," in the Yungas forest region. This discovery marks the first new wild cat species described in 100 years, with the last being the Uruguayan pampas cat in 1923. The Leopardus tilcayo is small, measuring about 18 inches long and weighing around 3 pounds, with a distinctive leopard-like coat. Genetic analysis has confirmed its unique status. The discovery underscores the biodiversity still present in remote regions and highlights the risk of species disappearing before they are identified.
