2026-11-13 11:42:18 EST

This phenomenon, which can influence the jet stream, has historically been linked to colder, drier weather and increased snowfall in the UK, as seen with the 2018 Beast from the East. However, the early timing makes precise impacts uncertain, and it is not connected to current cold spells.
