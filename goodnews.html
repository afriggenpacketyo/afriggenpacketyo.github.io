New regulations to curb 'rip-off' private parking charges introduced

2026-10-20 03:44:52 EDT

New rules have been announced by Andy Burnham aimed at protecting drivers from what are described as "rip-off" private parking charges. These changes will grant drivers a guaranteed five-minute period to decide on parking and a 10-minute window to leave without penalty. Additionally, charges can be cancelled for issues beyond a driver's control, such as breakdowns, and for honest mistakes like stopping to check directions. Maximum charges on appeal will be capped at £20, with the overall maximum charge frozen at £100. Operators who fail to comply risk losing access to DVLA data, making enforcement more difficult.
