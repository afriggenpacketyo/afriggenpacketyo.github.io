Retirees face year-end deadline for required minimum withdrawals to avoid tax penalties

2026-11-21 09:30:02 EST

Retirees are reminded of the upcoming December 31st deadline for Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs) from their retirement accounts. Failing to take these mandatory withdrawals can result in a significant IRS penalty of up to 25% of the amount that should have been withdrawn. Most individuals must begin RMDs at age 73, with calculations based on account balances, age, and IRS life expectancy factors. Experts advise against waiting until the last minute to avoid calculation errors or overlooking accounts. While penalties can be reduced or waived for reasonable errors, proactive planning and timely correction are crucial. For those who donate to charity, Qualified Charitable Distributions (QCDs) offer a tax-efficient way to satisfy RMDs.
