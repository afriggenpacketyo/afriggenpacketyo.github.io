World's oldest shipwreck discovered off Turkish coast, containing ancient cargo

2026-10-14 13:44:53 EDT

Archaeologists have discovered what is believed to be the world's oldest shipwreck off the coast of Turkey, dating back 4,400 to 4,700 years. While the ship's structure has not survived, its cargo of approximately 30 ceramic jars, known as amphorae, has been remarkably preserved, with some still containing residue from their ancient contents. This trade ship, about 33 feet long, was likely transporting goods across the Mediterranean from the eastern Mediterranean coast. The discovery provides insights into ancient trading networks and seafarer life during the same era the Pyramids of Giza were being built. Analysis of the amphorae suggests they originated from the Jordan Valley and were likely carrying olive oil or wine. (not investment advice)
