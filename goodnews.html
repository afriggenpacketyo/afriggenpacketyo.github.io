Appeals Court Rules Against Prediction Markets, Signaling Supreme Court Battle

2026-08-28 17:57:47 EDT

The 9th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals has ruled against prediction market platforms, including Kalshi and Crypto.com, denying their requests to prevent Nevada's regulation of sports-related event contracts.
