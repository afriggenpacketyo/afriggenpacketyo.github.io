Knott’s Berry Farm and Great America Ticket Holders May Be Eligible for Refund in Class-Action Lawsuit

2026-11-13 00:18:22 EST

Millions of theme park visitors who purchased tickets for Knott's Berry Farm and the soon-to-close Great America may be eligible for an $8 refund as part of an $11 million class-action lawsuit settlement. The suit alleges that Six Flags Entertainment Corp., the parks' operator, engaged in "drip pricing" by not disclosing processing fees until the checkout stage. California residents who bought non-season tickets between June 28, 2020, and July 1, 2024, and were charged a processing fee are eligible. Claims must be filed by December 22, 2026.
