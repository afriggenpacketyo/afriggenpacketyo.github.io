2026-10-19 15:45:46 EDT

Japan has launched its Mars Moons eXploration (MMX) mission, an ambitious project aiming to collect and return a sample of moondust from Phobos, one of Mars's two small moons. Expected to arrive in August 2027, the mission seeks to resolve the mystery of the moons' formation, examining theories of planetary impact debris or captured asteroids. If successful, this endeavor, which includes deploying a small rover, would be the first sample return from Mars or its moons, offering pristine material for Earth-based scientific analysis by 2031 and potentially containing grains of Mars itself.
