Understanding Health Anxiety: Recognizing Signs and Coping Strategies

2026-10-23 10:57:30 EDT

Hollie Sharman's experience of frequent hospital visits for perceived severe chest pain, despite no medical findings, highlights the isolating struggle of health anxiety. Clinical psychologist Dr. Kirren Schnack defines health anxiety as a "constant worry about being seriously ill when you are not." Key signs include obsessive body checking, constant reassurance-seeking from others or online, and avoidance behaviors. Dr. Schnack advises challenging worst-case thinking, limiting reassurance, burning off anxious energy, grounding oneself with sensory experiences, and practicing breathing techniques. The article emphasizes responsible health behavior while providing practical strategies to manage and overcome this pervasive condition.
