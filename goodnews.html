Chinese Company Achieves Breakthrough in Hydrogen-Boron Nuclear Fusion

2026-09-28 09:00:20 EDT

A Chinese commercial fusion company has achieved a significant and valuable advance in nuclear fusion research by successfully controlling high-parameter hydrogen-boron plasma. This milestone marks the first time a commercial entity has independently achieved a hydrogen-boron fusion reaction on its own device, diverging from the predominantly used deuterium-tritium (D-T) fuel in mainstream research like the ITER project. This breakthrough in neutron-free magnetic confinement fusion is particularly notable for offering a cleaner energy path, as hydrogen-boron fusion produces significantly fewer radioactive byproducts compared to conventional methods, potentially leading to safer and more sustainable future energy solutions.
