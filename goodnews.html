Understanding the 2026 Autumn Equinox and Its Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The autumn equinox, marking the start of astronomical autumn, will occur at 01:06 BST on September 23, 2026. This precise moment signifies when the Sun crosses the celestial equator, resulting in roughly equal day and night lengths globally. While the equinox itself is not visible, it holds cultural and spiritual importance, with gatherings at sites like Stonehenge. Due to light refraction, the actual "equilux," when day and night are precisely equal, happens a few days after the equinox, around September 25 or 26 in the UK, depending on latitude.
