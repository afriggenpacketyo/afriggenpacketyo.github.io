2026-07-26 05:00:00 EDT

Danielle Boyer, a member of the Sault Ste. Marie Tribe of Chippewa Indians, created SkoBots, customizable toy robots that help Indigenous children learn their ancestral languages like Anishinaabemowin. These robots, resembling woodland creatures, use artificial intelligence to respond with prerecorded audio, ensuring accuracy and avoiding AI "hallucinations" that could invent fake words. Boyer's non-profit mentors children in building and programming SkoBots, fostering interest in language preservation, science, math, and ethical AI use. The initiative aims to combat language loss, a legacy of US Indian boarding schools, and reconnect youth with their cultural heritage, making language learning engaging and meaningful.

Undiagnosed ADHD in Women Linked to Unique Health Challenges

2026-07-26 05:00:00 EDT

ADHD often presents differently in girls and women, with symptoms like distraction and disorganization being overlooked compared to hyperactivity in boys, leading to delayed diagnoses often until adulthood. This delay carries significant health costs, including higher risks of anxiety, depression, social difficulties, abuse, teen pregnancy, obesity, and cardiovascular disease. Studies indicate women with ADHD die nearly nine years earlier on average. Midlife, particularly perimenopause, can exacerbate symptoms due to hormonal fluctuations. Recognition of these gender differences is increasing diagnoses, and comprehensive treatment involving medication, therapy, and lifestyle changes is crucial for improving women's self-esteem and overall well-being.
