Research Confirms 'Hang-xiety' as a Real Phenomenon Linking Hangovers and Anxiety

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

"Hang-xiety," the pervasive anxiety that often accompanies a hangover, has been scientifically validated as a real phenomenon, according to experts and recent studies. Alcohol impacts brain chemicals, particularly GABA and glutamate, initially induces relaxation but subsequently triggers a rebound effect of heightened alertness and arousal as it leaves the system, leading to feelings of dread. A 2023 study revealed that 18.3% of students reported experiencing hangover anxiety, with research further indicating that naturally shy individuals may be more susceptible to its intensified effects. Recognizing hang-xiety as a temporary physiological state, rather than proof of a social catastrophe, can significantly mitigate its power.
