UK Experiences Unusually Calm Autumn with No Named Storms So Far

2026-10-16 21:53:34 EDT

The UK is experiencing an unusually placid autumn, having yet to record its first named storm of the season, a phenomenon attributed to prevailing high-pressure systems diverting typical Atlantic weather patterns. This has resulted in warmer and significantly drier conditions across much of England and Wales, exacerbating drought concerns in southern regions. However, the Met Office forecasts a transition to a wetter and windier late autumn and early winter, as the strengthening El Niño in the Pacific is expected to alter the jet stream's path. While forecasts indicate a potential for disruptive weather soon, the absence of early storms is not unprecedented, with approximately half of seasons since 2015 seeing the first storm after November 1st.
