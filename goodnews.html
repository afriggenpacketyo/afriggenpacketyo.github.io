California has enacted "Jay Leno's Law" (Senate Bill 1392), expanding smog check exemptions for certain classic collector cars manufactured between 1976 and 1985. This legislation is a significant win for classic car enthusiasts and the lowrider community, who argued for leniency due to the difficulty and cost of meeting emissions standards for vintage vehicles. A special license plate honoring lowrider heritage was also signed into law.

Individual Differences in Caffeine Metabolism Explain Varied Effects

2026-09-16 20:09:43 EDT

The varying effects of caffeine on individuals stem from differences in how their bodies metabolize the substance, a process influenced by genetics, smoking habits, medications, and pregnancy. Caffeine's half-life can range from six to 20 hours, meaning its effects can linger, leading some to feel more wired than others. Smokers tend to metabolize caffeine faster, while certain medications and hormonal changes can slow it down. Regular coffee drinkers may develop tolerance, making their usual dose less potent. Sleep experts recommend avoiding caffeine for at least six hours before bed to ensure better sleep quality, though individual sensitivities vary widely. Moderation is advised, as excessive caffeine intake can lead to unpleasant side effects.
