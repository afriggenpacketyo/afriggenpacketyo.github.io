Understanding How Settled Debt Appears on Your Credit Report

2026-09-14 10:28:05 EDT

When a debt is settled for less than the full amount owed, it can still impact your credit report. Typically, it will be marked as 'settled' or 'settled for less than the full balance,' indicating that the debt was resolved but not fully repaid according to original terms. The account should reflect a zero balance after settlement, and any prior negative history, such as late payments or charge-offs, will generally remain for up to seven years. It's crucial to verify that the reporting accurately reflects the settlement terms and to dispute any discrepancies with credit bureaus or the creditor.
