FTC Warns Companies on "Personalized Pricing" Practices Using Consumer Data

2026-08-21 10:43:31 EDT

The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) is issuing a stern warning to companies, stating that "personalized pricing," where consumer data dictates individual prices, could violate federal law by misleading consumers. The FTC argues that consumers reasonably expect uniform prices for products and services, and that undisclosed price adjustments based on online browsing habits, purchase history, or estimated willingness to pay are deceptive practices. While the FTC cannot outright ban the practice, it has the authority to discipline businesses that fail to clearly disclose how personal data is used to set prices. Consumer advocates strongly support this initiative, citing investigations that found significant, undisclosed price fluctuations, though they note the onus remains on consumers to diligently read complex disclosures.
