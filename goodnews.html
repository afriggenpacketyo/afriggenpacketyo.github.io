NASA's Swift Space Telescope Re-enters Earth's Atmosphere After Failed Rescue Mission

2026-11-04 23:40:00 EST

NASA's Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory, a highly successful space telescope, concluded its nearly 22-year mission with a fiery re-entry into Earth's atmosphere over North Sumatra, Indonesia. Originally launched in 2004 for a two-year mission, the $500 million observatory significantly surpassed expectations, studying cosmic phenomena like gamma-ray bursts, supernovae, and black holes, and contributing to nearly 7,000 scientific papers. Its orbital decline was exacerbated by powerful geomagnetic storms, and a subsequent rescue attempt by a private robotic servicing spacecraft, named LINK, unfortunately failed due to attitude control issues. While most of the satellite was expected to burn up during re-entry, no immediate ground damage was reported.
