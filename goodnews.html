New Denisovan Fossils in China Uncover Ancient Hunting Practices

2026-09-12 22:00:10 EDT

Recent discoveries of Denisovan fossils in Bianfu Cave, southwest China, are significantly advancing scientists' understanding of this extinct human group, which diverged from modern humans and Neanderthals hundreds of thousands of years ago. These new findings establish Bianfu Cave as the most abundant Denisovan locality outside the original Denisova Cave in Siberia, providing crucial new evidence. Research indicates that this archaic human group established a stable subsistence pattern, demonstrating a high adaptation to local resources through specialized hunting and intensive exploitation of animals within coniferous forests and forest-steppe environments. This paints a picture of an ancient "hunting paradise" that supported their existence.
