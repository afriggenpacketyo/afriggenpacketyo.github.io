Understanding 401(k) Vesting Schedules: How Employer Contributions Become Yours

2026-08-11 09:00:01 EDT

Understanding 401(k) vesting schedules is crucial for employees, as employer contributions often aren't immediately owned but become fully vested after a certain period of service. Common models include cliff vesting, where 100% vests after a set time (e.g., three years), and graded vesting, where a percentage vests each year. While personal contributions are always yours, knowing your company's vesting terms can be worth thousands, especially when considering a job change, as it impacts how much employer-contributed money you retain.
