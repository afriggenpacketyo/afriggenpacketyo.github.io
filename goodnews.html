Astronomers Discover Potential 'Gold Factory' Relic in Milky Way

2026-10-13 02:00:08 EDT

A collaborative team of Chinese and US researchers has potentially uncovered the relic of a cosmic "gold factory" within the Milky Way galaxy, observed as a mysterious glow of high-energy light. Scientists propose this phenomenon is the aftermath of a violent collision between two neutron stars, which are incredibly dense remnants of collapsed stars. Such an event would have been extraordinarily bright, comparable to Jupiter in the night sky, and would have spewed out atoms and a flood of neutrons. These processes are crucial for forming the universe's heaviest elements, including gold, platinum, and uranium, with the wreckage ultimately collapsing into a black hole. The small, bright nature of the glowing cloud suggests this rare blast occurred less than 1,000 years ago, indicating a relatively recent cosmic event.
