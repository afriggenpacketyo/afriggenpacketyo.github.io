High-Fat Western Diets Linked to Increased Colorectal Cancer Risk

2026-08-17 02:49:00 EDT

German researchers have established a causal link between high-fat "Western diets" and an increased risk of colorectal cancer, which is notably one of the deadliest cancers affecting adults under 50. Their study, published in the journal Gut, revealed that consuming fatty foods fosters specific gut bacteria that produce deoxycholic acid (DCA), a secondary bile acid known to promote tumor growth. Experiments conducted in mice demonstrated increased tumor growth and cell division when DCA-producing bacteria were present, a finding further supported by analysis of human stool samples. This foundational research underscores the significant impact of diet on human gut health and suggests potential future treatments like microbiome transplants, alongside crucial preventative lifestyle modifications.
