UK Pharmacies Granted Expanded Powers for Prescription Swaps

2026-11-28 01:00:11 EST

UK pharmacies are receiving new powers to adjust prescription medicines during shortages, allowing them to offer patients alternative strengths or forms of the same drug without requiring a new GP visit. This change, amending the 1968 Medicines Act, aims to streamline patient access to necessary treatments and reduce delays caused by out-of-stock items. For instance, a pharmacist can now provide two 5mg tablets instead of a 10mg pill, or a cream instead of an ointment. Public health minister Diana Johnson stated this flexibility will make life easier for patients, ensuring they receive their medication with less hassle.
