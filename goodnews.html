Understanding the Taxability of Life Insurance Payouts

2026-11-04 12:18:28 EST

Generally, life insurance proceeds are not considered taxable income for beneficiaries. However, there are specific circumstances where taxes may apply, such as receiving payouts in installments, making cash withdrawals from a policy's cash value that exceed premiums paid, or surrendering/selling a policy for more than its cost basis. Employer-provided life insurance exceeding $50,000 in coverage is also taxable. If a policy's proceeds are paid to an estate, estate taxes could be triggered for estates exceeding a certain net worth threshold.
