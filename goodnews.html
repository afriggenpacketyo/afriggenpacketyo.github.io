Researchers Explore Drug-Based Prevention for Childhood Trauma in Mice Study

2026-08-14 02:22:00 EDT

Researchers in Munich and Stockholm have investigated whether childhood trauma can be prevented with a drug, rather than just treated. A study on mice, published in June, found that a compound called SAFit2, given to mothers during stressful periods, could prevent social hierarchy disadvantages in offspring linked to early hardship. The drug targets the protein FKBP51, which regulates stress hormone receptors, effectively reversing trauma's imprint on brain regions related to emotional control. While promising for prevention, the drug is still experimental, tested only on male mice, and its human application is at least a decade away, focusing on identifying at-risk individuals before harm occurs.
