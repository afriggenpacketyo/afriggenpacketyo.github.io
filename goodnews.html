Chinese Team Discovers World's Oldest Amber in Xinjiang Coal Seam

2026-07-27 21:00:07 EDT

Researchers led by Luo Cihang from the Chinese Academy of Sciences' Nanjing Institute of Geology and Palaeontology have unearthed the world's oldest known amber. Discovered in a coal seam in Xinjiang, the microscopic pieces date back to the Middle Devonian period, approximately 393 million to 383 million years ago. This finding predates the previously confirmed oldest amber by about 70 million years, pushing back the timeline for the isolation and chemical analysis of this ancient tree resin. The palaeontologists were initially skeptical of their discovery, which was detailed in Science Advances, marking a significant advancement in understanding ancient ecosystems and geological history.
