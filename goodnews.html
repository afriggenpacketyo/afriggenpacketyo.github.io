Insurers define pre-existing conditions broadly, often including symptoms, veterinary advice, or treatment prior to the policy's effective date or during its waiting period. However, new and unrelated health problems may still be covered, and some curable conditions might eventually become eligible after a specified symptom- and treatment-free period.
