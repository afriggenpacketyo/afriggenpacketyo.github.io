Singapore Develops World's Most Accurate Clock, Setting New Precision Standard

2026-10-02 04:00:11 EDT

Singaporean scientists have developed the world's most accurate clock, utilizing a single ion of lutetium, a rare earth metal. This groundbreaking device is so precise it would lose only one second every 260 billion years, a timeframe nearly 20 times the age of our universe, significantly surpassing previous global records. The achievement is approximately four times more accurate than a calcium-ion clock developed by a Chinese Academy of Sciences team and also exceeds an aluminum-ion clock unveiled by the US National Institute of Standards and Technology. This article highlights Singapore's remarkable scientific advancement in metrology, positioning it at the forefront of global precision timekeeping research and demonstrating significant innovation.
