Second Wave of Native-Born Sea Turtles Hatches in Southern California

2026-11-16 08:00:00 EST

A second group of 21 olive ridley hatchlings successfully emerged and were released in Huntington Beach, California, following an earlier release of 54 hatchlings in Seal Beach. These events mark the first known native-born sea turtle nests on the U.S. West Coast, with eggs initially laid in September and later transferred to the Aquarium of the Pacific for protection from high tides. An extreme marine heat wave and a strong El Niño are believed to have contributed to Southern California's water temperatures becoming conducive to the turtles' typical tropical nesting grounds, hundreds of miles north of their usual range. More eggs from both nests are still anticipated to hatch in the coming days, highlighting a unique ecological phenomenon.
