Fossilized Teeth Reveal 66-Pound Giant Rodent Roamed Ancient Thailand

2026-11-05 07:11:38 EST

Scientists have identified a previously unknown species of ancient rodent, estimated to have weighed a colossal 66 pounds, that roamed what is now southern Thailand approximately 33 to 35 million years ago. This remarkable discovery, based on two fossilized teeth unearthed in a coal mine in 2004, provides crucial new clues about how early rodents evolved to such enormous sizes. Named Yakamys sirindhornae in honor of Thailand's Princess Sirindhorn, this "giant rat" belonged to the now-extinct Ischyromyidae family, one of the earliest major groups of rodents. The findings suggest that ancient rodents may have initially evolved in this region before dispersing to other parts of the world.
