2026-11-02 14:00:07 EST

Most of the spacecraft is expected to burn up, but denser components made of tungsten and some mirror parts could survive reentry. NASA estimates the risk of harm to anyone on Earth as unlikely, roughly 1 in 2,100, with any surviving debris likely falling into the ocean. The Swift team mourns the end of its 22-year mission, during which it discovered over 2,000 gamma-ray bursts and 1,400 exploding stars, leaving a significant gap in cosmic observation capabilities.
