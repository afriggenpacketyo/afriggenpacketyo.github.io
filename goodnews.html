Glasgow Museums Remove Mummified Remains from Public Display Citing Ethical Concerns

2026-10-23 13:01:26 EDT

Two museums in Glasgow, Kelvingrove Gallery and The Hunterian Museum, have removed or covered mummified human remains from public view due to evolving ethical considerations. This decision follows a reassessment of their duty of care towards the deceased, aligning with revised guidelines from the International Council of Museums (Icom) that emphasize upholding the 'inherent dignity' of human remains. While other Scottish institutions, like National Museums Scotland, are not making similar changes, Glasgow Life stated their move reflects contemporary museum practice and a new policy advocating for documented consent. The museums are open to repatriation requests from originating countries or institutions.
