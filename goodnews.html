Understanding 401(k) Protections from Debt Collectors

This article clarifies that the Employee Retirement Income Security Act (ERISA) generally shields most employer-sponsored 401(k) plans from ordinary debt collectors and private creditors. However, these robust federal protections are not absolute, with notable exceptions for federal tax debts and domestic relations orders, such as child support. It is critical to understand that withdrawing funds from a 401(k) can nullify these protections and lead to substantial income taxes and penalties.
