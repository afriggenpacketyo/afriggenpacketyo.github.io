Electric Vehicles May Be Causing Increased Motion Sickness in Passengers

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

Some passengers are reporting increased motion sickness in electric vehicles (EVs), with individuals like Lola Baird experiencing severe nausea, prompting concerns about EV design. Experts suggest this phenomenon, observed in a growing number of patients, stems from a "mismatch in sensory cues." The absence of traditional engine sounds and vibrations, which usually signal acceleration, combined with the abrupt deceleration of regenerative braking, can disrupt the body's balance system. As EV adoption rapidly increases, manufacturers are exploring solutions like smoother acceleration software, subtle seat vibrations, and adaptive braking to mitigate these effects and improve passenger comfort.
