College Student Overcomes Rare Brain Tumor After Misleading Symptoms

2026-11-28 08:00:08 EST

Niko Pitinii, a 21-year-old University of Pittsburgh student, experienced severe fatigue, rapid weight loss, and fainting spells, initially baffling doctors. A brain scan ultimately revealed a golf ball-sized tumor, and subsequent bloodwork showed high levels of beta-hCG, a hormone often linked to pregnancy but also secreted by rare germinoma brain tumors. Pitinii underwent successful surgery to address hydrocephalus caused by the tumor, followed by intensive chemotherapy and radiation. Now cancer-free and nominated for a national Courage Award, he has returned to school and plans to pursue a master's in social work, aiming to help others navigate similar health challenges.
