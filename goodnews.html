Scientists Discover Two New Species of Sea Spiders Along Canadian Coastline

2026-09-27 18:31:16 EDT

Researchers in Canada have discovered two new species of sea spiders in the shallow waters of the Salish Sea, marking the first such findings in over a century for that specific area. These marine arthropods, measuring about a centimeter across, were collected off Vancouver and Quadra islands. One species, Callipallene pilosuspedes, features distinctive red eyes, a triangular three-lipped mouth, and curved limbs called ovigers used for self-grooming. The other, Tanystylum kiixin, named after an ancient Indigenous village, has shorter, stockier legs and was found covered in debris. These discoveries significantly enhance understanding of marine biodiversity and highlight the unique characteristics of these fascinating creatures.
