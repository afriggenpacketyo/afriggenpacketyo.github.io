California Introduces New License Plate Sequence After Exhausting Current Combinations

2026-09-11 18:59:37 EDT

California is implementing a new license plate sequence for the first time since the 1980s, having exhausted all combinations of its previous format (one number, three letters, three numbers). The state's Department of Motor Vehicles announced the new sequence will feature three numerals, followed by three letters, and then one numeral (e.g., "000AAA0"). Although production of the new plates has begun, they have not yet been issued to drivers, as over 110,000 plates from the old format remain. This change, expected to last 40 years, ensures a continued supply of unique identifiers for registered vehicles in the state.
