Cleveland Clinic Initiates Drone Delivery Service for Prescriptions in Ohio

2026-08-11 08:41:13 EDT

Cleveland Clinic has partnered with Zipline to launch a pioneering prescription drone delivery program for patients in and around Beachwood, Ohio. This marks the first long-term deployment of a medical drone service by a U.S. health system, designed to ease the burden of pharmacy visits for ill or mobility-challenged individuals. Drones retrieve packaged medications from the pharmacy, fly to the patient's home, and lower the prescription via a tether while remaining airborne. Patients can track their orders through the MyChart portal without additional fees. Initially limited to shelf-stable prescriptions within a five-mile zone, the program aims to expand its offerings and service areas, potentially transforming medication access and convenience.
