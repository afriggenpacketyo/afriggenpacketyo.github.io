Voyager 1 Reaches Historic One-Light Day Distance from Earth

2026-11-15 17:41:22 EST

NASA's venerable Voyager 1 spacecraft has reached an extraordinary milestone, now positioned one light-day away from Earth, a staggering distance exceeding 16 billion miles. Launched on September 5, 1977, the probe has been traveling for nearly five decades, having famously conducted flybys of Jupiter and Saturn, and becoming the most distant human-made object in existence in 1998. Voyager 1 continues its groundbreaking interstellar mission, meticulously charting and measuring magnetic fields, particles, and plasma waves beyond the sun's protective heliosphere with its two remaining science instruments. NASA is commemorating this historic occasion with a symbolic "Shine Bright for Voyager's Light-Day" event, encouraging the public to participate by transmitting Voyager's morse code call sign into the night sky.
