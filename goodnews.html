Understanding Eligibility for Medicare Supplemental Insurance

2026-08-10 12:42:34 EDT

Medicare supplemental insurance, known as Medigap, helps cover out-of-pocket costs not paid by Original Medicare, a crucial consideration for many retired Americans. Eligibility generally requires enrollment in both Medicare Part A and Part B, and Medigap cannot be paired with Medicare Advantage plans. A federal six-month Open Enrollment Period starting at age 65 offers protections against denial or higher premiums due to health conditions, though rules vary for those under 65. Deciding if Medigap is worthwhile involves assessing anticipated healthcare needs and financial risk tolerance, as policies have additional premiums and don't cover all services like routine dental or vision care.
