Rare Kemp's Ridley Sea Turtle Embarks on 5,000-Mile Journey Home

2026-08-09 02:30:35 EDT

Rhossi, a rare Kemp's ridley sea turtle discovered near death on a Welsh beach in December 2023, is now embarking on a 5,000-mile journey back to its home in the Gulf of Mexico. After two-and-a-half years of dedicated care at Anglesey Sea Zoo, where the turtle grew from under 1kg to 21kg, conservationists have initiated a meticulously coordinated transport operation. This involves a helicopter ride to London and a cargo flight to Houston, Texas, with staff diligently applying lubricants like KY Jelly and Vaseline to prevent dehydration and skin damage during the extensive travel. The effort underscores a significant commitment to wildlife rescue and rehabilitation.
