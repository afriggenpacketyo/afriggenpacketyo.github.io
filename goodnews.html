Serving three terms from 1978, Kassebaum Baker, daughter of Kansas Governor Alf Landon, was renowned for her independent-minded Republican politics and exceptional ability to foster bipartisan cooperation. She was instrumental in the creation of a significant 1996 health insurance law and, later in her life, notably diverged from the Republican party to endorse Democratic candidates. Kassebaum Baker remained an enduring icon for Kansans, celebrated for her modest demeanor, unwavering commitment to public service, and willingness to speak her mind, including her criticism of Donald Trump's actions in 2021.
