Century-Old Fossil Reveals New Megaraptoran Dinosaur Species, Invictibellatrix amberina

2026-10-14 12:35:10 EDT

A previously unknown dinosaur species, Invictibellatrix amberina, has been identified from a skull fossil that lay hidden in a British museum for nearly a century. This "long-clawed predator," a megaraptoran cousin of the T. rex, would have been 10-13 feet long and hunted with its powerful arms and giant curved claws, rather than its mouth. Researchers from University College London believe this creature, which lived 120 million years ago, would have been a formidable threat to humans if they coexisted, potentially "more dangerous than a T. rex" for its size. The article details this exciting paleontological discovery and its implications for understanding ancient ecosystems.
