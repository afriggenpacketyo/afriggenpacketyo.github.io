New Urine Test Shows High Accuracy in Detecting Bladder Cancer

2026-09-01 21:39:17 EDT

An at-home urine test, currently being trialed by the NHS, has demonstrated over 90% accuracy in detecting bladder cancer, according to new findings published in European Urology Oncology. Developed by the University of Birmingham and Nonacus, the Galeas test identifies genetic material shed by cancer cells in urine, checking for over 450 DNA mutations across 23 genes. Researchers hope this innovation could significantly reduce the need for invasive cystoscopies and shorten waiting lists, helping doctors prioritize patients at greater risk. Early detection and treatment of cancer are vital, and this test offers a less invasive, faster way to diagnose or rule out the disease.
