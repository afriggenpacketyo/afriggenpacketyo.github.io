National Popular Vote Compact Nears Electoral College Circumvention Threshold

2026-11-21 05:00:00 EST

A national effort to ensure the popular vote winner becomes president is nearing a critical milestone, potentially bypassing the Electoral College. Following recent Democratic midterm wins, five states with full Democratic control could join the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact, which pledges electoral votes to the national popular vote winner. If these states join the existing 18 states and D.C., the compact would surpass the 270 electoral votes needed to take effect. While political, practical, and legal hurdles remain, proponents are optimistic about its implementation, possibly even for the next presidential election. Opponents argue such a profound change should occur via a constitutional amendment.
