2026-09-27 07:35:12 EDT

The German Olympic Sports Confederation (DOSB) has chosen Munich as Germany's candidate city to bid for the Summer Olympics and Paralympics in either 2036, 2040, or 2044. Munich was selected over a rival bid from the Cologne-Rhine-Ruhr region, with Berlin having withdrawn its bid earlier. The choice of 2036 is politically sensitive due to the 100th anniversary of the Nazi-era Berlin Olympics, making 2040 (50 years since reunification) a preferred option for some. Munich plans to utilize existing infrastructure, including its 1972 Olympic Park, to host most events within city limits.
