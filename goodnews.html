Study Warns Current Breast Cancer Screening Misses High-Risk Younger Women

2026-08-03 21:08:13 EDT

A new study warns that NHS criteria for breast cancer screening in women under 50 are too narrow, potentially missing up to 95% of those at higher risk of developing the disease. Current guidelines focus almost exclusively on inherited genes, neglecting other significant factors like lifestyle, reproductive history, and genetic information. Researchers, using a new risk assessment tool called Boadicea, found that 73% of younger women who develop breast cancer within a decade have no family history, which is the key criterion in existing guidelines. This highlights a critical need for a more comprehensive approach to identify at-risk individuals and improve early detection.
