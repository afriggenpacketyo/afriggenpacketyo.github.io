Canadian Town's Kindness During 9/11 Inspires Broadway Musical

2026-09-06 10:19:54 EDT

The small Canadian town of Gander, Newfoundland, became an unexpected haven for thousands of stranded passengers after the 9/11 attacks grounded flights. Residents opened their homes and community spaces, providing food, shelter, and comfort to the "plane people" for several days. This extraordinary display of generosity and human spirit, where locals referred to the arrivals as "plane people," inspired the Tony Award-winning musical "Come From Away." The musical, which focuses on the acts of kindness that unfolded in the aftermath, has resonated globally, highlighting the power of community and compassion in times of crisis. The story continues to be celebrated, with a professional production staged locally in Gander, bringing the narrative full circle. The town's welcoming spirit transformed a moment of global tragedy into a testament of human resilience and connection.

Eight Essential Services Reliant on Data Centers

2026-09-06 10:17:07 EDT

Data centers are the unseen backbone of modern life, powering a vast array of everyday services that Americans rely on constantly. These facilities, filled with thousands of servers, process digital requests and store information for everything from smartphone apps and streaming services to online shopping and artificial intelligence. As the economy increasingly moves online, data centers have become critical infrastructure, with thousands operational and more planned due to the acceleration of cloud computing and AI. Their essential functions include making apps work, ensuring smooth streaming and gaming, storing personal data, powering search engines, enabling online commerce, running navigation systems, facilitating remote work, and driving the next generation of technology. The demand for these services underscores the indispensable role of data centers in contemporary society.

Robert De Niro Discusses Revitalizing Lower Manhattan Post-9/11

2026-09-06 10:08:32 EDT

Following the 9/11 attacks, actor Robert De Niro and his business partner Jane Rosenthal sought to help revive Lower Manhattan, which had been significantly impacted by the tragedy. They established the Tribeca Film Festival in May 2002, just eight months after the attacks, as a means to heal the shattered city and provide something for people to look forward to. The festival quickly drew large crowds and notable figures, serving as an "act of defiance" and a symbol of the city's resilience. De Niro expressed pride in the festival's role in bringing films and economic activity to Lower Manhattan, ultimately helping to revitalize the area. The festival's mission continues to use art as an antidote to trauma, asserting that New York City remained resilient despite the devastation.

US Envoys Visit Ukraine for Peace Talks Following Russia Discussions

2026-09-06 10:02:27 EDT

President Trump's special envoys, Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, arrived in Ukraine for peace talks aimed at ending the ongoing conflict with Russia. This visit follows their meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow, where they reportedly discussed substantive plans for de-escalation. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy expressed the need for "security guarantees" and peace, with European security advisers expected to participate in some discussions. Both Ukraine and Russia agreed to a temporary pause in strikes to facilitate the talks. The envoys carry a proposal from President Trump, who expressed optimism about a potential resolution. The diplomatic efforts aim to find mutually beneficial solutions and bring an end to the hostilities.
