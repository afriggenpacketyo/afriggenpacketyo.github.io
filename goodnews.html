Noah Adams, Longtime Host of NPR's All Things Considered, Dies at 84

2026-10-18 12:30:00 EDT

Noah Adams, a distinguished writer, broadcaster, and storyteller, who hosted NPR's "All Things Considered" for 18 years, has died at 84. Adams was known for his thoughtful radio pieces, capturing the essence of places and people through keen observation and simple, vivid language. He guided listeners through major historical events and preferred to report from outside Washington, focusing on rural America and genuine human stories. Adams' unique interviewing style, particularly his ability to connect with subjects, influenced future public radio programs like "This American Life." He also authored five books, with his 1981 documentary "Father Cares: The Last of Jonestown" lauded for its experimental use of sound and writing.
