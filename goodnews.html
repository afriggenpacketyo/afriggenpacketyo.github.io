2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

A growing number of passengers report experiencing heightened motion sickness in electric vehicles (EVs), prompting experts to investigate the underlying causes. Research suggests this phenomenon stems from a sensory mismatch, where the absence of traditional engine sounds and vibrations in EVs disrupts the body's usual cues for movement. Regenerative braking, which can cause abrupt decelerations if not applied smoothly, is also identified as a significant factor contributing to nausea. While not universal, this issue affects some individuals severely, leading to discomfort. Academics offer practical tips for passengers to mitigate symptoms and suggest car manufacturers can implement software and sensory feedback solutions.
