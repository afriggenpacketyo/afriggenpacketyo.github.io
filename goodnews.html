Archaeologists Discover World's Oldest Shipwreck Off Turkey's Coast

2026-10-14 01:00:35 EDT

Archaeologists have announced the discovery of the world's oldest shipwreck off Turkey's Kekova Island, dating back 4,400 to 4,700 years to the Early Bronze Age. Although the wooden structure has not survived, over 30 ceramic storage pots, known as amphorae, were found on the seabed, some remarkably still containing traces of their original cargo. Experts determined the ship was a 10-meter trade vessel, identifying its age through distinctive pottery styles matching those found in ancient Egypt and the Middle East. The team is now analyzing the viscous residue within the amphorae to identify the traded goods, potentially olive oil, wine, or tree resins, marking a significant "time capsule" from antiquity.
