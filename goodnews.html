New Zealand Proposes Social Media Ban for Children Under 16

2026-08-24 01:45:00 EDT

New Zealand's government is set to propose landmark legislation that would ban social media access for children under the age of 16, aligning with similar initiatives globally, including neighboring Australia. The proposed Online Safety Bill mandates platforms like Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, and Facebook to implement robust age verification mechanisms, such as facial age estimation and digital ID, with non-compliance potentially incurring substantial fines up to 10% of their global revenue. Prime Minister Christopher Luxon emphasized the significant harm to children's mental health, sleep, and education as the driving force behind this crucial measure. However, the bill faces an uncertain future, as coalition partners have voiced skepticism regarding its potential efficacy.

Three Rescued Beluga Whales Arrive at SeaWorld San Diego from Canada

2026-08-23 23:32:01 EDT

Three rescued beluga whales have successfully completed an impressive 2,500-mile journey from Canada to SeaWorld San Diego, following the permanent closure of Marineland in Niagara Falls. This complex relocation is part of an "unprecedented international whale rescue effort" aimed at finding suitable homes for 30 belugas, with other destinations including Atlanta and Valencia, Spain. The whales were meticulously transported via a Qatar Airways Boeing 777 cargo plane, joining three other belugas previously moved to San Diego from Marineland. While this marks a significant milestone, the process of adapting each whale to its new environment with individualized care is ongoing, and their public introduction date is pending.
