Study Suggests Rule-Breaking Behavior Can Be Contagious, Especially Within Political Groups

2026-11-23 01:33:00 EST

New research from the University of Amsterdam indicates that observing others break rules, particularly within one's own political affiliation, significantly increases the likelihood of an individual doing the same. An online experiment with US adults showed participants were nearly twice as likely to cheat after seeing someone from their party do so, with blatant violations having a larger effect. A small percentage even escalated the rule-breaking beyond what they observed. The article highlights that while good examples had little impact, a few "bad actors" can erode group norms, though real-world stakes and social pressures were absent from the study.

Minor Magnitude 3.3 Earthquake Strikes Off Los Angeles Coast Near El Segundo

2026-11-23 01:03:41 EST

A magnitude 3.3 earthquake occurred Sunday evening off the coast of Los Angeles County's South Bay, marking the fourth quake in the region within four days. The epicenter was located a few miles from El Segundo, Manhattan Beach, and Redondo Beach, with residents reporting feeling a noticeable jolt as far as Thousand Oaks and Long Beach. The U.S. Geological Survey classified the quake as weak, with no damage expected, rating it a 3 on the Modified Mercalli Intensity Scale. This latest tremor follows a magnitude 3.9 quake in Commerce and a 3.3 quake near San Pedro earlier in the week.
