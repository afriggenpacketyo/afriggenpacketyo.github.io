2026-07-31 02:30:22 EDT

The library, which opened in 1872, estimates the overdue fine, adjusted for inflation, would be around 28,000 Australian dollars, though historical borrower records are missing. This unique return highlights antiquated library rules, such as requiring overdue debts to be paid before borrowing again and prohibiting lending to intoxicated individuals, offering a whimsical glimpse into the past.
