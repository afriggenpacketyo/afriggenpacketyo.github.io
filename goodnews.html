Federal Judge Rules Trump DOJ Unlawfully Fired Prosecutor Maurene Comey

2026-10-26 16:03:58 EDT

A federal judge has ruled that the Department of Justice's termination of New York prosecutor Maurene Comey, daughter of former FBI Director James Comey, was unlawful. Judge Jesse Furman granted Comey retroactive reinstatement and back pay, rejecting the DOJ's arguments about the attorney general's authority to dismiss without cause. The DOJ has stated its intention to appeal the decision. Comey's lawyers hailed the ruling as a victory for the rule of law, emphasizing that the president is not above the law. Comey, who was involved in high-profile cases, argued her firing stemmed from President Trump's animus towards her father.
