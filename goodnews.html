Eligibility for Medicare Supplemental Insurance Explained

2026-08-10 12:42:34 EDT

Medicare supplemental insurance, also known as Medigap, helps cover out-of-pocket costs not covered by Original Medicare. To qualify, individuals generally need to be enrolled in both Medicare Part A and Part B and cannot have a Medicare Advantage plan. The Medigap Open Enrollment Period, beginning at age 65 and Part B enrollment, is crucial for guaranteed issue rights, preventing higher premiums due to health conditions. Eligibility rules can differ for those under 65, particularly those with disabilities or end-stage renal disease, with state laws playing a significant role.
