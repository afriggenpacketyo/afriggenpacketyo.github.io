First-Ever Adult Tyrannosaurus Rex Trackway Discovered in North Dakota Badlands

2026-09-10 11:38:43 EDT

Paleontologists have made a significant discovery in North Dakota's badlands: the first known trackway created by a full-grown Tyrannosaurus rex. This rare find consists of four connected, three-toed footprints, each roughly three feet long, offering unprecedented insights into the T. rex's locomotion. Researchers estimate the seven-ton animal was walking at approximately 3.5 to 4.5 mph, a detail previously unconfirmable from isolated prints. The trackway, dating to about 66.5 million years ago, provides a unique glimpse into the behavior of these apex predators shortly before the asteroid impact that wiped out the dinosaurs. Three of the four footprints are slated to be airlifted for preservation at the Denver Museum of Nature & Science.
