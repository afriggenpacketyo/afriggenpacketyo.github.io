Since 2020, a '10-year rule' generally requires certain heirs to withdraw the entire balance within a decade, which can increase adjusted gross income and tax liabilities. Financial experts advise projecting income over this period to strategically plan withdrawals during lower-income years. Original account owners should also consider beneficiaries' tax brackets when designating heirs, potentially leaving tax-free assets like life insurance policies to higher earners. Roth IRA conversions during one's lifetime can also offer future tax-free benefits for heirs.
