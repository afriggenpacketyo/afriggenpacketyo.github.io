Mesh Networks Offer Potential Solution to State-Imposed Internet Blackouts

2026-07-25 07:08:00 EDT

This article examines the potential of LoRa mesh networks as a tool to overcome state-imposed internet blackouts, referencing their use in countries like Iran and Ukraine. These networks utilize small, affordable radio modules that transmit encrypted data packets over several kilometers, creating a decentralized, self-repairing communication system that bypasses traditional internet infrastructure. While currently limited to small text messages and facing risks like transmitter location tracking, the technology offers a robust method for secure communication in environments prone to censorship. The article also notes the challenges of establishing sufficient network density and the potential legal repercussions for operating unauthorized equipment in some nations.
