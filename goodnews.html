Wayward SpaceX Rocket Booster Expected to Collide with Moon

2026-08-04 18:28:42 EDT

An 8,800-pound SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket booster is on a collision course with the moon, expected to impact the lunar surface at 5,400 mph early Wednesday. The rocket, which launched two lunar landers in January 2025, has been in orbit since. The impact, near the moon's Einstein and Bell craters, poses no danger to Earth and is expected to create a crater 65 to 100 feet in diameter and about 16 feet deep. NASA plans to collect data and refine tracking techniques, as such artificial impacts, though rare, offer valuable insights into lunar geology and future space missions.
