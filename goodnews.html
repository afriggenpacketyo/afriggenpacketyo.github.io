The Unique Evolution and Cultural Significance of Thanksgiving in Liberia

2026-11-25 09:14:34 EST

Thanksgiving in Liberia, celebrated on the first Thursday in November, has evolved from its American origins into a distinct national holiday deeply rooted in religious gratitude and national sovereignty. Established in 1883 by President Alfred Francis Russell, it commemorates divine protection against territorial incursions and gives thanks for freedom, unity, and harvest. Liberian families typically begin the day with church services or mosque prayers, followed by large outdoor feasts featuring local dishes like jollof rice and fufu and soup, often with chicken instead of turkey.
