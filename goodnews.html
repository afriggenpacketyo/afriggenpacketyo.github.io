Travel Insurance Coverage for Weather-Related Flight Disruptions

2026-11-11 08:17:51 EST

Travel insurance can provide coverage for flight delays and cancellations caused by severe weather, but policy terms vary significantly. Trip delay coverage may reimburse expenses like meals and lodging incurred during disruptions, provided minimum delay requirements are met. Trip cancellation or interruption coverage can protect prepaid, nonrefundable costs if severe weather prevents travel. It's crucial to distinguish insurance benefits from airline refunds, as airlines are generally obligated to refund canceled flights. Travelers should carefully review policy definitions of severe weather, covered reasons, delay thresholds, and reimbursement limits before purchasing insurance, especially since coverage typically excludes events already known at the time of purchase.
