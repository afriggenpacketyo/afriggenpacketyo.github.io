Orionid Meteor Shower Active Until November, Peaks Mid-October

2026-10-02 03:16:45 EDT

The Orionid meteor shower is currently active and will remain visible until November 7th, with its peak expected overnight from October 21st to 22nd. This annual display occurs as Earth passes through debris left by Halley's Comet, producing fast meteors that can leave lingering trails. Under ideal conditions, viewers can expect to see 10 to 20 meteors per hour. For the best viewing experience, it is recommended to find a dark location away from city lights and allow eyes to adjust, as binoculars or telescopes are not necessary.
