Research Suggests Chinese Script Evolution Mirrors Natural Selection

2026-09-01 00:00:05 EDT

Published in "Digital Scholarship in the Humanities," the article reveals that a small number of core components, such as 日 (sun), 月 (moon), and 人 (human), are repeatedly combined to create new characters, while new components are invented when needed. This evolution from complex to simplified forms, influenced by usage frequency, reflects an economic principle in language.
