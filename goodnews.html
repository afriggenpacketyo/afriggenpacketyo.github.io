Covered Call ETFs Offer Income but Pose Hidden Tax Liabilities

2026-10-18 09:10:33 EDT

Covered call ETFs, popular among investors for generating monthly cash flow, often come with unexpected tax implications that can significantly reduce after-tax returns. While these funds hold equities and sell call options for income, their distributions can be complex, including dividends, capital gains, or non-dividend distributions (return of capital). Return of capital distributions, while tax-deferred, reduce an investment's cost basis, potentially increasing future capital gains tax liability upon sale. Financial advisors recommend using tax-deferred accounts like IRAs for these ETFs to mitigate immediate tax burdens, and urge investors to scrutinize tax-cost ratios and net asset value growth.
