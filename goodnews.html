Scientists Uncover First Skeletal Evidence of Diplodocus Dinosaur in Europe

2026-09-15 10:42:00 EDT

Researchers in Spain have made a groundbreaking discovery, unearthing the first skeletal evidence of the Diplodocus dinosaur outside of North America, with various vertebrae and chevron bones dating back approximately 150 million years. Found at the rich La Tejeria fossil site in eastern Spain, this find challenges previous understanding that Diplodocus remains were exclusive to the Morrison Formation in North America. The article, published in the Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology, highlights that this discovery constitutes "one of the strongest pieces of evidence" supporting the theory of continental faunal exchange between North America and Europe during the Late Jurassic period, possibly facilitated by ephemeral land bridges across the proto-Atlantic Ocean. This significant paleontological finding provides crucial insights into the ancient distribution of these iconic long-necked herbivores and the geological history of continental drift.
