Box breathing technique offers powerful stress control, experts say

2026-08-28 21:24:36 EDT

Box breathing, a simple four-step breathing technique, is being lauded by experts as a powerful tool for managing stress and anxiety. The method involves inhaling, holding, exhaling, and holding again for equal counts, often four seconds each. It's recommended by therapists, the US Navy SEALs, and even sports teams for its ability to calm the nervous system and focus the mind. Studies suggest it can prevent significant increases in heart rate and stress markers, with the hold after exhalation being particularly effective in managing CO2 levels and reducing panic responses.
