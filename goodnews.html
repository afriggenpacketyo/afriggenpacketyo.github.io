Finland Pioneers World's Largest Sand Battery for Renewable Energy Storage

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in southern Finland is utilizing the world's largest commercial sand battery to address renewable energy intermittency, a critical challenge for clean power. This 13-meter-tall facility stores 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy using 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone, providing nearly a month of heat in summer and a week in winter for 5,000 residents. Developed by Polar Night Energy, the project has significantly cut greenhouse gas emissions by almost 70% and reduced wood chip use by 60%. The sand battery stores heat from cheap electricity, discharging it via heat exchangers for district heating, offering a sustainable, rare-earth-free alternative to traditional batteries and inspiring international interest.
