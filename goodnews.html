Federal Reserve Renovations Cleared of Criminal Wrongdoing by Inspector General

2026-09-30 18:17:05 EDT

A Federal Reserve Inspector General report found no federal laws broken in the controversial $2.5 billion renovation of the Fed's headquarters, despite President Trump's criticisms of the project as "disgraceful." The year-long internal investigation, requested by former Fed Chairman Jerome Powell, concluded that poor project management significantly inflated costs, but identified no administrative misconduct. While features like a private elevator and extensive marble were deemed not to materially contribute to cost increases, the report highlighted deficiencies such as lacking construction cost estimates and insufficient internal governance. The new Fed Chair, Kevin Warsh, announced that the General Services Administration has been retained to advise on the project and a full audit will determine potential reimbursements.
