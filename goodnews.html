Understanding Travel Insurance Coverage for Weather-Related Flight Disruptions

2026-11-11 08:17:51 EST

Travel insurance can provide valuable coverage for flight delays and cancellations caused by severe weather, but the extent of protection varies significantly by individual policy. Trip delay coverage may reimburse reasonable expenses like meals, lodging, and transportation incurred during prolonged delays, typically after a specified minimum waiting period. Conversely, trip cancellation or interruption coverage protects prepaid, nonrefundable costs if severe weather prevents or derails the broader trip, which is distinct from airline refunds mandated by the U.S. Department of Transportation. Travelers should carefully review policy definitions, reimbursement limits, and timing requirements, as insurance is designed for unforeseen events, making early purchase crucial for travel to weather-prone destinations to ensure coverage. To qualify, recipients must already be receiving certain specified benefits, including pension credit, income support, or universal credit, with some exceptions.
