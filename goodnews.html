The Autumn Equinox Marks Astronomical Autumn's Start on September 23rd

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The autumn equinox, signaling the beginning of astronomical autumn, will occur at 01:06 BST on September 23rd, 2026. This precise moment marks when the Sun crosses the celestial equator, resulting in roughly equal day and night lengths across the globe. While not visually observable, the equinox holds cultural and spiritual significance for some, with gatherings at sites like Stonehenge. Due to light refraction, the actual "equilux," or equal light, occurs a few days later, typically around September 25th or 26th in the UK, depending on latitude.
