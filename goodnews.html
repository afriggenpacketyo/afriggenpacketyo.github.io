MLB All-Star Pitcher Tommy John, Pioneer of Life-Saving Surgery, Dies at 83

2026-08-16 21:08:44 EDT

Thomas "Tommy" John, a four-time MLB All-Star pitcher renowned for the groundbreaking elbow surgery named after him, has died at 83. John, who won 288 career games over 26 years, famously underwent an experimental elbow reconstruction in 1974 after a torn ulnar collateral ligament threatened to end his career. This procedure, now known as "Tommy John surgery," revolutionized baseball by saving the careers of thousands of pitchers. Days before his passing, he penned an emotional farewell letter to fans, expressing gratitude to Dr. Frank Jobe for the surgery that allowed him to continue pitching and profoundly impact the sport for generations.
