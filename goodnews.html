Understanding 'Hang-xiety': The Anxiety That Can Accompany a Hangover

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

'Hang-xiety' refers to the anxiety experienced after drinking alcohol, a phenomenon explained by the way alcohol affects brain chemistry. Alcohol initially enhances calming neurotransmitters like GABA while suppressing excitatory ones, leading to relaxation. However, as alcohol wears off, the brain can become overstimulated, triggering anxiety. Studies indicate that a significant percentage of people experience hang-xiety, and it may be more pronounced in individuals prone to shyness. Experts suggest reframing anxious thoughts as temporary and seeking support to manage the condition.
