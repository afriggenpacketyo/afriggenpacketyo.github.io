Rare Pink Seaweed Confirmed as New Species After Four-Decade Scientific Pursuit

2026-07-31 19:43:10 EDT

A rare pink-striped seaweed, first discovered 40 years ago by Prof Christine Maggs, has finally been confirmed as a new species to science. This elusive seaweed, found living on maerl beds in UK waters, presented a decades-long scientific puzzle due to the difficulty of obtaining a large enough sample for formal identification. Recent samples from Cornish maerl beds, combined with meticulous DNA sequencing, allowed Prof Maggs and her team to confirm its unique identity. Named *ceramium ruenessianum*, this discovery highlights the rich, often unseen, biodiversity of maerl beds and underscores the critical importance of protecting these threatened marine habitats.
