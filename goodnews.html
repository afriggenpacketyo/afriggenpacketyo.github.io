Electric Vehicles May Be Causing Motion Sickness in Some Passengers

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

Some passengers are reporting increased instances of motion sickness when traveling in electric vehicles (EVs), attributing it to the unique engineering of these cars. Experts suggest that the lack of engine noise and vibrations, which normally provide auditory cues for movement, may contribute to this phenomenon. Regenerative braking, where the car slows down when the accelerator is released, can also cause abrupt deceleration, potentially exacerbating motion sickness. While not all EV passengers experience this, carmakers are exploring software and design adjustments to mitigate the issue.
