FAA Integrates AI into Air Traffic Control, CEO Assures Human Oversight

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) is integrating artificial intelligence into its ambitious overhaul of the nation's air traffic control system, a development that has sparked public anxiety. Phillip Buckendorf, CEO of Air Space Intelligence (ASI), the company behind the software, clarified that AI will primarily optimize airspace efficiency by analyzing over 200 data sources for flight routing and scheduling, while human controllers will unequivocally retain ultimate decision-making authority. The system, known as SMART, aims to enhance on-time arrivals and reduce delays by offering predictive insights. Despite criticism from lawmakers, Buckendorf asserts the technology provides world-class tools to human operators, not an automation of control functions.
