2026-10-12 10:23:07 EDT

Columbus Day, also recognized as Indigenous Peoples Day, is an official federal holiday observed on the second Monday in October, leading to closures for federal government offices and some state workers. While the U.S. Postal Service suspends regular mail delivery and many banks are closed, the stock market, including the NYSE and Nasdaq, remains open for regular trading hours. Major retailers like Costco and Walmart also operate on their normal schedules, as the holiday is not universally observed in the private sector. The holiday's recognition varies across the U.S. due to ongoing efforts to shift focus towards North America's original inhabitants.
