2026-11-09 09:42:10 EST

Pet insurance offers a way to manage the rising costs of veterinary services, which have outpaced inflation. Similar to human health insurance, it covers a portion of expenses for diagnostics, treatments, and prescriptions, typically on a reimbursement basis. Owners can choose from accident-only, accident-plus-illness, or wellness coverage, with customizable add-ons available. While policies generally exclude pre-existing conditions and have waiting periods, they offer flexibility in veterinarian choice. The cost varies by animal type, breed, location, and plan, with average annual premiums ranging from $112 for cat accident-only to $1,414 for dog comprehensive plans. Deciding if pet insurance is worthwhile depends on individual finances, local vet costs, and a pet's health profile.
