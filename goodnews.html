UK Launches Major Study on 'Forever Chemicals' (PFAS) in its Population

2026-11-03 02:19:27 EST

The UK government is initiating its first comprehensive study to assess the levels of 'forever chemicals,' or PFAS, within its population by analyzing blood and urine samples. Led by the UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA), the research aims to better understand potential health risks, although current evidence suggests impacts are likely limited, with strongest links to raised cholesterol and lower birth weights. PFAS, used widely since the 1940s in various products, persist in the environment, raising concerns. While two types (PFOA and PFOS) are already banned due to cancer risks, this study will establish baseline levels and build an evidence base, as the UKHSA does not currently recommend routine individual PFAS blood testing.
