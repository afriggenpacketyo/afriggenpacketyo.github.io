Voyager 1 Reaches New Milestone: One Light-Day from Earth

2026-11-15 17:41:22 EST

NASA's Voyager 1, which already holds the distinction of being the farthest human-made object launched into space, is poised to achieve a new monumental milestone: reaching a distance of one light-day from Earth, equating to more than 16 billion miles. Launched in 1977, this remarkable spacecraft has dedicated nearly 50 years to exploration, notably discovering Jupiter's faint ring system and capturing detailed photographs of Saturn's rings and moons. Voyager 1, along with its twin Voyager 2, stands as the sole spacecraft operating beyond the heliosphere, the protective shield of our solar system, continuing its mission to chart and measure interstellar magnetic fields, particles, and plasma waves. NASA is encouraging public participation in a symbolic celebration by sending Voyager's call sign in Morse code.
