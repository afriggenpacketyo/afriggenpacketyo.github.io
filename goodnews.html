Daylight Saving Time 2026 Ends Tonight, Most Americans 'Fall Back' an Hour

2026-10-31 15:00:23 EDT

Daylight Saving Time for 2026 officially concludes at 2 a.m. local time on Sunday, November 1, prompting most Americans to "fall back" an hour and effectively gain an hour of sleep. This twice-yearly time change, initially adopted during World War I to conserve fuel, shifts an hour of daylight from the morning to the evening during spring and summer, reverting to standard time in the colder months. Although legislation like the Sunshine Protection Act has aimed to establish year-round daylight saving time, such proposals have historically struggled to gain lasting approval in Congress, often due to concerns over late winter sunrises. Hawaii and most of Arizona notably do not observe this time change.
