2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

Astronomical autumn officially begins this week with the autumn equinox, occurring at 01:06 BST on September 23, 2026. This precise moment marks when the Sun crosses the celestial equator, resulting in roughly equal day and night lengths across the globe as neither hemisphere is tilted towards or away from the Sun. While the equinox itself is not directly visible, it holds cultural and spiritual significance for many, with gatherings often taking place at sites like Stonehenge.
