Covered Call ETFs Offer Monthly Income but Pose Hidden Tax Implications for Investors

2026-10-19 08:58:03 EDT

Covered call ETFs, popular for generating monthly income, present a complex tax landscape that can surprise investors, particularly regarding non-dividend distributions or "return of capital." While these distributions are tax-deferred, they reduce an investment's cost basis, potentially increasing capital gains tax liability upon sale. Financial advisors recommend holding these ETFs in tax-deferred accounts like traditional IRAs to mitigate immediate tax hits. Investors should also scrutinize a fund's tax-cost ratio and net asset value growth, as well as consider alternative income-generation strategies like bonds or index-based ETFs, which often have lower expense ratios and simpler tax profiles.
