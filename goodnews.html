Finland Unveils World's Largest Sand Battery to Tackle Renewable Energy Intermittency

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in southern Finland has deployed the world's largest commercial sand battery to address the intermittency challenge inherent in renewable energy sources. This 13-meter-tall and 15-meter-wide facility utilizes 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone to store 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy, providing significant heat demand for local residents. Developed by Polar Night Energy, the project has successfully slashed greenhouse gas emissions from the local heating network by nearly 70% and reduced wood chip use by 60%. The innovative technology stores heat from cheap electricity for extended periods, offering a sustainable, rare-earth-free alternative to traditional chemical batteries.
