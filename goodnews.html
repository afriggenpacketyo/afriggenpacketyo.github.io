California Adopts New "Zone Zero" Fire-Safe Landscaping Rules for Fire-Prone Areas

2026-08-19 16:13:51 EDT

California's Board of Forestry and Fire Protection has unanimously approved new "Zone Zero" landscaping requirements, mandating fire-safe measures within five feet of homes located in high-risk fire hazard zones. The regulations prohibit all plants within one foot of the house and require the immediate five-foot perimeter to be clear of combustible materials like firewood, leaves, and certain mulches, also restricting wooden sheds and attached wooden fences. These rules, affecting an estimated 2 million homes, will be implemented in two phases over three to five years, with immediate compliance for new constructions. While proponents emphasize the critical need for these measures to mitigate worsening wildfires, concerns persist regarding the financial burden on homeowners and the "one-size-fits-all" nature of the mandate.
