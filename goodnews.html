The resolution passed with 164 votes in favor, with the United States casting the sole "no" vote. It encourages adopting equal-area projections, such as Equal Earth, over the traditional Mercator projection, which significantly distorts the size of landmasses near the poles. The U.S., however, criticized the initiative as promoting an "ideological agenda."
