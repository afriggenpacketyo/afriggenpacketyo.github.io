Culver City, California to vote on lowering voting age to 16 for local elections

2026-10-28 07:00:00 EDT

Culver City, California, is set to vote on a ballot measure that could allow 16- and 17-year-olds to vote in city and school board elections. Supporters argue that teenagers mature enough to work, drive, and pay taxes are capable of making informed decisions on local matters. Opponents express concerns about the impressionability of younger voters and potential manipulation. This initiative follows a similar measure that narrowly failed in 2022. If passed, Culver City would join a growing number of municipalities nationwide expanding voting rights to younger citizens.
