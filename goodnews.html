Psychologist Recommends "Partial Yield" to De-escalate Couple Conflicts

Psychologist Mark Travers, specializing in couples, advocates for a technique called "partial yield" as the most effective method to de-escalate arguments before they escalate into personal attacks. A partial yield involves conceding specific, genuine faults early in a conflict without conceding the entire position, such as stating, "You're right, that part's on me." This approach rapidly defuses tension by acknowledging the partner's point, releasing their pent-up need to convince, and preventing the accumulation of unresolved grievances that often resurface later. The article stresses the importance of being specific, avoiding qualifying "buts," and offering the concession early, even when mostly right, to foster healthier communication.
