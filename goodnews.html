Jamaica to Formally Petition King Charles III for Slavery Reparations

2026-09-07 02:49:00 EDT

Jamaica's government plans to formally petition King Charles III on Monday, seeking reparations for its African ancestors who endured centuries of enslavement. Culture Minister Olivia Grange will lead a delegation asking the King to refer three key questions to the Privy Council concerning the legality of slavery under English and international law, and Britain's current legal duty for redress. This initiative marks the first time a Commonwealth country has pursued reparatory justice through a legal route, contrasting with Britain's historical compensation to slave owners. The petition aims to establish a legal precedent before deciding on further actions.
