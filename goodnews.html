2026-09-04 15:44:40 EDT

The UN General Assembly has voted to replace the traditional Mercator map with a new world map that more accurately depicts the true size of Africa. The 'Correct the Map' resolution, sponsored by Togo and supported by 164 nations, addresses criticisms that the 16th-century Mercator projection significantly distorts continental sizes, making Africa appear much smaller than its actual 14-times-larger size compared to Greenland. This non-binding vote will lead to changes in maps used by institutions globally, aiming to correct historical misrepresentations and promote a fairer perception of continents.
