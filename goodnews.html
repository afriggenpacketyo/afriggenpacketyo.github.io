Finland Unveils World's Largest Sand Battery, Pioneering Renewable Energy Storage Solution

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in southern Finland is now home to the world's largest commercial sand battery, a 13-meter-tall facility capable of storing 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy within 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone. Developed by Polar Night Energy, this innovative system addresses renewable energy intermittency by storing excess wind and solar electricity as heat, then discharging it via heat exchangers. The project provides nearly a month of summer heat for 5,000 residents and has reduced greenhouse gas emissions from the local heating network by almost 70%, offering a sustainable and rare-earth-material-free solution for long-duration energy storage.
