MLB All-Star Pitcher Tommy John, Surgery Namesake, Dies at 83

2026-08-16 21:08:44 EDT

Thomas "Tommy" John, a revered four-time MLB All-Star pitcher who achieved 288 career wins across 26 seasons, has passed away at the age of 83. His enduring legacy in baseball is defined by the experimental elbow reconstruction procedure he underwent in 1974, now universally known as "Tommy John surgery." This groundbreaking surgery by Dr. Frank Jobe not only saved his career but has since preserved the pitching arms of thousands of athletes. Following his recovery, John remarkably went on to win 164 additional games, concluding his illustrious career in 1989 after playing for several teams, including the Los Angeles Dodgers and New York Yankees. Days before his death, he shared a heartfelt farewell letter, thanking fans and acknowledging the surgery that enabled his extended career.
