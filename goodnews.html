UK and EU reach preliminary agreement to reduce post-Brexit trade barriers for food exports

2026-11-03 06:18:17 EST

The UK and EU have agreed on a draft deal aimed at eliminating post-Brexit checks on food and agricultural exports, with the text being circulated among EU nations for consideration. This "SPS" agreement is seen as a significant part of a broader "Brexit reset" and could lead to the resumption of exports for items like fresh sausages and oysters. The deal is expected to bring substantial economic benefits to the UK by removing costly and time-consuming export health certificates and other red tape. In return, the UK will likely commit to adapting to existing EU regulations on trade standards, a process known as "dynamic alignment."
