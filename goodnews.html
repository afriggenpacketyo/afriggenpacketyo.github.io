Pig Kidney Functions for 271 Days in Human Patient, Offering Hope for Transplants

2026-09-03 18:31:23 EDT

A pig kidney has successfully functioned in a human patient for 271 days, the longest recorded duration, offering significant hope for xenotransplantation. Doctors at Mass General Brigham used a genetically modified pig kidney as a temporary 'bridge' until a human donor was found. This advancement addresses the critical shortage of organs for transplants, with thousands waiting for kidneys annually. While the pig organ eventually failed, the procedure demonstrates the potential of using animal organs to save lives.
