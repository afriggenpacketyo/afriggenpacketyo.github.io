2026-10-23 07:09:08 EDT

Chinese scientists have successfully reconstructed the face of Denisovans, a mysterious species of ancient humans previously known only through DNA and fossil fragments. The work not only confirmed the remains belonged to the Denisovan lineage but also allowed researchers to create a visual representation of what these hominids, who lived thousands of years ago in parts of Siberia and East/Southeast Asia, might have looked like.
