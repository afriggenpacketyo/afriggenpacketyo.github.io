2026-10-14 00:00:09 EDT

This area, located up to 6,000 meters beneath the surface, is known for its immense reserves of vital metals like nickel, cobalt, and manganese, which could potentially surpass all known land-based reserves. While no commercial mining has yet occurred in this ecologically sensitive region, the International Seabed Authority has already granted 17 exploration contracts, with China holding five. This significant discovery underscores the rich, yet largely unexplored, biodiversity of deep-sea environments and raises new scientific and environmental considerations for future deep-sea mining activities in such unique ecosystems.
