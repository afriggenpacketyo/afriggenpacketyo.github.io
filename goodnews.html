First Known Adult T. Rex Footprints Discovered in North Dakota

2026-09-11 10:34:07 EDT

Scientists have discovered the first known series of footprints from an adult Tyrannosaurus rex in North Dakota's Hell Creek Formation, offering unprecedented insights into the creature's locomotion. The 23-foot-long trackway, consisting of four distinct three-toed prints, provides rare behavioral evidence of the famous dinosaur's movement across the ancient landscape. Researchers utilized high-resolution 3D scanning to document each footprint, estimating the T. rex's walking pace at approximately 3.5-4.5 mph, comparable to a brisk human walk. This significant paleontological finding, dating back 66.5 million years, was initially identified by a Colorado schoolteacher and museum volunteer, highlighting the importance of citizen science.
