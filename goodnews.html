China Initiates Construction of World's First Supercritical CO2 and Molten Salt Energy Plant

2026-09-22 01:41:58 EDT

China has broken ground on the world’s first utility-scale combined supercritical carbon dioxide power generation and molten salt energy storage project, marking a significant advancement in clean energy technology. Located at China Huaneng’s Bajiao power station in Yantai, the Ruitan demonstration project is slated for operation next year. This innovative system will leverage surplus electricity from existing coal power units to heat molten salt during off-peak hours.
