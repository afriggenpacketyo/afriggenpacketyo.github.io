Understanding the 2026 Autumn Equinox and Its Astronomical Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The autumn equinox, marking the start of astronomical autumn, will occur on September 23rd at 01:06 BST. This precise moment signifies when the Sun crosses the celestial equator, resulting in roughly equal day and night lengths globally. While the equinox itself is an invisible astronomical event, it holds cultural and spiritual importance for many, with gatherings at sites like Stonehenge. Due to light refraction, the actual period of equal light, known as the "equilux," happens a few days later, around September 25th or 26th in the UK, with northern regions experiencing faster daylight loss.
