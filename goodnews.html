Daylight Saving Time Concludes, Clocks Fall Back One Hour Sunday Morning

2026-10-31 22:55:42 EST

Daylight Saving Time officially concludes before dawn on Sunday, November 1, necessitating that most individuals set their clocks back one hour at 2 a.m., which effectively provides an extra hour of sleep. This seasonal adjustment results in earlier sunrises but also earlier sunsets. While modern cellphones typically perform this change automatically, traditional analog clocks require manual adjustment. Despite ongoing legislative efforts, such as the proposed Sunshine Protection Act, to establish permanent Daylight Saving Time, its passage this year remains improbable. Proponents of making standard time permanent, including sleep scientists and medical professionals, contend that such a move would be more beneficial for natural sleep cycles and could mitigate health risks and pedestrian collisions during darker morning commutes.
