Conservation Efforts Underway to Save Critically Rare Glutinous Snail in Wales

2026-09-12 02:09:37 EDT

Conservationists are undertaking a crucial mission to save the glutinous snail, identified as one of Europe's rarest species and found exclusively in Wales' largest natural lake, Llyn Tegid. Several British zoos are now establishing "ark populations" using creatures successfully bred by conservationist Ian Hughes in his Ceredigion garden, a significant breakthrough given its critical endangerment. This tiny mollusc, no bigger than a fingertip, is vital for healthy aquatic ecosystems but has been driven to extinction across England due to pollution and habitat loss. The initiative, supported by a £60 million UK government-backed scheme, aims to eventually restore the species to wild locations, safeguarding this unique Welsh natural treasure.
