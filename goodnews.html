Factors influencing individual caffeine sensitivity explained

2026-09-16 20:09:43 EDT

Individual responses to caffeine vary significantly due to factors like genetics, metabolism, smoking habits, and medication use. Caffeine's half-life, the time it takes for the body to eliminate half of it, can range from six to 20 hours, influencing how long its effects last. Smoking can accelerate caffeine metabolism, while certain medications and pregnancy can slow it down. Tolerance also develops with regular consumption, making a person less sensitive. Sleep experts recommend avoiding caffeine several hours before bedtime, as it can affect both the ability to fall asleep and sleep quality. Moderation is key, as sensitivity varies greatly among individuals.
