England and Wales to Abolish Leasehold System, Introduce Commonhold

2026-11-19 04:26:59 EST

England and Wales are set to introduce new laws to scrap the leasehold ownership system for flats, replacing it with commonhold where residents collectively own and manage their buildings. The proposed Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill aims to cap ground rents at £250 annually, eventually reducing them to a "peppercorn" rate after 40 years. Housing Minister Matthew Pennycook condemned the current system as "blighting lives" and an "anachronism." While the Residential Freehold Association criticizes the plan as an "unprecedented abuse" of rights, the government seeks to align England and Wales with other countries that already utilize shared ownership models, addressing long-standing leaseholder complaints.
