Recognizing Stroke Symptoms: The BE FAST Acronym for Quick Action

2026-11-01 17:22:12 EST

Prompted by Senator Chris Murphy's recent stroke, this article emphasizes the critical importance of knowing the warning signs of a stroke using the easily remembered "BE FAST" acronym. BE FAST stands for Balance loss, Eye changes, Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, and Time to call 911. Acting quickly to get medical care upon recognizing these symptoms significantly improves chances of survival and minimizes long-term brain damage, which occurs when blood flow to the brain is interrupted. Understanding and applying these symptoms is crucial for public health, as timely intervention can prevent permanent disability or even death.
