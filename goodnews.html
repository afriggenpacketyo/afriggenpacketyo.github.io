BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit Despite Technical Glitch

Despite a technical glitch during the orbital insertion maneuver, which caused the spacecraft to enter safe mode and only complete 77% of the burn, mission control confirmed its successful lock onto Mercury's orbit. The anomaly has been traced to a software issue related to attitude guidance and thruster control. The spacecraft will now make further adjustments before releasing two science orbiters in December to commence scientific observations of the planet.
