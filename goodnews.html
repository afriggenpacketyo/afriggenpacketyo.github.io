Understanding the Impact of Full Judgment Payment on Wage Garnishment Cessation

2026-09-01 10:20:37 EDT

Paying a debt judgment in full should ultimately stop wage garnishment, as the creditor no longer has a balance to collect. However, the garnishment may not cease immediately due to necessary administrative steps, such as the creditor filing a satisfaction of judgment with the court and formally terminating the garnishment order to notify the employer. There can be a lag between the day the payment is made and the day the garnishment actually stops, potentially leading to another deduction if payroll has already been processed. Borrowers are advised to obtain written confirmation of payment, verify that all necessary paperwork has been filed, and confirm with their employer to ensure the process is complete and prevent any overpayments from occurring.
