2026-09-21 00:00:10 EDT

Chinese scientists have achieved a breakthrough in underwater energy, successfully testing the world's first practical submarine solar power plant at a depth of 10 meters in the open sea. Developed by a team from Yunnan University, this perovskite-based photovoltaic system charged batteries and powered an LED panel, demonstrating the viability of harvesting solar energy even in the ocean's dim, blue-green light. This innovation overcomes previous challenges with conventional silicon solar cells in narrow underwater light spectrums. The technology could significantly advance future underwater operations by providing a sustainable power source for various submerged applications.
