California Supreme Court Permits Electronic Recording in Civil Cases Lacking Court Reporters

2026-08-10 15:13:16 EDT

The California Supreme Court has ruled that electronic recording is permissible in civil cases when a court reporter is unavailable and parties cannot afford a private one. This landmark decision aims to ensure "meaningful access to the judicial process" for indigent litigants, particularly in family court disputes where over a million hearings annually lacked verbatim records, hindering appeals. Chief Justice Patricia Guerrero emphasized that a record is "presupposed under California law" and that the shortage of stenographers should not deny fundamental appeal rights. The ruling applies statewide, addressing a long-standing issue of judicial access and potentially increasing job opportunities for court reporters.
