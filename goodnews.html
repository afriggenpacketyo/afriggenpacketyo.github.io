Researchers Document First Northeast Pacific Sighting of Elusive Bigfin Squid

2026-09-05 06:00:00 EDT

Researchers from the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute have captured the first live sighting of the elusive bigfin squid in the northeast Pacific Ocean. This rare deep-sea creature, characterized by its long, pale "legs" and undulating fins, was observed two miles deep by an underwater vehicle during an expedition focused on studying seamounts. Only about 70 such sightings have ever been recorded globally, making this discovery highly significant for marine biology and our understanding of deep-sea ecosystems. The event underscores the many mysteries still held by the deep ocean, inspiring awe and further research among the scientists involved.
