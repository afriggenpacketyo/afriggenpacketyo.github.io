England and Wales to Abolish Leasehold System for Flats

2026-11-19 04:26:59 EST

The UK government is set to introduce new laws to dismantle the contentious leasehold ownership system for flats across England and Wales, transitioning instead to a commonhold model where residents collectively own and manage their buildings. Housing Minister Matthew Pennycook condemned the existing leasehold framework as an "anachronism" that significantly "blights lives." The forthcoming Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill will also cap ground rents for current leaseholders at £250 per year, eventually reducing them to a nominal "peppercorn" rate after four decades. While these reforms aim to empower homeowners and align the UK with international norms, they are met with strong opposition from the Residential Freehold Association, which predicts billions in losses for pension funds invested in property.
