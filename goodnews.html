New Drug Offers Hope to Woman Allergic to Sunlight

2026-10-03 10:10:29 EDT

A clinical trial for a drug called bitopertin is offering significant hope to individuals with erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP), a rare condition causing extreme pain upon sun exposure. Emily Pearson, who has suffered from EPP since childhood, experienced a life-changing improvement in her tolerance to sunlight after participating in the trial. The drug works by lowering the levels of protoporphyrin IX in the skin, a molecule activated by sunlight that causes painful reactions in EPP patients. Pearson, now able to run marathons and enjoy outdoor activities without severe pain, describes the medication as a 'game changer' that has dramatically improved her quality of life. Phase 3 trials are ongoing.
