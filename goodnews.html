The Privy Council unanimously ruled that sections of Trinidad and Tobago's Sexual Offences Act, criminalizing "buggery" and "serious indecency" among same-sex couples, violated Jones' fundamental rights to private and family life under the country's 1976 constitution. This decision, after nearly a decade-long challenge, is a significant victory for LGBTQ+ rights in the Caribbean, sending a clear message that laws criminalizing such activities cannot be justified and affirming that constitutional rights protect individual dignity and equality regardless of public opinion.
