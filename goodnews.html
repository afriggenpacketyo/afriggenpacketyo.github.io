2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a joint European and Japanese endeavor, successfully placed a pair of robotic spacecraft into orbit around Mercury after an arduous eight-year cruise through the inner solar system. This marks only the third time a spacecraft has visited the sun's closest planet and the first in over a decade, representing a significant scientific milestone. The two linked explorers will separate next month to map and extensively study Mercury's dense interior, magnetic field, impact craters, and volcanic plains from different altitudes.
