BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Orbit Around Mercury After Eight-Year Journey

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

After an eight-year, six-billion-mile journey through the inner solar system, the BepiColombo mission, a joint European and Japanese endeavor, successfully entered orbit around Mercury. This marks only the third time a spacecraft has visited the sun's closest planet and the first in over a decade. The two linked robotic explorers will separate next month to conduct detailed studies of Mercury's dense interior, exterior magnetic field, and surface features from different altitudes. Despite the extreme temperatures and technical challenges, the mission aims to operate for at least one Earth year, providing unprecedented comparative measurements of one of the solar system's least explored planets.
