Perseid Meteor Shower Peaks Alongside Partial Solar Eclipse

2026-08-10 21:59:24 EDT

The annual Perseid meteor shower is set to reach its peak this week, offering skywatchers a dazzling display of "shooting stars" as Earth passes through debris from the Swift-Tuttle comet. This year, the event coincides with a deep partial solar eclipse visible in the UK, creating a unique astronomical opportunity. The best viewing times for the Perseids are the early morning hours of August 12 and 13, with up to 100 meteors per hour possible under dark, light-pollution-free skies, aided by a new moon. While the eclipse will not fully darken skies in the UK enough to see meteors simultaneously, both events promise an exceptional night of stargazing.

Study Links Severe COVID-19 to Reactivation of Dormant Viruses

2026-08-10 19:17:48 EDT

A new study suggests that severe COVID-19 may trigger the reactivation of dormant viruses, such as various herpes viruses and Anelloviridae, which reside inactive in the body after prior infections. Researchers observed this phenomenon in over 1,100 hospitalized COVID-19 patients, noting that Anelloviridae reactivation was associated with long-term physical disability and long COVID. The study also found Epstein-Barr virus and cytomegalovirus reactivated in response to inflammation, rather than immune suppression. These findings could significantly advance the understanding of long COVID and aid in developing new diagnostic tests and treatments, though further research is needed to establish causality and explore the role of these reactivated viruses.
