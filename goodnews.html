Each September, the Nobel Committee sends nomination forms to hundreds of eligible individuals and organizations, generating a list that can reach 200 names. The committee then narrows this to five, whose works are reviewed by Academy members over the summer. The criteria remain confidential, with laureates often waiting decades between nomination and award. The process considers ambitious, influential, and universal literature, often from authors who have won other major literary awards, ensuring a rigorous evaluation.
