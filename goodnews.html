United Nations Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Global Representation

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map that more accurately depicts the true proportions of continents, particularly Africa. This resolution, sponsored by Togo and backed by African Union members, aims to replace the traditional Mercator map, which distorted landmasses by enlarging regions near the poles. The Mercator projection, useful for navigation, made Greenland appear similar in size to Africa, whereas Africa is actually 14 times larger. The UN encourages governments and institutions to adopt this new projection, recognizing the influence maps have on global understanding and perceptions.
