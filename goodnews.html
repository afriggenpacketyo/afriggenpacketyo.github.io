Australia's Official Music Charts Ban Wholly AI-Generated Music to Protect Human Artistry

2026-08-25 00:54:01 EDT

Australia's official music charts, overseen by the Australian Recording Industry Association (ARIA), have implemented new rules banning wholly AI-generated music, requiring all eligible tracks to be "substantially human made." Effective August 31, this policy aims to "promote the human nature of artistry" and addresses concerns raised by incidents like an AI-assisted cover topping the dance singles chart. While AI tools can still be utilized for tasks such as mastering or providing drum machines, the core creative elements, including songwriting, lead vocals, and primary instruments, must originate from human artists.
