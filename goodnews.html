Wayward SpaceX Rocket Booster Set to Collide with the Moon

2026-08-04 18:28:42 EDT

An 8,800-pound SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket booster is on a collision course with the moon, expected to crash into the lunar surface at 5,400 mph early Wednesday. This rare man-made impact, near the Einstein and Bell craters, poses no danger to Earth. The booster, which launched lunar landers in January 2025, has been in orbit since, with its trajectory altered by solar activity and gravity. NASA and independent astronomers confirmed the impact, which is expected to create a crater 60-100 feet wide and 12-16 feet deep. Scientists plan to analyze before-and-after photos from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter to gain insights into lunar geology and refine space tracking techniques.
