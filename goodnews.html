BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Orbit Around Mercury After Eight-Year Journey

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a collaborative effort involving European and Japanese spacecraft, has successfully entered orbit around Mercury after an arduous eight-year cruise through the inner solar system. This significant achievement marks the first visit to Mercury in over a decade, with the mission aiming to unravel the planet's many mysteries. The two robotic explorers, which will separate next month, are tasked with mapping and studying Mercury's dense interior, exterior magnetic field, and diverse surface features, including craters and mountain ridges, from distinct altitudes. Despite the extreme temperatures and technical challenges, scientists eagerly await new data streams in the spring, hoping to gain unprecedented insights into the solar system's least explored inner planet.
