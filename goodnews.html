England and Wales to Abolish Leasehold System, Introduce Commonhold

2026-11-19 04:26:59 EST

The UK government plans to introduce new laws to abolish the outdated leasehold ownership system for flats in England and Wales, replacing it with commonhold, where residents collectively own and manage their buildings. Housing Minister Matthew Pennycook described the current leasehold system as "blighting lives" and an "anachronism in the 21st century." The forthcoming Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill will also cap ground rents, the annual fee paid by current leaseholders, at £250 from 2028, eventually reducing them to a "peppercorn" rate after 40 years. This significant reform aims to align England and Wales with property ownership standards in many other countries globally, addressing widespread complaints from millions of leaseholders.
