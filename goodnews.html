Research Explores Why Electric Vehicles May Induce Motion Sickness in Passengers

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

Some passengers are reporting increased motion sickness in electric vehicles (EVs), a phenomenon attributed to sensory mismatches between what the body perceives and what it expects. Experts suggest that the absence of engine sounds and vibrations, which traditionally signal acceleration, along with the "harsh deceleration" of regenerative braking, can trigger nausea. While not all EV passengers experience this, and some even report less sickness, the issue is gaining attention from researchers and even EV manufacturers like XPeng. As EVs become more prevalent, understanding and mitigating these effects through design adjustments and passenger coping strategies is becoming increasingly important for a comfortable transition to electric transport.
