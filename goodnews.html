Covered Call ETFs Offer Monthly Income, But Investors Face Complex Tax Implications

2026-10-19 08:58:03 EDT

Covered call ETFs, popular for generating monthly cash flow, present a potential tax surprise for investors, particularly Baby Boomers seeking income. These funds, which hold equities and sell call options, distribute income in various forms, including dividends, capital gains, and return of capital (ROC). ROC distributions, while tax-deferred, reduce an investment's cost basis, leading to higher capital gains tax liability upon sale. Many investors overlook these complexities until tax time, impacting their after-tax returns. Financial advisors recommend using tax-deferred accounts for covered call ETFs and carefully evaluating a fund's tax-cost ratio and Net Asset Value growth to understand true returns.
