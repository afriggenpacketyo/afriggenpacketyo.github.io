Germany's Economic Growth Faces Headwinds from Demographics and Debt

2026-11-15 11:12:00 EST

Germany's economy is showing signs of recovery with projected growth of 1.3% for 2026, driven by exports and government spending. However, long-term challenges persist, primarily an aging population increasing social welfare costs while the working-age population shrinks. This demographic shift places immense pressure on public finances, with social welfare, defense, and debt interest consuming a large portion of the federal budget. Experts warn that current growth rates are insufficient to maintain prosperity, and Germany faces the risk of losing its AAA credit rating if debt levels continue to rise.
