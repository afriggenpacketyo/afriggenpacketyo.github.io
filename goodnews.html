California Mandates Energy Efficiency for Replacement Tires, First in US

2026-08-19 19:48:06 EDT

California has become the first U.S. state to mandate energy efficiency for replacement tires, a significant auto industry regulation aimed at reducing emissions and fuel costs for drivers. The new rules, unanimously approved by the California Energy Commission, will begin phasing in during 2029, requiring replacement tires to be at least as energy-efficient as those sold on new vehicles. Environmental and clean-air advocates praise the initiative, while some industry groups express concerns about increased costs for consumers. The regulations are projected to save Californians nearly $1 billion annually in gasoline and electricity costs and reduce carbon dioxide emissions by 2 million metric tons per year.
