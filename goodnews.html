Germany Considers Reinstating Mandatory Civilian Service Alongside Military Conscription

2026-08-08 01:46:00 EDT

Germany is debating the reintroduction of mandatory civilian service, a constitutional alternative to military conscription for conscientious objectors, which was suspended in 2011. The Family Affairs Ministry has proactively assessed organizations' capacities to offer such service, with 22 out of 23 expressing willingness. This move aligns with the Bundeswehr's goal to significantly increase troop numbers to 260,000 by 2030 and fulfill NATO requirements. While the current government maintains voluntary military service, the option to reactivate conscription and civilian service remains. Critics, however, warn against using mandatory service as a solution for underfunded social services, advocating instead for improved conditions for voluntary programs.
