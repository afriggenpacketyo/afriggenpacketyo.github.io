NASA's Dragonfly Mission to Titan Aims to Search for Life

2026-08-22 06:00:00 EDT

NASA is actively developing Dragonfly, a sophisticated $3.3-billion nuclear-powered helicopter-like spacecraft, for a highly anticipated 2028 launch to Titan, Saturn's largest moon. This ambitious mission, which has been a quarter-century in the making, aims to explore Titan's unique methane-laden atmosphere, its exotic lakes, and its potentially life-supporting subsurface ocean in search of signs of past or present extraterrestrial life. Scientists are particularly hopeful for groundbreaking discoveries, noting Titan's surprisingly Earth-like geological features despite its extreme cold. Dragonfly currently stands as NASA's last large-scale planetary science mission, with considerable expectations riding on its success amidst fluctuating space exploration funding.

Mahatma Gandhi's Handwritten Notes Fetch Record $1.7 Million at Auction

2026-08-22 04:43:00 EDT

A rare collection of 688 handwritten notes by Mahatma Gandhi, addressed to his close associate Anand Hingorani, has sold for a record $1.7 million at a Mumbai auction. These deeply personal notes, penned between 1944 and 1946 following the death of Hingorani's wife, offered intimate insights on profound themes such as truth, God, and non-violence, later compiled into the book "A Thought for the Day." The sale, which also included other personal artifacts like Gandhi's spinning wheel, represents the highest price ever paid for an Indian historical document at auction, underscoring the enduring value and global historical significance of Gandhi's legacy.
