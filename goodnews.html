Supreme Court to Review Legality of 160-Year-Old Federal Ban on Home Liquor Distilling

2026-10-19 09:52:40 EDT

The Supreme Court announced it will consider the constitutionality of a nearly 160-year-old federal ban on distilling liquor at home, a law originally enacted in 1868 to prevent widespread tax evasion by commercial distillers. The Hobby Distillers Association and one of its members argue that this prohibition exceeds Congress's enumerated powers, contending it criminalizes conduct not directly subject to taxation and paradoxically reduces potential tax revenue. Lower courts have sided with the plaintiffs, suggesting the ban is not "plainly adapted" to support the government's taxing power. The Solicitor General, however, contends the restrictions are necessary because home distilling can more easily conceal taxable activity, setting the stage for a significant legal battle over federal authority.
