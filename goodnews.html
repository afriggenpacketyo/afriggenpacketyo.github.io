California Targets "Montana Loophole" Used by Luxury Car Buyers to Evade Taxes

2026-10-05 22:47:52 EDT

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed new legislation aimed at closing the "Montana loophole," a practice used by luxury-car buyers to evade state sales and registration taxes. This scheme involves creating shell companies in states without such taxes, like Montana, to register high-end vehicles. The state estimates it loses millions annually due to these schemes. The new law expands the definition of a resident for sales tax purposes, making companies liable if at least one member resides in California, and authorizes tax agencies to impose liability on individual business members, enhancing enforcement against tax evasion.
