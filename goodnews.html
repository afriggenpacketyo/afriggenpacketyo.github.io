2026-08-06 22:22:43 EDT

Researchers at the La Brea Tar Pits and Museum in Los Angeles have announced a significant discovery: an extinct ice age spadefoot toad, *Spea labreae*. This find is notable as it represents one of only two ice age amphibians ever found in the U.S., the other being a tree frog in Florida. The discovery, which occurred two years ago but was recently published, underscores the immense value of re-examining existing museum collections. Scientists emphasize that amphibians are crucial indicators of past climates due to their sensitivity to environmental changes and their limited travel, making this rare fossil find highly impactful for understanding how local environments transformed over time.
