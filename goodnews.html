Strategically positioned between "sundown towns," it evolved into a vibrant community hub, hosting various social events. Colombia's Frailejons: Fuzzy "Water Guardians" Under Threat

2026-11-24 05:00:00 EST

Colombia's high mountain wetlands, or páramos, are home to the unique frailejon plants, fuzzy "water guardians" crucial for the nation's water supply. These sunflower-related flora efficiently absorb moisture from cloud cover, sustaining the páramos that feed major rivers. However, two-thirds of the nearly 150 frailejon species face escalating threats from agricultural expansion, cattle ranching, coal mining, and climate change, which introduces invasive species. Significant conservation efforts are underway, including widespread reforestation campaigns and a popular cartoon character, Ernesto Pérez, which has boosted public awareness. Volunteers, inspired by these initiatives, are actively working to protect these ecologically and culturally significant plants.
