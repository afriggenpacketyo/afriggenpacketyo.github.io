Knott’s Berry Farm and Great America Settle Class-Action Lawsuit Over Hidden Processing Fees

2026-11-13 00:18:22 EST

Over a million theme park goers who purchased tickets to Knott’s Berry Farm and the soon-to-close California’s Great America may be eligible for an $8 refund as part of an $11-million class-action lawsuit settlement. The suit alleged that Six Flags Entertainment Corp., which now owns the parks, engaged in "drip pricing" by not disclosing processing fees until late in the online purchase process. California residents who bought non-season tickets online between June 28, 2020, and July 1, 2024, and were charged a processing fee, are eligible for this settlement. Claimants can submit an online claim to receive $8 cash, or they will automatically receive an $8 credit for Six Flags' website, with the deadline to file set for December 22, 2026.
