UK Prime Minister Reverses Course on Jury Trial Reforms Amid Public Outcry

2026-10-03 23:07:30 EDT

Prime Minister Andy Burnham has withdrawn contentious proposals to limit jury trials, a plan initially introduced by the previous government to tackle significant court backlogs. The original intent was to allow judges to decide less serious cases and keep medium-level crimes with magistrates, reserving juries for only the most serious offenses, including murder and rape. This reversal comes after widespread opposition from criminal lawyers and politicians who argued it would erode the 800-year-old right to trial by jury, despite the growing backlog of nearly 81,000 cases. The Ministry of Justice now indicates that only complex fraud and financial offenses will be judge-alone, with other medium-level crimes remaining under magistrates' purview.
