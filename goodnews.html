BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

After an eight-year journey through the inner solar system, the BepiColombo mission, a joint European and Japanese endeavor, has successfully entered orbit around Mercury. This marks only the third time a spacecraft has visited the sun's closest planet and the first in over a decade. The $2 billion mission involves two linked robotic explorers that will separate next month to map and study Mercury's dense interior, magnetic field, and surface features from different altitudes. Despite the extreme temperatures and technical challenges, scientists anticipate a wealth of new data to unravel the planet's mysteries, with the mission expected to last at least one Earth year.
