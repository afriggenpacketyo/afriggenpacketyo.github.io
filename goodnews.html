Lennard argues that society is frequently constrained not by genuine uncertainty, but by unexamined "certainties" – deeply entrenched beliefs concerning border regimes, enforced gender binaries, or the inevitability of capitalist frameworks. The article highlights how historical movements, such as the George Floyd uprisings, have successfully ruptured political certainties, demonstrating that ongoing practices of resistance, community building, and critical engagement with dominant narratives are essential for progress, even in the absence of a definitive blueprint for systemic transformation.
