BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Orbit Around Mercury

2026-11-21 22:55:02 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a joint European and Japanese endeavor, successfully placed a pair of robotic spacecraft into orbit around Mercury after an arduous eight-year cruise through the inner solar system. This marks only the third time a spacecraft has visited the sun's closest planet and the first in over a decade, highlighting a major scientific achievement. The two probes will separate next month to independently map and study Mercury’s dense interior, exterior magnetic field, and complex surface features like impact craters and volcanic plains. The $2 billion mission aims to provide unprecedented comparative measurements, overcoming extreme temperatures to gather data for at least one Earth year.
