Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs) Affects Athletes, Highlighting Under-recognized Health Risks

2026-10-20 01:45:34 EDT

This article highlights Relative Energy Deficiency in Sport (REDs), a syndrome impacting athletes due to insufficient energy intake, leading to severe health issues like brittle bones and hormonal disruption. Initially known as the "female athlete triad," REDs affects both men and women, though male cases are often under-recognized due to subtle symptoms and stigma. The "lighter is faster" culture in sports contributes to its prevalence, with athletes initially seeing performance gains before experiencing decline and injury. Experts advocate for greater awareness, education, and mandatory health checks, as seen in World Climbing, to prevent long-term health consequences and promote sustainable athletic careers.
