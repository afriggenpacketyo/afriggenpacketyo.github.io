Under the updated National Planning Policy Framework, coming into force Monday, developers must now prove there's no reasonable prospect of keeping a pub business running, including marketing it for sale for at least 12 months. This measure also aims to prevent owners from deliberately killing off trade to justify conversion. While hospitality groups welcome the protection, they emphasize that high taxes and employment costs are the primary drivers of pub closures.
