Prague Museum Honors Roma Resistance Fighters of World War II

2026-10-25 05:17:00 EDT

Prague's Museum of the Romani National Revival has opened an exhibition to honor Czech and Slovak Roma who fought as resistance fighters and soldiers during World War II, challenging their historical portrayal solely as victims of the Holocaust. This groundbreaking research identified over 1,400 Romani fighters, including figures like Anton Facuna and Josef Serinek, posthumously awarded the Medal for Heroism. The exhibition aims to educate both Roma and non-Roma about these overlooked heroes, addressing decades of marginalization in historical writing. This initiative also seeks to empower the Roma community, who continue to face social disadvantage and underrepresentation in Czechia.
