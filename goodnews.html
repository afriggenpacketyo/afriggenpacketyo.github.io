Switzerland to Vote on Stricter Interpretation of Its Historic Neutrality Policy

2026-09-27 03:49:00 EDT

Switzerland is holding a referendum to determine if its longstanding policy of neutrality, a constitutional principle since 1848, should be applied more strictly. The Pro Schweiz association, linked to the right-wing populist Swiss People's Party, advocates for "permanently and without exception" neutrality, arguing the government has become too close to the EU and NATO since Russia's war in Ukraine. Opponents, including nearly all major political parties and the Foreign Minister, contend that neutrality requires flexibility in the modern world and should not equate perpetrators and victims.
