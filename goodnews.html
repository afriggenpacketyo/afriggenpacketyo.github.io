AI and Advanced Tech Aid Restoration of Italy's Basilica of Saint Francis

2026-10-04 20:05:48 EDT

The Basilica of Saint Francis of Assisi, significantly damaged by powerful earthquakes in 1997, is undergoing an innovative restoration effort utilizing artificial intelligence and advanced technology. Art historians and software engineers are collaborating with Franciscan friars to digitally reconstruct the 13th-century church, employing gigapixel cameras, drones, and lasers to create the highest-resolution 3D blueprint of a building ever made. This comprehensive digital twin will serve as an exact reconstruction roadmap for potential future disasters and enable AI to piece together over 80,000 fragments from the collapsed roof. The project also aims to enhance accessibility to the basilica's priceless art through virtual reality, effectively preserving history while significantly expanding its global reach.
