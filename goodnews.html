D-Day Veteran Kenneth Hay Dies at 101, Remembered for Wartime Service and Education

2026-09-21 05:04:28 EDT

Kenneth Hay, a D-Day veteran from east London, has passed away at the age of 101. Hay, who served in the 4th Dorset Infantry Regiment, survived capture by the German 12th SS Panzer Division weeks after landing in Normandy and endured a harrowing 1,000-mile forced march as a prisoner of war. Liberated by U.S. forces in 1945, he later dedicated years to educating schoolchildren about World War Two, earning the nickname "Grandad Ken." His remarkable life and unwavering passion for sharing his experiences were honored by the British Normandy Memorial, for which he was an ambassador.
