2026-08-22 22:00:10 EDT

2026-08-22 21:37:25 EDT

Governor Gavin Newsom has signed legislation, Assembly Bill 2624, which will allow workers and volunteers at immigrant-serving organizations to shield their home addresses from public records. The bill also criminalizes the online posting of their personal information with the intent to incite violence or harassment. This measure aims to protect individuals who provide legal, humanitarian, and other services to immigrants from intimidation and threats. The program is set to become active in October 2027. Critics have raised concerns that such laws could potentially be used to restrict citizen journalists, but proponents argue it is necessary to counter a climate of fear fueled by anti-immigrant rhetoric.
