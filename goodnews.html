Former U.S. Senator Nancy Kassebaum Baker Dies at 94

2026-08-22 16:40:38 EDT

Nancy Kassebaum Baker, the first woman elected to the U.S. Senate without succeeding a spouse, has passed away at the age of 94. A prominent independent-minded Republican from Kansas, she served three terms after her 1978 election, following in the progressive footsteps of her father, Governor Alf Landon. Kassebaum Baker was widely respected for her ability to work across party lines, notably co-sponsoring a significant health insurance law with liberal U.S. Senator Ted Kennedy. Even after retiring, she continued public service and later broke with her party, endorsing Democratic candidates and publicly advocating for Donald Trump's impeachment in 2021. Her legacy reflects a commitment to bipartisan cooperation and principled independence.
