Navigating Credit Card Balance Transfers After Missed Payments

2026-07-28 10:13:09 EDT

Transferring a credit card balance after missing payments is generally possible but becomes more difficult with increased delinquency, as payment history significantly impacts credit scores. While a single late payment might not prevent qualification, it could lead to lower credit limits or higher ongoing interest rates. If payments are significantly overdue, securing a balance transfer becomes much harder, as issuers perceive higher risk. For those with chronic payment issues, broader debt relief strategies like debt settlement or management programs may be more effective than a balance transfer, which works best when debt can be paid off during the promotional period.
