2026-11-13 23:00:33 EST

While the test accurately distinguishes advanced CTE from healthy individuals, it currently cannot reliably identify early-stage CTE. If confirmed in larger studies, this breakthrough could enable earlier intervention and drug trials, significantly advancing the understanding and management of CTE, which has affected numerous former NFL players and remains a major concern in sports.

The measure, which passed with just over 51% of the vote, also bans retroactive taxes, except in cases of state-declared emergencies, though it does not apply to real estate. Supporters, including Google co-founder Sergey Brin and various business associations, argued the measure protects retirement savings and combats already high living costs. Opponents of Proposition 42, who backed Proposition 40, may attempt to introduce a wealth tax again in 2028, requiring voters to nullify this new ban.
