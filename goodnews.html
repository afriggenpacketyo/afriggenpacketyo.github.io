Second Wave of Olive Ridley Sea Turtle Hatchlings Released in California

2026-11-16 08:00:00 EST

A second group of 21 olive ridley sea turtle hatchlings was successfully released into the ocean at Huntington Beach, California, just two days after the first recorded native-born sea turtles emerged. These hatchlings originated from eggs laid on Huntington City Beach in September, which were subsequently moved to the Aquarium of the Pacific to safeguard them from high tides. This positive development follows the release of 54 hatchlings from a Seal Beach nest, collectively marking the first documented sea turtle nesting events on the U.S. West Coast. Scientists believe an extreme marine heat wave and a strong El Niño have created favorable water temperatures for these turtles to nest in the region.
