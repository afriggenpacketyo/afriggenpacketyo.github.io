Vietnam Secures Free Trade Agreement with European Free Trade Association

2026-10-18 05:19:00 EDT

Vietnam has successfully concluded a free trade agreement with the European Free Trade Association (EFTA), which includes Norway, Switzerland, Iceland, and Liechtenstein, announced during a ceremony in Hanoi. This significant deal, the culmination of talks that began in 2012, aims to substantially reduce or eliminate customs duties on a wide range of products, including industrial, fish, and agricultural goods. For example, over 98% of Norway's exports to Vietnam are projected to be tariff-free within seven years. The agreement is designed to bolster Vietnam's position as Southeast Asia's fastest-growing economy by diversifying its trade markets and enhancing its integration into global value chains.
