California's Proposition 2: Modifies 'rainy day' fund rules

2026-10-01 06:00:00 EDT

Proposition 2 proposes to modify California's state appropriations limit, allowing more flexibility in how budget surpluses are managed. The measure would exempt deposits into the "rainy day" fund and a temporary holding account from the spending limit and increase the cap on the rainy day fund from 10% to 20% of general fund revenue. Supporters argue this provides greater stability during economic downturns, while opponents suggest it could lead to unchecked government spending. The proposition aims to give lawmakers more options beyond taxpayer rebates or increased school funding during periods of surplus.
