East Germany's 1977 Coffee Crisis Led to Vietnam's Rise as a Major Exporter

2026-11-27 07:03:00 EST

This profound discontent prompted the government to seek a long-term solution, leading to significant investment in Vietnam's nascent coffee industry. East Germany provided machinery, infrastructure, and housing, paying with military equipment rather than hard currency. Although the coffee plants matured too late for East Germany, which ceased to exist, this initiative inadvertently transformed Vietnam into one of the world's largest coffee exporters today, a legacy largely unknown to many.
