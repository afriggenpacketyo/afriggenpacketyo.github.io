Japan's "Stigmatized Properties" Offer Bargains for Non-Superstitious Homebuyers

2026-10-28 20:00:15 EDT

Ryua, a young entertainer, secured a nearly 30% rent discount on her Tokyo apartment where a man committed suicide, humorously claiming she now cohabits with his ghost. Unlike many Western nations, Japanese estate agents are legally mandated to disclose deaths such as suicides, murders, or even lonely deaths where bodies were undiscovered for extended periods. This widespread aversion to "yuurei," or lingering troubled spirits, creates unique real estate opportunities for individuals who are not superstitious to acquire homes at significantly reduced prices.
