150-Year Overdue Book on Greek Antiquities Returned to Australian Library

2026-07-31 02:30:22 EDT

A book titled "Antiquities of Athens," published in 1858, has been returned to Kiama Library in Australia approximately 150 years overdue. Local resident Ross Simmons discovered the water-damaged book during home renovations, bricked inside a fireplace. The book, marked as No. 506 in the library's original 1872 collection, was likely borrowed by Simmons' great-great-grandfather. While the overdue fine would be around 28,000 Australian dollars, the library cannot identify the last borrower due to lost records. The unique return will now be displayed in the library's local history collection.
