2026-08-06 22:22:43 EDT

This marks only the second ice age amphibian found in the U.S., offering significant insights into past climates and ecological shifts. The discovery, made accidentally by a postdoctoral fellow, highlights how even small, fragile skeletons like amphibians can provide crucial environmental data due to their sensitivity to climate change.
