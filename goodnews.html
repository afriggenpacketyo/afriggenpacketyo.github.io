Understanding the Process of Stopping Wage Garnishment After Full Judgment Payment

2026-09-01 10:20:37 EDT

Paying a debt judgment in full should ultimately stop wage garnishment, as the creditor no longer has a balance to collect. However, the garnishment may not cease immediately, as administrative steps are required. The creditor must file a satisfaction of judgment with the court, and additional paperwork may be needed to formally terminate the garnishment and notify the employer. This process can cause a lag, potentially leading to another deduction if payroll has already been processed. Borrowers are advised to seek written confirmation and verify with both the court and their employer. (not investment advice)
