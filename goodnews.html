Cialis Ingredient Shows Potential for Muscle Regeneration in Early Research

2026-10-13 15:54:48 EDT

Researchers in Italy have found a potential link between tadalafil, the active ingredient in the erectile dysfunction drug Cialis, and muscle regeneration. A seven-day study on mouse muscle cells showed tadalafil increased muscle precursor cells and aided self-repair by promoting growth and reducing cell death. While promising, the research also indicated a delay in the maturation of fully developed muscle cells, suggesting it may create more cells for regeneration but not necessarily increase human muscle size or function. Experts emphasize that this laboratory cell study requires further animal and human trials before drawing clinical conclusions.
