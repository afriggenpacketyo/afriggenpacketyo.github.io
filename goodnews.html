UN General Assembly Adopts Resolution for New World Map Accurately Depicting Africa's Size

2026-09-04 20:53:11 EDT

The UN General Assembly voted to replace the traditional Mercator map with one that more accurately reflects Africa's true size, passing the "Correct the Map" resolution with 164 nations supporting it. The Mercator map, in use since the 16th century, distorts sizes, making countries near the poles appear larger and those near the equator, like Africa, seem smaller. Critics argue this minimizes the development needs of equatorial nations. The non-binding resolution encourages the use of equal-area projections, such as the Equal Earth map, for a fairer global representation.
