This technology uses small, license-free radio modules to transmit encrypted data packets over several kilometers, creating a self-repairing, decentralized communication network. Users can exchange short text messages that bypass firewalls and surveillance, as demonstrated in Ukraine. While offering resilience against censorship, the article notes limitations such as small data capacity and the risk of physical tracking of transmitters, along with legal consequences for unauthorized use in some regions.
