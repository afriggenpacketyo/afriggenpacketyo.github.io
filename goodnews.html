UN Advocates for New Map Projection to Correct Continental Size Distortions

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has called for replacing the widely used Mercator map with a more accurate projection, such as the Equal Earth map, to better represent continental sizes. The "Correct the Map" resolution aims to rectify the Mercator projection's distortion, which makes Africa appear similar in size to Greenland, despite being 14 times larger. While Mercator excelled for navigation by preserving compass bearings, it stretches landmasses near the poles. The new Equal Earth map, adopted by the African Union, accurately depicts landmass sizes and shapes but sacrifices the straight lines essential for navigation, highlighting the inherent challenges of mapping a sphere onto a flat surface.
