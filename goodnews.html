Washington D.C. and Denver to Vote on Banning Foie Gras Sales

2026-10-13 07:00:00 EDT

Voters in Washington D.C. and Denver will head to the polls in November to decide on ballot measures that could ban the sale of foie gras, a controversial delicacy produced by force-feeding ducks to significantly enlarge their livers. Animal welfare advocates argue the practice is "uniquely cruel," citing extensive research indicating severe tissue damage, impaired mobility, and other welfare compromises for the birds, despite claims from producers of humane methods. Foie gras producers and high-end chefs contend that the dish is unfairly singled out, arguing that other animal agriculture practices are arguably worse and impact a far greater number of animals, and that the ban targets a luxury product. Previous bans on foie gras sales are already in place in California and New York City, with Brazil also recently prohibiting its sale and production.
