Experts Question Efficacy of Popular "Cycle-Syncing" Workout Trend

2026-09-09 19:22:05 EDT

The online trend of "cycle-syncing" workouts, which suggests adjusting exercise routines based on menstrual cycle phases, has seen a surge in popularity on social media. However, researchers like Kelly McNulty from Leeds Beckett University caution that this advice, often shared by wellness creators, lacks scientific evidence. While some women experience cycle-related changes in energy or strength, McNulty emphasizes that individual menstrual cycles are highly variable, making blanket recommendations difficult to apply. Experts also warn against a "nocebo" effect from rigid plans and the potential for limiting strength gains by consistently easing off training. Instead, they advocate for "cycle-awareness," encouraging women to intuitively adapt workouts based on personal feelings rather than strict, phase-based rules.
