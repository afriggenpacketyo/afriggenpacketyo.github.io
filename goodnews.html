BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit

2026-11-21 22:21:07 EST

The BepiColombo mission, a collaborative effort between European and Japanese space agencies, successfully entered orbit around Mercury after an arduous eight-year cruise through the inner solar system. This marks only the third time a spacecraft has visited the sun's closest planet. The two linked robotic explorers are set to separate next month, allowing them to independently map and study Mercury's dense interior, exterior magnetic field, impact craters, and volcanic plains from different altitudes. This $2 billion mission aims to deliver unprecedented scientific data, comparing simultaneous measurements from both spacecraft, despite the immense technical challenges posed by Mercury's extreme temperatures.
