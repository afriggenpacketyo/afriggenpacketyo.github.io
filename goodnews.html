Oldest Pearl Harbor Survivor, Freeman Johnson, Dies at 106

2026-11-16 17:10:51 EST

Freeman Johnson, the oldest known survivor of the 1941 Pearl Harbor attack, passed away Saturday at the age of 106 in his Massachusetts home. As a fireman below deck on the USS St. Louis, Johnson never witnessed the Japanese fighters directly but experienced the attack through vibrations and machine gun fire. His ship, famously nicknamed "Lucky Lou," was the only major vessel to emerge from the assault unscathed. Johnson, who modestly stated he "just did what I was told," found his greatest pride in his marriage and family life rather than his wartime service. He retired at 90 after a long career and spent his later years living with his daughter.
