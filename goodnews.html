Compiled by Neo-Confucian scholar Zhang Huang, this 127-volume work synthesized over 200 sources to document concepts of heaven, Earth, and humanity. Information designer Paul Kahn highlights its ambition to gather all knowledge an educated person needed, contrasting it with Euro-Mediterranean perspectives of the era. The encyclopaedia's eventual printing after Huang's death underscores its historical significance as a vast repository of knowledge.
