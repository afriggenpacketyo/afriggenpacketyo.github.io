Perseid Meteor Shower Peaks This Week, Coinciding with Partial Solar Eclipse

2026-08-10 21:59:24 EDT

The highly anticipated annual Perseid meteor shower is set to reach its peak on Wednesday night, promising skywatchers a spectacular display of meteors. This year, the celestial event offers an additional treat, coinciding with a deep partial solar eclipse that will be visible in the UK in the early evening. Dr. Becky Smethurst, a Royal Astronomical Society Research Fellow, described this unique timing as "the perfect cosmic coincidence of events." The Perseids are caused by Earth passing through debris trails left by the Swift-Tuttle comet, and with a new moon providing dark skies, optimal viewing conditions are expected away from city lights. While a total eclipse would offer a rare chance to see meteors during the day, the partial eclipse still adds to an exceptional night of stargazing.
