California Introduces New License Plate Sequence After Decades

2026-09-11 18:59:37 EDT

California is implementing a new license plate sequence, marking the first change since the 1980s, as the state has exhausted all combinations of its previous format. The old sequence, consisting of one number, three letters, and three numbers (e.g., 1AAA000), ended with 9ZZZ999 this year. The new configuration will feature three numerals, followed by three letters, and then one numeral (e.g., 000AAA0). Although the new plates are already in production, they have not yet been issued to drivers, with over 110,000 plates of the old format still awaiting distribution. This new sequence is projected to last for approximately 40 years, with each configuration offering over 100 million combinations.
