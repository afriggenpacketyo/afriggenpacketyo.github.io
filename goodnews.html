Atlantic Hurricane Season Hits 60-Year Record Low for Hurricane Formation

2026-09-15 11:44:00 EDT

The Atlantic hurricane season of 2026 has set a 60-year record for the longest stretch without a hurricane, with none forming by September 15. This unusually quiet period aligns with forecasters' predictions for below-normal activity, primarily driven by a strong El Niño effect. El Niño typically causes warmer sea surface temperatures and reduced wind shear in the Pacific, but has the opposite effect in the Atlantic, creating conditions unfavorable for tropical system development. Historically, powerful El Niño events have consistently correlated with calmer Atlantic hurricane seasons, offering a scientific explanation for this notable lack of storm activity.

Spanish Scientists Uncover First Skeletal Evidence of Diplodocus in Europe

2026-09-15 10:42:00 EDT

Spanish scientists have made a groundbreaking discovery, unearthing the first skeletal evidence of the Diplodocus dinosaur outside of North America at the La Tejeria fossil site in eastern Spain. The team from the Dinopolis Foundation found various vertebrae and chevron bones, dating back approximately 150 million years, which are confidently identified as belonging to the long-necked herbivore. This finding challenges the previous understanding that Diplodocus remains were exclusive to North America. It strongly supports the theory that ephemeral land bridges or episodic sea regressions allowed for the exchange of continental faunas between North America and Europe during the Late Jurassic period, offering new insights into dinosaur migration.
