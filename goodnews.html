China Begins Construction on World's First Combined Supercritical CO2 and Molten Salt Energy Plant

2026-09-22 01:41:58 EDT

China has broken ground on the world’s first utility-scale combined supercritical carbon dioxide power generation and molten salt energy storage project in Shandong province. The Ruitan demonstration project aims to reduce energy waste and improve grid stability by using surplus electricity from existing coal units to heat molten salt during low demand. This stored heat will then power a supercritical unit that uses carbon dioxide, rather than steam, to generate electricity during peak demand. Expected to be operational next year, the first phase will feature a 50-megawatt supercritical unit and a 100MW molten salt energy storage system.
