Italian police divers have discovered a remarkably well-preserved ancient Roman shipwreck off the coast of Mazaro del Vallo in western Sicily, believed to be 2,100 years old. The significant underwater archaeological site, found at a depth of 46 meters, contains hundreds of amphorae, likely used to transport wine and other goods. Italian Culture Minister Alessandro Giuli hailed it as one of the most important underwater discoveries in recent years. The finding, prompted by a tip-off from local fishermen, offers extraordinary insights into Mediterranean trade routes from the 2nd to 1st centuries BC, revealing a piece of history resting silently on the seafloor.
