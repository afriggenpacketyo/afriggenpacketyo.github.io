China Initiates Construction of World's First Supercritical CO2 and Molten Salt Energy Plant

2026-09-22 01:41:58 EDT

China has broken ground on the Ruitan demonstration project, the world's first utility-scale combined supercritical carbon dioxide power generation and molten salt energy storage plant. Located in Shandong province, this innovative facility aims to reduce energy waste and enhance grid stability by utilizing surplus electricity to heat molten salt during low demand. The stored heat will then power a supercritical unit that uses carbon dioxide to generate electricity during peak hours. Expected to begin operation next year, the initial phase will feature a 50-megawatt unit and a 100MW molten salt energy storage system.
