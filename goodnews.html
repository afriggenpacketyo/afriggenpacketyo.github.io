New Jersey Enacts Law Banning Surveillance Pricing to Protect Consumers

2026-07-26 11:08:40 EDT

New Jersey Governor Mikie Sherrill has signed the Fair Price Protection Act, establishing her state as the third in the U.S. to ban surveillance pricing. This landmark legislation prohibits businesses from utilizing shoppers' personal data, such as online activity, location, or purchasing history, to charge different prices for identical products. Governor Sherrill stated the law aims to protect families from arbitrary price manipulation and ensure fair competition among businesses. Additionally, the act imposes a one-year moratorium on the rollout of new electronic shelf labels, allowing for a study of their impact, a measure praised by labor unions.
