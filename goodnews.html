2026-11-16 08:00:00 EST

A second group of 21 native-born olive ridley sea turtle hatchlings was successfully released in Huntington Beach, California, following a first wave released days earlier. These turtles emerged from eggs laid in September on Huntington City Beach, which were relocated to the Aquarium of the Pacific to protect them from high tides. This marks the first known sea turtle nesting on the U.S. West Coast, with previous northern nests typically 700 miles south. An extreme marine heat wave and strong El Niño are believed to have pushed water temperatures conducive to nesting in Southern California.
