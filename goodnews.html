Rule Change Revitalizes Reverse Swing in One-Day International Cricket, Impacting Play

2026-10-19 01:27:56 EDT

A significant rule change in One-Day International (ODI) cricket, implemented in July 2025, has revitalized reverse swing, profoundly impacting the dynamics of the game. The alteration permits captains to select which of the two older white balls to use after 34 overs, allowing the chosen ball to become scruffier and achieve greater movement in the air, benefiting pace bowlers. Statistics reveal a notable increase in swing for pacers during the crucial final 10 overs, resulting in demonstrably lower economy rates and improved bowling averages. While England has seen particular advantages, this shift is affecting teams globally, necessitating tactical adjustments and potentially altering scoring patterns during the critical death overs of an innings.
