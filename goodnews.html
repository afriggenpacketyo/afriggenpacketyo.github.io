Scott Colom Becomes Mississippi's Third Black U.S. Senator, Following Post-Civil War Predecessors

2026-11-12 09:12:55 EST

Scott Colom's recent historic victory in Mississippi's Senate race positions him as only the third Black U.S. Senator from the state, marking a significant milestone over 150 years since the last Black senator served. He follows in the footsteps of Hiram Revels, who served from 1870 to 1871 as the first Black person in the chamber, and Blanche Bruce, who completed a full term from 1875 to 1881. Both Revels and Bruce were pivotal figures during the Reconstruction era, advocating for racial equality before the oppressive Jim Crow laws enforced segregation across the South. Colom's election not only signifies a return to Black representation but also resonates deeply with the state's complex historical narrative of civil rights and political progress.

Understanding Pet Insurance: Deductibles, Payouts, and Waiting Periods Explained

2026-11-12 08:35:07 EST

Pet insurance functions similarly to human health insurance, involving premiums, deductibles, reimbursement rates, and waiting periods that significantly influence coverage and out-of-pocket expenses. Policies typically feature annual or per-incident deductibles, with lower deductibles generally correlating with higher premiums, and reimburse a specified percentage of veterinary costs after the deductible is met. Crucially, most policies impose waiting periods before coverage commences, and commonly exclude pre-existing conditions, routine preventative care like vaccinations, and breed-specific hereditary issues. Understanding these intricate details, including potential age limits and the claims process, is paramount for pet owners to select appropriate coverage, manage their financial responsibilities, and ensure their pets receive necessary care without unexpected financial strain.
