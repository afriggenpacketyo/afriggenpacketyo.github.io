Finland Deploys World's Largest Sand Battery for Renewable Heat Storage

2026-07-27 04:46:51 EDT

A small Finnish town is utilizing the world's largest commercial sand battery to address renewable energy's intermittency challenge, storing clean energy as heat. This 13-meter-tall facility uses 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone to provide 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy, significantly reducing carbon emissions and wood chip use for heating. Developed by Polar Night Energy, the technology operates like a giant thermos, heating sand with cheap grid electricity and discharging it via heat exchangers for local district heating. This innovative solution offers long-duration energy storage without rare earth materials.
