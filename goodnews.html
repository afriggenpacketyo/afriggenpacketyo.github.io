Greece Enacts Social Media Ban for Minors Under 15, Effective 2027

2026-10-23 05:04:00 EDT

Greece's parliament has passed a bill banning the use of online social media services by minors under 15, set to take effect on January 1, 2027. The measure, primarily supported by the New Democracy party, aims to protect children's mental health from the negative impacts of uncontrolled internet use, such as addiction, diminished attention spans, and reduced outdoor activity. Minister Dimitris Papastergiou defended the "necessary, effective, proportionate" step, stating that social media platforms would have until the new year to implement age verification. Greece thus joins a growing number of countries, including Australia, in introducing such age limits, emphasizing a governmental duty to safeguard childhood.
