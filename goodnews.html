Lock of George Washington's hair auctioned for $4,000

2026-11-21 14:12:45 EST

A lock of hair belonging to George Washington, America's first president, was sold at auction for $4,000. The artifact was previously owned by collector John K. Lattimer, a descendant of John Gadsby, who hosted Washington for his final birthday celebrations. Historical accounts suggest Gadsby obtained the hair through his association with Washington in his final days. The lock of hair is preserved in a framed, gold-gilded wood frame. This sale adds to a history of various Washington artifacts being sold at auction for significant sums.
