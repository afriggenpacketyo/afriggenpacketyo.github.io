2026-10-14 21:30:12 EDT

Researchers estimate it was 10-13 feet long, feathered, and would have preyed on smaller dinosaurs, lizards, and mammals, posing a greater threat to humans than a T. rex if they had coexisted. The discovery, made by scanning a skull fragment, sheds light on the Early Cretaceous period and honors those facing adversity with its name, meaning "unconquered female warrior."
