Scott Colom Becomes Mississippi's First Black U.S. Senator in Over 150 Years

2026-11-12 09:12:55 EST

Democrat Scott Colom has made history by winning a close race in Mississippi, becoming the state's first Black U.S. Senator in over 150 years and only the third in its history. This landmark victory in a deeply conservative state marks the first time Mississippi has sent a Democrat to the U.S. Senate since 1982, and the first Black senator since 1875. Colom follows Hiram Revels, who served from 1870-1871, and Blanche Bruce, whose term ran from 1875-1881, both of whom served during the Reconstruction era before the implementation of repressive Jim Crow laws. This outcome signifies a historic shift in the state's political landscape.
