Understanding the Necessity of a Power of Attorney Alongside a Will

2026-11-24 14:02:20 EST

This article elucidates the critical distinction between a will and a power of attorney (POA), advocating for the necessity of both documents for thorough estate planning. While a will primarily governs the distribution of assets post-mortem, a POA empowers a designated agent to manage financial matters during one's lifetime, particularly if incapacitation occurs. The absence of a POA can force families into lengthy and costly court proceedings to gain authority over financial decisions. A durable POA is highlighted as essential, as it ensures continuity in managing bills, investments, and other financial obligations even if the grantor becomes unable to act for themselves.

Mission control briefly lost contact, and the spacecraft subsequently entered safe mode after executing only 77% of its planned thruster burn. Engineers identified the issue as a software problem related to attitude guidance and thruster control, but confirmed BepiColombo's successful orbital capture after six hours of meticulous trajectory analysis. The spacecraft will now undertake several weeks of small thruster burns to fine-tune its orbit before deploying two dedicated science orbiters in December, which are slated to commence scientific observations of Mercury's interior, surface, and exosphere in April 2027.
