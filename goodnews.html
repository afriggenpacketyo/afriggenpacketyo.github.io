EU Court Rules CBD Foods Cannot Be Labeled Organic Without Prior Market Authorization

2026-11-05 09:51:00 EST

The European Union's top court has ruled that foods containing cannabidiol (CBD) cannot receive organic certification unless they have first obtained authorization for sale within the bloc. The court determined that an organic label could mislead consumers into believing CBD foods are already approved for market. CBD is classified as a "novel food" in the EU, requiring a safety assessment and authorization before legal marketing, which it has not yet received. This ruling specifically impacts CBD-based or enriched foods, not all cannabis-derived products like hemp seeds or oil, which can still be certified organic if they meet standards.
