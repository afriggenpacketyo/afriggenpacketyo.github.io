Port of Los Angeles Achieves Record-Breaking Cargo Volume in September

2026-10-12 17:21:11 EDT

The Port of Los Angeles experienced its busiest month ever in September, moving over 1 million 20-foot-equivalent container units and surpassing its previous record. This achievement concludes the port's most active quarter in history, driven by lower tariffs and increased demand ahead of the holiday season. Executive Director Gene Seroka noted that major retailers are rapidly replenishing inventories, while security and environmental challenges in the Suez and Panama canals are diverting more shipments to Southern California. The port, a vital economic engine, also set a record for the highest volume moved through any port in the Western Hemisphere, despite weaker outbound export volumes.

Destination Trail, the event host, confirmed all runners were safely accounted for and transported back to headquarters or awaiting pickup.
