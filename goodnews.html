Germany Conducts First Raids Against English Channel Migrant Smuggling Under New Law

2026-08-18 01:02:58 EDT

Germany has conducted its first raids under a new law specifically targeting gangs using the country as a staging post for small boat crossings on the English Channel. A joint operation involving UK, French, and German authorities in North Rhine-Westphalia seized over 9,000 life jackets, dozens of dinghies, and 360 pumps from a secret warehouse. This equipment could have transported more than 2,000 migrants illegally, according to the UK's National Crime Agency. The new legislation, passed last December, makes facilitating migrant smuggling to the UK a specific criminal offense, closing a legal loophole that existed post-Brexit. British investigators believe Kurdish criminal networks are largely responsible for controlling this illicit business in western Germany.
