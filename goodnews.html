New Ice Age Amphibian Discovered in La Brea Tar Pits Collection

2026-08-06 22:22:43 EDT

Researchers at the La Brea Tar Pits and Museum in Los Angeles have announced the discovery of a new extinct ice age amphibian, the spadefoot toad (Spea labreae). This marks one of only two ice age amphibians found in the U.S., significantly enhancing understanding of past climates. The discovery, made by Dr. Alberto Cruz while reviewing rarely studied collections, highlights how amphibians, sensitive to climate change, offer a window into ancient environments. Despite their fragile skeletons making fossilization rare, the Tar Pits' unique conditions preserved this specimen. The find underscores the ongoing scientific importance of existing museum collections, even those unexamined for decades, and suggests more discoveries are anticipated during the museum's renovation.
