Mahatma Gandhi's Handwritten Notes Achieve Record Price at Mumbai Auction

2026-08-22 04:43:00 EDT

A collection of Mahatma Gandhi's handwritten notes has sold for a record $1.7 million at a Mumbai auction, establishing it as the highest price ever paid for an Indian historical document. The 688 notes, penned by Gandhi to his close associate Anand Hingorani between 1944 and 1946, offer profound personal insights into his thoughts on truth, God, and non-violence. This significant collection, which was meticulously preserved by Hingorani's family, also included Gandhi's portable spinning wheel, manuscripts, and other personal items. The auction underscores the enduring global interest in Gandhi's philosophical principles and his pivotal non-violent struggle for India's independence from British rule.
