Maine Enacts Law Limiting Police Cooperation with Federal Immigration Agencies

2026-07-28 21:30:53 EDT

A new Maine law, effective this week, significantly limits state and local law enforcement cooperation with federal immigration agencies. The "ICE Out of Policing" act prohibits police from assisting with civil immigration enforcement, including contacting Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) for translation or sharing personal data, though criminal investigation exceptions exist. Further legislation restricts immigration officers' access to public schools, libraries, healthcare facilities, and child-care facilities without judicial warrants, and prevents landlords from disclosing tenant immigration status without consent. Immigrant rights advocates champion these reforms, asserting they will enhance community trust in local officials and ensure public safety for all residents, irrespective of their immigration standing.
