While living kidney donations have seen revolutionary advancements through infrastructure like the National Kidney Registry, liver donation currently lacks a comparable national matching system, standardized protocols, and adequate surgical training across centers. Key barriers include the resource-intensive nature of these complex surgeries, the limited cold ischemia time for organ transport, and the financial and logistical burdens placed on living donors. Experts are advocating for universal donor protections, expanded surgical training, and the establishment of a national registry, alongside leveraging new organ preservation technologies, to scale up this critical medical procedure and ultimately save countless more lives.
