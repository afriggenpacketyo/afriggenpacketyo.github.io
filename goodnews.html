Severe COVID-19 Linked to Reactivation of Dormant Viruses, Study Suggests

2026-08-10 19:17:48 EDT

A new study suggests that severe COVID-19 infections may trigger the reactivation of certain dormant viruses that persist within the human body after prior infections. Researchers from Boston Children's Hospital and collaborators monitored 1,154 hospitalized COVID-19 patients, identifying 11 different dormant viruses, including various herpes viruses and Anelloviridae, that reactivated within 40 days of hospital admission. Notably, the reactivation of Anelloviridae, a virus carried by most adults, was linked to long-term physical disability and the development of long COVID. These significant findings suggest a previously unrecognized mechanism of viral reactivation during severe illness, potentially paving the way for better understanding and future treatments for long COVID.
