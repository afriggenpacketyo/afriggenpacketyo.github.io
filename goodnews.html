2026-08-10 21:59:24 EDT

This year, the event uniquely coincides with a deep partial solar eclipse visible in the UK, creating an exceptional stargazing opportunity. The Perseids originate from debris left by the Swift-Tuttle comet, burning up as they enter Earth's atmosphere.
