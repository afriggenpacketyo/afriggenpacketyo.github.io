Recommendations include a less burdensome "fast track" process for individuals with the most severe conditions and end-of-life care, aiming to free up assessment capacity for other cases. The goal is to create a more personalized, flexible system that actively supports disabled people to participate fully in everyday life and work, rather than posing a barrier.
