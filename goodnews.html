Prime Minister Burnham Reverses Plans to Restrict Jury Trials in England and Wales

2026-10-03 23:07:30 EDT

Prime Minister Andy Burnham has announced the scrapping of controversial plans to curb some jury trials in England and Wales, a move initially proposed to address severe court backlogs. The original proposals would have allowed judges to decide cases with sentences under three years without a jury. This significant reversal, widely welcomed by criminal lawyers and Conservative critics, follows strong opposition to eroding the 800-year-old right to trial by jury. While the decision is seen as a victory for fundamental justice principles, concerns persist regarding the government's alternative plan to shift more medium-level cases to magistrates, potentially overwhelming an already strained volunteer-based system.
