Understanding Anhedonia: The 'Silent Sister' of Depression Often Overlooked

2026-10-29 16:00:00 EDT

Psychiatrist Dr. Judith Joseph highlights anhedonia, the reduced ability to feel joy or interest, as the "silent sister" of depression, often missed in high-functioning individuals. Her research indicates a link between high-functioning depression, anhedonia, and trauma, particularly in parents and caregivers. Since anhedonia isn't an official diagnosis, many don't seek help until burnout. Joseph advocates for greater awareness and provides a self-assessment quiz, alongside a "Five V's" framework—Validation, Venting, Values, Vision, Vitals—to help individuals reclaim joy and address this existential crisis before it escalates.
