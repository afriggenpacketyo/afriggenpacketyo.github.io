First Diplodocus Skeletal Evidence Discovered in Europe by Spanish Scientists

2026-09-15 10:42:00 EDT

Researchers in Spain have uncovered the first skeletal evidence of the Diplodocus dinosaur outside of North America, finding various vertebrae and chevron bones dating back approximately 150 million years. This significant discovery at the La Tejeria fossil site challenges previous understandings of the long-necked herbivore's geographic range, which was previously confined to North America. The findings, published in the Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology, strongly support the theory of ancient land bridges or episodic sea regressions that facilitated continental faunal exchanges between North America and Europe during the Late Jurassic period, connecting areas like Iberia to what is now Canada.
