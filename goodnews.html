BepiColombo Spacecraft Successfully Enters Mercury's Orbit Despite Glitch

2026-11-24 10:39:22 EST

The BepiColombo spacecraft, a joint mission by the European Space Agency and Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, successfully entered Mercury's orbit after an eight-year journey, despite encountering a technical glitch. A scheduled thruster burn was interrupted, causing the spacecraft to enter safe mode and complete only 77% of the maneuver. However, ESA scientists confirmed that BepiColombo had still locked onto Mercury's orbit after six hours of analysis. The spacecraft will now perform further thruster burns to adjust its orbit and is expected to release two science orbiters in December to begin scientific operations by April 2027, studying the planet's interior, surface, and surrounding space.
