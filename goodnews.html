A second group of 21 baby olive ridley sea turtles has successfully hatched and been released into the waters of Huntington Beach, closely following an earlier release of 54 hatchlings. These events mark the first recorded sea turtle nests on the U.S. West Coast, with the initial eggs laid in September on Huntington City Beach and Seal Beach. To ensure their survival, the eggs were meticulously transferred to the Aquarium of the Pacific to shield them from high tides. The presence of these turtles, typically found nesting in tropical regions far to the south, is linked to an extreme marine heat wave and a potent El Niño phenomenon elevating Southern California's water temperatures.
