SpaceX Rocket Booster Creates 60-Foot Crater on Moon, NASA Images Reveal

2026-08-18 18:53:46 EDT

NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter has captured detailed images of a new 60-foot-wide, 10-foot-deep crater on the moon, formed by the impact of an 8,800-pound SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket booster. This booster, which had drifted in space for over a year after launching private moon landers, crashed at 5,400 miles per hour on August 5, with South Korea's Danuri spacecraft first beaming back photos. The unprecedented views, documented by the spacecraft a week after the collision, reveal dark and bright streaks of excavated material, offering fresh insights into the moon's surface composition and the effects of solar wind exposure. This unique artificial impact provides valuable data for understanding lunar geology and supporting future human missions, building on previous intentional impacts to search for water ice and aid in commercial lunar exploration.
