Community Shield Victory Rarely Predicts Premier League Success, Statistics Show

2026-08-16 01:29:55 EDT

The Community Shield match between English champions Arsenal and FA Cup winners Manchester City often serves as a curtain-raiser, yet historical data suggests winning this trophy rarely correlates with subsequent Premier League success. Since the Premier League's inception in 1992-93, only eight of 34 Community Shield winners have gone on to claim the league title, with Manchester City in 2019 being the most recent example. Interestingly, teams that lost the Community Shield have won the Premier League more often, with 10 titles. This article highlights that nearly half of Premier League champions were not even involved in the Community Shield, indicating its limited predictive power for the season ahead.
