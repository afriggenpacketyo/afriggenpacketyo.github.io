2026-11-26 09:03:15 EST

The UK's net migration fell to 77,000 in the year to June, marking the lowest level since 1997, excluding the pandemic years, according to the Office for National Statistics. This substantial decrease represents a 92% reduction from the record high of 944,000 recorded in the year to March 2023. The significant drop is primarily attributed to the tightening of visa rules, which impacted arrivals for work, study, and asylum claims, policies initiated by the previous Conservative government and continued by the current Labour administration. Both major political parties have sought to claim success for this reduction, with the Home Secretary emphasizing the government's achievement in "restoring control."
