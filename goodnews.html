BepiColombo Spacecraft Nears Mercury After Eight-Year, Six-Billion-Mile Interplanetary Journey

2026-11-20 00:59:56 EST

The BepiColombo spacecraft is nearing its final destination, Mercury, after an arduous eight-year journey spanning almost six billion miles through the inner Solar System. This joint European and Japanese mission aims to unravel Mercury's major mysteries, including the puzzling presence of ice in its polar craters despite its proximity to the Sun, and the strange hollows dotting its surface. The spacecraft has endured extreme temperatures and intense solar radiation, performing complex gravitational assists to slow down enough to be captured into orbit. Scientists hope to gain unprecedented insights into the planet's formation, evolution, and its unusually large metal core, potentially shedding light on its violent early history.
