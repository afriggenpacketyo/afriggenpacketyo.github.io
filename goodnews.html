2026-10-05 03:34:47 EDT

Chinese doctors have achieved a "new milestone" by successfully shrinking a fast-growing lung tumor in an unborn baby while still in the mother's womb, using a precise needle-based procedure. This unprecedented surgery, performed by a medical team from Shenzhen and the Chinese University of Hong Kong, offers new hope for families facing similar dire prognoses. The technology employed vibrates water molecules to generate intense heat, effectively destroying tumor tissue from the inside out.
