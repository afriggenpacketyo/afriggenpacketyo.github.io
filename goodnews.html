Projected 3.2% Inflation Adjustment Could Alter 2027 Income Tax Brackets

2026-09-11 15:28:32 EDT

Federal income tax brackets are projected to increase by 3.2% for the 2027 tax year due to inflation adjustments, according to estimates from Bloomberg Tax. This adjustment aims to prevent 'bracket creep,' where individuals are pushed into higher tax brackets due to inflation without a corresponding increase in their real income. The IRS typically announces these inflation-adjusted brackets in the fall. The projected increase follows a higher inflation rate in 2026, partly influenced by the Iran war, which has impacted fuel prices. These adjustments are crucial for taxpayers to maintain their purchasing power and avoid an effective tax hike.
