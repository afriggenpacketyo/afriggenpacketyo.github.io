Robert Hall and Michael Woodford Win Nobel Prize in Economics for Work on Macroeconomic Stabilization

2026-10-13 06:30:00 EDT

Robert Hall and Michael Woodford have been awarded the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for their work on "microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilization," particularly their contributions to New Keynesian economics. Their research explains how "sticky prices"—prices that don't adjust quickly to economic changes—can lead to prolonged slumps, unemployment, and the need for government intervention. This framework revitalized Keynesian ideas by incorporating individual decision-making, providing a theoretical basis for policies like the Federal Reserve's "forward guidance" during recessions. Their work helps policymakers understand how to combat inflation and unemployment more effectively. (not investment advice)
