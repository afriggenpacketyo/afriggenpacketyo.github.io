Renowned Assemblage Artist Betye Saar Dies at 99

2026-07-27 17:30:17 EDT

Betye Saar, the influential Los Angeles assemblage artist, has died at the age of 99, just days before her 100th birthday. Saar was celebrated for transforming bigoted bric-à-brac, such as Sambo dolls and mammy figurines, into powerful works that explored racial history in the United States. Her iconic 1972 piece, "The Liberation of Aunt Jemima," which depicted a mammy figure armed with a rifle and grenade, became a central work in the Black Arts Movement and influenced generations of artists. Her legacy is noted for recycling objects to convey new meaning and empowering symbols.
