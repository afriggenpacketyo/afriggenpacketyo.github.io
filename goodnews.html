Despite common misconceptions, these inch-long beetles possess functional eyes, but their considerable size combined with relatively small wings makes controlling their flight trajectory quite challenging. August marks the peak season for these insects, whose thriving population is attributed to human-driven factors such as compost piles, irrigated landscapes, and abundant fruit trees. Climate change is also believed to be expanding their hospitable habitats. While they primarily consume decaying fruit and contribute to ecosystem decomposition, residents are generally advised to gently guide them outdoors if they enter homes, as they are not considered pests.
