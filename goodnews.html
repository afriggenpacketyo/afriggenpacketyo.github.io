Tropical Storm Dolly Forms in Atlantic, Expected to Weaken Before Reaching Leeward Islands

2026-08-27 11:25:49 EDT

Tropical Storm Dolly has officially formed in the Atlantic Ocean, marking the fourth named storm of the 2026 Atlantic hurricane season. The National Hurricane Center reported Dolly was still far out at sea with maximum sustained winds of 40 mph, just above tropical storm minimum strength. While it is traveling rapidly towards the Leeward Islands, forecasters anticipate it will weaken into a tropical wave before making landfall, likely by Saturday. Despite the expected downgrade, the system could still bring significant heavy rain and potential flash flooding to parts of the Leeward Islands, Virgin Islands, Puerto Rico, and Hispaniola through Monday.
