This incredibly rare encounter, one of only about 70 ever observed globally, occurred unexpectedly during a mission primarily focused on studying underwater mountains and whale activity. The mysterious creature, characterized by its long pale "legs" and languidly undulating fins, "mesmerized" the scientists, underscoring the vast unknown depths of the ocean. The team gathered samples and data to further understand deep-sea biodiversity and whale behavior, with this serendipitous discovery highlighting the continuous thrill of scientific exploration in marine environments.
