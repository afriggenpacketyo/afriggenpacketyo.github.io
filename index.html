Coral Research Suggests Intensification of El Niño Events Due to Climate Change

2026-09-12 04:53:00 EDT

A recent study by US researchers, published in Science, suggests that El Niño events have become significantly stronger compared to the pre-industrial period. Scientists examined fossilized and living corals around the Galapagos Islands, using their growth rings and chemical composition to reconstruct ocean temperatures over the past 1,000 years. This analysis helps close a data gap, indicating that recent strong El Niños are unusual and potentially linked to a warming world.
