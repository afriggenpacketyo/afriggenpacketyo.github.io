AI startup Character.ai files for bankruptcy after a 'reverse acquihire' by Google

2026-10-16 07:00:00 EDT

AI startup Character.ai, known for its AI-powered companion chatbots, has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. The company experienced rapid growth and high user engagement after its launch, but faced challenges in monetization and legal issues, including litigation over alleged harm to minors and copyright concerns. Google previously acquired the founders and key talent through a "reverse acquihire" in 2024, obtaining a nonexclusive license to the technology without acquiring the entire company. This left the remaining entity in a precarious financial position, struggling to raise funds and change its business model. The situation highlights the risks faced by AI startups, even those with significant user bases and promising technology, and the complexities of "acquihire" deals in the tech industry.
