The Bears' defense played a pivotal role, effectively neutralizing Jalen Hurts and the Eagles' offense by forcing three turnovers. Keenum expressed profound emotion and gratitude for the opportunity, particularly with his children present, making this comeback a special highlight in his career with his eighth NFL franchise.
