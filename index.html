Orionid Meteor Shower Active Until November 7th, Peaks October 21-22

2026-10-02 03:16:45 EDT

The Orionid meteor shower, one of the best-known celestial displays, is currently active until November 7th, with its peak occurring overnight from October 21 to 22. These particularly fast meteors, entering the atmosphere at around 41 miles per second, originate from dust and debris left by Halley's Comet, which was last seen in 1986 and will reappear in 2061. Earth encounters this comet's material twice annually, also causing the Eta Aquariid shower in May. Stargazers are advised to find a dark location away from city lights, allow eyes to adjust, and observe with the naked eye for the best chance to see 10-20 meteors per hour.
