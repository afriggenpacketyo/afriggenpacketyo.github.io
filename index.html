His remarks followed the release of the producer price index (PPI), which showed a 6.5% annual increase, the highest since November 2022, and came after a higher-than-expected consumer price index (CPI) report. Goolsbee indicated that inflation is proving more persistent than anticipated and is potentially spreading beyond its initial drivers, such as supply shocks and AI-driven demand. While not speculating on specific actions, he suggested that current trends are not on a path back to the Fed's 2% inflation target, implying a likelihood of further monetary tightening.
