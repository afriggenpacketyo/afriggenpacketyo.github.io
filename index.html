Experts Confirm 'Hang-xiety' is a Real Phenomenon Linking Hangovers and Anxiety

2026-10-06 19:31:52 EDT

"Hang-xiety," the anxiety that can accompany a hangover, is a recognized and real phenomenon, explained by alcohol's profound effects on brain chemistry, sleep, hormones, and the nervous system. Alcohol initially enhances GABA, the brain's braking system, and suppresses glutamate, involved in excitation and alertness, leading to feelings of relaxation. However, as alcohol wears off, the brain attempts to restore balance, often swinging towards greater alertness and arousal, which can trigger significant anxiety. Studies indicate that not everyone experiences "hang-xiety," and its severity doesn't always correlate with the amount consumed, with existing psychological characteristics like shyness playing a role. Experts advise recognizing it as a temporary state to blunt its power.
