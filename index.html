2026-11-14 08:48:37 EST

A comprehensive review of youth unemployment in the UK suggests a significant shift in government spending, moving away from benefits towards employment support. FAA Lifts Nationwide Ground Stop After System Outage Caused Widespread Flight Disruptions

2026-11-14 08:47:41 EST

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) has lifted a nationwide ground stop that severely disrupted thousands of flights across the United States, attributing the issue to a "telecommunication outage" within its air traffic control system. The outage, believed to be caused by overloaded routers, led to over 10,000 delays and more than 4,000 cancellations on Friday alone. While officials stated there was no indication of a "nefarious act," the incident has renewed calls for emergency funding to modernize the aging air traffic control infrastructure. The FAA emphasized thorough system testing before resuming operations, but residual cancellations and delays are expected to continue as the system unwinds. The disruption highlighted the vulnerability of the current system and the urgent need for upgrades.
