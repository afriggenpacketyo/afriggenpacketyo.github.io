Understanding the 2026 Autumn Equinox and its Astronomical Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The 2026 autumn equinox will occur in the early hours of Wednesday, September 23, marking the precise moment when the Sun crosses the celestial equator. At this time, neither the Northern nor Southern Hemisphere is tilted towards or away from the Sun, resulting in roughly equal day and night lengths globally. While the equinox itself is an astronomical event, it holds cultural and spiritual significance for some, with gatherings at sites like Stonehenge to mark the occasion. Due to light refraction, where light is bent by the atmosphere, the actual "equilux," or equal light, occurs a few days after the equinox, varying by latitude across the UK.
