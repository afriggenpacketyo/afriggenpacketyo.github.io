Iran retaliated with ballistic missile strikes on U.S. forces in Jordan and the United Arab Emirates, accusing those bases of supporting the U.S. attack on Larak Island. While the UAE denied missile hits, it reported intercepting Iranian drones. This escalation comes amidst weeks of threats and new U.S. economic sanctions, reigniting concerns over regional conflict and the critical Strait of Hormuz shipping lanes.
