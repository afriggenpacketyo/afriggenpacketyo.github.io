This opinion article advocates for Britain to contemplate a "Hong Kong-style handover" of the Falkland Islands, known to Argentinians as the Islas Malvinas, to Argentina. The author posits that Argentina's claim is built upon robust historical and legal underpinnings, citing its inheritance of sovereignty from Spain in 1816 and its subsequent exercise of governmental authority over the archipelago prior to Britain's forceful expulsion of Argentine authorities in 1833. This act, which Argentina has consistently denounced as an illegal usurpation, forms a core part of its enduring protest. The piece underscores the geographical disparity—the islands' proximity to Argentina versus their vast distance from Britain—characterizing them as a lingering vestige of empire. It explicitly dismisses the principle of self-determination for the current islanders, instead framing the conflict as a fundamental clash between territorial integrity and colonial occupation, noting that the United Nations' call for a negotiated settlement remains unaddressed.

Alan Carr Receives Emotional Special Recognition Award at National Television Awards

2026-09-08 21:28:19 EDT

Comedian Alan Carr experienced a deeply emotional evening at the 31st National Television Awards, where he was honored with the Special Recognition prize, a unique award not determined by public vote. The accolade followed a celebratory "Traitors-themed musical performance" dedicated to him, capping a year where his participation in "The Celebrity Traitors" brought him unprecedented popularity. Carr, who had humorously downplayed his chances of winning, was visibly moved and held back tears during his acceptance speech. Beyond his personal recognition, "The Celebrity Traitors" also secured the Reality Competition prize, a testament to its widespread appeal. The ceremony also saw Sir David Attenborough named Best Presenter in his 100th birthday year, and "Our Girls: The Southport Families," a BBC program detailing the tragic murders of three children at a dance class in 2024, won in its category, with the victims' mother accepting the award.
