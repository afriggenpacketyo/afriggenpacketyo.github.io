EU Proposes New Digital Fairness Act to Protect Consumers from Online Deception

2026-11-12 12:19:00 EST

The European Commission has put forth a new Digital Fairness Act (DFA) aimed at curbing manipulative online practices and empowering consumers. Key provisions include requiring companies to remind users before free trials end and obtain explicit consent before charging for subscriptions. The proposed law also seeks to simplify online subscription cancellations and mandate reminders before automatic contract renewals. Additionally, it targets "dark patterns," such as deceptive scarcity tactics used in hotel bookings or dynamic pricing in online queues, and aims to make apps and websites less addictive by allowing users to opt out of features like autoplay videos. While some experts believe opt-out options don't go far enough, the legislation seeks to balance consumer protection with industry innovation. Companies found in violation could face fines up to six percent of their global annual revenue.
