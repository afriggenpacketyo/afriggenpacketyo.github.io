Gen Z Driving U.S. Entrepreneurship Boom, Fueled by AI and Economic Shifts

2026-08-06 17:10:51 EDT

Entrepreneurship is experiencing a significant boom across the U.S., with Generation Z members emerging as a leading force in this surge, as reported by the Bank of America Institute. New business applications filed by Gen Zers saw a remarkable 66% increase in June compared to the previous year, significantly outperforming other demographic groups. Experts largely attribute this burgeoning trend to the advent of artificial intelligence, which effectively lowers barriers to entry by automating fundamental tasks and substantially reducing startup costs, thereby democratizing entrepreneurship. Furthermore, a challenging economic landscape and notable job market dislocation are increasingly motivating younger Americans to forge their own career paths and create new opportunities.
