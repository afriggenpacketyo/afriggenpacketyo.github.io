Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass has halted a plan to implement protected bike lanes and other safety measures on Forest Lawn Drive, a key commuter route, due to strong community opposition. The original plan, which would have removed traffic lanes, was criticized by local cemeteries, neighborhood councils, and clergy for potentially causing severe traffic congestion, particularly during funeral services. City Councilmember Nithya Raman, a mayoral challenger, is now criticizing Bass's decision, especially in the wake of a recent fatal crash on the dangerous road, arguing that the safety improvements are urgently needed. The mayor's office is currently developing alternative designs that aim to improve safety for all travelers without eliminating existing traffic lanes.

The Rise and Fall of a Key Pilot for El Chapo's Sons, Now a Potential Cartel Witness

2026-08-21 06:00:00 EDT

Mauro Alberto Núñez Ojeda, known as "Jando," rose from a low-level drug mule to become the commander of El Chapo's sons' air force, flying high-ranking cartel members and weapons across Latin America. His mysterious identity allowed him to evade capture for years, even after delivering Joaquín Guzmán López and Ismael "El Mayo" Zambada to U.S. authorities in a dramatic kidnapping that led to their arrests. Jando was later captured in Mexico after a shootout and quietly transferred to U.S. custody. Now, having pleaded guilty to drug conspiracy, he is presumed to be a key witness against other major cartel leaders, potentially reversing his allegiance and providing critical information to authorities.
