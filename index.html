2026-10-20 14:48:00 EDT

Cornell University has refuted the account of Jane Doe, a former student who alleged she felt "unsupported and disbelieved" by University President Michael Kotlikoff after reporting a sexual assault. Doe's letter to the Faculty Senate claimed Kotlikoff questioned her delay in reporting the incident, which she interpreted as doubt. In a statement, Cornell asserted that Doe's description of the president's comment is "incorrect" and based on a "documented first-hand account." The university clarified that Kotlikoff's question was directed at bystanders who did not utilize the anonymous tip line, not at Jane Doe herself. The university is facing a no-confidence vote regarding Kotlikoff's leadership amid the ongoing civil lawsuit filed by Jane Doe against the university and other parties.
