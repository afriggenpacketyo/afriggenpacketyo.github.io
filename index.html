Uber Ordered to Pay $40 Million After Driver Expelled Passenger Who Died on Freeway

2026-09-17 19:40:47 EDT

Uber has been ordered to pay $40 million to the parents of Emily Normandin-Parker, a 23-year-old UCLA graduate who was killed on a freeway after her Uber driver allegedly ordered her and a friend out of the vehicle. The driver, Vu Tran, stopped at an "unsafe and illegal" freeway gore point after Normandin-Parker's friend became sick, demanding a cleaning fee before expelling them. This article highlights that a judge concluded Uber is responsible for its driver's conduct, rejecting the company's claim of exemption under Proposition 22. Uber expressed disagreement with the ruling, but Tran is no longer a driver, and the family plans to use the settlement to fund a foundation advocating for rider safety.
