Four-time Olympic champion Michael Johnson has publicly confirmed that athletes participating in his now-bankrupt Grand Slam Track (GST) competition have received their owed payments, offering clarity after the organization's collapse in December 2025. Johnson attributed the bankruptcy to a "catastrophic capital issue" caused by investors withdrawing funds, and revealed he personally loaned GST $2 million while foregoing his own salary to ensure the athletes were compensated. He also addressed earlier reports of a $500,000 payment to himself, explaining it was a reimbursement for travel expenses he covered when the company faced severe cash-flow problems and its credit card was maxed out. Johnson candidly shared the emotional distress and self-doubt he experienced amidst the controversy.
