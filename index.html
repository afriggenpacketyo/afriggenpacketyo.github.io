Fiji declares HIV a national crisis with a significant rise in cases

2026-09-17 05:03:00 EDT

Fiji has declared HIV a national health crisis, implementing a "whole-of-government response" due to a substantial increase in cases. The country recorded 2,016 new HIV diagnoses in 2025, a sixteenfold increase from 2019, indicating the virus is spreading beyond high-risk groups. An estimated one in every 60 adults now lives with HIV, a significant jump from one in 167 five years ago. The government is expanding access to free testing, prevention, and treatment services, with support from international partners. UNAIDS welcomed the move, emphasizing that AIDS is not over and countries need robust health systems to respond urgently.

Psychological technique of memory reconsolidation can boost self-esteem

2026-09-17 05:02:00 EDT

Psychologist Marisa Franco suggests that reshaping memories through a technique called memory reconsolidation can significantly improve self-esteem. This process involves revisiting difficult past experiences and actively intervening with your current self to offer reassurance and support to your younger self. By reframing these memories, individuals can reduce the negative impact they have on their present sense of self, leading to higher self-esteem and fewer trauma symptoms. This approach has been shown to create positive changes in the hippocampus, the brain's memory storage center.
