The article highlights an apparent lack of transparency and federal regulatory gaps, as the USDA stated it doesn't regulate mink or have an approved vaccine for them. Jennifer Nuzzo, an epidemiology professor, criticized the secrecy and noted the government's reduced monitoring of farms compared to previous administrations. The situation raises fears of potential human infection if the virus combines with influenza, underscoring the critical need for robust surveillance, worker protection, and coordinated federal response to prevent a wider pandemic.
