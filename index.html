U.S. President Donald Trump held official talks with Irish President Catherine Connolly and Prime Minister Micheál Martin in Dublin, despite Connolly's past criticisms of him. The visit, however, was largely centered on his personal interests, as he quickly departed for his oceanfront golf club in Doonbeg to attend the Irish Open. This blending of official duties and private business drew annoyance from some Dublin residents and protesters, who criticized the disruption and questioned the president's priorities amidst global turmoil. Eric Trump defended the family's presence, citing their significant investment and job creation in the area, asserting they are loved in Ireland despite the planned demonstrations against his father.
