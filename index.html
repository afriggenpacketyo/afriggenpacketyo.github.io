Understanding the 2026 Autumn Equinox and Its Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The 2026 autumn equinox will occur at 01:06 BST on September 23, marking the start of astronomical autumn. This precise moment signifies when the Sun crosses the celestial equator, resulting in roughly equal day and night lengths globally, as neither hemisphere is tilted towards or away from the Sun. While the equinox itself is not visible, it holds cultural and spiritual importance for some, with gatherings at sites like Stonehenge. Due to light refraction, the actual "equilux" (equal light) occurs a few days later, around September 25 or 26 in the UK, varying by latitude.
