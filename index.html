Researchers Investigate Noah's Ark Site with Advanced Drilling Techniques

2026-10-02 08:00:48 EDT

A research project in Turkey has completed its first full field season investigating the Durupınar site, believed by some to be Noah's Ark. Researchers used deep-core drilling to collect hundreds of soil samples from beneath the formation, reaching depths of over 60 feet. These samples are now undergoing laboratory analysis to detect any signs of man-made activity or decayed wood. Previous sampling indicated differences in organic matter and mineral content inside and outside the formation, prompting deeper investigation. The team hopes this new data will challenge previous geological explanations of the site as a natural rock formation.

Europe Experienced Record Heat Stress This Summer, EU Reports

2026-10-02 07:53:00 EDT

A new report from the EU's Copernicus Climate Service indicates that a record 52% of Europe experienced "very strong" heat stress this summer, defined as temperatures feeling above 38 degrees Celsius. Around 80% faced "strong" heat stress (feeling above 32 degrees Celsius). This summer was the third-warmest on record for Europe and the hottest for Western Europe. The continent also saw its lowest average river flows since 1992 and significantly warmer ocean temperatures, contributing to widespread drought and wildfires.
