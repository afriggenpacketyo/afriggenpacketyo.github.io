Study Reveals Chinese Script Evolution Mimics Natural Selection, Driven by Efficiency

2026-09-01 00:00:05 EDT

New research, published in Digital Scholarship in the Humanities, proposes that Chinese characters have evolved over thousands of years akin to living organisms, driven by principles of natural selection and efficiency. Led by Huang Wei and Xie Yonghui, the study reveals that a small number of core components, such as 日 (sun), 月 (moon), and 人 (human), are repeatedly combined to form new characters, with entirely new elements invented only when existing ones proved insufficient. This evolutionary pressure resulted in a simplification from complex to simplified forms, with high-frequency components dominating the script, mirroring a fundamental linguistic tendency towards achieving greater output with less cognitive and physical effort.
