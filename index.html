Alcohol initially enhances GABA, a calming neurotransmitter, and suppresses glutamate, but as it wears off, the nervous system can become overstimulated, leading to anxiety. Studies indicate that a significant percentage of drinkers experience hang-xiety, with shyness potentially exacerbating the effects. Experts advise against using alcohol to manage stress and suggest reframing anxious thoughts as temporary states rather than reflections of social catastrophe.
