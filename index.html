Finland Unveils World's Largest Sand Battery for Renewable Energy Storage

2026-07-25 01:29:38 EDT

A small town in southern Finland has deployed the world's largest commercial sand battery to address the intermittency of renewable energy. This 13-meter-tall facility uses 2,000 metric tons of crushed soapstone to store 100 megawatt-hours of thermal energy, providing heat for 5,000 residents for weeks. Developed by Polar Night Energy, the sand battery has significantly cut greenhouse gas emissions and reduced wood chip use for heating, offering a combustion-free solution. The technology, which stores heat from cheap electricity and discharges it via heat exchangers, is seen as a promising, material-efficient alternative to chemical batteries.
