T. Cullen Davis, Texas oil tycoon acquitted of murder charges, dies at 93

2026-11-09 18:44:18 EST

T. Cullen Davis, a prominent Texas oil tycoon known for being acquitted in two high-profile murder cases in the 1970s, has passed away at the age of 93. Davis maintained his innocence throughout the trials, which involved a shooting at his mansion during a contentious divorce and an alleged murder-for-hire plot. Despite his immense wealth at the time of his first trial, he was ultimately cleared in both instances. His son described him as a man of virtue and integrity who became a devout Christian in 1981. The article recounts the sensational trials and Davis's subsequent business struggles, emphasizing his enduring claim of innocence.
