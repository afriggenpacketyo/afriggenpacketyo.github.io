UN General Assembly Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Representation of Africa

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to represent the true proportions of continents more accurately. This resolution, sponsored by Togo and backed by the African Union, passed with significant support, encouraging governments and institutions globally to adopt it over the traditional Mercator map. The Mercator projection, commonly used since the 16th century for navigation, is known for distorting landmasses by exaggerating regions near the poles while diminishing the size of Africa and South America. For instance, the Equal Earth projection reveals Africa to be 14 times larger than Greenland, correcting a long-standing geographical misrepresentation. Advocates emphasize that this change is crucial for education and shaping a more truthful collective perception of the world.
