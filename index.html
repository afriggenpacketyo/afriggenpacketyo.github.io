A prominent American university has agreed to a $2.1 million settlement with the Department of Justice, resolving allegations that it failed to disclose employees' affiliations with Chinese universities and state-run research organizations when applying for federal grants from NASA and the NSF. The investigation revealed that annual faculty activity reports identified these foreign government ties, but the university did not provide this crucial information to the granting agencies. Specifically, the university learned in 2019 that a principal investigator on NASA grants was a participant in China's Thousand Talents Programme, designed to recruit individuals with access to foreign technology and intellectual property. This article highlights the increasing scrutiny on academic institutions to ensure transparency regarding foreign government support, especially concerning programs linked to intellectual property acquisition, and the consequences of non-compliance.

Two Drivers Careen Off Pacific Coast Highway Near Mugu Rock Days Apart

2026-08-31 21:03:29 EDT

Two separate incidents saw drivers careen 75 feet off the Pacific Coast Highway onto a rocky beach near Mugu Rock within days of each other, highlighting the hazardous nature of this stretch of road. In the latest event, a 37-year-old Oxnard man, driving a 2006 Honda Accord at high speed, lost control, slammed off a rock, and plunged down a cliff early Sunday morning, sustaining only minor injuries despite the severe damage to his vehicle. California Highway Patrol officers responded to calls about the southbound vehicle, which landed upright on the shoreline. This accident followed a similar incident just days prior, where a woman also drove off the highway in the same vicinity, prompting another rescue effort. The article underscores the recurring dangers faced by motorists on this coastal route.
