UN Calls for Adoption of New World Map to Accurately Represent Continents

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has adopted a "Correct the Map" resolution, urging the global community to replace the prevalent Mercator projection with the more accurate Equal Earth map. The Mercator map, designed in 1569 for maritime navigation, severely distorts landmass sizes, making continents near the poles appear disproportionately large while shrinking those near the equator, such as Africa. This distortion has been criticized for its implicit political and economic implications, potentially misrepresenting the significance of certain regions. The Equal Earth projection, adopted by the African Union, offers a truer representation of continental areas, despite losing the Mercator's straight-line navigational accuracy.
