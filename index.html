Scientists Explain How 'Fire-Generated Thunderstorms' Intensify Wildfires

2026-07-27 07:11:07 EDT

Wildfires, including those devastating the Bordeaux region of France, are being intensified by a dangerous phenomenon known as "pyrocumulonimbus" or fire-generated thunderstorms, a sight previously unrecorded in the country. These powerful, towering clouds develop when the immense heat from a wildfire causes air to ascend rapidly, carrying smoke, ash, and water vapor high into the atmosphere, leading to the formation of cumulonimbus clouds. The turbulent winds associated with these fire clouds significantly complicate firefighting efforts by scattering embers widely and unpredictably, and their lightning strikes can spark new blazes. This scientific insight underscores a critical, climate-fueled challenge, making containment efforts more arduous, especially with further heatwaves anticipated.
