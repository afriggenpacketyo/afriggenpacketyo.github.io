Spanish police have initiated an involuntary manslaughter investigation following the tragic death of Tenerife forward Jesús de Miguel, 30, who suffered cardiac arrest after an on-field collision. During a second-division match, De Miguel was struck in the chest by Andorra goalkeeper Jesús Owono, an incident that resulted in Owono receiving a straight red card and the match being abandoned. The country’s National Police launched the probe as all deaths from non-natural causes are subject to judicial inquiry under Spanish law. Tributes and condolences poured in from numerous clubs and prominent players, including Gerard Piqué and Lionel Messi, mourning the rare and devastating loss of a professional athlete.
