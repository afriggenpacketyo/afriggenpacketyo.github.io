A prison official confirmed the move, citing privacy, safety, and security reasons, while Combs' spokesman stated the transfer aligns with decisions made for safety and security. Combs, who is currently serving a 50-month sentence for two prostitution-related crimes, is in the process of appealing his conviction and seeking release. His spokesman expressed gratitude for the Federal Bureau of Prisons' attention to Combs' well-being, amidst various tabloid reports concerning his health and alleged "luxury lifestyle" at his previous incarceration site. FCI Allenwood Low accommodates a significantly smaller inmate population compared to his former facility, Fort Dix.
