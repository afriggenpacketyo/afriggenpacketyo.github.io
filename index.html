The Social Democrats (SPD), leading the state coalition, saw a sharp drop in support. These elections served as a crucial barometer of public sentiment ahead of upcoming state polls, particularly in eastern German states where the AfD is expected to perform even stronger. The increased voter turnout also underscores the heightened political engagement surrounding these shifts.
