Judge Reyes expressed extreme disappointment and fury, stating the government's actions were an "absolute worst" effort at providing notice, contradicting previous assurances to the court. She extended a temporary restraining order and demanded immediate testimony from National Park Service personnel to explain who authorized the tree removal, questioning whether the court had been deliberately misled.
