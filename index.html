2026-09-29 10:50:09 EDT

Nigerian dancer Benjamin Daniel, widely known as "Ben Dancer," has successfully completed an extraordinary 168-hour (seven-day) non-stop dance marathon in Lagos, aiming to secure a new Guinness World Records title. Cheered enthusiastically by friends and supporters at Freedom Park, the 33-year-old continued dancing for an additional nine hours beyond his initial target time. His dedicated team has now submitted the comprehensive evidence required for verification against the current record of 127 hours, set by an Indian dancer in 2023. Ben Dancer's performance, which drew upon Nigeria's rich dance traditions and vibrant Afrobeats culture, was intended to showcase cultural diversity while rigorously testing his physical endurance. He is currently undergoing a medical check-up following the physically demanding feat.
