Tracing the Chinese Origins of the Phrase 'Gung-Ho' Amidst US-China Diplomacy

US President Donald Trump's reference to the "Chinese phrase" "gung-ho" during his welcome of Chinese President Xi Jinping at the White House sparked linguistic curiosity, particularly among Chinese audiences. The term "gung-ho" originates from the Chinese "gong he" (工合), an abbreviation for "gongye hezuoshe" (工业合作社), meaning "industrial cooperatives." These were vital grassroots labor units established in China in 1938, dedicated to producing industrial supplies for the war effort against Japan. This shared historical linguistic root, though perhaps unfamiliar to many, highlighted a subtle connection between the two nations' pasts.
