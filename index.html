AMD's Market Value Surges Towards $1 Trillion, Driven by AI Agent Demand

2026-09-28 18:27:10 EDT

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) recently saw its market capitalization briefly surpass $1 trillion, with CNBC's Jim Cramer confidently asserting that CEO Lisa Su's exceptional leadership continues to drive significant growth. Cramer attributes AMD's remarkable performance, including a nearly 30% gain in September alone, to the burgeoning demand for central processing units (CPUs) fueled by the rapid expansion of agentic AI platforms such as Meta's Muse. Under Su's guidance, AMD has undergone a profound transformation from a $2.5 billion company, steadily gaining market share against rivals like Intel and securing major commitments for its graphics processing units (GPUs) from prominent AI spenders including Meta and OpenAI.
