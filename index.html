2026-11-03 09:17:52 EST

The U.S. Supreme Court is scheduled to hear arguments in a pivotal case concerning Colorado's decision to exclude two Catholic preschools from a state-funded education program because of their policy to reject children with LGBTQ+ parents. The preschools contend that this exclusion amounts to unlawful religious discrimination, violating their First Amendment rights, and are seeking access to the same benefits afforded to other publicly funded educational institutions. Conversely, Colorado asserts that participation in its preschool program mandates adherence to equal-opportunity requirements applicable to all providers, irrespective of religious affiliation, sexual orientation, or gender identity. This case presents a significant legal and societal conflict between religious freedom and nondiscrimination laws, with the potential to redefine their interplay across the nation.
