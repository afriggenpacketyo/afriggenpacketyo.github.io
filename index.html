Local authorities reported new Russian strikes on a medical facility in Kherson city center and injuries from a drone attack on a car. Ukraine accused Russia of exploiting the humanitarian pause to strengthen its military positions, rotate units, and deliver ammunition, an accusation partially corroborated by the Kremlin. Although the ceasefire was brokered by U.S. First Lady Melania Trump, and both nations expressed gratitude, Russia has since dismissed the idea of further widespread truces, indicating the isolated nature of this brief respite.
