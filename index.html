This significant financial commitment targets critical sectors such as artificial intelligence, semiconductors, and advanced manufacturing, spurred by the recognition of frontier tech's importance amid escalating geopolitical tensions. India aims to cultivate robust local Deep Tech companies, viewing U.S. export controls and skepticism towards Chinese technology as catalysts for domestic innovation. (not investment advice)
