While the equinox itself is not visually observable, it carries considerable cultural and spiritual significance for many, prompting gatherings at ancient sites such as Stonehenge and Glastonbury Tor to mark the occasion and witness the sunrise. The article clarifies that while the equinox heralds the start of the season, it does not guarantee an immediate shift in weather patterns, though temperatures typically fall as the days progressively shorten.
