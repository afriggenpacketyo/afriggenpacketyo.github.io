Fery, 24, was unable to replicate his recent Wimbledon heroics, where he reached the semi-finals and catapulted into the top 40 rankings. Despite a promising start on Grandstand, Musetti's versatile "vintage" game, featuring an impressive one-handed backhand, proved too strong. Fery showed resilience, breaking back in the third set and saving match points, but ultimately couldn't maintain the momentum against the experienced opponent. This article details the challenges of a major tournament debut and the high level of competition, as Fery looks to build on his breakout season and gain further experience at Grand Slams.
