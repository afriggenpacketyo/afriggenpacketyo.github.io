Ranked Choice Voting Explained as Two States Utilize System for Congressional Races

2026-11-03 20:46:42 EST

Ranked choice voting (RCV) is being utilized in Maine and Alaska for congressional and state races, enabling voters to rank candidates in order of preference. The system ensures that if no candidate achieves a majority of first-choice votes, an instant runoff process eliminates the candidate with the fewest top rankings, redistributing their votes based on subsequent preferences until a majority winner is determined. Advocates highlight that RCV reduces the "spoiler effect" of third-party candidates and fosters more positive campaigning, ultimately yielding winners with broader electoral mandates.
