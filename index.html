The "Lost Funds" tracking tool identified specific examples, including maternal health grants in Michigan and assistance for minority farmers in Iowa, with California, Texas, and New York seeing the highest amounts of interrupted funding. Organizers assert these disruptions extend beyond typical administrative changes, highlighting a significant and widespread impact on state-level programs and their residents.
