Newport Beach Jeweler Sentenced to Five Years for Defrauding Clients

2026-08-31 16:16:12 EDT

Nelson Holdo, a Newport Beach jeweler, has been sentenced to five years in state prison after pleading guilty to three felony counts of stealing from his clients. Prosecutors allege Holdo defrauded clients of over $1.7 million by taking money for Rolexes, diamonds, and other jewelry without delivering the items, or by reselling clients' valuables and keeping the proceeds. During his sentencing, victims expressed how Holdo cultivated relationships to exploit their trust. Holdo had a history of complaints and business closures dating back to the 2000s. He was also ordered to pay back the defrauded amount as part of his plea deal.

Los Angeles Woman Gives Birth to Rare Triplets, Close to World Record Weight

2026-08-31 16:12:20 EDT

A Los Angeles woman, Nylah Craft, has given birth to naturally conceived triplets – Elio, Elias, and Enzo – at Kaiser Permanente South Ward Medical Center. Doctors describe the pregnancy and outcome as "very rare," noting that natural triplet conception occurs in about one in 10,000 pregnancies. The babies were born healthy at 36 weeks gestation, with a combined weight close to the Guinness World Record for triplets. Notably, two of the triplets are identical twins, and the third is a fraternal twin. The family, which also includes two older children, is receiving support from hospital staff and family members.
