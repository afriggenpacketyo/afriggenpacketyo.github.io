Actress Shelley Fabares, known for her roles as Mary Stone in 'The Donna Reed Show' and Francine Webster in 'One Day at a Time,' has died at the age of 82. Fabares began her acting career at a young age and also achieved musical success with her hit song 'Johnny Angel.' She starred alongside Elvis Presley in several films and later earned two Primetime Emmy nominations for her role in 'Coach.' Fabares dedicated her later years to caring for her mother, who had Alzheimer's disease, and advocating for research funding. Her family remembered her for her love, strength, and grace.
