2026-09-15 18:49:55 EDT

The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) successfully thwarted an ISIS-inspired "mass casualty attack" planned by Jonathan Kramer, a 21-year-old Pennsylvania man who had pledged allegiance to the Islamic State. Kramer was apprehended outside a hotel with a rifle, ammunition, and dozens of knives, following a covert investigation where federal agents tracked his movements and observed suspicious purchases. Officials stated that Kramer demonstrated a sustained devotion to ISIS, actively spreading propaganda and discussing various attack methodologies, including the use of chemical poisons, vehicle attacks, and drone-delivered explosives. Authorities credited crucial public cooperation in reporting Kramer's "dangerous and troubling behavior" as instrumental in enabling law enforcement to intervene and prevent what could have been a horrific act of violence within the community.

Canada Pitches Itself as "Safe Harbor" for Global Investors at Summit

2026-09-15 18:46:20 EDT

Prime Minister Mark Carney has actively promoted Canada as a "safe harbor" for major global investors, aiming to attract C$1 trillion in investments over the next five years to bolster the nation's economic resilience and reduce its reliance on the United States. At a high-profile summit in Toronto, Carney showcased over 160 diverse projects, ranging from data centers to critical infrastructure and port expansions, while also offering new tax incentives and streamlined approval processes for large-scale developments. The summit reportedly generated nearly C$500 billion in new investment commitments across various sectors, with AI infrastructure projects drawing significant interest due to Canada's favorable cold climate and clean energy grid. Despite protests from groups concerned about the potential privatization of public resources, the initiative seeks to strategically strengthen Canada's economy and diversify its international partnerships.
