On-loan Manchester City midfielder Jack Grealish has issued an apology to Everton fans and the club for his recent absence from games due to a disciplinary breach. Grealish missed three matches, including Premier League and Carabao Cup fixtures, before returning to action in a behind-closed-doors victory against Derby County. The 31-year-old, who is trying to rebuild his career at Everton after falling out of favor at City despite his £100m transfer in 2021, expressed his love for the club and eagerness to play in front of the fans again. His manager, David Moyes, had previously indicated the need to wait and see how the issue would be resolved during the international break, and Grealish's apology signals a step towards moving past the incident.
