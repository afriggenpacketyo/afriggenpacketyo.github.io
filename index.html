WestJet Flight Attendants Strike Over Pay, Disrupting Summer Travel

2026-08-02 02:38:20 EDT

Flight attendants at WestJet, Canada's second-largest airline, have initiated a strike, impacting travel during a peak summer holiday period. The core issue revolves around pay, specifically compensation for duties performed before a flight departs, known as ground pay. WestJet has begun cancelling flights and is offering free cancellations and schedule changes for affected passengers. Contract negotiations between the union and the airline have been ongoing since September 2025, with a key sticking point being the union's demand for payment for up to 35 hours of unpaid work per month. The airline maintains its current pay model, which includes compensation for pre-takeoff duties, is standard across North America. This disruption affects numerous domestic and international destinations served by WestJet.
