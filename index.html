The reduction in Capital One holdings, made ahead of its earnings report and accompanied by a "hold" rating downgrade, reflects concerns about front-loaded investments and the bank's vulnerability to credit exposure. This article details the Trust's portfolio adjustments, which also include increasing financial exposure through investments in Bank of New York, a firm with lower credit sensitivity.
