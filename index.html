Brink criticized Freedom's attempts to gain eligibility for the WNBA draft by claiming transgender status, calling it a bid for relevance and an attack on a vulnerable group. She emphasized that transgender issues are "a non-issue" and that individuals should not be targeted for clickbait. Brink reiterated her past support for transgender athletes, advocating for acceptance and inclusivity in sports. Her statements come amid ongoing debates about transgender participation in women's sports.
