Proposed U.S. Diesel Export Ban Could Worsen Global Shortage, Experts Warn

2026-09-23 09:13:15 EDT

Calls from some Republican lawmakers to ban U.S. diesel exports as a solution to record-high domestic prices are being met with warnings from energy experts that such a move could backfire. Average diesel costs have reached a record $6.53 a gallon, significantly impacting agriculture, trucking, and small businesses. Experts argue that barring exports would worsen the global supply crunch, as U.S. refineries produce more diesel than domestically consumed, and prices are already driven by international disruptions from the Iran and Ukraine wars. An export ban could lead to reduced refinery output or shifts to other fuels, potentially driving prices higher domestically. Alternative solutions suggested include waiving the Jones Act and temporarily suspending renewable fuel mandates.

Third Rescued Beluga Whale Dies at Illinois Aquarium After Transfer from Canada

2026-09-23 09:00:18 EDT

A third beluga whale, Osiris, rescued from the closed Canadian marine park Marineland, has died at the Shedd aquarium in Chicago. Osiris, a 26-year-old female, struggled to acclimate to her new environment, experiencing inconsistent appetite and health declines. Her death follows those of two other rescued belugas, Peekachu and Rain, at the Shedd, and another beluga, Jelly Bean, in Spain. The Shedd aquarium acknowledged the significant vulnerabilities of the rescued animals upon arrival and the inherent risks involved in such complex rescue operations. Beluga whales are particularly susceptible to environmental changes, and their populations are classified as endangered. The transfers were part of an effort to rehome animals from Marineland after its closure and prevent euthanasia.
