The attack involved an explosives-laden car and a subsequent gun battle with militants who were reportedly still holed up in a section of the complex. The Pakistani Taliban (TTP) has claimed responsibility for the assault. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif condemned the act, emphasizing the nation's resolve, while the Interior Ministry highlighted the 'cowardly actions' of the attackers. The incident occurs amid strained relations between Pakistan and Afghanistan, with Pakistan blaming the Afghan Taliban for providing safe havens for militants.
