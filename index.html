Guilfoyle vehemently denied all accusations, vowing to "vigorously defend" her name and integrity, asserting that any money sought was for unpaid public appearances predating her ambassadorial nomination. The State Department's inspector general is reportedly reviewing the allegations, which had strained U.S.-Greek relations and prompted calls for a formal investigation from Democratic lawmakers.
