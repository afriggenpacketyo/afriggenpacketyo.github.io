Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation has prompted concerns about the chipmaker's extensive financial commitments to the AI industry, earning it the moniker "central bank of AI," and potential systemic financial risks. USPS Announces Permanent Fuel Surcharge and Rate Hikes Starting 2027

2026-11-12 18:07:31 EST

The U.S. Postal Service has announced that its temporary 8% fuel surcharge will become a permanent fixture in 2027, coupled with an additional 3% price increase, culminating in an 11% hike in postage rates. This significant adjustment, approved by the USPS Board of Governors and effective January 17, 2027, aims to generate much-needed revenue for the financially challenged agency, which reported a substantial $6.5 billion loss for fiscal year 2026. Furthermore, the USPS plans to implement average increases of 5.9% for domestic shipping services and 7.9% for international rates. This strategic move directly responds to soaring diesel prices and forms a key component of the agency's broader 10-year plan designed to reduce expenses and ultimately restore long-term profitability.
