Former NFL star JJ Watt has finalized a multi-year agreement with CBS Sports to continue as the lead NFL game analyst alongside play-by-play announcer Jim Nantz. This deal guarantees Watt will call his first Super Bowl in February 2028. Watt expressed his honor and privilege in taking on this role, highlighting his enthusiasm for broadcasting. After a distinguished 12-season playing career, Watt transitioned to broadcasting with CBS in 2023, initially as a studio analyst before moving to the broadcast booth. His promotion to the top booth follows Tony Romo's departure from the network. CBS Sports chairman David Berson praised Watt's insights, preparation, and team-oriented approach.

Dollar General Agrees to $1.9 Million Settlement Over Price Overcharging in New York

2026-10-22 13:19:07 EDT

Dollar General has agreed to a $1.875 million settlement to resolve allegations of repeatedly overcharging customers across New York State. The New York Attorney General's office found that the company frequently charged higher prices at checkout than advertised on shelf labels, signs, and its mobile app, with some discrepancies as high as 39%. The settlement requires Dollar General to donate food and personal items to a regional food bank and implement comprehensive price-accuracy safeguards at its over 600 New York stores. The company will also reformat its receipts to improve clarity for consumers. This agreement follows a Guardian investigation that reported similar overcharging issues at Dollar General stores nationwide.

Trump Ordered to Answer Questions Under Oath in BBC Defamation Lawsuit

2026-10-22 13:14:04 EDT

A U.S. judge has ordered President Donald Trump to undergo a deposition by December 11 in his defamation lawsuit against the BBC over a documentary that allegedly edited his 2021 speech. U.S. Magistrate Judge Enjoliqué Lett mandated the deposition, warning of potential penalties, including dismissal of the lawsuit, if Trump fails to comply. Trump's lawyers had previously postponed a deposition, citing "matters of national importance." The BBC argued that Trump was available for political events on the day of the scheduled deposition. Trump's legal team has proposed a deposition date in January, asserting they are not avoiding the process. The lawsuit claims the BBC "intentionally, maliciously, and deceptively doctoring" the speech, while the BBC has apologized for the editing but disputes the defamation claim.
