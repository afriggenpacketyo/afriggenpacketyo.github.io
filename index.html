Study Links Earlier Bedtimes to Weight Loss in Prediabetic Adults

2026-11-07 17:34:20 EST

New research from NYU Rory Meyers College of Nursing suggests that falling asleep earlier may contribute to weight loss in adults with prediabetes. The study, published in Nursing Research, found that each hour earlier participants fell asleep was associated with approximately 1.3 pounds of weight loss over eight weeks, though it doesn't prove causation. While sleep coaching improved sleep quality, it didn't significantly increase weight loss compared to lifestyle education alone, nor did it improve blood sugar levels. Researchers hypothesize that earlier bedtimes might limit late-night eating and better align food intake with the body's optimal metabolic window.
