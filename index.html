Media literacy initiatives, though valuable, show mixed results, sometimes leading to a complete erosion of public trust in all information sources. The piece stresses the critical need for independent oversight bodies and robust safeguards to ensure that laws ostensibly designed to combat disinformation are not weaponized to stifle legitimate criticism and suppress free expression, which is a significant challenge to stability.
