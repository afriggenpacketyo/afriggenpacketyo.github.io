Understanding the Autumn Equinox 2026 and Its Astronomical Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The 2026 autumn equinox will occur on September 23 at 01:06 BST, marking the astronomical start of autumn. This precise moment signifies when the Sun crosses the celestial equator, resulting in roughly equal day and night lengths globally as neither hemisphere is tilted towards or away from the Sun. While the equinox itself is invisible, it holds cultural and spiritual importance, with gatherings at sites like Stonehenge. Due to light refraction, the actual "equilux" of equal light happens a few days later, specifically September 25 or 26 in the UK, depending on latitude.
