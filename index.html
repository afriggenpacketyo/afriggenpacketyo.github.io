The Green Party of England and Wales is reviewing its social media operations following a controversy involving leader Zack Polanski, who re-posted a photo containing an image of a guillotine and a slogan referencing Nigel Farage. While the party insists the review is not directly linked to this incident, it has raised questions about the sustainability of Polanski managing his own high-profile social media channels. This article highlights the risks associated with his spontaneous approach, despite its role in the party's surge in popularity. The incident, which Farage claimed was incitement to murder, has drawn criticism both externally and internally, prompting calls for "tight controls" on Polanski's online output.
