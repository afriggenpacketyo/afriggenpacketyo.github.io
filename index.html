Significant federal funding cuts during the Trump administration, particularly impacting the CDC and FDA, have left local food safety programs critically understaffed, impeding essential investigations and containment efforts. Epidemiologists, including former FDA official Susan Mayne, emphasize the disruption of international partnerships and a lack of clear public information, contributing to increased vulnerability to foodborne illnesses. The article asserts that years of underinvestment, exacerbated by recent budget reductions, are now manifesting as direct harm to public health and a diminished capacity to effectively track and control outbreaks.
