Understanding Pneumonic Plague: Symptoms, Transmission, and Dangers

2026-10-06 11:35:22 EDT

Pneumonic plague is a severe lung infection, considered the most virulent form of plague, distinct from bubonic and septicaemic types. Symptoms include shortness of breath, chest pain, and coughing up blood, with a very short incubation period, sometimes just 24 hours. While rare, it can spread human-to-human through infected droplets, requiring close contact. Historically linked to the Black Death, pneumonic plague is nearly always fatal if untreated, but responds well to early antibiotic intervention. Experts emphasize the importance of lab research on Yersinia pestis for diagnostics, vaccines, and surveillance, despite public alarm over lab incidents.
