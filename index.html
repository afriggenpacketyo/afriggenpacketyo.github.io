The senators argue this service could constitute an "outrageous abuse of the President's office for his personal benefit" and potentially violate laws against insider trading and market manipulation. They are urging the SEC to conduct a legal analysis before the service launches on August 1. A Trump Media spokesperson dismissed the concerns, stating the senators misunderstand free markets or public versus nonpublic information. The company's stock has seen a significant decline since its public trading began.
