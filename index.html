Ten lions have died in a suspected poisoning incident within Tanzania's Ngorongoro Conservation Area, a UNESCO-listed site. Preliminary findings suggest the lions ingested poison, and an investigation is underway to determine the cause and identify those responsible. The Ngorongoro area has experienced ongoing tensions between authorities and the local nomadic community, with past clashes resulting in fatalities. Lions are classified as vulnerable, and Ngorongoro is known for its dense lion population. This incident follows reports of other wildlife deaths from suspected pesticide poisoning in neighboring Kenya.
