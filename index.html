Mixi Cruz, a courageous survivor, recounts her harrowing personal experience of sexual exploitation from a young age, powerfully illustrating how perpetrators exploit profound vulnerabilities and employ emotional manipulation to isolate their victims. Civil society organizations, such as the Citizens' Council for Security and Justice, report that a staggering seven out of ten human trafficking survivors are female, with over half being minors, and they estimate the true number of cases is dramatically higher than official reports. Despite dedicated awareness campaigns, the article suggests that an increase in tourism, particularly during high-profile events like the World Cup, can inadvertently create more opportunities for exploitation, exacerbated by factors such as language barriers and a pervasive culture of impunity.
