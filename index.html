UN Advocates for New World Map to Correct Landmass Distortions

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has passed a "Correct the Map" resolution, urging the replacement of the widely used Mercator projection with the Equal Earth map to more accurately represent continent sizes. The Mercator map, developed in 1569, distorts landmasses, making regions near the poles appear larger than they are, a critical issue for accurately perceiving continents like Africa. While the Mercator map excels in navigation by preserving angles, the Equal Earth projection, adopted by the African Union, prioritizes true landmass size and shape, though it sacrifices accurate distances and straight lines for navigation. This initiative addresses the psychological and geopolitical implications of map distortions.
