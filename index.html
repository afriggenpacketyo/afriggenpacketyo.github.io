U.S. layoffs have reached a four-year low, with employers announcing a total of 573,195 job cuts through September of this year, representing a nearly 40% decline compared to the first nine months of 2025. On a monthly basis, layoffs in September dropped by 20% to 43,281, marking the lowest level observed in four years. This encouraging labor data, combined with a 3% increase in employers' hiring plans for the year, strongly suggests a stable and potentially reaccelerating labor market. Furthermore, initial jobless claims also declined, reinforcing the indication that businesses are hesitant to dismiss their current workforce. These positive trends offer the Federal Reserve continued flexibility for potential future interest rate hikes, even amidst persistent inflation concerns. (not investment advice)

Steelers Trade Cornerback Joey Porter Jr. to Cowboys Following Contract Dispute

2026-10-01 11:53:27 EDT

The Pittsburgh Steelers have officially traded their fourth-year cornerback, Joey Porter Jr., to the Dallas Cowboys in exchange for a 2028 second-round draft pick and a 2027 sixth-round pick. This significant move follows a prolonged contract standoff and a lingering back issue that kept Porter out of training camp and preseason activities. Porter, the son of a former Steelers legend, had publicly stated his commitment to playing for Pittsburgh this season despite the unresolved dispute over his rookie deal, which was set to expire. This transaction marks the third Steelers player to be traded to the Cowboys in the last two years, underscoring a notable trend of substantial roster changes and strategic personnel decisions for the Pittsburgh franchise.
