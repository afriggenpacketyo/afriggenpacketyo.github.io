FDA Regulations Define What Can Be Labeled as 'Peanut Butter'

2026-09-27 16:25:36 EDT

US Food & Drug Administration (FDA) regulations mandate that a product must contain at least 90% peanuts to be legally classified and labeled as 'peanut butter.' Products containing additional ingredients, such as palm oil, even if they still maintain a high peanut percentage, are required to be labeled as 'peanut butter spread.' This legal distinction originated from a 1959 FDA survey that found manufacturers were reducing peanut content by substituting cheaper oils. Following a public hearing and a 1961 proposal, the FDA's 90% peanut standard was eventually approved by the US Appeals Court, guiding consumers on product composition today.
