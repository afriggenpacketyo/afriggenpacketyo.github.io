French authorities confirmed that medical facilities storing blood were impacted by recent cyberattacks on refrigeration systems, similar to those affecting European supermarkets. The French Blood Establishment (EFS) reported that while temperatures were set to rise, staff interventions ensured no blood products were lost or health impacts identified. Theories about the culprits are swirling, with Russian hacktivist groups initially claiming responsibility for hacking a component supplier, Danfoss, though experts question their direct involvement in the supermarket attacks. Cyber-security researchers are using "honeypots" to study attacker methods, noting the attacks were highly targeted in Europe and could involve Iranian state actors or even sophisticated teenagers, with no formal attribution yet made.
