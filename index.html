UN Calls for New World Map to Accurately Reflect Continent Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has passed a resolution, "Correct the Map," advocating for the replacement of the widely used Mercator projection with a map that more accurately represents the true sizes of continents. The Mercator map, created in 1569, distorts landmasses, making continents like Africa appear significantly smaller than their actual size, while exaggerating areas near the poles. Critics argue this distortion has psychological and political implications, implicitly downplaying the importance of certain regions. The UN adopted the Equal Earth projection, which offers a better representation of landmass sizes and shapes, though it sacrifices the straight lines essential for navigation on Mercator maps.
