ODI Cricket Rule Change Revitalizes Reverse Swing for Bowlers

Consequently, pacers' economy rates and bowling averages have improved, making it harder for batters to score quickly at the death. While England has notably benefited, the impact varies globally, with less pronounced effects in Australia and South Africa, potentially altering future World Cup strategies.
