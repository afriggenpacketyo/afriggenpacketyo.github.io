The Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) maintains exclusive jurisdiction over such contracts, arguing that their classification as swaps is independent of the underlying subject matter. This ruling is seen as a significant victory for Nevada's gaming regulators and may reshape the future of prediction markets.
