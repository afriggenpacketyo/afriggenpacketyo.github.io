A police department in Trinidad, Texas, has been ordered to close by state officials after failing to demonstrate it provided any benefit to the community and met minimum operational standards. The investigation was prompted by the department's arrest of a resident who complained on social media about contaminated water, and a subsequent arrest of a supporter. The Texas Commission on Law Enforcement cited issues with use-of-force policies, active shooter preparedness, vehicle pursuits, and professional conduct. The department also lacked basic resources like bulletproof vests. Police duties will be transferred to the Henderson County Sheriff's Office until a permanent solution is found, as city officials acknowledge past neglect.

U.S. Space Force confirms deployment of weapons in orbit for the first time

2026-09-15 09:13:00 EDT

The U.S. Air Force Secretary has officially confirmed that the Space Force has deployed "on-orbit space control weapons," marking the first public acknowledgement of such capabilities. While the specific nature and function of these weapons were not disclosed, experts suggest they could range from reversible interference to kinetic destructive effects. This announcement comes amid growing concerns about counterspace development by adversaries like Russia and China. The U.S. disclosure is seen as a strategic move potentially aimed at deterrence and solidifying the Space Force's role as a warfighting branch. However, it is also viewed as a potentially destabilizing statement that could prompt responses from other space-faring nations.

Lower-paid workers are receiving significant pay raises by switching jobs, report finds

2026-09-15 09:09:01 EDT

Lower-paid, hourly workers are experiencing their fastest wage growth in over three years by changing jobs, according to research from the Bank of America Institute. In July, the average wage increase for job switchers reached 12.5%, indicating increased mobility and bargaining power for these workers. While pay bumps are below pandemic levels, economists are encouraged by this acceleration, noting a narrowing pay differential between lower and higher-income earners. Factors contributing to this trend include a skills mismatch in the labor market and a higher demand for specific qualifications. Conversely, white-collar workers in AI-exposed industries are exhibiting "job-hugging" behavior due to weaker job growth and increased competition.
