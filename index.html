Climate Change Drives Tourists to Cooler Destinations, Raising Environmental Concerns

2026-08-17 07:03:00 EDT

As heatwaves intensify in Southern Europe, tourists are increasingly seeking cooler destinations, a trend dubbed "coolcations." This shift is placing new pressures on fragile natural environments in places like Norway, where increased visitor numbers could disturb nesting eagles and widen hiking trails to damaging extents. Tour operators are adapting by guiding visitors to managed trails, but destinations must also actively manage tourism to prevent nature from being overwhelmed. Some countries are experimenting with tourism taxes to fund infrastructure, while others are exploring ecotourism initiatives. However, even traditionally cooler destinations are warming, raising concerns about the long-term sustainability of this travel trend.
