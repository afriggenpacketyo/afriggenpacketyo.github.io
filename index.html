He ascended to the throne in 1995 at just three years old, becoming the 13th monarch of a kingdom founded in the early 19th Century. While Uganda is governed by an elected president, its traditional kingdoms retain significant cultural influence. King Oyo was widely respected for actively promoting causes such as youth empowerment, education, and climate conservation. His Prime Minister announced his passing as an "immeasurable loss" and confirmed that the continuity of the Crown is assured, as he leaves behind a male heir whose identity will be revealed according to Tooro Kingdom traditions.
