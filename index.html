2026-10-22 18:23:30 EDT

Latina DJs and artists are commemorating a decade of neoperreo, an innovative Latin American genre that originated online, fundamentally reimagining reggaeton for femmes and "frikis," encompassing cis and trans women, as well as alternative subcultures. Born from SoundCloud and DIY parties, neoperreo fuses techno with dembow rhythms, characterized by metallic vocals and empowering lyrics that boldly reclaim female sexuality. Pioneering artists like Tomasa del Real and DJ Lizz successfully cultivated a global network and established safe, inclusive spaces for outsiders, particularly queer and trans Latines, who discovered liberation within its experimental sound and vibrant community. Despite initial resistance, the genre has flourished, subtly influencing mainstream music while steadfastly preserving its distinctive, subversive identity.
