2026-07-30 06:00:00 EDT

Parents in Boyle Heights are expressing profound concern over the impending reopening of eight schools situated within a mile of the Lineage warehouse fire, which has enveloped neighborhoods in the stench of rotting food, flies, and rats. Families worry about the adverse effects on their children's health, concentration, and ability to engage in outdoor activities. While Los Angeles Unified officials insist schools are safe, with enhanced cleaning and air filtration, parents and community leaders are demanding more decisive action, including quicker cleanup of the fire site and potential temporary relocation of students. This situation highlights deep-seated environmental justice issues affecting lower-income communities.
