OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang are reportedly scheduled to attend a White House state dinner with Chinese President Xi Jinping next week, alongside other tech leaders like Apple's Tim Cook. This gathering occurs amid a heated debate among AI executives regarding the pace of AI development and regulation, with Altman and others advocating for a slowdown and government guardrails, while figures like Huang and Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg argue against government intervention. The dinner's guest list highlights the high-stakes intersection of artificial intelligence, national security, and international relations, particularly concerning US-China competition in the AI sector. The White House has not officially confirmed the guest list.
