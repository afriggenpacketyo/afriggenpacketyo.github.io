These concerted efforts aim to erode public trust in US security commitments and disrupt the democratic process. Taiwan, consistently ranked among the countries most severely targeted by foreign disinformation, continues to confront substantial challenges in safeguarding its electoral integrity amidst Beijing's persistent claims over the island.
