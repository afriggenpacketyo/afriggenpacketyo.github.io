Episode eight of "The Celebrity Traitors" saw the "Original Celebrity Traitor" Maya Jama, along with recruits King Kenny and James Acaster, successfully murder faithful Miranda Hart, reducing the remaining players to ten. Miranda, a self-declared "Miss Marple wannabe," was eliminated after being perceived as a good detective, much to her frustration, reflecting on her personal journey on the show. The mission, set in a "field of screams," involved retrieving gold and a game-changing power: a double dagger, which was awarded to traitor King Kenny. This strategic blunder by the faithfuls, who unwittingly saved the traitors, leaves the series on a knife edge with the cloaked interlopers in a strong position going into the final two episodes.
