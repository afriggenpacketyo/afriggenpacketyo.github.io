2026-09-18 11:14:00 EDT

Temple Israel synagogue in Michigan has reopened its sanctuary for the Jewish High Holidays, six months after it was targeted in a "targeted act of violence" by a truck packed with fireworks and gasoline. The attack, which occurred in March, caused significant damage and water and smoke damage to relics. The congregation is returning to celebrate Rosh Hashanah, emphasizing a message of resilience and commitment to Judaism. The synagogue estimates rebuilding costs could exceed $40 million, plus an additional $12 million for enhanced security measures. Despite the trauma, the community expresses feelings of uplift, optimism, and joy upon returning to their home.
