2026-09-27 16:25:36 EDT

U.S. Food & Drug Administration regulations mandate that products must contain at least 90% peanuts to be legally classified as "peanut butter." If a product includes additional ingredients like palm oil, even if it still has a high peanut content, it must be labeled as "peanut butter spread." This regulation stems from a 1959 FDA survey that found manufacturers were reducing peanut content by substituting cheaper oils.
