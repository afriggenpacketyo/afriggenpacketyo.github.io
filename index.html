Actress Wunmi Mosaku has confirmed the birth of her second child, a son, and shared details in the October issue of British Vogue. Mosaku, who also has a nearly three-year-old daughter, reflected on her pregnancy during awards season, praising designers for embracing her bump and hoping for continued inclusivity in fashion. She also spoke about the physical and emotional challenges of pregnancy as a Black woman, citing medical bias and statistics. Mosaku discussed her upcoming maternity wear line, Iyadé, inspired by her own experiences with disappointing maternity and nursing wear. The feature highlights her personal journey and her commitment to creating supportive fashion for mothers.

Arizona medical examiner works to accurately track heat-related deaths amid climate change

2026-09-16 19:07:40 EDT

Greg Hess, the medical examiner for Pima County, Arizona, is leading efforts to accurately count heat-related deaths, which are significantly underreported nationally. His office is developing innovative strategies to identify and track deaths exacerbated by extreme heat, a growing problem due to climate change. Current national estimates are considered a vast undercount, as heat is often not listed as a primary cause of death even when it contributes significantly. Hess's team collects detailed contextual data from death scenes to attribute deaths to heat exposure. This data is crucial for informing public health policies, such as the placement of cooling centers and identifying at-risk populations, ultimately aiming to save lives in the face of rising temperatures.
