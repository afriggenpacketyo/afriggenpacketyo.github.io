2026-08-08 08:00:00 EDT

This opinion article highlights Europe's summer of extreme heat, with all 27 cities monitored by Italy's health ministry on red alert and record temperatures across western Europe. The heatwave has led to devastating consequences, including at least 5,700 "excess deaths" in France, many among the elderly and isolated, and nearly 100 drownings in Germany. Funeral homes have faced unprecedented capacity issues, a situation the author chillingly compares to conditions in war zones. Scientists directly link the steady rise in heat to climate change, predicting increased wildfires and significant agricultural losses across the European Union, underscoring the urgent and escalating impact of environmental shifts on human life and infrastructure.
