This extensive operation, which has led to 40 arrests of individuals allegedly affiliated with Hamas or Palestinian Islamic Jihad, coincides with the closure of West Bank crossings into Israel until after Tuesday's Israeli elections. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, facing reelection pressure, is keen to demonstrate a strong security stance amidst a surge of violence that has resulted in numerous Israeli and Palestinian fatalities this year.
