Former Australian Bishop Christopher Saunders Sentenced for Sexual Abuse

2026-10-07 00:22:07 EDT

Former Australian bishop Christopher Saunders has been sentenced to six and a half years in prison for the sexual abuse of two young Aboriginal men. Saunders, who resigned as Bishop of Broome in 2020, was found guilty of one count of rape and 12 counts of indecent assault.
