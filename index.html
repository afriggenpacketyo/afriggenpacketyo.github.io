A California nonprofit, Camp Agape California, faced a significant setback when a 24-foot trailer packed with sleeping bags, towels, and other essential supplies for its free summer camping trip for children of incarcerated parents was stolen. Co-founder Elizabeth Vallejo, who has channeled personal grief and hardship into the camp's purpose, emphasized that the organization's faith-based mission to help children heal, practice forgiveness, and avoid the criminal justice system remains unwavering. Despite the substantial loss, the organization, which expects to host over 200 children and relies on 200 volunteers this year, remains committed to ensuring the camp happens. They have since replaced the stolen trailer and are close to reaching their $28,000 fundraising goal through GoFundMe.

Europe Experiences Unprecedented "Summer of Heat" with Tragic Consequences

2026-08-08 08:00:00 EDT

Europe is enduring an unprecedented "summer of heat," with all 27 cities monitored by Italy's health ministry on red alert and record-breaking temperatures across western Europe. This extreme weather has led to tragic consequences, including at least 5,700 "excess deaths" in France during June, many among the elderly and isolated, and nearly 100 drownings in Germany. Funeral homes have struggled with capacity, with one Paris director comparing the situation to war zones. Scientists unequivocally link the steady rise in heat to climate change, predicting increased farm losses and more frequent, devastating fires across the continent. The article portrays a grim outlook, suggesting this summer's heat may be a precursor to future, even hotter, seasons, impacting daily life and public health.
