Ukraine's Navy, despite lacking traditional ships, has achieved remarkable success using powerful sea drones, damaging or sinking numerous Russian vessels and effectively gaining control of the Sea of Azov. While military analysts believe Ukraine is not yet ready for a ground offensive, President Zelenskyy emphasizes preserving lives over immediate recapture, highlighting the strategic shift in the conflict.
