Robicheaux, who previously faced and successfully fought high-profile sexual assault charges, had pleaded guilty to a felony count of possessing an assault rifle and a misdemeanor count of possessing psilocybin. The medical board alleges that he failed to check in for required drug tests more than a dozen times in 2025, declined random drug testing on four occasions, and did not attend any substance abuse meetings in December 2025. Furthermore, he reportedly ceased making monthly payments to the board after September 2025. His attorney had previously stated that Robicheaux's life was devastated by the initial criminal case.
