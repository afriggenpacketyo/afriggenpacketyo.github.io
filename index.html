Experts Advocate for 'Human in the Loop' Approach to AI Integration in Healthcare

2026-07-30 16:00:00 EDT

Experts are emphasizing the "human in the loop" principle for the ethical integration of artificial intelligence in healthcare, aiming to bridge the gap between patient trust and technological advancement. While many clinicians are adopting AI for tasks like note-taking and diagnostics, patients express reservations about AI operating autonomously. This approach ensures that human oversight remains central to AI-driven medical decisions, fostering greater comfort and confidence among patients. Open communication between doctors and patients about AI usage and data protection is crucial for building trust and ensuring that AI serves as a supportive tool rather than a replacement for human judgment.

CDs vs. High-Yield Savings Accounts: Choosing the Best Option for August 2026 Savers

2026-07-30 15:59:02 EDT

As savers head into August 2026, the choice between Certificates of Deposit (CDs) and high-yield savings accounts depends on individual financial goals and market outlook. CDs offer fixed interest rates, providing predictability and security in an unpredictable economic climate, with current rates significantly higher than in previous years. High-yield savings accounts, conversely, offer flexibility and the potential to benefit from anticipated Federal Reserve rate hikes, which could increase earnings. While both accounts protect principal and offer competitive interest rates, CDs lock in funds, whereas savings accounts allow for easier access to money. Savers may even consider opening both to balance security with flexibility.
