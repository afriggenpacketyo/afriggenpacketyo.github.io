Joe Swanberg's romantic comedy, "The Sun Never Sets," explores the intricate dynamics of post-breakup dating through the character of Wendy (Dakota Fanning), a 31-year-old construction worker in Anchorage, Alaska. The article praises the film for its naturalistic approach to depicting the challenges of finding a partner and the relatable complexities of navigating relationships. While commending Fanning's skilled portrayal of a reactive protagonist caught between multiple ex-lovers and new suitors, the review ultimately criticizes the movie's concluding "happy ending" as a betrayal of its otherwise honest and nuanced exploration of love's capricious nature. The film effectively illustrates how a discarded partner can suddenly regain allure once others express interest, adding layers to its romantic dilemma.

60/100
