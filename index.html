Understanding the 2026 Autumn Equinox and its Astronomical Significance

2026-09-21 20:05:52 EDT

The 2026 autumn equinox will occur in the early hours of Wednesday, September 23rd, marking the astronomical beginning of autumn. This precise moment signifies when the Sun crosses the celestial equator, resulting in roughly equal lengths of day and night across the globe. While the equinox itself is invisible, it holds cultural and spiritual importance for many, with gatherings often taking place at sites like Stonehenge. Due to light refraction in the atmosphere, the actual "equilux," when day and night are truly equal, happens a few days after the equinox, around September 25th or 26th in the UK, depending on latitude.
