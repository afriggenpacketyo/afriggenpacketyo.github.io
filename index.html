2026-09-22 18:26:04 EDT

As part of the settlement that approved Paramount's $111 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, an editorial oversight board will be established to monitor adherence to journalistic independence for CNN and CBS News. This board's appointees will be drawn from Paramount's leadership, leading critics to question its actual independence. While similar oversight mechanisms have been implemented in media ownership changes, concerns remain about their effectiveness. The creation of this board aims to address regulatory concerns, but its impact on the editorial direction of CNN and CBS News, particularly in light of recent changes and relationships with influential figures like President Trump, remains uncertain.
