This concept explains how prices in an economy can remain fixed for periods, even as economic conditions change, significantly impacting inflation and recession management. Their contributions helped revive Keynesian economics, which emphasizes government intervention to stabilize the economy, by providing a more robust theoretical framework grounded in individual decision-making. The prize committee used the analogy of an ice cream menu to illustrate how firms set prices, highlighting that these prices don't adjust continuously. This work has reshaped macroeconomic theory and policy approaches.
