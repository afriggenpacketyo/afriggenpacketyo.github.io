UN Calls for Adoption of New World Map to Correct Continental Size Distortions

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The United Nations has advocated for replacing the widely used Mercator map with the Equal Earth projection, aiming to accurately represent continent sizes, especially Africa, which appears significantly smaller than its actual scale on Mercator maps. While the Mercator projection was historically vital for navigation due to its accurate compass bearings, it inherently distorts landmass sizes further from the equator. The Equal Earth map, already adopted by the African Union, rectifies these size inaccuracies but does so by sacrificing the straight lines and true distance measurement that made Mercator maps useful for sailors. This global shift acknowledges the psychological and geopolitical impact of map representations, striving for a more equitable and accurate perception of the world's geography.
