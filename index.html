Turkey's habit of hoarding gold is hindering economic stability, government states

2026-09-15 20:55:41 EDT

Turkish officials and economists are warning that the widespread cultural practice of hoarding gold and foreign currency outside the formal banking system, known as "under the pillow" savings, is detrimental to the country's economy. This behavior stems from a deep-seated distrust in institutions, fueled by a history of high inflation and currency devaluation. Households store approximately 5,000 tons of gold, valued at around $600 billion, which is not being lent to businesses and contributes to the current account deficit. While the government has introduced incentives to encourage citizens to deposit their gold, these efforts have largely failed due to ongoing skepticism about economic policies and institutional credibility. Restoring trust is seen as essential for stabilizing the economy and attracting foreign investment.
