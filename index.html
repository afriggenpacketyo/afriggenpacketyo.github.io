Russia Strikes Second Major Bridge in Kyiv Amid Escalating Attacks

2026-10-03 07:48:41 EDT

Russian forces have targeted a second major bridge in Ukraine's capital, Kyiv, according to Mayor Vitaliy Klitschko. The attack on Saturday morning injured two people and damaged the road surface and overhead trolleybus wires on the Pivnichnyi (Northern) Bridge, causing traffic disruptions. This follows multiple Russian drone strikes on the Pivdennyi (Southern) Bridge earlier in the week, signaling a new phase in Moscow's strategy to target critical infrastructure. The bridges are vital for connecting the eastern and western parts of the city, impacting daily commutes for millions of residents.
