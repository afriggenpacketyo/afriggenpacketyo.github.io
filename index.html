Shelton, who reached his first Grand Slam final at the US Open, seeks to improve his consistency and performance against elite players. Ferrero, known for his astute tactical approach, will work alongside Shelton's father and current coach, Bryan, with a focus on refining Shelton's game for major tournaments, including all four Grand Slams next season.
