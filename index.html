2026-10-20 17:52:49 EDT

Lieutenant General Joe Hilbert, commander of the US Eighth Army in South Korea, stated that his troops must be ready for deployment beyond the Korean peninsula if ordered, arguing that it would be "irresponsible" to focus "myopically" solely on threats there. He emphasized that while his command's primary focus remains fulfilling US commitments under its mutual defense treaty with Seoul, the Eighth Army's strategic location does not limit the scope of missions it could undertake. Hilbert cited the Eighth Army's active participation in multinational exercises across the Asia-Pacific region as a clear example of its broader activities and readiness to serve as part of a "global force" wherever needed by national command authority.
