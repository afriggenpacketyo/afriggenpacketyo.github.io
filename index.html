2026-10-06 23:57:52 EDT

California Republican gubernatorial candidate Steve Hilton expressed support for tax cuts and proposed a significant housing reform plan during a town hall, while also differentiating himself from President Trump on certain policies. Hilton criticized federal funding cuts and the administration's approach to states' rights, advocating for decentralizing power. His 'Califordable' plan includes eliminating income taxes for those earning $150,000 or less, slashing oil industry regulations, and promoting a boom in single-family home construction. He also voiced strong opposition to offshore wind projects, deeming them 'grotesque.' Hilton emphasized his pragmatic, problem-solving approach and willingness to work across the aisle.
