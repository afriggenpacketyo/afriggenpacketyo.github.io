Maine's new Democratic Senate nominee, Troy Jackson, is launching his general election campaign with a laser focus on incumbent Republican Senator Susan Collins, a strategy that contrasts sharply with his predecessor, Graham Platner. Jackson, a progressive fifth-generation logger and former state Senate president, is emphasizing Collins' 30-year tenure and her voting record, particularly her alignment with President Trump "96 percent of the time." This article highlights Jackson's commitment to traditional Democratic messaging, aiming to unite the party and appeal to moderate and independent voters by addressing issues like Collins' perceived corruption and her stance on ICE actions. Despite a significant fundraising disparity, with Collins holding a substantial financial advantage, Democrats are determined to unseat the long-serving senator in November, believing Jackson has the right message to win.

AI Industry's Super PACs Unfazed by Backlash, Aim to Reshape AI Politics

2026-07-26 07:00:00 EDT

Leading the Future (LTF), a powerful AI industry super PAC, is reportedly unfazed by public backlash and controversy, aiming to reshape AI politics and marginalize technology skeptics. This article details how LTF, co-led by seasoned political veterans Josh Moffatt and Mike Vlasto and modeled after successful pro-crypto PACs, has spent millions to defeat AI safety proponents, sparking significant criticism from within the tech industry and political circles. LTF seeks to influence AI policy across both parties, backing pro-business candidates and pushing for a national framework of AI rules. Despite facing reputational damage from controversial tactics like "sockpuppet" meme accounts, the PAC, described as having seemingly limitless cash, is committed to a long-term fight to define America's future in AI, viewing the upcoming elections as just "the first inning."
