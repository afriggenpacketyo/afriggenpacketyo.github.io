AstraZeneca, whose share price has quadrupled during CEO Pascal Soriot's 14-year tenure, has demonstrated strong growth, particularly driven by its successful cancer and rare disease drugs. The reported talks occur years after AstraZeneca successfully fended off a takeover bid from larger U.S. rival Pfizer, underscoring its continued strategic ambitions and pursuit of market leadership in a highly competitive industry.
