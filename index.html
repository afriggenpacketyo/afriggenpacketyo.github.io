A ceasefire, brokered by First Lady Melania Trump, has begun in the Russian-occupied Ukrainian city of Oleshky, allowing for humanitarian aid to reach stranded residents facing severe shortages. The temporary truce is intended to facilitate the delivery of essential supplies and a civilian evacuation. However, Russia has continued its overnight attacks on other Ukrainian cities, including Kyiv and Bryansk, resulting in casualties. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy described these actions as Russia's "constant steps toward escalation." The developments precede further trilateral peace talks between Ukraine, Russia, and the U.S., with security guarantees being a key point of discussion.
