Understanding Travel Insurance Coverage for Weather-Related Flight Disruptions

2026-11-11 08:17:51 EST

Travel insurance can provide valuable protection for flight delays and cancellations caused by severe weather, though coverage details vary by policy. Trip delay benefits may reimburse reasonable expenses like meals, lodging, and transportation incurred during a qualifying delay, which typically has a minimum duration. Trip cancellation or interruption coverage, meanwhile, protects prepaid, nonrefundable costs if severe weather prevents or derails the broader trip. Travelers must carefully review policy definitions of severe weather, required waiting periods, and reimbursement limits. Importantly, insurance is designed for unforeseen events, meaning purchasing coverage after a storm has been named usually precludes claims related to that event.
