The article emphasizes that this evolution in coverage is a direct response to the era's intensely competitive and "existential" political climate, alongside a broader public distrust in media. Innovative tools such as CNN's Sightline and Fox News' AI-powered app will provide personalized access to election data, aiming to foster greater trust by openly demonstrating the intricate processes behind election projections, despite the potential for volatile early returns.
