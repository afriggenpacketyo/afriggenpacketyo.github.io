This news comes after Wendy's reported its sixth consecutive quarter of same-store sales declines, a performance that has allowed Restaurant Brands International's Burger King to surpass it as the second-largest US burger chain by system sales. Trian, which owns a substantial 7.85% stake in Wendy's and has board representation, previously explored a takeover in 2022, signaling renewed interest in turning around the company's performance.
