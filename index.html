2026-11-09 05:00:00 EST

Despite significant Democratic victories in the midterm elections, particularly on immigration issues in states like Texas, federal immigration enforcement remains robustly funded. This article highlights that while voters, especially the Hispanic community, expressed dissatisfaction with anti-immigrant policies, Congress allocated approximately $240 billion to ICE and Customs and Border Patrol for 2025 and 2026. This substantial funding supports mass deportation operations for the remainder of Trump's term. While incoming Democrats aim for increased oversight, accountability, and reforms like body cameras and banning face coverings for agents, President Trump's promotion of Stephen Miller as chief of staff signals a continued commitment to aggressive tactics. This poses a major hurdle to policy changes, indicating an ongoing clash between legislative intent and executive action.
