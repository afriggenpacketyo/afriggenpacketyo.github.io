Andrew Power, CEO of Digital Realty, believes that a potential slowdown in AI development will not significantly impact the long-term demand for data center real estate. He argues that substantial digital transformation and cloud computing growth, independent of AI, will continue to drive demand. Power notes that hyperscalers have had to balance allocating capacity between commercial cloud businesses and AI labs, and that demand in key markets is already outpacing supply. Analysts concur that the growth in data centers will largely stem from AI inference and broader business adoption, even if model development slows. Iran's 'Woman, Life, Freedom' movement persists despite war and regime suppression

2026-09-15 08:27:00 EDT

The 'Woman, Life, Freedom' movement in Iran continues to resonate despite the ongoing war and a violent crackdown by the regime. The movement, sparked by the death of Jina Mahsa Amini in police custody, initially aimed to end the hijab mandate and state violence. While these goals have not been fully achieved, activists believe the protests have fundamentally changed societal awareness and empowered individuals. Even amidst the current conflict, parts of the Iranian regime are still debating stricter punishments for hijab violations. Young Iranians are increasingly defying regulations, establishing a new 'normal' of personal freedom regarding dress. Bangladesh court sentences seven Hasina allies to death over 2024 protest crackdown

2026-09-15 08:21:00 EDT

A court in Bangladesh has sentenced seven senior figures from former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina's Awami League party to death in absentia for their alleged roles in the deadly crackdown on anti-government protests in 2024. The convictions relate to crimes against humanity, including inciting killings during the uprising that led to Hasina's ouster. The United Nations estimates that around 1,400 people died during the unrest. The seven individuals, who remain at large, include former party officials. This verdict brings the total number of convictions related to the uprising to 68, with 22 death sentences issued. The proceedings are part of ongoing efforts to hold those responsible for the violence accountable. (not investment advice)
