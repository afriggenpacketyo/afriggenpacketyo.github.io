2026-08-02 06:55:43 EDT

President Donald Trump announced the cancellation of planned strikes on Iran, stating that an agreement is close to being reached. He indicated on Truth Social that Iran and other Middle Eastern countries requested the pause, contingent on a deal that would include the immediate and complete opening of the Strait of Hormuz and an end to Iran's nuclear threat. Trump also mentioned Israel's support for the pause, pending a rapid agreement. This decision follows reports of intensified U.S. preparations for strikes and comes amid ongoing diplomatic efforts, with Iran's foreign minister engaging in calls with regional counterparts. Saudi Arabia has urged prioritizing dialogue to de-escalate tensions.
