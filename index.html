Culling of invasive mule deer on Catalina Island begins

2026-09-09 23:23:40 EDT

The Catalina Island Conservancy has confirmed that the culling of approximately 200 invasive mule deer has commenced this week. This controversial project, part of an ecosystem restoration plan, involves contracted hunters using rifles to reduce the deer population, which has been damaging native flora and fauna since their introduction in the 1920s. Legal challenges to halt the culling were unsuccessful, with a judge denying a request for a preliminary injunction. The conservancy aims to test methods for future, larger-scale eradication efforts over the next four to five years, with some deer carcasses intended to feed endangered condors and foxes.
