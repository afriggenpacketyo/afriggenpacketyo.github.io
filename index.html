This resolution is deemed an "important victory" by twigs' attorney, allowing her to freely discuss her experiences and advocate for other women who have faced sexual abuse. This article highlights a significant step in the ongoing legal and public discourse surrounding celebrity sexual assault allegations and NDAs, ensuring greater transparency and support for survivors.
