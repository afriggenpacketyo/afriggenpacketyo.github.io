2026-09-10 17:16:26 EDT

California Governor Gavin Newsom signed over ten bills aimed at making social media and AI chatbots safer for young people, addressing concerns about mental and physical health harms. Key legislation includes Senate Bill 1119, known as Adam's Law, which mandates risk assessments and parental notifications for chatbot operators if a child threatens self-harm, inspired by a teen's suicide after conversing with ChatGPT. Another bill, Assembly Bill 1709, will bar "addictive features" like autoplay for users under 16. While tech industry groups oppose these measures citing First Amendment concerns, California aims to lead in regulating technology for child safety.
