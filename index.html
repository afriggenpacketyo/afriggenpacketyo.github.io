2026-10-21 19:11:06 EDT

Harry Kane delivered a remarkable performance in Bayern Munich's 2-2 Champions League draw against Arsenal, further bolstering his Ballon d'Or credentials. Kane showcased his versatility by launching an 80-yard pass from a goal kick and later provided a crucial assist and scored a penalty. His contributions have been vital to Bayern's success under manager Vincent Kompany, who has granted him significant trust. Kane's goal against Arsenal marked his 16th career goal against the club and his 10th consecutive Champions League match with a goal, a feat matched only by Cristiano Ronaldo. His exceptional form has positioned him as a strong contender for the prestigious individual award.
