2026-10-03 03:37:38 EDT

Robert Lewandowski scored an impressive hat-trick, propelling Poland to a dominant 6-0 victory over 10-man Romania in the Nations League, increasing his international goal tally to 92. In the same group, former Manchester City forward Edin Dzeko emotionally retired from international football following Bosnia-Herzegovina's 1-1 draw against Sweden. Dzeko was celebrated with fireworks, a guard of honor, and a standing ovation for his 152nd and final international appearance. The article also briefly notes other Nations League results, including Kevin de Bruyne's two goals in Belgium's 3-0 win against Turkey and Hungary securing their first campaign victory against Georgia.
