UN Calls for New World Map Projection to Accurately Reflect Continent Sizes

2026-09-05 12:14:24 EDT

The UN General Assembly has adopted the "Correct the Map" resolution, advocating for the replacement of the widely used Mercator projection with the Equal Earth projection. The Mercator map, created in 1569 to aid European sailors, distorts landmass sizes, making continents near the poles appear much larger than they are, while those near the equator, like Africa, appear significantly smaller. Critics argue this distortion has psychological and geopolitical implications, implicitly downplaying the importance of vast regions. The Equal Earth map aims to rectify these size discrepancies, though it sacrifices the Mercator's accurate straight lines for navigation.
