2026-10-20 10:01:49 EDT

Options market-makers are pricing in an approximate 6% swing for SpaceX's stock following its earnings report after the bell on Tuesday. This expected move is less than half the 13% drop seen after its inaugural report in August, which proved to be a low point before a 60% rally. Despite upcoming share lockup periods, daily trading volume has shown a bullish lean towards call contracts in the past week. Traders are actively buying calls, with notable interest in higher strike prices for month-end, indicating expectations for a significant rally. Australian Basketballer Joe Ingles Reflects on NBA Career and Life Changes

2026-10-20 10:00:37 EDT

Australian basketballer Joe Ingles, 39, has achieved a significant personal milestone, surpassing 1,500 days without alcohol since a serious knee injury in 2022. Now playing in the NBL after 12 seasons in the NBA, Ingles describes his abstinence as an easy adjustment, initially part of his recovery but now a permanent lifestyle choice. He and his wife, Renae, note that their decision often makes others uncomfortable, reflecting a cultural norm around drinking in Australia. Ingles remains engaged with the NBA, offering insights on players like Ben Simmons and Josh Giddey, and is feeling '21' as he continues his career.
