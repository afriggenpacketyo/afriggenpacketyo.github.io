The alleged attack occurred in Belmarsh Prison on January 1, 2025, during which Sharif reportedly sustained cuts to his face and received medical treatment. Urfan Sharif, 43, along with Sara's stepmother and uncle, was convicted at the Old Bailey for murdering 10-year-old Sara after a "campaign of torture" lasting two years. Sansom and Watson are scheduled to appear at Westminster Magistrates' Court for their first hearing on August 26, facing these serious charges.
