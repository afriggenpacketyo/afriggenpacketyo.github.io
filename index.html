New DNA Analysis Strengthens Evidence of Jefferson Fathering Children with Sally Hemings

2026-09-22 06:00:00 EDT

New DNA analysis, detailed in Richard Kurin's book "History by a Hair," provides stronger evidence that Thomas Jefferson fathered children with Sally Hemings, an enslaved woman. The study involved sequencing Jefferson's DNA from a lock of hair and comparing it with descendants of Hemings' sons, Madison and Eston. Results showed Hemings descendants possessed more Jefferson DNA than some known Jefferson descendants, suggesting a paternal link. This finding addresses long-standing historical debates about Jefferson's legacy, slavery, and race, though questions about the nature of their relationship—coercive versus consensual—remain subjects of discussion. The research aims to provide scientific clarity on this pivotal historical question.
