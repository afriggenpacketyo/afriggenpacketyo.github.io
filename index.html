Iowa Residents Brace for Controlled Bomb Blast Amid Public Outcry and Confusion

2026-11-24 16:49:07 EST

Residents across Iowa, Illinois, and Missouri are bracing for a controlled detonation of 68 barrels of dangerously unstable lead azide at an Iowa ammunition plant, scheduled for Wednesday. Despite public outcry, lawsuits, and political wrangling, officials deemed an in-place detonation the only viable option due to the material's instability. The chaotic planning, with shifting schedules and unclear toxin mitigation advice, has frustrated locals who received only five days' warning, particularly ahead of Thanksgiving travel. The explosion risks releasing toxic chemicals in a 100-mile radius, raising concerns about agricultural contamination and long-term health impacts, and prompting questions about mismanagement and transparency.

President Trump Awards Medal of Freedom to Rudy Giuliani

2026-11-24 16:42:06 EST

President Donald Trump awarded the Medal of Freedom, the nation's highest civilian honor, to former New York City Mayor Rudy Giuliani at the White House on Tuesday. Giuliani expressed being "honored and humbled," sharing credit with those who worked with him, including those who lost their lives. White House spokesperson Olivia Wales praised Giuliani's "lifetime of service" and decisive leadership in rooting out crime in New York City and his efforts in the aftermath of 9/11. This award follows Trump's previous announcement and pardon of Giuliani for alleged involvement in attempts to overturn the 2020 election, after Giuliani was disbarred in two jurisdictions in 2024 for "false and misleading" statements. The ceremony was not open to the White House press pool, adding to its controversial nature.
