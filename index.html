United Nations Endorses New Map Projection for Accurate Global Representation

2026-09-06 03:29:22 EDT

The United Nations General Assembly has officially endorsed the Equal Earth projection, a new world map designed to more accurately represent the true proportions of continents, particularly highlighting Africa's actual size. This resolution, spearheaded by Togo and supported by the African Union, received overwhelming approval from 164 member states, with only the U.S. voting against it. The initiative encourages governments and educational institutions worldwide to transition from the traditional Mercator map, which has long distorted landmasses by exaggerating areas near the poles while diminishing continents like Africa. Proponents emphasize that accurate cartography is crucial for shaping global understanding, education, and collective perceptions, making this a significant step towards rectifying historical geographical misrepresentations.
