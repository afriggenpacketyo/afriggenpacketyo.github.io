Historic Mt. Waterman Ski Resort Relisted for $3.3 Million After Previous Deal Collapses

2026-10-24 15:57:20 EDT

The historic Mt. Waterman ski resort near Los Angeles has been relisted for $3.275 million after a previous acquisition deal collapsed due to numerous obstacles, including a government shutdown and damage to a ski lift from a December 2025 Pineapple Express storm. The former buyers, an investment group, had grand plans for "country-club style" upgrades but reportedly failed to secure necessary permits and funding, also citing undisclosed issues with existing assets. With a new transferable 20-year special use permit now firmly in place, the listing agent is optimistic about finding the right buyer for the 390-acre resort, known for its steep slopes, especially as California braces for a potentially snow-rich El Niño winter.
