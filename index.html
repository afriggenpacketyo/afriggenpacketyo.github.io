2026-10-08 10:42:00 EDT

EU Trade Commissioner Maros Sefcovic is in Beijing for talks aimed at addressing the bloc's significant trade deficit with China. Sefcovic emphasized the need to "rebalance EU-China trade" and stressed the importance of tangible outcomes from the discussions. The EU faces an "unsustainable" trade deficit, with many in Europe attributing it to China's alleged unfair trade practices, a claim Beijing rejects. Discussions will also cover a dispute over hybrid car exports and potential EU measures like quotas and tariffs. German Chancellor Friedrich Merz and French President Emmanuel Macron have called for tougher EU measures against unfair trade practices, highlighting concerns about European industrial competitiveness and economic dependencies on China. India faces water and power shortages after driest monsoon in 11 years

2026-10-08 10:40:00 EDT

India is experiencing significant water and power stress following its driest monsoon in 11 years, impacting agriculture and energy generation. Farmers like Ramesh Reddy have suffered substantial financial losses due to crop failures from drought. The monsoon deficit, attributed to a severe El Nino, has left major reservoirs with below-average water levels, affecting hydropower output and leading to electricity shortages. Coal-fired power plants face critically low stock levels, prompting government orders for industrial plants to feed surplus electricity into the grid.
