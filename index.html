A remarkable rescue operation in Tacoma, Washington, has successfully saved sixty cats and kittens from an abandoned 40-foot sailboat found anchored off Owen Beach. Police initially responded to reports of the vessel earlier this month, discovering dozens of malnourished and frightened felines living in extremely unsafe conditions on board. This article details the week-long, painstaking effort by law enforcement and Tacoma Animal Control crews, who diligently located and removed all visible animals, though the search for any potential stragglers continues. The incident is now the subject of an active criminal investigation, with authorities planning to relocate the sailboat if its owner fails to claim it.

UN Reports Ukraine's Civilian Casualties Reached Highest Monthly Level Since Early 2022 Amid Intensified Attacks

2026-08-13 08:25:00 EDT

The UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine has reported a grim milestone: civilian casualties in July climbed to their highest monthly level since March 2022, with at least 437 individuals killed and 2,610 injured. This article highlights a significant 30% increase from June and a 70% rise compared to July 2025, attributing the surge primarily to intensified Russian attacks utilizing long-range missiles and drones, particularly targeting urban centers. It also notes Ukrainian drone strikes on major Russian oil refineries, forcing one facility to shut down for several months. The report starkly underscores the escalating human cost and the continued strategic targeting of infrastructure in the ongoing, brutal conflict.
