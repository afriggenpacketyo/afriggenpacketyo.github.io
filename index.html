2026-09-16 05:00:00 EDT

The story of Maud Stevens Wagner, a Kansas native who joined the circus and became a pioneering tattoo artist at the turn of the 20th century, is highlighted for its impact on tattoo history. Wagner, who adopted the name Maud Stevens, forged a unique path as an entertainer and artist during a time with limited career options for women. Her striking tattoos, captured in iconic photographs, have become a significant part of tattoo iconography. Alongside her husband, Gus Wagner, she is credited with popularizing tattooing in middle America. The preservation of their extensive collection of tattoo memorabilia provides invaluable insight into early American tattooing practices and their legacy.

AI-Generated Political Ads Proliferate in U.S. Campaign Season

2026-09-16 05:00:00 EDT

Artificial intelligence-generated political advertisements are becoming increasingly common across the U.S. as the campaign season progresses, appearing in both high-profile and down-ballot races. These ads, often satirical or parodic, use AI to create manipulated images or videos of candidates, sometimes distorting their stances or appearance. While some states have enacted laws requiring disclosure of AI use, the current trend leans towards "slopaganda" or political cartoons rather than deceptive deepfakes. Experts suggest AI is a new tool for political messaging, facilitating exaggerated portrayals of opponents. The unpredictable impact of these lifelike AI-generated visuals on voters remains a significant concern for election integrity.
