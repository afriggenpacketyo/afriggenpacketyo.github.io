A covert cybersecurity operation has successfully secured tens of thousands of refrigeration devices across major UK supermarkets, warehouses, and other food-related businesses from potential hacking threats. The operation, involving researchers, IT teams, and the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC), addressed a significant vulnerability in widely used refrigeration controllers. Recent cyber-attacks in Europe have led to substantial food spoilage and financial damage. The UK's proactive measures, including taking devices offline and monitoring temperatures manually, aimed to prevent similar disruptions. While the manufacturer of the controllers disputes the severity of the vulnerability, they have been praised for their response to the identified security holes.
