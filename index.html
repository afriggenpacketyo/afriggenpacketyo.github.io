A D.C. superior court judge has ordered the Kennedy Center to pay over $250,000 in legal fees to jazz musician Chuck Redd. Redd canceled his annual Christmas Eve performance last December in protest after President Trump's name was added to the arts center's facade. The Kennedy Center had initially vowed to sue Redd for $1 million, but the judge dismissed their breach of contract suit, granting his motion under the district's anti-SLAPP statute, a law designed to protect public interest speech. The article highlights that Trump, who ousted the Kennedy Center's previous leadership, remains chairman, and the future of the complex's public programming and educational activities remains uncertain amid a federal court order for updates on renovation plans and public access.
