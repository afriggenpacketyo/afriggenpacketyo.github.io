The liberal justices, however, argued that the schools were seeking a right to discriminate rather than equal treatment. Colorado's law requires participating schools to adhere to an 'equal opportunity' rule, forbidding discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity. This case is the latest in a series of Supreme Court rulings on religion in public life, building on precedents that strengthen religious freedom protections.
