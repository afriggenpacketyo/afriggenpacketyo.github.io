2026-08-18 09:51:25 EDT

A consortium that includes Amazon founder Jeff Bezos has acquired a substantial minority stake, close to 40%, in Liverpool Football Club from Fenway Sports Group (FSG), a larger percentage than initially disclosed. The investment group, named 1892 Holdings and spearheaded by British-Indian millionaire businessman Amit Bhatia, also counts Facebook co-founder Eduardo Saverin among its members. Pending regulatory approval, Bhatia is set to become Liverpool's vice-chairman and join an expanded board. Crucially, the agreement incorporates an option for the consortium to potentially purchase a controlling stake in the renowned football club within the next 12 months, although this specific provision does not yet represent a formal commitment.

Alex Albon Extends Contract with Williams F1 Team for 2027 Season

2026-08-18 09:48:11 EDT

Alex Albon has committed to the Williams Formula 1 team for the 2027 season, signing a one-year contract extension. Albon, who joined Williams in 2022 and has become their longest-serving driver, opted to remain despite the team's recent performance decline this season, following a strong fifth-place championship finish in 2025. His decision is partly attributed to a scarcity of convincing alternative drives among the top F1 teams and a continued belief in team principal James Vowles' ongoing transformation project. The short-term deal offers Albon strategic flexibility, allowing him to re-evaluate his future if Williams does not achieve the anticipated performance improvements next year.

Earl Spencer to Release New Book Detailing Princess Diana's Life from His Perspective

2026-08-18 09:45:03 EDT

Earl Spencer, Princess Diana's brother, is preparing to publish a new book titled "Swan Song: Diana, My Sister," which will recount his sister's life story from his unique perspective, nearly 30 years after her tragic death. Spencer stated his intention to speak "on Diana's behalf" in an "openly positive way," aiming to correct long-held misconceptions and offer a truthful portrayal of who she truly was. He hopes the article will help readers understand Diana as a "one-off, dynamic character, full of love, charisma and self-doubt," who significantly impacted the world despite her untimely passing at age 36. The book's release coincides with the approaching 30th anniversary of her death.
