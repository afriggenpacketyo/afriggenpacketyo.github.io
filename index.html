Electric vehicles may be causing increased motion sickness for some passengers

2026-10-02 22:36:19 EDT

A growing number of passengers are reporting experiencing heightened motion sickness when traveling in electric vehicles (EVs), a phenomenon that some experts attribute to the unique engineering of these cars. Unlike traditional vehicles, EVs lack the auditory cues and vibrations from an engine, which can disrupt the sensory input our bodies use to detect movement. Additionally, regenerative braking, a process where EVs slow down by converting kinetic energy back into battery power, can cause sudden deceleration that contributes to the feeling of being rocked back and forth. While many EV drivers do not report this issue, car manufacturers are exploring solutions like smoother acceleration software and subtle auditory cues to mitigate the problem.
