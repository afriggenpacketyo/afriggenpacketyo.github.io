While Salazar generally aligns with the Trump administration's agenda, she has taken a more moderate stance on immigration, supporting a pathway to legal status for undocumented immigrants alongside border enforcement. Arias also advocated for border security and deportations, but emphasized a more humane approach, prioritizing violent criminals. The district, which has a significant foreign-born population, will see Salazar face the Democratic nominee in the general election.
