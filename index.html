2026-07-28 19:15:37 EDT

Users of Anthropic's Claude chatbot are being advised to review their privacy settings after some chat conversations were found to be discoverable on search engines like Google and Bing. The issue arose because users could create publicly shareable links to their chat threads, which, if not properly configured with a "noindex" tag, could be indexed by search engines. This discovery highlights the fragility of "anyone with a link" sharing and the potential for inadvertent oversharing of sensitive information. Users are encouraged to be cautious when creating shareable links and to unpublish chats or documents if necessary to protect their privacy.

Seattle Mourns Victims of Fatal Festival Shooting

2026-07-28 19:12:09 EDT

Seattle is in mourning following a deadly street gunfight at the annual Bite of Seattle food festival that resulted in three deaths and several injuries. A candlelight vigil was held for the victims, identified as Junior Cee Niko Semo, 19, Carlos Israel Sanchez Villalba, 44, and Ashley Whitehead, 56. Online fundraisers for Villalba and Whitehead have garnered significant donations to help their families with funeral expenses and support. Mayor Katie Wilson condemned the gun violence, calling it "not acceptable," and the community is struggling to comprehend the tragedy. Police suspect gang involvement and are investigating the incident as a "targeted" act.
