Highly Contagious Bird Flu Detected in Wisconsin Backyard Flock; Birds Euthanized

2026-11-13 00:20:20 EST

A highly contagious strain of avian influenza (HPAI) has been confirmed in a backyard poultry flock in Rusk County, Wisconsin. State officials have euthanized all the birds on the property to prevent further spread of the disease. The H5N1 strain has been circulating among wild and domestic birds in North America since December 2021. While the virus is primarily a concern for poultry, it can sometimes spread to humans. The Wisconsin Department of Agriculture, Trade and Consumer Protection is urging poultry owners to implement strict biosecurity measures to protect their flocks.
