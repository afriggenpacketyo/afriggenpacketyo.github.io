2026-10-27 06:00:00 EDT

The California coast is experiencing unprecedented flooding, with sea levels reaching record highs in areas like San Pedro, primarily driven by record-hot ocean temperatures exacerbated by an intensely strong El Niño. Scientists explain that warmer water expands, causing sea levels to rise, with current levels nearly two feet higher than astronomical tides alone would predict. This "El Niño Macho," potentially comparable to or larger than the 1997 event, combined with a foot of sea-level rise over 125 years due to climate change, is causing significant damage and ecological concerns. Experts warn of even higher tides around upcoming holidays, increasing the risk of severe coastal flooding.
