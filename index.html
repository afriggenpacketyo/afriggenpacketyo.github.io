Khan's political party, Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI), and his family have consistently voiced grave concerns about his deteriorating health since his imprisonment in 2023 on what they claim are politically motivated charges. The Supreme Court's order also included provisions for Khan to meet his personal physician and have regular contact with his sons.
