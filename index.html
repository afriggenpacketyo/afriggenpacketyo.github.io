Natalie Harp, a 35-year-old executive assistant, has become one of President Donald Trump's most frequent and loyal aides, often seen by his side and trusted with typing his Truth Social posts. Harp rose to prominence after crediting Trump's "Right to Try Act" with saving her life during her battle with bone cancer, leading to her involvement in his 2020 re-election campaign. Described as a "gatekeeper" and "comfort blanket," her role extends beyond typical secretarial duties, influencing the information Trump receives. Her presence gained recent spotlight when Senator Jon Ossoff criticized her reported presence on a secret flight with Trump, prompting a strong defense from the White House.
