While the exact cause is not fully understood, an FDA analysis of clinical trials indicated a slightly higher incidence of NAION in patients taking GLP-1 drugs compared to those on a placebo. Patients experiencing symptoms such as blurred vision or blind spots are advised to contact a healthcare professional immediately.
