A rare first edition of Harper Lee's classic novel, 'To Kill a Mockingbird,' has been found among donations at an Oxfam bookshop in London. The 1960 U.S. first edition was identified by the shop manager and has since been acquired by the British Library for its national collection. The charity highlighted the discovery as part of its "Second Hand September" campaign, emphasizing the potential for valuable treasures to be found among donated items. The book's journey from a donation to a prestigious national library underscores the enduring significance of literary works and the unexpected finds possible in second-hand bookshops.

Chinese state media emphasizes 'constructive strategic stability' for Xi-Trump summit

2026-09-22 06:11:30 EDT

Chinese state media, including the Communist Party's People's Daily, has set a positive tone for the upcoming summit between President Xi Jinping and U.S. President Donald Trump, emphasizing the goal of "constructive strategic stability." The article suggests that the leaders will provide "strategic guidance" to foster a stable bilateral relationship, building on progress made during Trump's previous visit to Beijing. This framing indicates China's desire to manage tensions and promote predictability in ties with the U.S. The emphasis on stability ahead of the high-stakes meeting suggests an effort to de-escalate potential conflicts and find common ground amidst ongoing global challenges.
