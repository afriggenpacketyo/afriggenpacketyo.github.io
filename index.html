A nearly three-decade-old missing person case has finally been resolved with the discovery of a single human bone washed ashore on Centerville Beach, bringing long-awaited closure to the family of Jonathan Raxa, a father of three. Raxa disappeared in December 1999 after his fishing boat, the Silver Spray, was tragically destroyed by a powerful 18-foot wave in Humboldt Bay, an area known for its treacherous conditions for commercial fishing. The bone, identified as a sacrum (the triangular bone at the base of the spine), was matched through DNA analysis to a sample obtained from Raxa's son three decades prior. This poignant discovery underscores the critical importance of preserving DNA samples from family members in missing person investigations, ultimately providing answers after years of profound uncertainty.

California Continues Battle Against Trump's Election Fraud Claims

2026-09-21 06:00:00 EDT

Furthermore, federal officials have declined to rule out the deployment of armed federal agents to polling places. California Secretary of State Shirley Weber has dismissed these allegations, steadfastly asserting that the state's elections are both safe and secure.
