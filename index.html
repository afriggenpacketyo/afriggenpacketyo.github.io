2026-11-21 14:43:19 EST

Peter Fluck, one of the creative minds behind the iconic satirical puppet show "Spitting Image," has passed away at the age of 85. The show, which ran from 1984 to 1996, was renowned for its sharp caricatures of politicians, celebrities, and royalty, earning multiple awards including Baftas and Emmys. Fluck's family stated that he died at home after a recent illness, remembering his unique and impactful contribution to satire. He eschewed fame and continued his creative work from his studio in Cornwall for many years.

COP31 climate talks face deadlock over fossil fuel discussions

2026-11-21 14:33:49 EST

The COP31 climate summit in Turkey has reached a deadlock, with negotiations extending significantly beyond their scheduled end time due to disagreements over a new global climate deal. A key sticking point is the absence of any commitment to reducing global fossil fuel demand in the latest draft agreement, a provision strongly advocated by the EU and Pacific island nations. Another contentious issue is the African Group's push for increased funding for adaptation measures, which the EU has indicated it will not support. The prolonged negotiations have led to many delegates rescheduling their flights, raising concerns that the summit might conclude without a deal.
