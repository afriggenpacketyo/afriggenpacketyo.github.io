A reporter reflects on the stark contrast between "two Delhis" observed during a weekend when international dignitaries visited the city for a high-profile event. One Delhi is characterized by its everyday messiness, with rubbish piled by roadsides, non-existent footpaths, chaotic traffic, and pervasive dust that affects daily life. The other is the meticulously prepared, "dressed" version of Lutyens’ Delhi, a quieter, greener stretch of tree-lined avenues and high-walled colonial bungalows where VIPs reside, showcasing a polished facade for global guests. This personal note highlights the city's inherent duality and the effort to present a particular image on the global stage, contrasting it with the often-challenging realities faced by its ordinary residents.

2026-09-19 23:57:23 EDT
