Authorities are actively investigating the motive, suspecting a sour business relationship between Zhengfeng Bo and Aikebaer, who were associates in a commercial real estate firm. Jianquan Bo has been charged with murder, and Aikebaer's family, who described him as a loving husband and father, is left bewildered and desperate for answers regarding the senseless violence.
