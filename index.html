Los Angeles County Fire Department responded to a call about an unconscious person on the night of July 5, but details regarding the individual's condition and whether it was related to the ride remain unconfirmed. Cal/OSHA has opened an inspection into the reported incident, and no further details are available as the investigation is ongoing.
