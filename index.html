Beef tallow skincare products are emerging as a significant trend, marketed as a 'natural' and uniquely compatible moisturizer due to its purported similar fatty acid profile to human skin. Despite its growing popularity, dermatologists and cosmetic chemists note a lack of modern scientific studies specifically on pure beef tallow's benefits for skin conditions. While tallow contains some lipids found in human skin, the comparison is often overstated, and trace vitamins are not comparable to formulated products. The trend is also linked to branding that appeals to a 'rugged, of-the-earth' masculinity and a general 'chemophobia.' Experts advise caution, suggesting that while unlikely to cause harm, more evidence-backed moisturizers exist. The article details one journalist's two-week experiment, noting the staying power and glow of tallow-based creams.
