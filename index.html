Pettis contends that power dynamics, rather than virtue, determine who suffers in such a crisis, suggesting China is more exposed than the U.S. due to its substantial current account surplus and low household consumption relative to GDP. This article explores the historical patterns of economic imbalances and their painful resolutions, focusing on the potential implications for major global economies. (not investment advice)
