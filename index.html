China's U.S.-Bound Shipments Decline in July, Signaling Economic Slowdown

2026-07-30 18:01:01 EDT

China's U.S.-bound shipments experienced a decline in July for the first time in several months, according to a recent survey by China Beige Book, signaling a notable slowdown in one of the economy's few remaining growth drivers. This downturn follows a brief recovery in June, which was largely attributed to businesses frontloading shipments ahead of anticipated higher U.S. tariffs. The comprehensive survey also revealed a deceleration in overall factory activity and the worst performance in manufacturing employment across all surveyed sectors. Additionally, retail sales dropped in July from both the prior month and the year-ago period, with the travel and restaurant industries experiencing a particularly sharp downturn, prompting Beijing to emphasize the urgent need to expand domestic demand and international trade cooperation.
