Hedge Funds' Growing Role in US Treasury Market Raises Stability Concerns

2026-09-29 20:19:09 EDT

Hedge funds now hold a record 7% share of the approximately $30 trillion U.S. Treasury market, stepping in as traditional investors reduce their holdings. This shift is aiding the government in finding buyers for its growing debt but also introduces potential vulnerabilities due to hedge funds' aggressive leverage. Experts warn that forced deleveraging by these funds could magnify systematic risk and lead to broader liquidity issues or financial instability. The trend is driven partly by pension funds shifting allocations away from long-term government bonds towards higher-yielding assets. Regulators have flagged concerns about hedge fund leverage and its potential to create "new financial stability vulnerabilities" in the bond market, particularly during periods of stress or market dysfunction. While hedge funds can provide liquidity, their reliance on leverage poses a systemic risk.
