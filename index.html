2026-10-15 20:34:45 EDT

This advanced technique analyzes all genetic material in a single respiratory sample, allowing for the identification of bacteria, viruses, and fungi that standard tests might miss. The test has already proven life-saving for young patients like 10-year-old Willow Crowther, whose treatment was significantly improved after metagenomics identified a resistant bacterial infection.
