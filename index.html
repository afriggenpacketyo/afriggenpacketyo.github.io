Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky announced that US intelligence sharing on Russian missile attacks "works now," following talks with President Trump's special envoy Steve Witkoff in Oslo. This statement comes after Kyiv's previous accusations of Washington withholding key intelligence, which US Secretary of State Marco Rubio had rejected as "scandalous." Finnish President Alexander Stubb reported "genuine progress" on peace proposals, which are now awaiting Russia's response during Witkoff's upcoming meeting with President Putin. The discussions, which also included Jared Kushner virtually, aimed to align US, Ukrainian, and European positions amidst strained US-Ukraine relations and Trump's repeated calls for Ukraine to "make a deal" to end the war.
