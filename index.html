2026-09-02 16:11:07 EDT

Certificate of Deposit (CD) accounts remain a credible and secure savings option with high, fixed interest rates, but savers must actively avoid specific mistakes this September for optimal success and maximum returns. Financial experts advise against opening an account before the Federal Reserve's September 16th meeting, as a likely interest rate hike could lead to even higher CD rates from banks. Additionally, it is currently more advantageous to open long-term accounts, which offer noticeably higher rates than short-term options, provided the deposited funds can remain untouched until the maturity date to avoid early withdrawal penalties. Lastly, savers should diligently explore online banks for potentially more competitive rates than their traditional brick-and-mortar institutions, thereby maximizing their potential interest earnings.
