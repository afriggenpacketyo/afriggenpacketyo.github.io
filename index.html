This opinion piece asserts that Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass's re-election campaign suffered a major setback following a notably poor debate performance against challenger Nithya Raman in Sherman Oaks. Raman, previously seen as an underdog, delivered concrete answers and deftly parried Bass's attacks, while the mayor appeared tired, exasperated, and resorted to seemingly petty criticisms. The article suggests that Bass's first term has been plagued by a series of stumbles, contributing to a widespread perception of the city as "rudderless" and lacking clear direction. Raman's strong performance, despite facing an uphill battle against the city's established power structure, offers voters a distinct choice and a compelling message of hope for a more "affordable, functional, safe, joyful, ambitious" Los Angeles, potentially reshaping the mayoral race.

Federal Probe into Mark Walter's Financial Dealings Raises Questions About Dodgers Ownership

2026-08-20 06:00:00 EDT

A federal inquiry into billionaire Mark Walter's extensive insurance empire, involving billions of dollars in undisclosed loans to his own companies, is now raising significant questions about the future ownership of the highly successful Los Angeles Dodgers. Walter's recent sale of the Lakers, just one year after acquiring the basketball franchise, has fueled intense speculation about his other prominent sports franchises. While Walter vehemently denies any wrongdoing and no charges have been formally filed, sports business experts acknowledge the considerable uncertainty this probe creates for the Dodgers, currently valued between $10 billion and $13 billion. The situation underscores the complex financial scrutiny and potential instability faced by high-profile sports owners, even those presiding over wildly successful teams, highlighting the intricate intersection of personal finance and public sports ownership.
