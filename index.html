The Trump administration has appealed to the U.S. Supreme Court to allow restrictions on mail-in voting, following a federal judge's extension of an order blocking new limits. The administration argues that the injunction risks "confusion and chaos" as states begin mailing ballots. This marks the third time the administration has sought Supreme Court intervention in the dispute over an executive order that would restrict USPS delivery of ballots to voters not on an approved federal list. Voting rights groups and Democratic states contend the changes are unconstitutional, while election officials cite insufficient time to implement new directives. The outcome could significantly impact the upcoming midterm elections, where control of Congress is at stake.
