JetBlue Airways is implementing a significant overhaul of its fare options in anticipation of the domestic launch of its BlueFirst class, introducing new tiered choices across its economy ("Main"), extra legroom ("Even More"), and first-class cabins. Travelers will now select from "Base," "Standard," and "Flex" options within each class, which offer differing benefits concerning carry-on allowances, seat selection, change/cancellation fees, and TrueBlue loyalty points accrual. The "Base" fare represents the lowest price point, whereas the "Flex" option uniquely provides refunds to the original form of payment. This restructuring replaces the former "Core" fares and aims to leverage the high consumer demand for enhanced comfort and perks, bringing JetBlue's offerings more in line with competitors who have similarly diversified their premium fare structures.
