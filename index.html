The family of Valentina Foster, a seven-year-old girl tragically killed alongside her 34-year-old aunt Natalie McDonald in a house fire in Grangetown, near Middlesbrough, has expressed profound grief, stating their lives are "changed forever" by this "devastating loss." Valentina's parents lovingly described their daughter as "deeply loved" and affirmed that her memory would eternally reside in their hearts. A man has been charged with their murders in connection with the blaze, which occurred in the early hours of August 26. The family's heartfelt tribute underscores the immense and irreplaceable loss of their cherished daughter and sister, who will be missed beyond words by all who knew her.

Manchester City Agrees £125M Fee for Chelsea Midfielder Enzo Fernandez

2026-09-01 11:55:10 EDT

Manchester City has reached an agreement to acquire Chelsea midfielder Enzo Fernandez for a fee of £125 million, which constitutes a joint-British transfer record, mirroring Liverpool's expenditure for Alexander Isak last summer. Fernandez, who initially joined Chelsea from Benfica in 2023 for £107 million, is now scheduled to undergo a medical examination before signing a long-term contract with Manchester City, where he will be reunited with manager Enzo Maresca. This transfer represents City's second record-breaking acquisition this summer, following the £116 million signing of Elliot Anderson. Fernandez's move further highlights the significant financial outlays in the Premier League, making him the fourth player this summer to command a transfer fee exceeding £100 million.
