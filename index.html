British number one Katie Boulter experienced a "bad day at the office" at the US Open, suffering a swift 6-1, 6-0 defeat in just 58 minutes against Wimbledon runner-up Karolina Muchova in the second round. Boulter struggled significantly with the match conditions and her serve, hitting eight double faults and winning only 33% of points behind her first serve, while committing 22 unforced errors. Former world number five Daniela Hantuchova suggested Boulter needs significant changes to her game, particularly her serve, to improve her performance. Despite recent personal celebrations, this marks a disappointing tennis season for Boulter, with only two wins across the four Grand Slam tournaments.
