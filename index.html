West Ham Co-Owner David Sullivan Advised to Avoid Home Matches Amid Sexual Misconduct Allegations

2026-08-03 16:09:46 EDT

West Ham co-owner David Sullivan has been formally advised by London Stadium to stay away from the club's home matches due to significant concerns about potential fan unrest following serious allegations of sexual misconduct. A joint investigation by BBC Panorama and The Times revealed that seven women have accused Sullivan of sexually exploitative and predatory behavior, allegations he has categorically denied as "factually incorrect and entirely false." Sullivan had previously stepped down as joint chairman to focus on fighting these "decades-old allegations" and was banned from contact with the club's women's and youth teams due to safeguarding concerns. Despite the recommendation, it is understood that the club expects Sullivan to defy the request and attend games.
