Virginia Senator L Louise Lucas Charged in Marijuana Enterprise Scheme

2026-10-22 16:39:16 EDT

Virginia State Senator L Louise Lucas and six other individuals, including family members, have been charged in a federal racketeering case alleging involvement in a criminal marijuana enterprise. Prosecutors accuse Lucas of overseeing the acquisition and illegal distribution of marijuana, as well as using her political influence for personal and familial gain. Lucas denies the charges, asserting they are politically motivated. While small amounts of cannabis are legal in Virginia, restrictions on sales remain. The indictment comes as early voting is underway for the midterm elections, and its timing has drawn criticism regarding the Department of Justice's policy on pre-election legal actions.
